# Kind Robots Solitaire: deck design bible

date: 2026-10-06 · task: kr-solitaire/t-006 · status: draft for t-007 (generation) and Silas's renames

Everything here is a one-line edit. Suit names, colours and the court cast are agent picks from
DESIGN-BRIEF.md; Silas may rename any of them and the prompt table below is regenerated from this
file, not hand-patched.

## The four suits

| Suit | Plays as | Colour | Pip shape | Personality | Robot motif (Aces and pip cards) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gears | hearts | red (deep red and cream) | gear-tooth round pip | warm helpers | a small round robot with a brass cog for a chest and a kettle-shaped head |
| Circuits | diamonds | blue (deep blue and cream) | diamond circuit-node pip | thinkers | a slim robot with a glass dome head and a coil of copper wire for a scarf |
| Sprockets | clubs | green (forest green and cream) | three-lobed sprocket pip | builders | a stout robot with a tool belt, a wrench in one hand and a square head with a lamp on top |
| Sparks | spades | gold (golden yellow and cream) | four-point spark-star pip | dreamers | a floating round robot with a lantern body and two thin antenna wisps |

For the Klondike alternating-colour rule, Gears and Circuits count as "red" and Sprockets and Sparks
as "black". The engine (`utils/solitaireEngine.ts`) treats suit indices 1 and 2 as red, so the suit
index order is Sparks = 0, Gears = 1, Circuits = 2, Sprockets = 3 (spades, hearts, diamonds, clubs).
The game must also draw each pip in its own
shape so no card relies on colour alone (colour-blind players read the shape and the corner index).

## Style bible

One style tail, appended verbatim to every prompt so the 52 faces read as one deck:

> `gouache painting on cream card stock, flat matte colour fields, thick warm outlines, rounded shapes, even soft studio light`

- Cards render at 2:3 (768x1152), matching the five existing card backs and the gallery
  (survey.md recommendation). The game shows them at 2:3.
- Every prompt asks for a wide empty cream margin; corner indices, pips and rank letters are
  composed in code over that margin, never baked into the art, so text stays crisp.
- Palette words per suit come from the table above; the cream matches the card stock in the game.
- Keep the whole deck on one model and one preset (Krea 2) so the line weight does not drift.

## Seed policy

Each suit owns a thousand-block: Gears 1000s, Circuits 2000s, Sprockets 3000s, Sparks 4000s. Within
a suit the seed is base + 1 for the Ace up to base + 10 for the Ten, then +11 Jack, +12 Queen,
+13 King. A reroll for a weak card adds 100 to its seed (1005 becomes 1105), and the prompt and
model metadata stay with the card either way. The seed column below is the first-attempt seed.

## Prompt contract

Written to the Krea 2 rules in ART-PROMPTS.md: the physical subject leads, every sentence says what
is in the frame (nothing is ruled out), no conditionals, and no commissioning vocabulary. The
validator in `scripts/build_cthulhuquarium_art_queue.py` rejects negations and is the model for the
t-007 queue check. A card with a person-shaped subject says so once; Aces and pip cards use a small
robot as the only figure.
