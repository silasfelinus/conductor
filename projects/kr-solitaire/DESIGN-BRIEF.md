# Kind Robots Solitaire — Design Brief

date: 2026-10-02
status: draft (Silas-directed pitch; scope confirmation t-002 is a soft checkpoint and blocks nothing)
author: claude

## What it is

Klondike solitaire in the browser, played with a custom Kind Robots deck: four robot suits, ranks
Ace to King, illustrated Jack, Queen and King per suit, and a choice of card backs drawn from the
Kind Robots card art. Pure client-side; no LLM, no account.

Silas's pitch, 2026-10-02, verbatim: *"a solitaire game using our cards for backing, plus specially
designed kind robots 52 card deck."*

## The deck

Four suits, each a robot family with its own colour, pip shape and personality. Suggested set
(Silas can rename): **Gears** (red, hearts-equivalent, the warm helpers), **Circuits** (blue,
diamonds, the thinkers), **Sprockets** (green, clubs, the builders), **Sparks** (gold, spades, the
dreamers). 13 cards each: Ace is the suit's emblem robot, 2 to 10 are pip layouts with a small robot
motif, J/Q/K are full-art character cards drawn from existing Kind Robots characters. 52 faces, one
or more card backs (the existing Kind Robots card art), and a joker/box-front as a bonus.
Every face keeps the corner index (rank + suit pip) readable at small sizes; legibility beats art.

## Who it serves

Anyone. It is the lowest-friction way to feel the Kind Robots art style and it works on a phone in
a waiting room. Silas's kids are the first playtesters.

## Game features

Klondike draw 1 and draw 3, drag and tap-to-move, undo without limit, hint, auto-complete when
solvable, win animation, move counter and timer, saved stats, a **daily deal** (same shuffle for
everyone, seeded by date), and a back-picker. Later: Spider, FreeCell, Pyramid, using the same deck.

## Architecture

- Home is kind_robots: `/play/solitaire`, Pinia `solitaireStore`, no network calls.
- Rules are a pure, seedable engine in `utils/` (deal, legal moves, move application, undo log,
  win and auto-complete detection) with DB-free tsx tests, including a deal-solvability sampler.
- The deck is a manifest of 52 face images plus backs under `public/`, loaded lazily; a CSS/SVG
  fallback deck renders when images are missing so the game is playable before the art exists.
- Daily deal seed is derived from the date; no server needed.
- Stats live in localStorage with a try/catch (private windows throw).

## Art plan (pre-generated by sessions)

52 faces at card aspect (5:7) via ArtJobs under the Krea 2 contract (concrete nouns first, no
negations, no art-direction jargon), consistent through one style bible and a fixed seed policy per
suit. Face cards drawn from existing characters (an approved reference set). Corner indices and pips
are composed in code over the art, not baked into it, so the text is always crisp and accessible.

## MVP scope

Engine, fallback-art deck, draw-1 play with drag and tap, undo, win detection. Then art, daily deal,
draw-3, stats, back-picker, polish.

## Out of scope / guardrails

- No LLM; no gambling mechanics or real-money anything.
- No publishing or deploy steps; no leaderboards in v1.
- Art is generated, never scraped; keep prompt and model metadata with every card.
