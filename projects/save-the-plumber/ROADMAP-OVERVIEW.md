# Save the Plumber

**Goal:** Original long-form 2D platforming adventure: a customizable young adult heroine rescues her plumber boyfriend. Eight worlds, 32+ substantial authored stages, eight bosses, secret routes, persistent map and saves, kinetic traversal, distinct abilities, art/animation/audio, cinematic opening and multiple endings. SMB3/SMW/Wonder are depth benchmarks, not assets or layouts to copy.
