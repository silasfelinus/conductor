## Enemy families and interaction budget

Start with **pumpbugs** (speed changes on slopes), **rivet rollers** (thrown kinetic wheels), **umbrella mites** (hover/rain patterns), **moss lanterns** (provide temporary light and platform), **glass gulls** (steal but can drop collectibles), **cork crabs** (block pipe mouths), **siphon sprites** (carry health from puddles), **clockwork minnows** (jump with current), **gasket golems** (throwable armor), **spring hares** (bounce chains), **paper kites** (ride/wind hazard), **puddle mimics** (reveal on approach), **chimney spirits** (jump arcs) and **tea-kettle knights** (steam tells). The stage 2 authoring task expands to 40+ *behaviorally distinct* designs with telegraph animation and counterplay; recolors alone are not new enemy kinds.

## Required story beats

- **Opening**: plumber discovers starward water flow and hides the first safety-valve blueprint. His capture is brief, comprehensible and skippable.
- **Interludes**: at the end of each world the heroine finds a new tool-mark trail, a small clue and a fresh map vista. Show her growing competence through action, not lectures.
- **Finale**: Lady Spigot's city-sized pump can be switched to return water to the ground. The plumber helps from inside the device rather than waiting motionless. The two reunite and celebrate, with a distinct extra coda for optional hidden valves.
- **Credits**: the eight restored environments scroll past in an authored closing scene.

## Level-acceptance sheet

Before any level is called complete, record: authored map version, intended first-clear minutes, reachable entrance/exit, collision replay seed, enemy roster/variations, three intentional skill beats, accessible telegraphs, one genuine secret, a distinct visual scene, music/sound cues, any save/checkpoint transitions and a real phone/tablet/desktop UI capture. A placeholder tile sequence does not meet this contract.
