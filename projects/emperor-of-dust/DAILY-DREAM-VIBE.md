# Emperor of Dust: daily creation vibe pitch

**Ash, Brass & the Last Hundred Days**

A pre-Zuzu Weird West throne made from salvage, keeping a starving crossroads alive. Warm brass and kiln-ochre lights cut through ash-grey dawns and dirty rain. Ragged banners, distant watchfires, patched leather, ruined human infrastructure, animal-folk leaders with recognizable, varied silhouettes, and dusty roads radiating from a narrow central tower. An empire held together by imperfect bargains rather than raw conquest.

**Tone:** brutal choices with compassion still possible; dry humor in tense briefings, a little beauty among the scars. Young Komodo's flawed intelligence, faction double-dealing and recovering communities matter. The player rules a numerical Horde, but *followers* are distinct leaders with loyalties and relationships. Injury with day-count recovery is common; permanent follower death is rare, deliberate, foreshadowed and remembered. Power has a material price.

**Image lane:** favor the available Illustrious and Arthemy checkpoints; use the actual resource-backed checkpoint and existing Zuzu world house references where characters are canonical. Avoid inventing undocumented character traits, modern weapons or a generic fantasy palace. Map/scene art can be wider, roster portraits frame a single leader, injured portraits retain that person's recognizable identity, and splash plates dramatize character without changing species or costume.

**Daily expansion ingredients:** morally knotty dispatch incidents with 0–3 follower assignments, at least two genuinely viable options and consequences for Popularity, Prosperity, Support Hoard, faction relations, Horde or follower injury. Locations should contain a playable rule and at least one consequential encounter. Rewards should have concrete effects, costs and counterplay, not just flavor.

This is a persistent project art/story pitch, **not** the day's single original free-form Daily Dream vibe, and not a request to scaffold another project. Follow `GAME-BIBLE.md`, `SYSTEMS-V02.md`, `worlds/zuzu/README.md` and the existing follower art ledger when production work begins.
