**The land, place by place:**

- **The Fortress (C2).** A walled tower on a flat-topped mesa just north of centre, with straight
  roads radiating from its gate, as in v0.2. It overlooks the Saltroad to the south, the Mirefen
  to the north and the Mission Road running east to the Abbey.
- **Mirefen (B1–D1).** A long band of wetland across the north: reeds, still green lanterns, stilt
  huts and the Mire Mothers' coven-house. The **Drywater river** leaves its southern edge.
- **Black Stones watering hole (B2).** Standing black rocks around a pool on the fen's dry edge.
  Strangers meet here, since water is wealth (WORLD-GUIDE.md).
- **Thistledown (D1–E1).** The north-eastern Freehold homesteads, above the Abbey, past the
  fen's eastern edge: farms on the last good soil before the Verge.
- **The Abbey of the Silent Bell (D2–E2).** Adobe walls and a bell tower with no clapper on the dry
  hills east of the fortress, at the end of the **Mission Road**, above Hollow Bell. Its crypt is
  where the Day 40 horror rises, so leave room around it for the overlay.
- **Hollow Bell (D4).** A **small southern town**: a cluster of homes, a market square and a modest
  chapel whose bell gives the town its name. Not a single great tower. A stone **aqueduct** carries
  Mirefen water south along the Drywater to it; one span is broken in Act I and can be repaired in
  Act II. It sits just north of the sunken rail yards.
- **The Black Verge (east edge, E1–E3).** Dark, high country of black pines and ruined manors past
  Thistledown and the Abbey. Fogged until Day 21.
- **Storm Crow Heights (A1–A3).** Cliffs and caves along the west, with roosts on the ledges. The
  crows face east, toward the rail yards (an Act I clue).
- **The Saltroad (row 3).** The trade road runs from the **salt flats** (A4) north-east, then due
  east past the fortress's market villages (B3–C3). It crosses the Drywater at the **Eastern Ford**
  (D3) and turns south to end at Hollow Bell. Its villages are strung along it.
- **The Border Freeholds (D3, D1–E1).** Farmland along the Drywater at the **Eastern Ford**, and the
  north-eastern homesteads of **Thistledown**.
- **The Badlands (B4–B5).** Eroded hoodoos and stone chimneys south-west of the fortress. The
  **Mole tunnels** run beneath them. Draw them as dotted lines or a cutaway reaching north under the
  Saltroad toward the fortress and the fen's edge, where floods are a threat.
- **Jackalwire Rail Canyon (C5).** The Drywater has carved a canyon with an old human rail line
  along its floor, trestle bridges, and the Pack's painted-wagon camps.
- **The Sunken Rail Yards (D4–E5).** The rail line runs east out of the canyon into a sunken field
  of rusted engines and cars: the Rustline, magpie and rat salvagers. Fogged until Day 11.

## The fortress

The fortress is the Emperor's Tower on its mesa, drawn as a cutaway or a three-quarter view. It has
five rooms, one per screen in the game, plus the places the story needs:

```
                        ┌──────────── MAP ROOM ────────────┐   top of the tower
                        │ painted table map, four windows, │   (Lamplight scribes)
                        │ a telescope toward each region   │
                        ├────────── LAMPLIGHT ARCHIVE ─────┤   middle floors
   THE YARD             │ shelves, ledgers, Hollin's perch │
   kitchens, bread      ├────────── THRONE ROOM ───────────┤   keep, ground floor
   ovens, cistern ──────│ the throne, petitioners' floor,  │── Komodo's study
   (behind the keep)    │ Old Houses' gallery above        │   (ledgers; an empty
                        └───────────────┬──────────────────┘    chair on Day 40)
   BARRACKS (west wing)          inner court           QUEST ROOM (east wing)
   bunk hall (6 bunks, two  ─────────┼──────────  quest wall of pinned notices,
   empty bays for 9 and 12),         │            a dispatch table, a balcony
   infirmary corner (Kiyo's          │            over the roads where parties
   kettle), practice yard,           │            ride out at dusk
   stables                           │
                        MARKET: outer court inside the gate
                        stalls under salvage awnings, the Ledger's brass-stamping house, a well
                        ═════════════ GATEHOUSE (the Gate Watch) ═══════════ south, to the Saltroad
```

- **Throne room:** audiences and recruiting. A long hall with the throne at the north end, a
  petitioners' floor, and the Old Houses' gallery above. Young Komodo's small study opens off it.
- **Map room:** the top of the tower, around a big painted table map. This is where the act-end
  changes show first.
- **Barracks:** the west wing. The bunk hall visibly grows from 6 bunks to 9 and 12, with an
  infirmary corner where the injured portraits belong.
- **Market:** the outer court inside the gate, where the Ledger's merchants set up.
- **Quest room:** the east wing, with the quest wall and the dispatch balcony. Its day/night
  animation looks out from that balcony.
- **Day 40 state:** the gate broken, the tower burning, and Komodo's chair empty.

**The Act V hideout** has the same five functions in rougher form: an audience corner, bunks, a
peddler, a map scratched on a wall and a quest wall. Draw one generic interior first and dress it
seven ways (the tunnels, a cave, the bell cellars, the wagons, a caravan, a barn, a rail station).
