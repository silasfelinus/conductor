# Emperor of Dust playtest, 2026-10-21 PT

Silas: *"Get to another playtest run. Assess mood, clarity of challenges, uniqueness and interesting and
challenging choices, not just rescue the unicorn versus throw the puppies in the lava."*

Five act reviewers scored every audience, beat and quest on Mood, Clarity, Uniqueness, Choice quality and
Consequence (rubric: [PLAYTEST-RUBRIC.md](PLAYTEST-RUBRIC.md)); a sixth played three full persona campaigns
(runs.md, added when it lands). Reports: [act1](act1.md) · [act2](act2.md) · [act3](act3.md) · [act4](act4.md) ·
[act5](act5.md). Each report ends with self-contained rewrite proposals for every scene scoring ≤ 2.

## Verdict
The prose and the best moments are strong (Day 1's dust-lung turn, Wren, "It is not a coin. It is a boy.",
the bandaged-paw girl, the nail holes, Corvane's "We are not cruel. We are exact.", the cook's letter in the
flour). The weaknesses are structural, and the same ones recur in every act:

1. **Consequence leaks.** A large share of the flags each act sets are never read (Act II 26/62, Act IV
   55/105, Act V ~47/119). Big choices go unremembered: sending every child to the Abbey (Day 30), the
   suspicious player's precautions against Komodo (Days 35–40), the Day 10 oath, the Day 7 storm.
2. **Choices that do not split players.** Dominant options (best on every axis) in every act; kindness
   bought with brass (~15 Act I options); hideout terms where accepting beats paying on every axis; Act IV
   alliances that cost no rival in 31 of 52 cases; fake choices (Kiyo "goes home" and doesn't, Day 50 dig
   vs hold changes nothing).
3. **Debts, laws and threats are never collected.** The Ledger loan's repayment, the "first law" tax rate,
   the tincture fee that "will rise", Vane's filed bolt, the Verge "who do not accept apologies", the
   covenant terms the player chose.
4. **Text and mechanics disagree.** Narration says Thistledown is saved while the quest stays open; the
   Watch marches to the Ford and the quest still reads "Nobody rode to the Ford"; holding Komodo in the
   cells, then dawn says he opened the gate; effects that contradict the line (Yard +1 on rising interest).
5. **One scene template.** Free probe → sympathetic hidden truth → the same answers re-offered (Act I 25/25,
   Act II 13/14, Act III 16/16), Komodo's aside as the last line (24/25), Komodo flagging the false report
   on nearly every non-lie day (a structural tell). Repeated images ("Nobody…": 79 in Act V; annual
   rituals in ~70 epilogue variants).
6. **Act-specific design misses.** Act III: the Abbey reads as evil by Day 23 (≈30 tells, 4
   counterweights), so "kindness as the trap" collapses into trust-the-villain. Act IV: Komodo's tells too
   loud by Days 36–38; the Day 40 quiet word lets the player leave early and skips its best question for the
   best-prepared players. Act V: the finale is a pure stat roll (alliances never reach the Day 50 odds); The
   Bell Restored is effectively unreachable (clapper quest returns too late); A Hundred Candles overrides
   the Day 49 vote with no warning.
7. **Clarity for a new player.** The throne screen shows only brass costs and posted quests, not what an
   answer changes, and does not say that an "Ask" keeps the scene open; Day 1 names nine factions.
