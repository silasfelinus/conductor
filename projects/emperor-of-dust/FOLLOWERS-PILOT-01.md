# Emperor of Dust — follower cast pilot, characters F001–F010
*Preproduction writing, 2026-10-12 PT. Creative prototypes, not ten completed playable leaders. These ten are a **quality bar** for the commissioned 100-character pool, not a substitute for the remaining ninety. None of the three art variants per character has been generated or delivered.*

## Design ground rules
- Each leader is an individual with personal goals and relationships, not merely a species archetype or a stats roll. Factions affect access and consequences; membership does not predetermine virtue.
- The Emperor's Horde is an independent **headcount**. These commanders lead assigned troops, become unavailable through outward travel/mission/return, and can be **injured for days**. Permanent death is a rare story hazard.
- Ratings: **Might / Diplomacy / Ingenuity**, each 0–5. Specialties are situational tags; not extra global stat bars. Initial leader set F001–F005; the rest need authored recruitment gates. Stats are starting designs needing simulations and playtest.
- For each character the art jobs are **portrait**, **injured**, **splash**, each with the same appearance reference; later emotion images are optional, not baseline. Color and readable shape must show injury, not icon-only overlays. Stable IDs `followers/FNNN/{portrait,injured,splash}`.

## F001 — Rook Bramble
**Hare outrider, initial court leader; 3 / 2 / 4.** Tags: navigation, tracking. Hollow Bell / Border Freeholds; wary of imperial taxes.
**Bio:** Rook can follow wagon ruts on stony ground and still miss a lie told by a friend. Raised on the northern boundary, they carry a debt to a raider who once saved their family. Rook narrates dangerous news with dry jokes, then grows uncharacteristically silent when the Jackalwire Pack is named. They want the Tower to protect the roads without owning them.
**Tension/arc:** May testify against their old rescuer or shelter them; trusts Duchess Flint's fairness but resents Bram Coil's ration rules.
**Visual brief:** Rangy sand-brown hare, long asymmetrical ears, patched outrider jacket, blue trail scarf, brass compass.
**Healthy portrait:** Alert sideways glance, dust on scarf, upright ears. **Injured portrait:** One ear bandaged, arm supported by sling, trying to smile. **Splash:** Silhouette on a ridge tracking a wagon in violet dusk.

## F002 — Saffron Kett
**Hyena machinist, initial court leader; 3 / 1 / 5.** Tags: mechanics, demolition. Jackalwire-born, currently court-aligned.
**Bio:** Saffron builds bridges no one wants to pay for and sabotages traps that threaten children. Her speech is fast, tangled with half-finished inventions, and she distrusts the palace's orderly recordkeeping. A former raider band's insignia is welded to the inside of her toolkit. She wants to prove that building is a more dangerous form of bravery than destroying.
**Tension/arc:** Old bandmates may ask her to fix a raider water engine; a refusal risks Horde losses, assent risks Hollow Bell.
**Visual brief:** Compact, barrel-chested spotted hyena, striking amber work glasses, scarred green overalls, copper-wire tool belt.
**Healthy portrait:** Curious grin and grease-dusted cheek. **Injured portrait:** Goggles cracked, hand in a practical splint, stubbornly reviewing schematics. **Splash:** Sparks cascading from a repaired bridge pulley above a dry gorge.

## F003 — Wisp Fen
**Heron field medic, initial court leader; 1 / 3 / 5.** Tags: medicine, wetlands, anatomy. Saltroad / Mirefen, suspicious of swamp witch leadership.
**Bio:** Wisp writes the names of the recovered, not only the dead, in her river notebook. Her empathy does not make her naive: she knows when an invitation to a free clinic is a demand for silence. She has treated patients who came out of the mire with identical unexplained scars. Wisp hopes to create a public infirmary beyond faction control.
**Tension/arc:** Witch healers offer medicine in exchange for an unexplained favor; Wisp can expose their methods or ask for help saving a follower.
**Visual brief:** Tall pale heron, moss-gray woven cape, strapped satchel, soft violet eyes, long sleeves for clinical tools.
**Healthy portrait:** Level compassionate gaze and open satchel. **Injured portrait:** Wing loosely braced, feathered brow furrowed by impatience. **Splash:** Holding a lantern above wounded travelers on a flooded path.

## F004 — Duchess Flint
**Tortoise mediator, initial court leader; 2 / 5 / 3.** Tags: arbitration, defense. Border Freeholds, honorary palace title.
**Bio:** Flint accepted a duke's empty honorific to secure safe passage for refugees, then kept it as a joke that nobody in the Tower understands. She speaks slowly, asks very precise questions, and never lets the Emperor call casualties an abstraction. Her public flaw is refusing to compromise even when a concession might save more lives.
**Tension/arc:** Can broker a lasting constitutional agreement with the Moles, but only after the court demonstrates real power-sharing.
**Visual brief:** Older dark-shelled tortoise, patterned gold repair in shell, layered travel shawl, modest signet on a cord.
**Healthy portrait:** Patient searching eyes and tilted chin. **Injured portrait:** Bandaged shoulder, walking stick within frame, tired but composed. **Splash:** Standing between rival delegations in the dust of a broken council hall.

## F005 — Pip Thread
**Moth archivist, initial court leader; 1 / 4 / 5.** Tags: archives, codes, witnesses. Hollow Bell / palace records.
**Bio:** Pip can memorize a torn court register after a glance, yet finds ordinary social conversation exhausting. Once they traced a missing-person claim to a ledger page that had been replaced by the mayor's office. Pip prefers documentary proof to accusation, sometimes waiting too long to act. They collect old human-world street signs with no idea what they originally sold.
**Tension/arc:** Offered rank if they suppress a compromising ledger; trusts Wisp's witness interviews, distrusts the Crows' elegant certainties.
**Visual brief:** Broad-winged dusk moth with ink-black feelers, practical ocher waistcoat, spectacles on a chain, small folio.
**Healthy portrait:** Wings angled like a cloak, careful smile. **Injured portrait:** Torn wing tip in cloth support, ink spots on protective wrappings. **Splash:** Midnight lantern-lit records room, papers turning in a storm draft.

## F006 — Nera Gale
**Storm Crow dissident, recruitable through the early rain dispute; 2 / 4 / 5.** Tags: weather, investigation. Storm Crow Conclave, opposition wing.
**Bio:** Nera believes weather should not be property, a position that makes her useful to the Emperor and dangerous to her own peers. She speaks politely while dismantling other people's assumptions and is terrified that her cleverness serves someone else's plan. Her private hobby is growing fragile seeds where storms should kill them.
**Tension/arc:** Can expose Crow rain tolls or negotiate a limited covenant, possibly estranging her mentors.
**Visual brief:** Slender midnight-plumed crow, ash-blue mantle, a constellation of tiny copper chimes, storm-glass necklace.
**Healthy portrait:** Controlled expression, wind lifting crest feathers. **Injured portrait:** Wing bound under a rain-stained mantle, controlled expression slipping. **Splash:** On a lightning-cut observatory balcony with rain approaching dry farmland.

## F007 — Ash Quill
**Fox courier, recruited after missing-message investigation; 3 / 3 / 4.** Tags: speed, forgery detection. Saltroad / independent.
**Bio:** Ash delivers letters unopened, except the ones addressed to the sibling who vanished on the mission road. They invent cheerful nicknames for everyone they meet, partly to stop themselves from learning names they might later lose. They have forged travel authorizations to move vulnerable families past armed checkpoints and fear exposure.
**Tension/arc:** Loyalty turns on whether the Emperor chooses justice or convenience in the search for missing couriers.
**Visual brief:** Rust-red fox, close-trimmed travel coat, tied parcel tubes and kite-red messenger sash.
**Healthy portrait:** Bright alert smile, ears forward. **Injured portrait:** Torn sash, crutch in hand, determined not to be pitied. **Splash:** Sprinting above a stone causeway with sealed messages as dawn breaks.

## F008 — Bram Coil
**Buffalo quartermaster, recruited through food and road security; 5 / 2 / 3.** Tags: defense, logistics. Border Freeholds / Saltroad.
**Bio:** Bram once rationed food so strictly that a celebration ended in a riot. Since then he brings a private ledger to every feast and insists everyone eats before a victory speech. He can hold a defensive line but has trouble recognizing that desperate people sometimes need trust before calculation.
**Tension/arc:** Must decide whether to share supplies with raiders; may become a blunt but reliable partner to the Mole reformers.
**Visual brief:** Powerful dark buffalo with worn horn tips, teal-lined field coat, wooden ration abacus and leather pack.
**Healthy portrait:** Relaxed square posture, counting under his breath. **Injured portrait:** One horn wrapped and ribs secured, visibly annoyed at enforced rest. **Splash:** Protecting a wagon of food through a sand squall, shields set around civilians.

## F009 — Keel Understone
**Mole Revolutionary tunnel surveyor, recruited after a genuine truce; 2 / 4 / 5.** Tags: tunnels, excavation, local intelligence. Mole Revolutionaries, distrusts Mire Mothers.
**Bio:** Keel mapped the imperial wells for pay before discovering that a tax decree threatened the burrow settlements downstream. They argue against the throne at every opportunity, but also against reckless revolution that would flood poor homes. They speak in architectural metaphors and keep a scrapbook of buildings that could have been built differently.
**Tension/arc:** Demands practical autonomy and transparent access to water, not praise. May reveal a witch-controlled sinkhole network at personal risk.
**Visual brief:** Broad-fingered umber mole, battered surveying lantern, stitched orange underground work vest, charcoal blueprint scroll.
**Healthy portrait:** Direct skeptical glance, lantern high. **Injured portrait:** Dirt-streaked head wrap, one hand splinted, eyes still calculating. **Splash:** Charting a lantern-lit tunnel junction beneath the Emperor's roads.

## F010 — Lumen Shale
**Mole Revolutionary speaker and mutual-aid organizer, recruited through reconciliation; 1 / 5 / 4.** Tags: community organizing, listening, negotiations. Mole Revolutionaries / Border Freeholds.
**Bio:** Lumen does not want an emperor's favor; she wants a world where people need not beg a tower for permission to build a hospital. She is capable of changing her mind and deeply resents people who think that makes her weak. Her greatest misjudgment was publicly defending a marsh witch's supposedly charitable expedition, and she fears what her trust cost others.
**Tension/arc:** Can help transform enemy revolutionaries into uneasy allies if imperial promises become measurable reforms. Her clashes with Keel concern means, not the end of oppression.
**Visual brief:** Small silver-furred mole, thick fur collar, cobalt civic sash and patched satchel filled with meeting notices.
**Healthy portrait:** Open challenging expression, folded council agenda. **Injured portrait:** Supported shoulder, a meeting notice crumpled in fist, steady gaze. **Splash:** At an underground assembly as lanterns illuminate a crowd of disputing neighbors.
