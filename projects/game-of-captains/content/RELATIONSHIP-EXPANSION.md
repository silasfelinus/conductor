# Space Couch | Expanded relationship atlas (2026-10-26 PT)

Parent canon: [RELATIONSHIPS-BIBLE.md](RELATIONSHIPS-BIBLE.md). This atlas accompanies `game-of-captains/t-021` and the runtime authored catalogue `kind_robots/utils/gameOfCaptains/romanceStories.ts`.

## What is actually playable

The active Kind Robots cast consists of **twelve adult crew**. Each has an optional, individually written **three-chapter** romance route and a non-punitive refusal or breakup. Six additional **crew-to-crew** relationship routes cover all twelve, and the separately authored Four-Cushion Accord (captain + Vela + Taal + Omi) remains available with independent consent for all four. Pair routes are *the two crew members choosing each other*; the captain is invited to witness and support their choices, never to order affection, and is not silently added to the pair.

Only one three-chapter relationship arc is active per short voyage. Players can instead choose friendship. New crew quests, future recruitable officers and deeper multi-run relationships must not be confused with a shipped concurrent-romance simulator. A crew member's refusal has no staffing, skill, resources or mission penalties. **Nineteen** routes have different negotiation dialogue, optional good, open-ended, painful and amicable outcomes: twelve solo + six pairs + the original four-person Accord.

## Six crew-to-crew romantic pathways

All require both adults on the selected three-crew roster. Each has a chapter-one invitation, chapter-two conflict, chapter-three commitment/defer/part, unique epilogue and failure conditions grounded in the captain's actual intervention/trust decisions.

| Pair | Conflict & scene | Earned conclusion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Pell + Mox-9**, *The Repair You Can Refuse* | Repairing someone is not the same as asking whether they want to be fixed | Pell sets down his tool; Mox declines a backup of a first kiss |
| **Vela + Brin**, *The Clinic in the Orchard* | Sterile patient safety versus self-governing living ecology | A clinic with a voluntary garden door; both can refuse to enter |
| **Tess + Jex**, *A Song With No Coordinates* | Famous music risks disclosing hidden refugee coordinates | A welcome anthem without a publishable location |
| **Rue + Fen**, *A Border With Two Exits* | A lawyer drafted the clause an officer once enforced | Public reparations first, private intimacy only with mutual choice |
| **Sora + Kivo**, *The Unopened Message* | An engineer can retrieve a sibling's location; a scout can forbid that search | The letter stays sealed without making either adult less loved |
| **Taal + Omi**, *Thermal Duet* | Joy in one language sounds like objection in another | A chosen discord they do not have to translate away |

**Four-person special route:** the captain, Vela, Taal and Omi can mutually form a fourple, distinct from the optional Taal+Omi couple and Vela solo romance. Crew-to-crew affection isn't an alternate way to acquire the Four-Cushion Accord without Vela's freely given yes.

## Four authored but not yet recruitable future characters

These four belong to the sixteen-character longform CAST-BIBLE, **not the twelve-person playable current roster**. Do not claim they ship in the game until crew IDs, selection art, quest state and saves migrate safely.

1. **Nyra Tern: The Apology That Cannot Undo a City.** Invitation on Mirror Census: Nyra asks the captain to accompany her to the public hearing over the forged evacuation order that saved one population and doomed another. Negotiation on Courtesy Twins: they argue about whether public apology is another performance for the living. Finale on Quiet Sun: if the captain insists on an erased record, Nyra leaves; if they stay beside the witnesses without usurping them, Nyra chooses love and a lifelong reparation effort. Line: “Do not love the version of me who saved everyone. I have never met her.”
2. **Gleam-of-Rain: One Thought at a Time.** Invitation in Rootborne Gardens: after escaping collective consciousness, Gleam offers the captain a conversation that nobody else can hear. Negotiation on Haleth Reef: a chorus begs them to rejoin; the captain must defend freely reversible connection, not forcibly isolate Gleam. Finale on Gameworld: Gleam chooses a private bond while continuing voluntary chorus visits, or ends courtship if the captain treats mental access as a prize. Line: “I left a million voices to learn whether I could choose just one.”
3. **Harrow Nine: The Last Exit Is Not a Date.** Invitation at the Showrunner's Gameworld: a retired union organizer asks the captain to inspect the disabled arena exits together. Negotiation at Sesh Foundry: choosing spectacle or saving the workers becomes a personal rupture. Finale at Serendipity: when workers get their own charter, Harrow can choose companionship without making the captain their savior; capitulation to the Showrunner ends romance and may still preserve labor solidarity. Line: “You may kiss the organizer after you have read the grievance.”
4. **Edda Morn: The Ledger With One Missing Life.** Invitation at Quiet Sun: a retired accountant trusts the captain with evidence of diverted famine relief. Negotiation at Mirror Census: finding the exiled family will expose Edda's culpability; a captain who conceals it for affection is refused. Finale at Nacre Assembly: public restitution and unvarnished testimony make a mature, hesitant partnership possible, while coerced forgiveness ends it. Line: “I am not asking you to balance my books. I am asking you to leave the last column blank.”

## Quality and game/story contract

- **Adult only.** Names, cultures, pronouns, synthetic or collective consciousness, alien bodies and shared histories do not change autonomy or the ability to opt out.
- **Power imbalance matters.** The captain commands missions. Romantic invitations must be off-duty, with explicit right to refuse, and accepting/rejecting them cannot mutate crew skill, supplies or assignment options. For crew-to-crew courtship the captain's only legitimate role is to make space and withdraw if asked.
- **Consequences are real.** Captain trust/reputation/intervention gates reflect how they treated *other people* in the voyage. Some couples refuse to proceed; some remain friends, or separate without a happy ending. Supportive care is not a mechanical purchase of loyalty.
- **Save compatibility.** Keep the `game-of-captains-voyage-v1` key and optional `romance` object. An older Rue, Tess or Four-Cushion run resumes using unchanged identifiers and statuses. Replaying a click or restoring a save must not duplicate a romantic turn or append the ending twice.
- **Narrative image inventory.** Each of the twelve solos requires a chapter-one invitation portrait / vignette, a boundary-conflict illustration, an earned commitment or breakup alternative, and an epilogue image; six pairs require at least three paired compositions each; the fourple requires three 4-person scenes. That is an **art requirement**, not proof of ArtJobs or delivered pictures. Prompt each scene with literal positions, props, emotional cues, clothing, and the unmistakable long red couch when aboard.
- **Adversarial traces.** Prove from new game all nineteen routes reach a genuine earned good outcome and a separate non-happy or declined outcome. Include forced-command attempts, incompatible crew, low trust, high intervention, no romance, v1 restore mid-arc, no double submission, and preservation of the original galactic ending.
- **Next tranche:** post-final-save multi-run bonding, dynamic relationship scenes when personal quests converge, conflict between multiple concurrent interests, four future crew playable migrations, 220 trio-specific interstitial adventures and 440 trio endings remain later work. The romance slice does not falsely complete these scales.
