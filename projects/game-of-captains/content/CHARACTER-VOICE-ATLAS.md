**Comedy and dignity:** The speech stutter and body conditions are personal details, not punchlines aimed at disabilities or different bodies. The jokes arise from extraordinary circumstances, bad regulations, the characters' own values, and absurd consequences. A stutter cannot be cured by a better timeline. Quill's fluid recycling is completely nonsexual. Both remain fully competent commanders with vulnerabilities.

## Game logic and scope

Selection UI carries, for each of the eighteen, a funny hook, particular eccentricity, clearly stated story promise, emotional history, and a crew counterpoint. Each captain also has an optional off-duty-ish briefing decision that can be made once per chapter: personal impulse (+10 percentage points on mission odds with a material cost) or restraint (no odds bonus, safer social/resource outcome). Crew present respond to that captain's choices. The decision remains in local saves and final log. Established Act I missions, crew quests, romance, villain outcomes and old save keys must survive.

This adds differentiated selection and gameplay **but does not yet make the full 30-mission, 40-encounter, 16-ending or trio-story quotas complete.** Longer-term expansion should add character-specific banter, disagreements, emergent relationship consequences, villain taunts and unique outcome art without hollow multiplication of generic text.
