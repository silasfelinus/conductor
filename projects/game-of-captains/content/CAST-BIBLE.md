# Space Couch | Captain and crew character bible (2026-10-26 PT)

**Status:** authored source of truth for writers and the next playable expansion, not a claim that these branches, portraits, or quest flags already run. Fulfills part of Conductor `game-of-captains/t-002`; existing first-slice code currently has six captains and twelve recruitable crew. Stable IDs, routes and save keys are unchanged. See [master issue #4182](https://github.com/silasfelinus/kind_robots/issues/4182).

## The voice and staging contract

An enormous red upholstered sofa travels through vacuum with four people sitting on it and an invisible force bubble protecting them. Every character has noticed. Nobody makes the same “it's a couch” joke twice. People argue about jurisdiction over the armrests, expenses for replacement cushion stuffing, and which way the diplomatic flag should fly in a vacuum. Expandable rooms unfold **behind** the backrest, never into a conventional bridge or spaceship. The safety bubble's emergency setting always keeps the passengers alive; low charge endangers a mission, not the right to breathe. Keep the absurd physical rules concrete, not arbitrary.

Comedy is dry, exact and character-specific. A catastrophe is recorded in a memo, not shouted at the audience. Consequence is not a punchline. Captain commands should alter obligations and the people who bear them. The crew's body, language, homeworld or gender never predetermines moral value or stat penalties. Culture comes from people and institutions, not blanket species traits.

**Opening sequence is two illustrated full-stage choices, not one long stacked form:** one captains screen with full-body portrait, six-stat readout, biography, tradeoff and signature opening; then a crew showroom with full-body portrait per candidate, interpersonal ties, quest destination preview and exactly three selection slots. The main red couch occupies the ship scenes, not every character portrait. On mobile use a swipeable art stage with a bounded dossier panel and visible confirmation; neither step should make the page scroll. Existing short-game selection remains a compatibility fallback until implementation.

## Six captains, six obligations

The six stable runtime IDs are `fluke`, `vale`, `solace`, `rex`, `quill`, `ro`. Each gets an original hero painting, at least an embarrassed and defiant expression, an opening-specific incident, a midpoint contradiction and a finale reckoning. They change **how** every ending happens, not simply its label. Stats in `utils/gameOfCaptains/crewAssignments.ts` are canonical until an intentional balance patch.

### Captain scene contracts

- **Act I:** a captain-specific opening with one measurable obligation, two distinct strategies, one failure outcome and a callback flag (`captain:fluke:log_debt`, `vale:anachronism`, `solace:ledger`, `rex:testimony`, `quill:sealed_record`, `ro:credentials`).
- **Act II:** a test that breaks the captain's advertised advantage; each captain must choose between reputation or someone else's agency. A matching crewmate may present a third route with its own cost.
- **Act III:** one irrevocable public stance. Each of the sixteen ending **families** gets a separately authored epilogue beat and consequence for each of six captains (96 authored variants, not 96 pre-seeded independent win routes). Three selected crew append earned quest-based resolutions, never generic interchangeable name substitutions.
- **Fluke continuity:** he is an adaptation on this couch voyage. Do not rewrite or merge his separate Dream #49 narrator, ship or seed into Space Couch.

## Sixteen crew, sixteen multi-world personal journeys

First twelve entries retain their current runtime IDs/names and original recruitment hook; final four are **new authored candidates for t-003 migration**, not playable yet. Each quest is a minimum of three separate beats, crossing at least two star systems. The player can complete, compromise, lose or decline an arc. Personal quests should alter a port, affect an antagonist and reward agency rather than obedience. Injury/absence/low trust can suspend but not delete a quest. Always give a meaningful refusal that does not softlock the run.
