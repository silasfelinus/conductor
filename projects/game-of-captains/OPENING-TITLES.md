# Space Couch: opening-titles music video

Written 2026-10-27 PT at Silas's request: an opening for Space Couch with a Saturday-morning
superhero-cartoon feel, jazzy, surprise beats, high energy, a humming engine and loud percussive
horns. Storyboard art: [`art/OPENING-TITLES-ROUND-1.yaml`](art/OPENING-TITLES-ROUND-1.yaml)
(18 plates, one per beat below; queued is not rendered). Canon follows
`kind_robots/docs/space-couch/SHIP-BIBLE.md`: the ship IS the long red couch in open space inside a
faint force bubble. No hull, bridge or cockpit in any shot.

**Format:** cold open + 75-second theme. **Tempo:** big-band swing at 176 BPM, with two hard stops
and one half-time drop. **Key:** B♭, rising to C for the last chorus.
**Under everything:** the couch engine, a low B♭ hum like a refrigerator dreaming of greatness. It
never stops, even when the band does.

---

### 0:00 — Cold open *(no music)* · plate 01

Black. Stars come up slowly. One small **leather squeak**. The engine hum fades in and builds until
the image shakes slightly.

**NARRATOR** *(huge, sincere, deeply over-committed)*:
> In the cold and uncaring vacuum of space... where no chair has dared to go... one piece of
> furniture... chose... to RECLINE.

A slow push in on the **red couch**, drifting sideways through a violet nebula. The shield glints
once, like a soap bubble catching light.

**HORNS: stab. stab. STAB.**

### 0:08 — Title slam · plate 02

On the third stab, the drums kick in and **SPACE COUCH** slams onto the screen in big chrome comic
letters. The couch flies straight through the title and the letters wobble like jelly.

**CHOIR** *(full gospel-and-brass shout)*: **SPACE! COUCH!**

### 0:12 — Verse 1 *(walking bass, ride cymbal, hum underneath)* · plates 03–06

The camera runs along the backrest. Each crew member gets a freeze-frame, a name card and a
one-beat horn sting.

> *Who's got the stars in the palm of their hand?*
> **BOSUN PELL**, holding a wrench like a microphone (*trombone blat*)
> *Who reads the sky like a cheap paper map?*
> **TESS HELIO**, pointing at a star that should not be moving (*trumpet shake*)
> *Who's got opinions and, sadly, they're right?*
> **MOX-9**, already annoyed (*a single muted "wah"*)

**Captain slot:** a slot-machine wheel spins through all six captains (Fluke, Vale, Solace, Rex,
Quill, Ro) and stops on whichever one the player picks. The default edit lands on **CAPTAIN FLUKE**,
saluting the wrong direction.

> *And the one in the middle who's sitting up STRAIGHT!*

### 0:22 — First surprise beat · plate 07

The band **stops dead**. Only the engine hum is left. Everyone freezes mid-pose. A cushion drifts
away very slowly. Mox-9's eyes follow it. Somewhere, a cowbell plays one note.

**PELL** *(flat)*: "I repair the ship. I do not repair the *cushions*."

**DRUM FILL**, and the band crashes back in.

### 0:27 — Chorus *(full shout, horns punching on 2 and 4)* · plate 08

> **SPACE COUCH!** *(SPACE COUCH!)*
> **Cushions to the stars!**
> No hull, no hatch, no bridge to guard,
> Just a bubble and a *whole* lot of heart!
> **SPACE COUCH!** *(SIT DOWN!)*
> **Sit down for JUSTICE!**
> When the galaxy calls, you don't stand up,
> **You SCOOT OVER!**

On "scoot over," all four crew slide one seat to the right on the beat. A tiny stowaway alien now
sits in the empty cushion and waves at the camera.

### 0:38 — Pocket rooms *(horn-section call and response)* · plates 09–11

The backrest unfolds like a pop-up book. Each room gets its own horn voice:

- **HOLODECK:** wah-wah trumpet. A neon grid blooms behind the couch, and a rehearsal dragon bows
  politely.
- **WAR ROOM:** trombone glissando. A huge holo-map flips open and the crew all lean in at the same
  time.
- **INFIRMARY:** flute trill. **Dr. Vela Sorn** turns around in a lab coat: "Primitive is not a
  diagnosis."

Each room folds shut with a **snare snap**, and the couch is the only thing on screen again.

### 0:46 — The villains *(half-time drop, bari sax, sinister big-band growl)* · plates 12–14

The tempo falls to half. The stars dim, and the shield flickers orange.

- **THE GALACTIC AUDITOR** looms over a planet, holding a clipboard the size of a moon. A holo-notice
  stamps across the couch: **YOUR KINDNESS HAS BEEN ASSIGNED AN INTEREST RATE.**
  *(Brass: "dun-dun-DUNNN")*
- **THE QUEEN OF BORROWED FACES** appears in every crew member's reflection at once.
- **DR. CURDLE** floats past on an orbital food dispenser wearing a mayoral sash. It has already
  elected itself. *(A kazoo sneaks in with the horn line.)*

### 0:54 — Second surprise beat · plate 15

Silence. Then a **double-time break**: drums go full jungle-swing and the horns fire stabs like a
Gatling gun.

**WARP.** The couch **barrel-rolls** through a starburst and the cushions scatter everywhere. Each
crew member catches a flying cushion on a horn hit: *bap, bap, bap, BAP.* Captain Fluke catches two,
then neither.

### 1:00 — Bridge *(it's a bar, not a ship's bridge)* · plate 16

The couch docks at the **Serendipity Space Bar**. The building's lights pulse in time with the
music. **BARKEEP VOX**, the warmly sardonic tardigrade, slides four drinks down the counter on four
horn hits. A neon sign over the bar reads: **NO VAPORIZING PAYING GUESTS.**

**FLUKE** *(voice-over, captain's log)*: "Captain's log. We have never once been lost."
**SHIP COMPUTER** *(dry)*: "Correction: we are lost right now."

### 1:05 — Final chorus *(key change up to C, everything on full)* · plate 17

> **SPACE COUCH!** *(SPACE COUCH!)*
> **Cushions to the stars!**
> Keep your feet off the force field, friend,
> And your heart where the people are!
> **SPACE COUCH!** *(SIT DOWN!)*
> **Sit down for JUSTICE!**
> When the universe asks who's gonna help...
> *(the band stops; only the hum is left)*
> **WE'RE ALREADY SITTING HERE!**

### 1:13 — Final shot · plate 18

A wide shot pulls back to the full red couch, small against an enormous starfield. The four crew are
relaxed and sharing a bowl of space popcorn, and the shield glints one last time.

**HORNS: stab. stab. ...** A long pause. The third stab never comes.

**MOX-9** *(off-screen)*: "I have opinions about that ending."

**STAB.** Smash to the logo.

## Production notes

- **Instruments:** five saxes, four trumpets, four trombones, upright bass, kit plus timpani, Hammond
  organ for the bubble shimmer, and one kazoo for Dr. Curdle. The engine hum is a separate stem
  under everything, side-chained so it pulses with the kick drum.
- **The superhero-cartoon feel comes from the narrator** taking everything seriously while the
  visuals undercut him, plus horns played as punctuation rather than melody. All lyrics, characters
  and gags are original Kind Robots canon.
- **Lettering is code overlay:** the title slam, name cards and the bar's house-rule sign render
  over blank surfaces in the plates.
- **Canon sources:** `kind_robots/utils/gameOfCaptains/` (captains, crew, villains, rooms) and
  `kind_robots/docs/space-couch/SHIP-BIBLE.md`.
