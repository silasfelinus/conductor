# Monster Recast — Production Style Guide

status: authoritative for production art and coloring conversion
updated: 2026-07-10
audience: teen and adult horror-coloring fans, approximately PG-13

## Core decision

The canonical source art for Monster Recast is a **finished graphic horror illustration in color**. Build the strongest colored image first, then derive the black-and-white coloring page from that same image with minimal compositional change.

The target is not a loose concept sketch, painterly poster, photoreal movie still, cute vector cartoon, or joke-heavy novelty illustration. It is a highly detailed comic/graphic-novel horror plate with:

- thick, confident black contours
- flat, clearly bounded color regions
- no soft gradients or airbrushed rendering
- no painterly haze
- strong silhouettes and decisive poses
- dense but organized detail
- many enclosed shapes that remain enjoyable to color
- serious menace with theatrical camp

The successful Frankenstein creation and she-wolf studies are the clearest tonal benchmarks: monumental, dramatic, richly detailed, and already close to coloring-page structure before conversion.

## Tone: serious camp, not gag illustration

Camp comes from casting, confidence, glamour, theatricality, scale, melodrama, and the premise itself. It should not turn the book into slapstick, novelty labels, or a crowded parade of jokes.

- Play the horror sincerely.
- Let the reversal or visual situation carry the humor.
- Prefer one strong camp flourish over many joke props.
- Do not add product labels, signs, captions, slogans, title cards, or funny written messages.
- Avoid cartoon mugging, exaggerated reaction faces, and "Wacky Races" energy.
- Preserve danger, ugliness, scars, burns, decay, physical power, and uncanny staging.

A viewer should first see a striking horror image. The wit should register immediately afterward.

## Colored master specification

Each selected page begins as a full-color master at portrait 3:4 or print-compatible 8.5x11 proportions.

### Line and color

- Use thick black outer contours and confident medium interior lines.
- Use flat color fills bounded by linework.
- Do not use gradients, soft shading, airbrush, bloom, photographic lighting, or painterly blending.
- Where depth or lighting is needed, create a separate, clearly bounded darker or lighter color shape.
- Limited hard-edged cast shadows are allowed when they create a useful enclosed region.
- Keep the palette controlled enough that adjacent shapes remain distinct.
- Avoid large muddy black areas that would disappear in the coloring conversion.

### Detail density

The pages should be richly detailed, but the detail must remain structured.

- Concentrate the highest detail on the face, hands, signature monster anatomy, costume, and key prop.
- Use repeated but varied closed shapes for scars, stitches, screws, fur clumps, bandages, scales, machinery, fabric panels, jewelry, and architecture.
- Give the background enough information to tell the story without competing with the figure.
- Favor large, medium, and small regions rather than uniform micro-detail.
- Keep a readable silhouette at thumbnail size.
- The premise must still read in one second.

Complexity is successful when it rewards close coloring without making the image difficult to understand.

## Composition and physical logic

Bold perspective is encouraged, but anatomy and spatial continuity must survive close inspection.

Before accepting a page, verify:

1. Every limb connects to the correct body location.
2. The figure occupies one coherent three-dimensional space.
3. Contact points touch the floor, furniture, wall, or prop where intended.
4. Occlusion is physically believable.
5. A figure emerging through an opening begins exactly at that opening's plane.
6. No body mass appears above, behind, or beside a portal where it could not physically exist.
7. Props meet hands and bodies at workable angles.
8. The pose can be understood without explaining it in text.

Do not accept a dramatic perspective merely because individual body parts look impressive. The complete action must make physical sense.

## Anatomy and mutation rule

Accidental AI mutation is not a design feature.

- Do not add extra arms, legs, fingers, heads, breasts, or duplicated anatomy.
- A character has ordinary limb count unless the concept explicitly requires supernatural anatomy and Silas has approved it.
- Pound Foolish has **exactly two arms**.
- Do not use multiplied limbs as shorthand for movement, grandeur, magic, or camp.
- Extra limbs carry a strong AI-generation stigma and undermine otherwise intentional creature design.

Nonhuman anatomy is welcome when conceptually required, but it must be designed, consistent, and clearly described.

## One image means one image

Every requested render is a separate file.

- No collage.
- No grid.
- No contact sheet.
- No multi-panel comic page.
- No decorative border that creates panel-like compartments.
- Do not combine a batch of prompts into one image.

Batch size describes the number of independent files to generate, never the composition of a single output.

## Colored-master to coloring-page workflow

The coloring page is not a separate redesign. It is the same successful illustration translated into clean black line art.

Preserve:

- exact pose and camera angle
- character proportions and body type
- facial identity and expression
- silhouette
- anatomy and limb placement
- foreground and background arrangement
- important props
- scars, burns, stitches, screws, fur, bandages, and other signature textures
- strategic obstruction and PG-13 coverage

Convert by:

- removing all color fills
- replacing any necessary hard-edged color boundaries with clean line boundaries
- retaining substantial environment and costume detail
- opening large black masses into colorable regions
- cleaning line tangencies and accidental gaps
- ensuring major regions are closed
- preserving the high-detail graphic-novel character of the colored master

Do **not** simplify the page into sparse children's line art. Do not add titles, labels, decorative text, captions, or black background rectangles. The final page should feel like the uncolored ink stage of the same finished illustration.

## Coloring-page line standard

- pure white page
- clean black lines only
- no grayscale
- no halftone
- no soft shading
- no dense crosshatching used as tonal fill
- varied line weight, with stronger exterior contours
- enough detail for teen and adult coloring
- a deliberate mix of large, medium, and intricate enclosed regions
- readable face, hands, pose, and visual hook
- print-safe margins and no cropped fingers, feet, props, or hair unless deliberately composed

Fine texture lines may describe hair, fur, scars, wrinkles, wood grain, fabric, or machinery, but should not turn broad regions into uncolorable gray noise.

## Character-specific production notes learned from demos

### The Perfect Woman

- Monumental, glorious, muscular, and unmistakably the primary monster.
- The low-angle laboratory composition works.
- Preserve the stitched anatomy and dense machinery.
- Do not turn her into a delicate bride figure.

### Moon-Torn

- The enormous moon, low angle, roof contact, powerful thighs, reaching claw, and grouped fur shapes are strong.
- This is a model for a colored image that can become a coloring page with minimal structural change.

### Broadcast Giant

- The television must remain tiny and off to one side in a diorama-like room view.
- The giant man is already mostly outside the television and advancing toward the viewer.
- Approximately the back two-thirds of one leg or thigh remains through the screen.
- His body begins at the television screen plane; no torso, hip, shoulder, fat-like mass, or other anatomy may appear vertically above or behind the television.
- One forward arm may partially cross the pelvis to preserve PG-13 coverage while maintaining the crawl.
- The nearest hand should be large in forced perspective and reach toward the viewer.
- At least one forward foot should visibly contact the floor.
- Hair is long, stringy, and wet, completely covering the eyes and most of the face.

### Pound Foolish

- Exactly two arms.
- Her scale, body volume, costume, and impossible closet carnival create the spectacle.
- Do not make her goofy or surround her with novelty jokes.

### Masked Bath Ritual

- Use a strict side view.
- The knife must meet the leg at a believable shallow shaving angle.
- Leg stubble consists of distinct rigid, pointy hairs in varied lengths and directions.
- Do not render the hair as soft fuzz, wisps, smoke, or fur.
- The original theatrical mask may use one bold, enjoyable accent color and graphic internal patterning.

### Burned Dream Killer

- The confident doorway composition works.
- Burns remain extensive and visibly textured; do not beautify them away.
- The sensuality comes from confidence and pose, not from erasing injury.

## Rejection checklist

Reject or revise an image when any of these are true:

- generated as a collage, grid, or contact sheet
- too jokey, cute, busy, or novelty-driven
- contains unnecessary text
- looks painterly, airbrushed, photographic, or softly shaded instead of graphic and flat-colored
- lacks enough enclosed regions to become satisfying line art
- simplifies the coloring page far below the detail of the colored master
- has accidental extra limbs or duplicated anatomy
- has impossible portal/emergence geometry
- has floating feet, broken contact points, or unusable prop angles
- obscures the core action with secondary props
- changes body type, age, scars, burns, ugliness, muscle, or creature anatomy during conversion
- reads as a copied franchise character rather than an original recast design

## Production priority

For page production, prioritize:

1. final colored graphic illustration master
2. faithful black-and-white coloring conversion
3. revision of anatomy, perspective, closures, and print usability
4. optional movie-still or painterly experiments only when they help solve staging or mood

The book is built from the colored-master/coloring-page pair. Other render modes are exploratory references, not equal production deliverables.
