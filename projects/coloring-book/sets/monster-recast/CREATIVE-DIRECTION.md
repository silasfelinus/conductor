# Monster Recast — Creative Direction v3

status: authoritative for concept development
updated: 2026-07-10
audience target: progressive horror fan, approximately age 15+

`STYLE-GUIDE.md` is authoritative for production rendering, linework, color treatment, anatomy checks, anti-collage rules, and colored-master-to-coloring-page conversion. This file governs concept, tone, rating, gender play, framing, and originality.

## The promise

Monster Recast combines three passions without apologizing for any of them:

- horror movies
- gender bending
- camp

Every successful image needs **both a horror engine and a camp engine**. Horror supplies menace, disfigurement, scale, body transformation, danger, uncanny staging, and atmosphere. Camp supplies theatricality, vanity, excess, knowing poses, melodrama, and the joy of seeing a familiar trope confidently recast.

Camp should be played seriously. Humor comes from the premise, casting, pose, glamour, reversal, or scale—not from novelty labels, crowded gag props, cartoon mugging, or slapstick. This is not a wacky-race parody book. The viewer should first see a striking horror image, then register the wit.

The collection is progressive, queer-positive, sex-positive, and gender-positive. It may be alluring, flirtatious, or tastefully titillating, but it is designed for a progressive teenage horror fan rather than an adults-only product.

## Rating line

Aim for the visual equivalent of adventurous PG-13 horror:

- implied nudity is allowed when strategically obscured
- lingerie, swimwear, fishnets, cleavage, bare legs, muscular torsos, and suggestive posing are allowed
- no visible genitals or nipples
- no explicit sexual acts
- no sexualized minors
- no sexual violence
- blood, scars, burns, decay, and body horror may appear, but avoid graphic gore and exposed viscera
- danger should feel real enough to preserve the horror
- humor must not erase injury, disfigurement, disability, age, or monstrosity

Titillation should come primarily from framing, confidence, posture, texture, scale, costume, and strategic obstruction rather than explicit anatomy.

## Gender is not a costume preset

A gender swap is not automatically a thin body, softened muscles, a frilly dress, lipstick, or a feminized version of an existing male silhouette.

The cast should include:

- statuesque and Amazonian bodies
- broad, muscular women
- zaftig and Botticellian bodies
- gaunt and genuinely ugly women
- older women
- lanky and delicate men
- muscular and decorative men
- trans and nonbinary characters
- scarred, burned, disfigured, hairy, bald, asymmetrical, and visibly monstrous bodies

Femininity may be severe, athletic, ugly, regal, predatory, practical, old, muscular, grotesque, glamorous, or barely signaled at all. Masculinity may be decorative, vulnerable, sensual, theatrical, monstrous, youthful, or soft without becoming a joke.

Do not erase the defining horror trait to make a character attractive. The burned dream killer remains a burn victim. The fly woman retains a fully detailed insect head. The female Nosferatu should be repulsively gaunt. The feminine hunter keeps the frightening mandibles and lethal physicality.

## Page design test

Every candidate should answer these questions:

1. **What is the instantly readable movie-horror lineage?**
2. **What is the gender or presentation recast?**
3. **What is the visual reversal or camp flourish?**
4. **What remains frightening?**
5. **What body type or physical texture makes this page distinct from the others?**
6. **What is the strongest camera angle or composition?**
7. **Does the anatomy and spatial logic make physical sense?**
8. **Can the colored image become a detailed coloring page without redesigning it?**

If an image is merely a pretty model in themed clothing, it fails. If it is merely a grim monster with no wit or gender play, it also fails. If the premise needs a caption to be understood, revise the composition.

## Canonical visual language

The production target is a finished, highly detailed **graphic horror illustration**:

- thick confident black contours
- flat bounded color regions
- no soft gradients, airbrush, or painterly haze
- hard-edged secondary color shapes where lighting or depth is needed
- strong silhouettes and bold poses
- dense but organized detail
- many enclosed sections suitable for later coloring
- serious horror tone with theatrical camp

The color illustration is the master artwork. The black-and-white page should preserve its exact pose, camera angle, proportions, expression, anatomy, staging, and environmental arrangement.

Do not generate a batch as a collage, grid, contact sheet, or multi-panel page. One requested image always means one independent file.

## Framing language

Favor decisive, cinematic viewpoints rather than neutral character turnarounds:

- extreme close-up modeled on a poster face
- low-angle giant reveal
- forced perspective that makes a room impossible
- doorway silhouette
- mask-on ritual
- laboratory activation tableau
- red-carpet catastrophe
- upright gurney transfer shot
- dressing-room mirror
- moon-swallowing howl silhouette
- side-view bathtub ritual
- ruined-gym aftermath
- throne or family portrait
- camera placed inside a closet, television, cage, coffin, or rubble gap

Classic poses and variations on famous trope scenes are welcome as **internal composition references**. Commercial art must still replace protected masks, makeup, exact costumes, actor likenesses, logos, and other character-specific expression with an original design.

Bold perspective must remain physically coherent. Limbs must connect correctly, feet and hands must meet surfaces, props must sit at workable angles, and figures emerging through portals must begin exactly at the portal plane. Never retain impossible anatomy merely because one isolated body part looks dramatic.

## Anatomy rule

Accidental AI mutation is not part of the design language.

- Do not add extra limbs or duplicated anatomy unless the concept explicitly requires it and Silas has approved it.
- Do not use multiplied arms as shorthand for motion, magic, grandeur, or camp.
- Pound Foolish has exactly two arms.
- Nonhuman anatomy must be intentional, consistent, and conceptually necessary.

## Homage-to-original workflow

The concept pool intentionally names films and characters so the creative reference is unambiguous. That is not the final sellable form.

For each approved concept:

1. generate rough internal studies that test framing, body, tone, and visual reversal
2. identify the exact features carrying the homage
3. preserve the broad trope and composition while replacing protected expression
4. assign an original name, biography, silhouette, costume system, props, and visual motifs
5. perform a copyright-distance review before selection
6. create the Kind Robots Character entry only from the final original design

Some concepts may remain too dependent on a protected mask or poster composition to use commercially. Those can still teach the art direction, but they do not automatically earn a place in the printed book.

## Colored-master to coloring-page workflow

Build the finished colored graphic illustration first. Then translate that exact image into line art.

Preserve:

- pose and camera angle
- body type and proportions
- facial identity and expression
- anatomy and limb placement
- scars, burns, stitches, screws, fur, scales, bandages, and creature texture
- important props and environmental storytelling
- strategic obstruction and PG-13 coverage

Convert through:

- clean bold black contours
- mostly closed regions
- no gray shading
- no halftone or tonal crosshatching
- limited solid-black masses
- hard color boundaries converted into useful line boundaries
- substantial detail retained from the colored master
- enough open regions for satisfying color choices

Do not reduce the result to sparse children's line art. The final page should feel like the uncolored ink stage of the same finished illustration.

## Approved exclusions

Do not pursue:

- The Thing gender swap — the creature is too mutable for the gender reversal to read cleanly
- Black Phillip — replace this with a woman presented as Satan herself, not a goat, demoness, or succubus
