## The concept

**Hollywood Recast 2** remixes famous classic-movie scene grammar through gender, body, age, presentation, costume, and cultural casting. Unlike Monster Recast, the focus is not movie monsters. Each page begins with a recognizable kind of cinematic moment—romantic reveal, dance number, chase, balcony scene, makeover, rain-soaked confession, heroic entrance, comic seduction—and recasts who receives the glamour, danger, desire, humiliation, or power.

The humor should come from confident casting and sincere performance, not mockery of gender expression or body type. A large bearded man may receive the full romantic-star treatment; a tiny elderly woman may occupy the traditionally masculine action-hero frame; a glamorous femme may inherit a hard-boiled antihero entrance. The new casting must be treated as beautiful, cinematic, and intentional.

This is a parked future set. It has no active roadmap task, generation queue, modeler request, publishing plan, or POD action. Preserve the seed until Silas chooses to develop it.

## First seed: Highland Heatwave

Internal lineage reference: the famous subway-grate breeze scene associated with *The Seven Year Itch*.

A large, jolly, deeply bearded adult Scotsman stands over a city ventilation grate at night as an upward rush of air billows his formal kilt into a dramatic star-making shape. He beams with unabashed delight rather than embarrassment. Give him a strong round body, magnificent beard, polished boots, dress sporran, waistcoat, flashes of tartan, and a theatrical hand gesture that sells the breeze. Keep all anatomy safely obscured through pose, kilt folds, sporran placement, and camera angle.

The finished public-facing composition must become an original parody/remix rather than an actor likeness or copied film still. Change the location design, facial identity, costume details, camera distance, supporting environment, and exact body pose while preserving the broad cinematic joke: an unlikely male romantic icon receives the glamorous windblown reveal with complete sincerity.

Working title: **Highland Heatwave**. Alternate titles can be explored later.

## Provisional visual language

Until this set receives its own style pass, use the production principles in `../monster-recast/STYLE-GUIDE.md`:

- colored graphic master first
- faithful black-and-white conversion second
- thick confident black contours
- flat bounded colors and hard-edged value shapes
- high organized detail with many colorable regions
- bold cinematic poses and physically coherent perspective
- one independent image per file, never a collage or panel grid
- no generated text inside the artwork

Hollywood Recast 2 may ultimately use a brighter old-Hollywood palette and less horror texture than Monster Recast, but it should retain the same print-friendly master-to-line-art discipline.

## Originality and parody guardrails

Internal planning may name a movie or iconic scene so the cinematic grammar is clear. That does not make an exact recreation automatically suitable for sale.

Before commercial selection:

1. identify which scene elements carry recognition;
2. replace actor likenesses, exact wardrobe, exact set dressing, logos, quoted dialogue, and copied framing;
3. change several foundational anchors while keeping the transformative casting premise legible;
4. evaluate whether the image communicates a genuine remix or commentary rather than functioning as substitute franchise art;
5. keep uncertain pieces as internal inspiration until reviewed.

No project document should claim that parody or homage guarantees freedom from copyright or publicity claims.
