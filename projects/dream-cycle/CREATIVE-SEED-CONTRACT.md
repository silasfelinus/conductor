# Dream Creative Seed Contract

**Effective:** 2026-07-24  
**Applies to:** every newly planned `type: dream` creation, including the rolling Daily Dream proposal lane and manually added backlog outlines.

## Start here — before inventing the place

Every Dream plan begins by choosing these three ingredients:

1. **One or two genres** — actual story engines such as courtroom drama, survival horror, sports comedy, hard science fiction, heist, western, romance, procedural, or a deliberate blend. Mood words such as “cozy,” “mystical,” or “whimsical” are not genres by themselves.
2. **One occupation** — a specific job, trade, duty, or vocation that creates routines, tools, pressures, and conflicts. Prefer concrete work over vague archetypes: avalanche forecaster, debt collector, coral surgeon, union organizer, goalie, forensic accountant.
3. **One animal or species** — terrestrial, aquatic, extinct, microscopic, alien, botanical, fungal, robotic, mythic, or otherwise non-default. “Human” is allowed only when it is a meaningful choice rather than an unexamined fallback.

Only after those are selected does the author invent the world, locations, cast, narrator, rewards, scenarios, and art direction.

## The fusion test

The three seeds are not decorative tags. Each must materially change at least two parts of the Dream, such as:

- the central conflict or scenario structure
- the work routines, tools, economy, or social hierarchy
- character bodies, senses, movement, needs, or communication
- location architecture and environmental rules
- rewards and abilities
- the visual language and art prompts

A seed fails when it can be removed without substantially changing the Dream. Rewrite the concept until the combination produces consequences.

## Variety guardrails

- Do not default to another enchanted lighthouse, mystical bell tower, magical archive, cozy market, lantern-lit workshop, or vaguely whimsical tower with renamed nouns.
- Architecture is a consequence of the seed fusion, not the starting idea.
- Recent Dreams must be checked so the new proposal does not repeat the same genre feel, occupation family, species family, palette, location type, or character archetypes.
- Wild combinations are welcome; coherence still matters. The goal is not random-word soup but a world that could only have come from this particular trio.

## Required outline record

Every new Dream outline includes a `## Creative seeds` section recording:

- **Genres:** one or two
- **Occupation:** one
- **Animal / species:** one
- **Fusion:** a short explanation of how the ingredients changed the resulting world

The Daily Dream JSON contract carries the same data as `creative_seeds` and rejects proposals that omit it.
