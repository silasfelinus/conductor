## The idea
An ancient wood has grown up through a corporate office tower, and the dryads who staff its lower floors now run the forest's civil service — enforcing centuries of folklore as binding statute law on anyone who wants to fly, walk, or trade through their canopy. A courier pilot keeps coming back for a permit renewal that's really an excuse to see the root-bound clerk she can't stop loving.

## Dream vibe (1)
**The Canopy Permit Office** — A civil-service outpost bound into an old forest, where dryad clerks enforce folklore as binding statute and pilots queue for stamped rights of passage.
Art: Interior of a moss-furred waiting room where steel filing cabinets are grown through with living root, tarnished brass wickets set into bark-covered walls, a knot of black-veined bone forming the front desk, pale green under-canopy light falling through a cracked skylight onto scattered stamped forms.

## Dream location (1)
- **Root Court Tower** — known for a corporate glass-and-steel office tower whose lower dozen floors have been swallowed by an ancient wood, its curtain wall cracked open by root growth. Local rule: every visitor must state their business aloud to the entry tree before the elevator doors will part. Best scene: the ground-floor atrium where escalators rust under hanging moss and a single working elevator car, its brass gate wrapped in bark, rises through a shaft lined with root-bored office doors. Art: a fifty-story glass-and-steel tower engulfed from the base upward by massive tree roots that have cracked the lobby floor and climbed the interior stairwell, fluorescent office lighting flickering behind vine-choked windows on the lower floors while the upper floors stand dark and untouched, exterior view at dusk with fog pooling at street level

## Character (1)
- **Cass Windrell** — a resourceful courier pilot who flies unlicensed cargo runs through the forest's restricted canopy airspace, chasing a loophole that would let a rooted clerk finally leave her post. Carries a scuffed leather permit satchel stuffed with forged and half-forged stamps. Complication: the clerk she loves is a dryad fused to a single office chair grown from her own root system — pulling her free would kill the tree and her both. Look: a lean woman in a quilted flight jacket patched with mismatched fabric, aviator goggles pushed up into close-cropped grey-streaked hair, one forearm wrapped in cracked leather strapping, knuckles stained dark with old stamp ink

## Reward item (1)
- **The Self-Stamping Bone Seal** (ITEM, RARE) — an instant, binding forest-law permit stamp that overrides ordinary bureaucratic review. Best used when a passage or exemption is needed before a deadline the normal permit queue can't meet. The catch: every use is logged against the bearer's own name in the forest's root-ledger, and the debt eventually comes due in a form the bearer doesn't choose. Look: a fist-sized signet carved from black-veined bone, one face worn to a soft shine by a knotwork sigil, a hinged brass arm folded against its side, faint red light tracing the carved grooves whenever it is pressed to paper

## Reward skill (1)
- **Root-Reading** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — the ability to draw the forest's stored case history directly out of a tree's growth rings through touch. Best used when contesting a ruling that has no paper record left to cite. The catch: each use leaves a little more bark-like scarring on the caller's arm that never fully fades. Look: thin vine-like tendrils growing visibly out from beneath the fingernails and across the back of the hand, briefly threading into the grain of touched wood, faint green bioluminescence pulsing under the skin along each vein
