## New Facets (invented for this dream)
- **Privately Decent** (`privately-decent`, ALIGNMENT) — Someone who behaves well specifically where no one would ever find out, and distrusts anyone whose decency only shows when it's being watched. It costs them the credit for every kindness that nobody saw.
- **Caught in Passing** (`caught-in-passing`, STYLE) — Information that only ever arrives sideways — caught in a snatch of conversation, a shout through fog, a half-heard argument — never delivered directly to the person who needs it. What's understood is always slightly less than what was actually said.

## The idea
A fogbound cliff warren has spent eleven years unable to say outright whether the elder it exiled deserved it, and the only version anyone gets is a half-heard fragment in the fog, gone before it finishes.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Cairnfall** — A fog that only lifts for the guilty is the one piece of evidence nobody can enter into a case file.
Art: A drystone cairn on a headland, barely visible through a solid bank of grey sea-fog, one pale shape moving low along its base.

## Dream location (1)
- **Cairnwarren** — Cairnwarren is stacked into a fogbound cliff face where rock hyrax colonies have burrowed for longer than anyone has kept count, and half the settlement's walls are actually old warren tunnels widened by hand. Local rule: No door in Cairnwarren locks from the outside; it is understood, without being written anywhere, that a door locked against its own household is a confession. Best scene: Fog rolls uphill against the wind and pours in through a warren mouth, and for a few seconds every lit window in Cairnwarren goes the same flat grey. Art: A cliffside warren-town half-dissolved in thick grey sea-fog, dozens of small round burrow-doors glowing faint blue-white from veins of pulse metal in the stone, the fog itself visibly rolling and thickening between the buildings.

## Character (1)
- **Fenna Muir** — Nobody in Cairnwarren can say exactly what Fenna Muir is for, only that things go worse whenever she's not around, so no one has worked up the nerve to ask her to leave. A hand-braided grass rope, coiled at her hip in the pattern crofters used to tie down roof-thatch two generations back home, on an island she's never actually seen. Complication: Whether Cairnwarren exiled the right person eleven years ago is still argued at low volume whenever the fog's thick enough that no one can be sure who's actually listening — and Fenna has never once corrected either version. Look: A rock hyrax built massive and slow, more boulder than animal at rest -- thick-set body the size of a large dog, blunt whiskered snout, small round ears nearly lost in a coat gone the soft, bleached pastel of a rainbow left too long in the sun. Old enough that the fur around her eyes has gone paler still, near white. Wears a single carved bone pin at her collar in a style pulled indiscriminately from both the men's and women's crofting dress passed down from a homeland she's never seen. Sits with her chin low and eyes half-lidded, watching everything and startled by nothing.

## Reward item (1)
- **Fogtap** (ITEM, RARE) — It carries a voice sideways through fog and stone for exactly as long as it takes to say one sentence, and not a syllable longer. Best used when: You need to know something is being said without anyone knowing you're the one hearing it. The catch: It never delivers a whole sentence — the start or the end is always lost to whichever direction the fog is moving that day. Look: A thumb-sized coil of pulse metal wound into a tight spiral, one end left open like an unfinished ring; it holds a faint blue pulse that brightens only when something nearby is being said aloud.

## Reward skill (1)
- **Sporewit** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — It lets the user read what's been rotting nearby, and roughly how long, from nothing but where the fungus has chosen to grow. Best used when: The only witness left to what happened is whatever's still decomposing at the scene. The catch: It only ever answers questions about decay — it has nothing to say about anyone still alive, guilty or otherwise. Look: Faint threads of pale fungal bloom surface through cracks in stone or wood wherever the user crouches and holds still, tracing the shape of whatever lies beneath before wilting back out of sight.

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **Undersaid** — In Cairnfall, Cairnwarren's ration council still hasn't decided whether Fenna Muir's exile eleven years ago was ever lawful, and the answer keeps arriving in pieces — overheard through fog, never once said to her directly.
