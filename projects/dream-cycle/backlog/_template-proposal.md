## Creative seeds
Choose these BEFORE inventing the place. See `../CREATIVE-SEED-CONTRACT.md`.

- **Occupation:** one specific job, trade, duty, or vocation.

## The idea
2-4 sentences. What is this world, why would someone want to spend time in it?

two locations, a shared vibe, three characters, a host, and two rewards. Build
from the creative seeds; do not start with an enchanted building and reverse-fit
some labels onto it.
