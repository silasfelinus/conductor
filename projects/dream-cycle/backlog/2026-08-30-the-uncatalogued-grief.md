## The idea
A shy undead filing clerk keeps reopening a sealed case of grief that a marmoset messenger insists belongs to someone who hasn't been born yet.

## Dream vibe (1)
**The Weeping Atlas** — Every unfiled sorrow leaves a fold in the map that outlives the mapper.
Art: A vast library-observatory where star charts bleed slowly into hand-drawn grief maps, the ink still wet after centuries, brass astrolabes tangled in loose thread.

## Dream location (1)
- **The Department of Recurring Losses** — It processes griefs that no one ever submitted, arriving pre-filed and already overdue. Local rule: Every ticket must be resolved before its curtain stops humming. Best scene: A marmoset clerk drags a case file out of a wall that holds no filing cabinet at all. Art: A zero-gravity office suspended inside a dim nebula, rows of floating aether-silk curtains glowing faint silver and rippling with no wind, brass conduits threading between drifting filing cabinets, marmosets clambering along the pipework with folders clamped in their tails

## Character (1)
- **Marion Delisle** — They are quietly reopening the one case they were never allowed to stamp closed. Carries: A bone bobbin wound with unraveling aether-silk thread hangs from their belt. Complication: The thread only ever unspools toward losses that they themselves caused. Look: a stooped clerk in a moth-eaten grey coat buttoned to the throat, hands stitched along the knuckles with pale silver thread, wide dark marmoset-round eyes that never quite meet yours, a bone bobbin of shimmering thread clipped to a frayed belt loop

## Reward item (1)
- **The Uncatalogued Ledger** (ITEM, RARE) — It reveals the one entry missing from any record. Best used when: A story arrives with a suspiciously clean ending. The catch: The missing entry always turns out to be about the reader. Look: a slim ledger bound in unraveling grey-silver aether-silk, its cover blank until opened, brittle pages whose ink visibly redraws itself in dim light

## Reward skill (1)
- **Cartographer's Hush** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — It traces the exact path a grief traveled to reach you. Best used when: The same loss keeps resurfacing in new places. The catch: Walking the traced path visibly ages the caster by the years it covers. Look: a thin line of frost creeping silently across the floor from the caster's feet, breath fogging heavier with each step, fine grey hairs spreading from the temples outward as the line lengthens
