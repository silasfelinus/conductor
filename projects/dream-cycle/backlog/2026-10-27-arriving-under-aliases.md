## New Facets (invented for this dream)
- **Lost at Cards** (`lost-at-cards`, BACKSTORY) — Someone or somewhere that changed hands as the stake in a card game, wager or dare rather than through birth, sale or inheritance. It belongs where it landed by pure luck, and whoever lost it at the table never quite stops planning to win it back.
- **Plus-One** (`plus-one`, ROLE) — A figure who attends only as someone else's guest, admitted on another's say-so and holding no standing of their own. Nobody plans around them, so they see everything, and they are always the first one asked to leave.

## The idea
In a steamy glasshouse that a smuggling clan's grandmother once won at cards, the clan's youngest daughter is marrying a customs officer's son, and the family has hired an honest, newly grown pufferfish to welcome ninety-nine known criminals through the door one at a time.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Arriving Under Aliases** — Every guest at this wedding lies about their name, and the greeter at the door swells up spiny at each one.
Art: A steamy greenhouse doorway framed in white-painted wood and honey-gold glass panes, a small round pufferfish hovering at head height beside the door puffed up into a tight spiky ball, a queue of wedding guests in rumpled suits and flowered hats stretching back along a gravel path between tomato vines, one guest with a black eye holding a present wrapped in pink ribbon, small silver fish swimming through the warm steam, condensation and fingerprints on the glass, golden afternoon light.

## Dream location (1)
- **Kettlewarm Glasshouse** — A sprawling greenhouse of honey-coloured solar glass, won from a river baron in a card game forty years ago, where the air hangs so warm and wet that small fish swim between the tomato vines. Local rule: Nobody walks in ungreeted, and a guest who gives a false name at the door must dance one lap around the cucumber frames before the party lets them sit. Best scene: A wedding dance spills between the vines after sunset while a shoal of sardines swirls through the steam above, and every pane glows gold with the afternoon it saved. Art: A long steamy greenhouse of honey-gold glass panes in a white-painted wooden frame, tomato vines and cucumber trellises in wooden crates, a trestle table with a crumpled lace cloth, cake plates and smeared glasses, paper bunting, small silver fish swimming through the warm steam between the vines, condensation and fingerprints on the glass, the panes glowing gold from inside.

## Character (1)
- **Kai** — They were won at cards as a hatchling in a jam jar, raised among the clan's tomato vines, and now greet every guest at the glasshouse door by name. A numbered aluminium racing-pigeon ring clinks on their left fin, a parting gift from the coal-town pigeon fancier who lost them in that card game. Complication: Every lie told within a fin's length makes them swell up round and spiny, and tonight ninety-nine professional liars are queueing to shake their fin. Look: A small round pufferfish the size of a cabbage hovering at head height in warm greenhouse steam, short fins whirring, pale sand-coloured skin freckled with salt-and-pepper spots that cluster thickly down the left side and thin out to a few on the right, short spines lying flat, one pectoral fin mended with neat copper-wire stitches, a pale patch of new skin across the belly where it has stretched and healed many times, the oversized dark eyes of a fish barely past hatching set wide and steady in an earnest, attentive, close-mouthed expression, a numbered aluminium pigeon ring on the left fin, a narrow sash of tarred brown hemp tied around the middle in a raftsman's double hitch, a pink silk ribbon bow tied loosely to the tail fin and half undone, as though tried on and still being considered.
