## The idea
An always-online survival streamer's nightly transformation into a lantern-eyed werebeast has become a subscription ritual for a congregation that broadcasts her hunger as their god's hunger.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Feed of the Devouring Choir** — Thriller pacing wrapped around a slow-motion religious horror broadcast.
Art: Neon streaming-ring light haloing a rain-slicked rooftop aerial, phone-camera glass reflecting too many candle flames from the street below.

## Dream location (1)
- **Halo & Static Café** — Merchandise shelves of chipped magical-girl figurines line the walls behind a boarded broadcast booth. Local rule: Every barista has watched a broadcast ritual happen and calls the silence loyalty. Best scene: A camera boom swings past cracked granite countertops just as the choir begins humming underneath the floor. ## Character (1)
- **Persimmon Vale** — She livestreams every meal, workout, and breakdown because silence would cost her the subscription income her sister's chemotherapy depends on. A cracked ring light stays clipped permanently to her wrist strap, the one piece of gear she never streams without. Complication: Every livestream chat comment donating money also feeds a ritual she cannot see the cult performing. Look: A lean young woman in a threadbare cardigan over a ring-light harness, jaw beginning to lengthen and eyes gone amber-slit under smeared concealer.

- **The Undertow Switch** (ITEM, Rare) — Holding this switch grants a seven-second delay before any livestream broadcasts. Look: A fist-sized lump of star-flecked granite carved into a conch spiral, one flattened face inset with a corroded brass toggle.
