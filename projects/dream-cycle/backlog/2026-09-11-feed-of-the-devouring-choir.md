## The idea
Stored daylight in a colossal amber foundry makes hidden vampires shed translucent outer skins that stand up and begin choosing their own movements, turning an old fear of exposure into first contact with beings nobody expected their bodies to create.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Sunskin** — The shed skins remember posture, hunger, and habit, but not who they are supposed to belong to.
Art: A dry amber foundry in hard noon light, vampire figures beside translucent shed skins that hold human shape like empty membranes, sharp shadows crossing pale stone.

## Dream location (1)
- **Amberworks Nine** — Its cathedral-sized furnaces grow radiant amber ribs around iron machinery, storing desert sunlight in thick golden layers for nighttime craft. Local rule: Any shed skin that moves on its own gets open floor around it until everyone can tell which movements are copied and which are chosen. Best scene: A row of translucent skins peels itself from warm amber columns and turns toward the roof vents while their startled owners remain perfectly still below. Art: An immense dry industrial nave with black iron walkways, towering amber ribs, pale dust, hard gold sunbeams, and translucent skin-like membranes lifting in the heat.

## Character (1)
- **Helen Park** — She wants the hidden vampire community to treat the moving skins as possible neighbors before fear turns first contact into containment. A folding black shade frame rides over one shoulder beside a ceramic tray holding three delicate pieces of her own discarded skin. Complication: Her newest shed skin reacts to people she secretly trusts before she has admitted that trust to herself. Look: A middle-aged vampire engineer with close-cropped dark hair, soot-streaked coveralls, tinted goggles, a folded shade frame, and a translucent duplicate membrane standing half a pace behind her.

## Reward item (1)
- **Second-Hide Mantle** (ITEM, RARE) — Wrapped around a willing creature, the membrane reproduces its current posture and body heat as a separate moving decoy. Best used when: A living body needs room to observe a threat without presenting its true position or intentions. The catch: The decoy copies unconscious habits the wearer may not realize they have. Look: A weightless translucent membrane shaped like a loose human torso and arms, pearly at the edges with tiny branching capillaries visible in sunlight.

## Reward skill (1)
- **Afterpulse** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — The user can feel which movement in a nearby duplicate was copied from habit and which began independently. Best used when: A reflection, clone, shed form, or imitation behaves almost exactly like its source but not quite. The catch: The distinction arrives as a physical twitch in the user's own muscles and becomes exhausting in a crowded space. Look: A faint second outline lagging behind a moving hand, then separating cleanly at one finger as the two gestures choose different paths.

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **Make Room for the Molts** — In Sunskin at Amberworks Nine, Helen Park uses the Second-Hide Mantle and Afterpulse while the foundry's newly mobile skins climb the amber ribs, trying to learn whether their shared upward movement is imitation, instinct, or the first choice of a new kind of neighbor.
