## The idea
A floating night market cooks luck itself into every dish, seasoned with nanotech-infused Luck Dragon scales. When a scheming ex-desperado engineer's newest recipe starts rewriting diners' fates permanently, the dragon whose scales built the market's fortune comes to collect what's owed.

## Dream vibe (1)
**The Lucky Ladle** — Every bowl tastes like a fate someone else was supposed to have.
Art: Neon-lit night-market steam curling off dragon-scale garnish, each wisp faintly glittering with nanotech shimmer.

## Dream location (1)
- **Mercado de la Fortuna** — Every dish served here tastes like probability itself, ladled to order over the lagoon's bioluminescent glow. Local rule: No dish may be eaten before its luck finishes settling in the bowl. Best scene: A diner tastes a stew and watches their own hidden fate surface in the steam. Art: A floating market of lantern-lit stalls draped in rippling shimmer cloth, reflected double in a glowing lagoon, warm and slightly uncanny.

## Character (1)
- **Rook Calloway** — He rigs a Luck Dragon's shed scales into nanotech seasoning, scheming to corner the market on fortune itself. A saddlebag of self-assembling dragon-scale nanite spice rides at his hip, ready to season any dish with stolen luck. Complication: The nanites aren't just flavoring luck; they're quietly rewriting the diners' futures for keeps. Look: a duster coat stitched through with shimmer cloth, a chrome gauntlet, and a dragon-scale bolo tie that never stops glinting

## Reward item (1)
- **Tidefortune Ladle** (ITEM, RARE) — Stirred through any dish, it surfaces the hidden fortune buried in a person or object. Best used when: A mystery sits thick with an unspoken truth, needing only a little steam to rise to the surface. The catch: It can only reveal fortune that it's also willing to quietly siphon off for itself. Look: a dented tin ladle the length of a forearm, its bowl worn to a mirror finish, the handle wrapped in salt-stiffened cord, a thread of lantern light still clinging to the rim

## Reward skill (1)
- **Nanite Seasoning** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — Edible nanites reprogram on the fly, seasoning a meal toward any outcome its cook chooses. Best used when: A fate teeters on the edge, needing only the gentlest nudge rather than a forceful rewrite. The catch: Overuse leaves a faint shimmering residue that marks you as someone who's been tampering with luck. Look: a pinch of grey dust falling in slow motion over a steaming bowl, each mote lit from within and drawing a hairline trail, the broth beneath turning iridescent where they land

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **The Bowl That Remembered** — At The Lucky Ladle, in the floating stalls of Mercado de la Fortuna, Rook Calloway's newest bowl of dragon-scale stew turns a gambler's luck permanently good, and the Luck Dragon whose scales seasoned it surfaces from the lagoon to reclaim what was stolen before the whole market's fortune unravels.
