## Creative seeds
Choose these BEFORE inventing the place. See `../CREATIVE-SEED-CONTRACT.md`.
- **Genres:** exactly 1-2 actual story genres, not mood adjectives.
- **Occupation:** one specific job, trade, duty, or vocation.
- **Animal / species:** one consequential species choice.
- **Fusion:** explain how all three materially alter the conflict, routines,
  bodies/senses, locations, rewards, or visual language.

## The idea
2-4 sentences. What is this place, why would someone want to spend time in it?
The concept must grow from the creative seeds rather than beginning with a
magical building and reverse-fitting labels onto it.
