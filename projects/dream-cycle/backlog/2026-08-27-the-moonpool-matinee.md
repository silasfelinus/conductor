## The idea
At midnight, an abandoned boarding-school auditorium performs a different era every time the curtain rises. Student playwright Delphine Tarrant discovers tonight's audience is trapped inside the production, and every improvised line she speaks becomes physically true for thirty seconds.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Opening Night Has No Year** — Every curtain call opens onto the wrong century.
Art: An empty school stage under blue house lights, red velvet curtains opening onto several incompatible eras at once while a broad dugong silhouette stands at center stage holding a tiny recorder.

## Dream location (1)
- **The Living Auditorium** — Its sets molt into other eras in the middle of a scene, leaving actors stranded mid-line in a century that did not exist a moment before. Local rule: You must never applaud after the house lights turn blue. Best scene: The crystal footlights crack and the stage floor becomes black seawater. Art: A decaying boarding-school auditorium grown through with pale biological ribs and rootlike tubing, jagged amber spire-crystal footlights along the stage, velvet curtains hanging like deep-sea fins.

## Character (1)
- **Delphine Tarrant** — She must finish the missing final act and get the stranded audience back to its own time before sunrise. A battered microcassette recorder hangs from her canvas strap alongside a wooden rehearsal sword that turns to real steel beneath the footlights. Complication: Every sentence she improvises becomes physically true for thirty seconds, including her mistakes. Look: A broad slate-gray dugong student writer and playwright with a whiskered muzzle and small dark eyes, wearing a school cardigan tailored around powerful flipper-like forearms, a microcassette recorder hanging from a canvas strap.

## Reward item (1)
- **The Wolfglass Mask** (ITEM, RARE) — Wearing it swaps one role in the current scene between two people, casting hero as monster or monster as hero for as long as the trait lingers. Best used when: The wrong person has been cast as the monster or the hero, and the roles must trade places before the scene resolves. The catch: The wearer keeps one borrowed trait until sunrise. Look: A half-mask chipped from translucent spire crystal in the shape of a storybook wolf, with one pointed ear broken off and warm amber fractures glowing through its snout.

## Reward skill (1)
- **Gaslight Cue** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — Speaking a single stage direction briefly rewrites the room's physical rules for whoever performs it. Best used when: The cast is trapped inside a scene that has turned physically real, with no exit but a new rule for the room. The catch: It only works while at least one audience member still believes the performance. Look: A row of amber gas flames leaps from dark footlights and bends sideways in unison, throwing impossible hinged shadows around the performer.

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **The Last Act Floods** — In Opening Night Has No Year at The Living Auditorium, Delphine Tarrant watches a 1926 fencing chorus and a class from 2094 collide onstage as the floor softens into black water, the balcony grows gills, and something beneath the orchestra pit begins reciting an ending she has not imagined.
