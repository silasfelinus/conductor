## The idea
A year after a fleet of carnivorous potted geraniums fell from orbit and ate the alien hunters who had brought them, the harbour town of Pelargon has made the plants its neighbours, and its oldest resident is throwing the first harvest fair that the new apex predators might actually enjoy.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Pelargon** — The geraniums came down from orbit, ate everything that arrived with them, and stayed for the parade.
Art: A small sunny harbour town seen from above at noon, every roof, window box and fishing boat crowded with enormous red potted geraniums whose blooms open like toothed jaws, cracked amber landing pots still steaming in the bay, bunting strung between the chimneys, crowds waving flags along the quay, bold saturated daylight.

- **Geranium Quay** — It is the fishing quay of Pelargon, where every window box now holds a carnivorous geranium from orbit and each street stands at a different hour because the landing pods shattered the town's timeglass.
