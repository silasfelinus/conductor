## The idea
In the flooded neon sprawl of Static Row, the last analog detectives are androids built from salvaged silent-film projectors -- their vocal cords fried in a corporate raid years back, so every case is pitched, argued, and solved entirely through pratfalls, pantomime, and flickering title-cards. It's a cozy mystery world where the stakes stay small -- a stolen noodle recipe, a kidnapped memory-cat -- but the solving is pure two-reel silent comedy, staged under the constant hum of cyberpunk neon and corporate surveillance drones.

## Vibe / genre dream
**Flicker & Static** — GENRE. A cozy pratfall noir where every clue lands like a punchline and silence is the sharpest weapon in the room.

## Locations (2)
- **Flicker Court** — known for a 1920s backlot-turned-detective-bureau, still dressed with painted cobblestone streets and fake skylines, now rigged with holo-projectors and film-reel evidence archives Local rule: Nobody speaks inside Flicker Court -- every case is argued entirely in projected title-cards and exaggerated gesture; talking out loud voids your standing with the Court Best scene: A midnight lineup where six suspects re-enact their alibi as a silent slapstick routine, and the real liar is the one whose pratfall lands a beat too perfect Art: A cavernous soundstage-turned-office, black-and-white painted backdrops glowing under cyan neon rigging, brass film-reel filing cabinets stacked to the rafters, a detective android mid-bow under a spotlight, title-card projector beams cutting through drifting fog
- **Static Row** — known for a market alley of noodle stalls, pawnshop data-brokers, and flickering holo-billboards that buffer and skip like scratched film stock -- the cozy-mystery heart of the district Local rule: Every stall owner keeps a 'flicker ledger,' a silent-film-style reel logging each transaction; splice or erase a page and the whole row remembers you did it Best scene: A noodle-cart chase where the thief keeps slipping on the exact same spilled broth in the exact same spot, three times, to increasingly furious silent-comedy effect Art: A rain-slicked night alley strung with paper lanterns and fiber-optic cable, neon signage buzzing and skipping like scratched film, steam rising off food stalls, a projector-headed figure sprinting past a toppling stack of crates

## Characters (3)
- **Buster Vell** — Lead Reel Agent with fried vocal cords, drives every case toward the tidiest, most theatrical solution because a tidy ending is the only thing that still feels like control Carries a chest-mounted title-card projector that free-associates his thoughts into flickering captions Complication: his projector is running low on clean reel stock -- every case burns a little more of his limited remaining 'voice' Look: a trench coat patched with old film leader tape, a matte-black film-camera head with a single glass lens for a face, white-gloved hands built for exaggerated gestures
- **Dot Vashti** — Static Row's back-alley mechanic and reel-splicer, keeps the Reel Agents running and quietly feeds them real cyberpunk hacking tools disguised as silent-film gags Carries a toolkit shaped like a popcorn box that is actually a portable data-splicer Complication: she's slowly replacing her own hands with projector-parts to understand the agents better, and can't tell anymore if she's helping them or becoming one Look: grease-stained overalls over a patchwork of analog dials, one cybernetic eye that displays everything in scratchy monochrome film grain
- **Mister Click** — a mustache-twirling corpo enforcer for the Static Row Holding Company, treats every eviction and shakedown like a vaudeville performance because menace always lands better with a bow Carries a cane hiding a genuine EMP charge behind a silent-comedy spring-loaded gag Complication: he's started enjoying solving the Row's small mysteries alongside the Reel Agents more than his corpo job, and it's making him sloppy at both Look: a pinstripe suit two sizes too sharp, a projector-bulb monocle, slicked hair under a bowler hat that never quite sits straight

## Rewards (2 — one skill, one item)
- **Title-Card Bluff** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — project a false title-card into a scene, feeding onlookers a fabricated 'caption' of events that they'll believe over what they actually saw Best used when you need a suspect -- or a corpo drone -- to believe the wrong story just long enough to slip past The catch: burns one of your own limited title-cards to pull off; bluff too often and you go permanently silent for the rest of the scene
- **Spliced Reel** (ITEM, RARE) — a film reel stitched from a dozen mismatched moments; playing it back reveals one hidden clue or memory the original scene tried to cut Best used when a case's real evidence has been erased, blackmailed away, or was never spoken aloud to begin with The catch: the reel only plays once before the splice degrades to static, and it never shows the whole truth -- only the piece someone thought was safe to cut

## Scenarios (1-2)
- **The Noodle Cart Caper** — A beloved Static Row noodle-stall's secret broth recipe vanishes overnight, and Buster Vell must stage a silent lineup at Flicker Court while Dot Vashti spliced-reels the stall's last night of footage -- only to find Mister Click's broth-stained fingerprints all over the 'crime.'
- **The Memory-Cat Job** — Static Row's beloved memory-cat -- a data-familiar that stores everyone's forgotten small kindnesses -- goes missing the same night Mister Click's company serves an eviction notice, and the Reel Agents must decide whether the cat was stolen, or fled somewhere it finally felt safe.
