## The idea
Once a year a salt-flat village hauls a giant wooden sea monster to the waterline in broad daylight, and this year the effigy has started to whisper and everyone hears something different.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Salt Flat Hauling Rite** — The whole village pulls the monster to the sea, and the monster hums along.
Art: A blinding white salt flat under a cloudless blue noon, hundreds of villagers in red and ochre hauling taut ropes on a forty-foot whisperwood anglerfish effigy on iron wheels, its brass lantern lure blazing, fresco-style wagon sides, bunting, flatbread and pomegranate carts, cobalt kingfishers flashing overhead.

## Dream location (1)
- **Brinewheel Flats** — A flat white salt plain where a village hauls its whisperwood effigy to the waterline each year, with a Float Rigger's tent standing at the middle of the ropes. Local rule: The haul rope is never let slack, and anyone who hears the effigy whisper passes the word hand to hand down the line. Best scene: The effigy reaches the waterline, the sea draws back a long step, and something far out answers the whisper once while the whole crowd cheers and nobody agrees on why. Art: A dazzling flat salt plain meeting a shallow green sea, ropes radiating from a huge pale wooden effigy, striped tents and food carts, banners snapping in wind, hard noon light and sharp blue shadows.

## Character (1)
- **Tamsir Quispe** — As the village's Float Rigger, this young kingfisher flies the haul lines up the effigy and is the last of their family's rigging flock still alive, which they treat as a promotion. A coil of indigo-dyed camel-hair cord looped over one wing and a boxwood marlinspike carved with Andean zigzags, gripped in the talons. Complication: The effigy whispers back in a village child's voice every time Tamsir ties a knot, and the knots they tie while distracted hold anyway. Look: A fledgling-sized kingfisher, long-billed and all angles, with a crest of aggressively natural red feathers blown straight up by the wind and a cobalt back that flashes in the noon glare, a thin indigo cotton scarf of Sahelian weave knotted at the throat and woven Andean wool bands round both ankles, rope burn across the talons, and a wide open beak held in a delighted grin at rest while the bright eyes stay fixed on the effigy.

## Reward item (1)
- **Whisper Cleat** (ITEM, RARE) — A rope tied to it carries any single spoken word the length of the line, unchanged. Best used when: A crowd must act together without anyone shouting. The catch: It will not carry a word spoken in agreement, only one that politely disagrees. Look: a hand-sized cleat of pale whisperwood with grain swirled like water, its rope hole rimmed in brass worn bright

## Reward skill (1)
- **Hauler's Lean** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — It spreads one person's strength through every hand on a rope, so a dozen pull like a hundred. Best used when: Something far too large must move and everyone is willing but out of step. The catch: Whoever starts it feels every hand's blisters at once. Look: a line of figures leaning at one identical angle along a taut rope, a faint ochre glow running from hand to hand

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **Dragging It Home** — In Salt Flat Hauling Rite at Brinewheel Flats, Tamsir Quispe must decide whether to let the effigy's whisper reach the waterline while the villagers, all politely dissenting, disagree about what it is saying.
