## The idea
In a solarpunk graveyard where a dead human generation-ship washes half-sunk against an alien shoreline, an otter smuggler has to move a debt-marked shipment of living bio-metal before the wreck's own ghosts collect what they're owed.

## Dream vibe (1)
**The Low Tide Armada** — Nobody notices a fleet that never announces itself.
Art: A loose flotilla of patched, sun-sailed hulls drifting low over grey water at dusk, running lights dimmed, otters moving between them without a sound.

## Dream location (1)
- **The Low-Water Hulk** — A grounded human generation-ship, hull cracked open at the waterline, now patched with solar-sail canvas and otter-built locks; officially a gravesite, unofficially a market. Local rule: No hull-plate marked with a name may be sold — only traded, and only in memory of who it came from. Best scene: The tide comes in through the old crew quarters at dusk, and every otter aboard goes quiet at once, out of a habit nobody younger than the wreck can explain. Art: A colossal grounded ship-hull tilted at the shoreline, solar-sail canvas patched over torn steel, bio-metal ribs fused pale and bone-like along the breach, otters working the tideline in dim gold light.

## Character (1)
- **Marl** — She's quietly moving a stolen shipment of bio-metal off the Hulk before the guild that's owed it works out where she's hiding. A patchwork diving satchel stitched with graft-plates of pale bio-metal, one mismatched earring salvaged from the wreck's dead and one bought new, worn to confuse whichever spirits keep tallying her debts. Complication: The bio-metal grafts came from the same gravesite she was raised in, and every plate she sells is, technically, someone she grew up believing was watching over her. Look: A lean otter in a patched vac-suit, a graft of fused bio-metal scarring one shoulder like healed-over stitches, one brass earring and one bone one, moving without wasted motion.

## Reward item (1)
- **Diver's Tally Plate** (ITEM, UNCOMMON) — It marks exactly how much salvage its bearer still owes, and to whom, without needing to ask. Best used when: Two crews are arguing over whose claim on a wreck is older. The catch: It only ever tells the truth about debts, including the ones its bearer would rather forget they owe. Look: A palm-sized bio-metal plate scored with overlapping tally-marks that shift and reorder themselves, edges worn smooth from being handled face-down.

## Reward skill (1)
- **Quiet Salvage** (SKILL, RARE) — It lets its user strip a wreck of everything valuable without the ship ever registering that it was boarded. Best used when: The salvage has to be gone before whoever's still living aboard notices. The catch: It only works on things nobody has said goodbye to yet — grief anywhere nearby breaks the technique instantly. Look: A diver's hands moving without a single wasted motion, bio-metal tools leaving no scuff, no sound, no wake in the water.

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **The Tally Comes Due** — In The Low Tide Armada at The Low-Water Hulk, Marl has one solar-sail tide to move her stolen bio-metal before the debt the wreck itself seems to remember comes looking for her.
