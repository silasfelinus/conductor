## The idea
A village lashed to the barnacled back of a sleeping manatee-god wakes to find the god moving, and the newest arrival — a transfer student who cannot hold her breath a full minute — has to cut the houses free before it sounds.

## Dream vibe (1)
**The Island That Started Swimming** — The kindest god in the sea is still a god, and it is leaving.
Art: An immense barnacled grey back breaching under a village of stilt houses, gold starfruit-fiber cables snapping taut against a green-black horizon, spray hanging in the air like smoke.

## Dream location (1)
- **Halewater Spine** — Every house here stands lashed by cable to the living hide of a sleeping god, a whole village riding one broad grey back. Local rule: Cables come free before the god dives, never while it is diving; timing is the only law that matters on Halewater Spine. Best scene: The deck tilts hard as the god rolls beneath it, and every taut cable in the village rings out the same single note at once. Art: Stilt houses of bleached plank standing on a vast barnacled hide, gold fiber cable strung between them over shallow tide pools, shrine buoys of carved shell knocking together in green light.

## Character (1)
- **Ines Abarca** — Ines has to cut the last houses free before the god sounds, working cable by cable against a deck that will not stay level. She carries a chalk float-board covered edge to edge in letters she has never sent home. Complication: She transferred in from a dry inland school and still cannot hold her breath a full minute. Look: A short, sun-scorched student in a borrowed oilskin two sizes too big, chalk dust white on her forearms, a cracked slate board roped across her back and a shell knife at her hip.

## Reward item (1)
- **The Sounding Cord** (ITEM, RARE) — The cord goes taut a few seconds before the god dives, giving one last warning breath to whoever is holding it. Best used when: It matters most when nobody can agree whether there is still time to cut one more house free. The catch: It only warns the hand actually holding it, so a shout of warning does no good to anyone else on deck. Look: A coil of pale gold starfruit-fiber rope stiff with dried salt, flecked green where it has lain against hide, its free end weighted with a fist of grey sea glass.

## Reward skill (1)
- **Borrowed Breath** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — It lets one diver spend a breath that another diver is still holding, stretching a single lungful between two people. Best used when: Someone younger is still under the water, air running out faster than they can kick for the surface. The catch: The lender surfaces gasping and cannot lend again that day, spent for every dive that follows. Look: Two silver bubble trails crossing in dark water, one thinning to nothing as the other swells and rushes upward.

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **The First Sounding** — On Halewater Spine, as The Island That Started Swimming turns for open water, Ines Abarca runs the tilting deck cutting stilt houses loose one cable at a time while the shrine buoys go under and the god's back slides beneath the waterline.
