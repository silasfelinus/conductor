## The idea
A centaur quartermaster-cook measures rations to the exact ounce, certain precision keeps the astral ore from rewriting anyone's body -- until her own hoofprints turn up on the ledger.

- **Quartermaster-Cook Ilsevet Korrin** — cook the legion's ration-stew to the exact ounce the ledger demands, certain an inexact recipe is what lets soldiers slip away unaccounted. Look: a centaur in a stew-stained officer's coat with brass quota-pins on the lapel, one foreleg dusted blue-white with ore residue creeping upward past the fetlock like frost, a cracked-leather tally-slate slung across her back
