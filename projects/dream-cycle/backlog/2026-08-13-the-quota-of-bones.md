## The idea
A centaur quartermaster-cook measures rations to the exact ounce, certain precision keeps the astral ore from rewriting anyone's body -- until her own hoofprints turn up on the ledger.

## Dream vibe (1)
**The Quota of Bones** — Every ration measured exact is a body being slowly rewritten.
Art: A long historical-fantasy war camp under twin moons, centaur silhouettes queued at a glowing ore-blue cook-fire, banners of a forgotten legion snapping in the wind.

## Dream location (1)
- **The Ration Yard** — known for weighing every soldier's ration against their own body. Local rule: no bowl is filled until a body-tally is chalked first. Best scene: a soldier's spoon-hand is found fused, faintly glowing blue, to the ladle they've used all season. Art: A fortified supply yard: stacked ore-crates glowing faint blue, long trestle tables scarred by tally-chalk, hoofprints frozen deep in old mud, a cook-fire pit ringed in blackened stone, legion banners gone grey with weather

## Character (1)
- **Quartermaster-Cook Ilsevet Korrin** — cook the legion's ration-stew to the exact ounce the ledger demands, certain an inexact recipe is what lets soldiers slip away unaccounted. Carries a lacquered tally-slate worn smooth at one edge. Complication: the slate's chalked totals now include her own hoofprints -- the ore has started rationing her too. Look: a centaur in a stew-stained officer's coat with brass quota-pins on the lapel, one foreleg dusted blue-white with ore residue creeping upward past the fetlock like frost, a cracked-leather tally-slate slung across her back

## Reward item (1)
- **The Unspent Ration** (ITEM, RARE) — one guaranteed exemption from a body-tally. Best used when your name is about to be chalked for issue. The catch: the tin is warm, and getting warmer. Look: a dented tin ration box the size of a fist, wax-sealed at the seam, a faint blue vein of light pulsing under the lid, condensation frost creeping across the cold metal

## Reward skill (1)
- **The Fair Count** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — the power to recount any tally so it favors the counter. Best used when a quota dispute could be settled by numbers alone. The catch: every fair count you win for yourself, you owe double at the next issue. Look: chalk numerals bloom and rewrite themselves in the air above a tally board, the old digits smearing to nothing like wet chalk caught in rain

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **The Last Ledger** — In The Quota of Bones at The Ration Yard, Quartermaster-Cook Ilsevet Korrin finds her own hoofprints chalked into the ore ledger, and the only way off it before the next issue bell is to win a ladle-duel against the yard's reigning quota-champion.
