## The idea
A crew of sky-pirates raids The Actuary, a floating vault-casino anchored in the Bloom — a hallucinatory sea where the water changes color, gravity, and truth in shifting bands. The vault doesn't bank gold; it banks captured luck, bottled in glass and only won at the table, never simply stolen. Swashbuckling, giddy, and just a little unmoored from reality.

## Vibe / genre dream
**Gilded Gambit** — GENRE. Reckless swagger over a dealer's cool nerve — every escape is a bet you talk your way into winning, and the world keeps changing the rules mid-hand.

## Locations (2)
- **The Riptide Larceny** — known for a patchwork galleon whose hull is lacquered in cracked rainbow paint that 'tastes' the color-currents to chart a course through the Bloom. Local rule: the ship only sails true while the crew argues its plan out loud — go quiet, and she becalms in flat, colorless doldrums. Best scene: barreling through a magenta squall while the crew shouts over each other, replotting the heist mid-storm as the ship threads a needle between hallucinatory reefs. Art: weather-beaten wooden galleon, hull in cracked rainbow lacquer, parachute-silk sails patched in clashing psychedelic prints, sailing through a sea shifting teal to magenta in swirling bands, bioluminescent spray, swashbuckling crew gripping rigging, dramatic low-angle painterly concept art, vibrant surreal color grading.
- **The Actuary** — known for a floating vault-casino at the Bloom's calm center, where impossibly precise croupiers bank in bottled outcomes instead of coin. Local rule: nothing may be taken outright — every theft must first be wagered and won at the table, and the house always deals one hand behind the truth. Best scene: a high-stakes card game at a floating table while the vault's walls slowly dissolve into color-fog around the players, game and heist blurring into one bet. Art: opulent floating casino of brass gears and stained glass suspended above swirling psychedelic sea-fog, croupiers in crisp black-and-white with kaleidoscope masks, a roulette wheel of captured light, chandeliers dripping liquid color, Escher-like surreal architecture, jewel tones against chaos.

## Characters (3)
- **Captain Juniper Vale** — captain of the Riptide Larceny, chasing one perfect score to buy back the years The Actuary took from her sister. Carries a spyglass that shows not distance but what a place will cost you to reach. Complication: she can't plan quietly — she thinks out loud by arguing, which makes secrecy nearly impossible. Look: weathered dark-skinned woman, salt-crusted coat stitched from mismatched sailcloth, one eye clouded silver from a Bloom-storm, a gold ring for every bet she's ever won.
- **Dice** — the crew's card-sharp and former Actuary dealer, recruited for an inside read on the house's games. Carries a deck that reshuffles itself whenever someone nearby tells a lie. Complication: still owes the house an old debt — setting foot inside risks being recognized and seized on sight. Look: androgynous, a sharp coat scavenged from a croupier's uniform, gloved hands always shuffling, eyes that flicker color like the Bloom itself.
- **Bosun Ansel Kade** — the ship's rigger and the only one who can read the color-currents well enough to actually pilot a Bloom crossing. Carries a coil of rope woven from threads of every sail he's ever lost, which he swears 'remembers the way home.' Complication: he's slowly going colorblind from too many crossings — the exact skill that makes him essential is failing him. Look: broad-shouldered, burn-scarred hands, smoked-glass goggles worn even indoors, hums sea shanties that quietly map out routes.

## Rewards (2 — one skill, one item)
- **The Shouting Chart** (SKILL, RARE) — the ability to plot a true course through chaos by arguing it out loud with someone who genuinely disagrees with you. Best used when you're lost, becalmed, or facing a choice with no clean answer. The catch: it only works with real disagreement — a companion who just agrees leaves you drifting.
- **A Marked Deck from the House** (ITEM, EPIC) — one card that, played once, becomes whatever hand you need it to be. Best used when the single unrepeatable bet that decides everything. The catch: use it and the House remembers your face forever — you can never sit at its tables again.

## Scenarios (1-2)
- **The Argument That Steered the Ship** — Mid-crossing, a magenta squall swallows the Riptide Larceny whole — the crew must genuinely out-argue each other about the safest route, out loud, or the ship goes nowhere at all.
- **One Hand at the Actuary** — Dice sits the high table under a borrowed face, wagering the ship itself against one bottled outcome, while Juniper and Ansel stall the dissolving color-fog until the hand resolves.

Best for: framing the player's choices as bets and narrating the heist's rising stakes. Expressions: NEUTRAL (calm dealing pose) plus EXCITED (mask spinning fast, cards fanned wide), WORRIED (mask cracked, one eye visible), and MISCHIEVOUS (sly grin, a card palmed up its sleeve).
