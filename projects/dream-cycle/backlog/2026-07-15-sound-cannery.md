## The idea
A seaside cannery town where instead of fish, workers can and preserve precious, vanishing sounds — a grandmother's laugh, a departed dog's bark, the last chord of a concert — sealed into tins that hum faintly when shaken. Across the yard, an old limestone cave trains wild echoes into a rehearsed chorus that performs once a season. People come here to save the sounds they can't bear to lose.

## Vibe / genre dream
**Tin & Echo** — GENRE. the hush before something precious gets sealed away, and the ache of hearing it again years later

- **The Sound Cannery** — known for canning precious, vanishing sounds into labeled tins that hum faintly when shaken Local rule: a sound can only be canned once, by the person who first heard it — no canning someone else's memory-sound for them Best scene: the assembly line falling dead silent the moment a rare sound rolls down the belt — a first laugh, a final goodbye — every worker turning to listen before it's sealed Art: a rustic tin-roofed cannery interior, a conveyor belt of dented tin cans glowing faintly amber from within, steam curling under warm work-lamps, shelves of hand-labeled tins stacked to the rafters, workers in canvas aprons with brass ear-trumpet tools, soft sepia-and-brass palette
- **The Hollow Choir** — known for a limestone cave where wild echoes are coaxed and trained over years into a rehearsed chorus Local rule: you may hum one note to the cave but never a full song — the echoes must find the rest themselves Best scene: the cave's echoes finally singing back a stranger's own half-remembered lullaby, note-perfect, after decades of quietly listening Art: a vast limestone cavern with faintly glowing fungal veins mapping sound-paths across the walls, shimmering humanoid echo-shapes like heat haze suspended mid-air, one soft spotlight of blue-green cave-glow on a lone singer, damp stone and deep shadow

## Characters (3)
- **Canner Odalys** — runs the cannery's sealing line, obsessed with getting the seal timing exactly right so no sound leaks or spoils Carries a brass ear-trumpet and a ring of wax-seal stamps Complication: keeps a shelf of her own uncanned sounds she means to seal but can't bear to let go of yet Look: soot-streaked canvas apron, tin snips tucked in her belt, ears slightly oversized and finely tuned from years of listening
- **Whistle, the Echo-Tamer** — trains the Hollow Choir's wild echoes note by note, patient as a shepherd Carries a cracked tuning fork that's the only thing that calms a spooked echo Complication: one echo in the choir won't sing anything but a stranger's scream from years ago, and Whistle hasn't figured out how to retrain it Look: cave-pale skin, sound-scarred fingertips, a coat stitched with tiny bells that never ring unless they choose to
- **Bram the Tin-Runner** — delivers finished tins to the people who ordered them, treating the run like sacred work Carries a wheelbarrow lined with straw so the tins don't rattle and wake early Complication: has started listening to tins before delivering them, and can't unhear some of what he's heard Look: lanky kid in a too-big cannery jacket, one ear permanently cocked toward whatever's nearby

## Rewards (2 — one skill, one item)
- **Perfect Seal** (SKILL, RARE) — the ability to can any sound at the exact right moment so it never fades or leaks Best used when a fleeting, precious sound is about to be lost forever The catch: once sealed, that sound can never be canned again by anyone else — you get one shot per sound. Look: a dented tin can held under a cannery press, the lid coming down in a ring of amber light, the sound inside frozen as one bright standing wave in the instant before the seam closes
- **Whistle's Cracked Tuning Fork** (ITEM, EPIC) — the power to calm and redirect even the wildest echo in the Hollow Choir Best used when an echo panics or spirals into a sound no one wants repeated The catch: the fork is already cracked — one hard use might finally shatter it for good. Look: a heavy brass tuning fork the length of a hand, one tine split by a hairline crack packed with old solder, the stem worn dark and smooth with finger-grease

## Scenarios (1-2)
- **The Tin That Wouldn't Seal** — Odalys can't get a dying grandmother's laugh to seal properly — every attempt hisses back open — and the only clue is buried in an old echo the Hollow Choir refuses to sing.
- **Bram's Guilty Round** — After secretly listening to a tin meant for a grieving customer, Bram has to decide whether to deliver it as-is or ask Whistle to help him find the words to explain what he heard.
