## The idea
On the Battersgill sea cliffs, tenant families bury part of every crop in pits shared with nesting puffin burrows, and a traveling banana wrangler discovers the manor's tithe ledger has been recording names instead of grain for three generations.

## Dream vibe (1)
**The Hollow Harvest** — Every crop here grows to feed something that was never hungry for food.
Art: Terraced cliffside fields at dusk, wheat stubble gold under drifting fog, round black burrow-mouths dotting the turf where puffins and tithe-pits share the same tunnels, one pit's rim lit faintly white by a buried bone-glass pane.

## Dream location (1)
- **The Puffin Terraces** — Stone-walled wheat terraces cut into the sea cliffs, worked by six tenant families who share their burrows with nesting Atlantic puffins each spring. Local rule: No family may dig a tithe-pit deeper than a puffin burrow reaches into the turf. Best scene: A wrangler pries up a tithe-pit's bone-glass lid at midnight and finds the burrow beneath lined with carved names instead of grain. Art: Green turf terraces stepping down a grey sea cliff, drystone walls slick with mist, round burrow-mouths breathing faint light where milky bone-glass panes sit half-buried in the topsoil.

## Character (1)
- **Ilsa Kroft** — She tends the manor's forced-ripening hothouse banana vines and has learned to read the roots' faint hum for trouble well before the vines show it. A scavenged soil-resonance rod in a dented tin casing, one puffin feather bound to its grip with tarred twine. Complication: The rod hums at the same pitch over the hothouse roots as it does over the tithe-pits, meaning the manor feeds both from the same buried source. Look: Weather-cracked hands stained banana-sap yellow, a canvas apron hung with tin scoops, close-cropped grey hair, a single puffin feather tucked behind one ear.

## Reward item (1)
- **The Domovoi Bead** (ITEM, UNCOMMON) — It fogs to milky white whenever the ground nearby has been fed something it shouldn't have been fed. Best used when: A field, cellar, or pit looks ordinary but the harvest from it has started tasting wrong. The catch: It stays fogged for a full day after warning you, so a second warning nearby cannot be told apart from the first. Look: A thumb-sized bead of cloudy bone glass threaded on knotted red wool, its surface etched with a tiny worn house-mark.

## Reward skill (1)
- **Root-Chant Reading** (SKILL, RARE) — It lets the user tune a resonance rod by humming an old harvest chant, drawing a shape of whatever is buried nearby out of the hum. Best used when: A buried pit or root system needs to be read without digging it up and tipping off whoever is watching it. The catch: The chant only resolves a true shape if hummed off-key exactly as the grandmothers taught it, which sounds like a mistake to anyone listening. Look: A tin rod's needle tracing slow amber light in the air above damp soil, tracking the shape of a chant hummed low and rough.

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **What the Ledger Ate** — In The Hollow Harvest at The Puffin Terraces, Ilsa Kroft hums the root-chant over a tithe-pit the night the tenant families finally refuse to bury another name.
