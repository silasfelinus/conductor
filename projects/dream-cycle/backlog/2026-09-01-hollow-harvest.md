## The idea
On the Battersgill sea cliffs, a black root system beneath the wheat terraces feeds on regrets whispered into the soil at night, and a traveling banana wrangler realizes the puffin burrows are the only ground it has never managed to reach.

## Dream vibe (1)
**The Hollow Harvest** — Every crop here grows to feed something that was never hungry for food.
Art: Terraced cliffside fields at dusk, wheat stubble gold under drifting fog, tangled black root-tips breaking the surface in knots that stop dead at each round dark puffin burrow, one knot glowing faint white from a buried bone-glass shard.

## Dream location (1)
- **The Puffin Terraces** — Stone-walled wheat terraces cut into the sea cliffs, where six tenant families plant beside puffin burrows because the black roots below have never once grown through nesting ground. Local rule: No one may speak their regrets aloud anywhere within reach of a puffin's burrow mouth. Best scene: A wrangler kneels at a burrow mouth at midnight and hears the ground beyond it humming back a stranger's whispered regret. Art: Green turf terraces stepping down a grey sea cliff, drystone walls slick with mist, black root-tips breaching the soil in tangled knots that stop dead at the ring of each round puffin burrow.

## Character (1)
- **Petra Iskra** — She herds the manor's forced-ripening hothouse banana vines and has learned to read the roots' faint hum for trouble long before the vines show any sign of it. A scavenged soil-resonance rod in a dented tin casing, one puffin feather bound to its grip with tarred twine. Complication: The rod hums exactly the same over the hothouse roots as it does over the black tangle beneath the wheat, meaning both are fed from the same buried source. Look: Weather-cracked hands stained banana-sap yellow, a canvas apron hung with tin scoops, close-cropped grey hair, a single puffin feather tucked behind one ear.

## Reward item (1)
- **The Domovoi Bead** (ITEM, UNCOMMON) — It fogs to milky white whenever the ground nearby has been fed something it shouldn't have been fed. Best used when: A field, cellar, or garden bed looks ordinary but whatever grows from it has started tasting wrong. The catch: It stays fogged for a full day after warning you, so a second warning nearby cannot be told apart from the first. Look: A thumb-sized bead of cloudy bone glass threaded on knotted red wool, its surface etched with a tiny worn house-mark.

## Reward skill (1)
- **Root-Chant Reading** (SKILL, RARE) — It lets the user tune a resonance rod by humming an old harvest chant, drawing the shape of whatever is buried nearby out of the hum. Best used when: A root system or buried tangle needs to be read without digging it up and drawing the attention of whatever tends it. The catch: The chant only resolves a true shape when hummed off-key exactly as the grandmothers taught it, which sounds like a mistake to anyone listening. Look: A tin rod's needle tracing slow amber light in the air above damp soil, tracking the shape of a chant hummed low and rough.

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **What the Roots Heard** — In The Hollow Harvest at The Puffin Terraces, Petra Iskra hums the root-chant beside a burrow the night the tenant families finally refuse to whisper another regret into the soil.
