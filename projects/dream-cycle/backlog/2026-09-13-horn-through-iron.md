- **Change Witness** (`change-witness`, ROLE) — A companion who stays present while another person undergoes a change that cannot be reversed or fully predicted. The role offers memory and perspective without claiming the right to stop, accelerate, or define the transformation.

## The idea
After helping steal a prismarine seed from a dead orbital vault, a loyal kudu warlock discovers that the shard is turning her horns into living geology and waking a ruined plateau beneath her feet; the real challenge is deciding what she is willing to become when neither the transformation nor the landscape wants to go back.

## Dream vibe (1)
**White Horns Under Red Sun** — Nothing is chasing her; the frightening part is that the new shape feels like home.
Art: A vast red plateau under a white-hot sun, a small kudu warlock with branching mineral horns facing a single colossal prismarine ridge pushing up through cracked earth.

## Dream location (1)
- **Redglass Basin** — A post-collapse settlement spread across enormous stone plates that creep a few centimeters each season, carrying homes, gardens, and old transit pylons into new arrangements. Local rule: When two moving slabs meet, the people living on both treat the new seam as shared ground until the land separates them again. Best scene: An entire neighborhood watches in silence as a newborn prismarine ridge lifts one edge of the basin and gently tips abandoned towers into a new horizon. Art: A monumental desert basin of rust-red stone plates, sparse modular homes and skeletal towers dwarfed by one luminous blue-green mineral ridge rising through the ground.

## Character (1)
- **Jori N. Brooks** — A kudu warlock who wants to understand whether the shard changing her horns is consuming her, collaborating with her, or revealing a form she had no language for. A soft canvas horn-wrap stitched with pockets for chalk, dried fruit, and fragments of the vault casing she once helped steal through. Complication: Each new mineral branch gives her a clearer sense of stress and movement in the land while making ordinary rooms and doorways harder to inhabit. Look: A tall kudu warlock in sun-bleached travel clothes, spiral horns branching into translucent blue-green crystal, fine red dust caught in her short fur, and a long canvas wrap trailing from one shoulder.

## Reward item (1)
- **Faultseed Ring** (ITEM, RARE) — Placed on a changing object or body, it preserves a tactile impression of its current shape so someone can later feel what that earlier state was like without restoring it. Best used when: A transformation needs to be remembered accurately without pretending it can be undone. The catch: It preserves only one state at a time, and recording a new one erases the old impression. Look: A wide dark-metal ring inset with a thin prismarine seam, its inner surface carrying a raised texture like a tiny topographic map.

## Reward skill (1)
- **Strata Sense** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — The user can feel layers of change inside stone, bone, wood, or metal as distinct pressures and choose one layer to move with rather than resist. Best used when: A body or structure is changing faster than force can safely stabilize it. The catch: Moving with one layer means accepting its direction; nothing in the technique can return matter to an earlier state. Look: Thin luminous contour bands travel across a surface in parallel waves, and one band brightens as the material bends cleanly along it.
