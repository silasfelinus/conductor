## New Facets (invented for this dream)
- **Growth Scaffold** (`growth-scaffold`, SETTING) — A structure that was never allowed to finish growing, scaffolded around a living or semi-living material and left permanently in place so tenders can prune, brace, and harvest what keeps forming. No one alive remembers what shape it was originally meant to reach.
- **Second-Skin Keeper** (`second-skin-keeper`, ROLE) — The person entrusted with what a transformed body leaves behind: a shed skin, a discarded shell, an outgrown name, kept safe because the change cannot be undone and the old self might still be needed as proof, evidence, or grief. A Second-Skin Keeper answers to whoever the transformed person used to be, not to who they are now.

## The idea
In a post-apocalyptic delta city, a gothic cathedral built from living prismarine bone slowly regrows a warlock's flesh into kudu horn and plated iron, and the change never reverses once the first ridge breaks skin.

## Dream vibe (1)
**The Bone-Iron Cathedral** — Every horn that grows through a warlock's skin marks one more year the cathedral will never let them leave.
Art: A colossal gothic cathedral of black iron and pale-green prismarine bone rising over flooded ruins, tiny figures dwarfed at its base.

## Dream location (1)
- **The Undernave** — Scaffolding wraps three open stories of pale prismarine bone that the cathedral has never stopped growing, and tenders prune it back every season to keep the walkways passable. Local rule: No tender may cut a growth that has already grown past their own reach. Best scene: A kudu herd that wandered in from the flooded ruins below stands motionless in the scaffolding's open center, dwarfed by pale bone growth climbing four stories into gloom. Art: vast interior scaffold wound around growing pale bone pillars, tiny figures and kudu on rope-bridge platforms far below the ceiling

## Character (1)
- **Ngozi Achike** — She has tended the cathedral's growing scaffold for eleven years out of a loyalty she chose once and can no longer take back, since the vow itself started changing her body the day she made it. A pruning hook worn down to a curved sliver rides looped through her belt, its edge dulled from a decade of cutting living bone instead of wood. Complication: The horn now growing through her collarbone marks each year of service permanently, and there is no version of leaving the cathedral that would let the growth stop or reverse. Look: a bronze-skinned woman with a pale spiral horn breaking through one collarbone, iron tools slung across a scaffold harness

## Reward item (1)
- **Growth Jar** (ITEM, RARE) — A sealed prismarine jar keeps whatever fragment it holds from decaying, aging, or changing at all, no matter how many years pass outside the glass. Best used when: Someone about to undergo a change they cannot take back wants proof of who they used to be, kept somewhere the change itself cannot reach. The catch: Once a fragment goes into the jar it can never be removed without crumbling to dust, so whatever is sealed inside is sealed there for good. Look: a fist-sized jar of milky prismarine shard, lid fused shut with verdigrised iron bands, faint internal glow through the cloudy glass

## Reward skill (1)
- **Boneglass Grafting** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — A sliver of bone or mineral fuses permanently into living flesh at the point of contact, growing in place exactly as if it had always belonged there. Best used when: An old injury, a missing limb, or a plain unremarkable body needs something that will never come loose, fall out, or wear away with use. The catch: Whatever is grafted this way cannot be removed by any means anyone has found, so the choice of where and what to fuse has to be final the first time. Look: a hairline seam of pale mineral fused into skin, faint warmth radiating from the graft line, skin slightly raised and shining where it healed over

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **What the Scaffold Keeps** — In The Bone-Iron Cathedral, Ngozi Achike climbs down into The Undernave to hand a jarred fragment of her former, unhorned self to the cathedral's Second-Skin Keeper, the only proof left that the growth now splitting her collarbone was ever a choice she made and not a fate she was born to.
