## The idea
A boardwalk arcade built down into the tidepools, where the games are alive: anemone
pinball, hermit-crab claw machines, a fortune-teller conch. At low tide new machines
surface; at high tide the arcade glows bioluminescent and the prizes change. Cozy,
slightly mysterious, salt-sticky nostalgia.

## Location dream
**Tidepool Arcade** — LOCATION. A half-sunken seaside arcade where marine life and
carnival machinery grew together. Flavor: "Insert sand dollar to continue." Art
direction: dusk boardwalk, bioluminescent teal-and-magenta glow on wet stone,
brass-and-barnacle cabinets.

## Characters (2-4)
- **Pearl the Attendant** — an elderly hermit crab in a change-apron who lives in a
  retired skee-ball machine; knows every game's mood.
- **Sonny Undertow** — a slick otter huckster who "won" his vest; runs sideshow bets
  on crab races, secretly rigs them so children always win.
- **The Conch Oracle** — a fortune-telling conch shell of unknown occupancy; speaks
  only in wave sounds Pearl translates, possibly making it all up.

## Rewards (3-6)
- **Sand Dollar Token** (COMMON) — the arcade's currency; warm to the touch.
- **Salt-Taffy Ticket Chain** (UNCOMMON) — redeemable for one impossible prize.
- **Bioluminal High Score** (RARE) — your initials glow in the tide for one night.
- **The Conch's Actual Fortune** (LEGENDARY) — a true prediction, terms and
  conditions apply.

## Scenarios (1-2)
- **Closing Time at Low Tide** — the player helps Pearl coax the machines to sleep
  as the tide turns, but one cabinet refuses — it's holding a prize nobody ever
  claimed.
- **The Rigged Race** — Sonny needs a shill for one honest race; the Conch has
  already predicted the winner and Pearl is pretending not to know.

## Narrator (if narrator: yes)
**Pearl the Attendant** as narrator bot: unhurried, fond, tide-metaphor speech,
apron full of tokens. Expressions lean warm (LOVING, JOYFUL, THINKING, SURPRISED,
WINKING; action: WHISPERING for oracle translations). Topics/threads: "Games of the
Arcade" (machine lore), "Tide Tales" (stories of past visitors), "Ask the Conch"
(fortune sessions Pearl reluctantly brokers).
