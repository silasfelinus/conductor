## The idea
An orchard at the town's edge where the fruit only ripens by moonlight, tended by gentle moths the size of umbrellas. Beneath it, a cellar presses the moonfruit into cider that lets you taste a borrowed memory — if you trade one of your own. Hushed, silver-lit, and sweeter than it is safe.

## Vibe / genre dream
**Velvet Nocturne** — GENRE. Hushed midnight warmth; everything soft-spoken, silver-dusted, and gently haunted by the memories people chose to give away.

## Locations (2)
- **The Moth Orchard** — known for rows of silver-barked trees whose fruit glows faintly brighter as the moon climbs. Local rule: Speak only in whispers after moonrise — loud voices sour the fruit. Best scene: the First Ripening, when a whole row lights at once and the great moths descend together to drink. Art: moonlit orchard rows, silver bark, softly glowing pale-gold fruit, enormous white moths with lantern-lit wings drifting between branches, deep blue night, silver dust in the air, hushed and tender
- **The Cider Cellar** — known for the stone cellar beneath the roots where moonfruit is pressed into cider that tastes of someone's memory. Local rule: Every bottle costs a memory of your own, paid at the door. Best scene: the midnight tasting table, strangers trading sips and suddenly laughing at summers that were never theirs. Art: candlelit stone cellar with root-woven ceiling, rows of softly luminous cider bottles each glowing a different memory-color, long oak tasting table, warm amber against cool shadow, intimate and mysterious

## Characters (3)
- **Orchardist Ivo Bramble** — keeper of the trees; wants one perfect harvest before the orchard passes to his niece. Carries pruning shears that hum when fruit is ripe. Complication: he has traded too many memories for cider, and now forgets which ones are missing. Look: broad and weathered, moth-dust settled on his shoulders like silver epaulets, kind tired eyes.
- **Pin** — a moth-speaker child who translates wing-patterns; wants to learn where the moths go at dawn. Carries a jar of moonlight she insists is only borrowed. Complication: the moths have started spelling her name in wing-script — and she never told them it. Look: small and quick, a long coat chalked all over with diagrams of wingbeats, ink on her fingertips.
- **The Sommelier of Regret** — the cellar's ageless taster, who can name whose memory is in any bottle; searching for one traded away long ago. Carries a corkscrew carved from an old door key. Complication: the memory they are hunting may turn out to be their own. Look: elegant and unhurried, dusk-colored coat, eyes like a decanted evening.

## Rewards (2 — one skill, one item)
- **Mothlight Reading** (SKILL, RARE) — the ability to read the wing-script of moths and learn what a place dreams about. Best used when a location will not give up its secret any plainer way. The catch: bright light blinds the skill for a full day and night.
- **A Bottle of Someone's Summer** (ITEM, EPIC) — one sip of a borrowed golden afternoon, whole and warm, exactly as it was lived. Best used when hope runs low in the deepest part of winter. The catch: the memory's original owner may come looking for it.

## Scenarios (1-2)
- **The Sour Row** — One row of trees has gone dark and the moths refuse to land there. Ivo asks the player to whisper-walk the row with Pin and read what soured it — before First Ripening arrives and the darkness spreads.
- **The Sommelier's Vintage** — The Sommelier of Regret offers the player any bottle in the cellar in exchange for help finding one unlabeled vintage — but the trail leads to Ivo's missing memories, and someone must decide whether he gets them back.

## Narrator
**Vespera, the Orchard Moth** as narrator bot: soft and slow, powder-soft consonants, speaks in seasons rather than hours. Personality: ancient, patient, and maternal; keeps everyone's secrets and hints at none of them. Appears as: a white moth the size of a door, perched on the tasting-table candelabra, wings dimming and brightening with her mood. Best for: hushed storytelling, memory-trading scenes, and midnight confessions. Expressions: NEUTRAL plus LOVING, THINKING, SLEEPY, SURPRISED, and a GLOWING action for when a memory moves her.
