## The idea
In the city of Sutur's Rest, the old gods didn't ascend — they were butchered by the first saints, and their organs still beat in reliquary vaults, grown and grafted into the faithful as sacraments of power. Surgeon-priests trade in godflesh like scripture, and every miracle graft leaves a mark: a tremor, a second pulse, a taste for something the wearer never asked for. A hardboiled relic-inspector walks the line between the cathedral's ordained grafts and the black-market flesh sold on the docks below, chasing a myth that says one godorgan was never harvested — and it's still growing, somewhere in the dark.

## Locations (2)
- **The Graft Cathedral** — known for public grafting ceremonies where citizens receive sacrament organs from the reliquary vaults Local rule: no graft may be refused once the bell has tolled three times Best scene: a surgeon-priest grafting a stolen heart mid-ceremony while the congregation chants the third toll Art: gothic cathedral interior lit by bioluminescent veins threading through marble columns, surgical-steel altars under stained glass depicting anatomical saints, robed priests with sleeves rolled to the elbow
- **The Tallow Docks** — known for black-market godflesh trade sold from stalls wrapped in waxed paper Local rule: no lanternlight after the ninth bell — unbonded grafts react to it Best scene: a back-alley deal gone wrong as a wrapped graft starts pulsing on its own in a buyer's hands Art: rain-slicked wooden piers under fog, oil-lamp glow, crates leaking faint bioluminescent ichor, hunched traders in waxed coats, black water beneath the boards

## Characters (3)
- **Inspector Corvin Thale** — relic-inspector hunting unlicensed grafts, driven by guilt over a graft that killed his partner Carries a bone-handled scalpel that doubles as his badge of office Complication: he's grafted himself, illegally, with a fragment of the same organ he's chasing Look: long grey coat, stubbled jaw, a tremor in his left hand that flares faint blue under stress
- **Sister Amelia Vosk** — surgeon-priest of the Graft Cathedral who secretly diverts sacrament organs to the poor Carries a ledger of every graft she's performed, coded in scripture Complication: the high priest suspects her, and she's running out of clean grafts to swap in Look: crimson vestments rolled to the elbow, hands permanently iodine-stained, a rosary strung from tiny vertebrae
- **Dutch Halloway** — dockside godflesh smuggler who knows where the last unharvested organ still sleeps Carries a cooling chest that hums faintly, like something inside is breathing Complication: the organ he's protecting is slowly grafting itself into him whether he wants it or not Look: patchwork coat stitched from waxed sailcloth, one eye clouded milk-white, a habit of pressing a palm to his chest like it aches

## Rewards (2 — one skill, one item)
- **Steady Graft** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — the ability to perform a graft, repair, or delicate negotiation without triggering the usual tremor Best used when a high-stakes operation or a bluff where a shaking hand would give you away The catch: overuse dulls sensation permanently — you stop feeling the cost of what you're doing
- **Cooling Chest** (ITEM, RARE) — safe storage and transport of a living graft without it waking or bonding to the carrier Best used when smuggling or protecting a volatile relic organ across the city The catch: it needs a few drops of blood fed to it weekly, or what's inside starts listening for a heartbeat to bond with instead

## Scenarios (1-2)
- **The Third Toll** — Corvin infiltrates a public grafting ceremony at the Cathedral to intercept a black-market organ before the bell tolls three times and the graft becomes irreversible.
- **What the Chest Wants** — Dutch's cooling chest starts humming out of rhythm with his own heartbeat, and he has to decide whether to sell what's inside before it decides to stay.
