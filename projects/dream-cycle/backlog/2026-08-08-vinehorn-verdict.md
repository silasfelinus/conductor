## The idea
A solarpunk labyrinth-city keeps its truths growing in living hedges, and the revenant Minotaur who guards the archive must solve a theft that could rig the city's next vote.

## Dream vibe (1)
**The Green Labyrinth Beat** — Every hedge-wall remembers who passed, and the Minotaur detective reads the vines like a ledger.
Art: A sun-drilled labyrinth city, solar vines glowing gold along granite walls, a horned investigator in a long coat checking a leafy case file.

## Dream location (1)
- **The Vinehorn Archive** — known for growing case files as living hedgerows instead of filing them. Local rule: no page may be cut from a living vine, only read where it grows. Best scene: a hedge exhibits testimony no one remembers giving. Art: granite archive walls veined with starlight, shelves of climbing hedge-vines

## Character (1)
- **Bailiff Ashorn** — guard the archive's living verdicts from being stolen. Carries a lantern grown from a single solar vine. Complication: cautious to a fault -- suspects everyone, including themself, since their own memories are borrowed from the hedges. Look: sun-bleached minotaur revenant in a moss-stitched coat, horns wrapped in copper wire
