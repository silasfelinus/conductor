## The idea
A river market town wakes a day after strangers from a later century landed in its festival, and the townsfolk is working out what the new normal is, starting with an orc who has become the town's most-leaned-on wall.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Marigold Ferry** — The party was yesterday; today the whole town has to live inside what it started.
Art: A broad sunlit river arrival crowded with marigold garlands, painted barges, striped awnings and swarms of bees moving along ribboned lanes, a battered silver craft tilted on the bank among the stalls, saturated colour everywhere.

## Dream location (1)
- **Quillamba Wharf** — A market town on a wide brown river, built of painted barges tied bow to stern, where a tilted starsteel craft now serves as the covered stall of the biggest bakery. Local rule: Anything that arrived with the strangers is kept on the water side of the market and fed first, whatever it is. Best scene: Ten thousand bees drift in a slow ribboned cloud over the stalls while the strangers' silver craft, draped in marigolds, hums one note under the music. Art: A sunlit river market of painted barges and striped awnings, a dented starsteel craft half sunk in the bank draped in marigold garlands, crowds and bee swarms, oversized food displays, saturated daylight.

## Character (1)
- **Ren Dobrin** — They were sent back to attend the festival and arrived the day after, so they now have to explain a night they missed to the people who lived it. A rope-managed barrow packed with the cast-off belongings of every earlier version of themself, each labelled in tarred twine. Complication: One of those earlier selves has started turning up in the crowd, wearing their old clothes and politely asking for them back. Look: Early twenties, curved and deliberate through the shoulders and hips, hair in tight coils grown out into a loose dark halo, an embroidered white blouse with hand-stitched birds at the yoke over a barge-hand's tarred rope belt, a boathook slung across the back, chin lifted and a pleased smile pressed hard into the lips as if hoping nobody notices how glad they are to be here.

## Reward item (1)
- **Ballast Hook** (ITEM, RARE) — It lets the holder shift any object by exactly as much as it weighed the last time someone leaned on it. Best used when: Something enormous has to be moved and nobody remembers what it once carried. The catch: Whatever it moves keeps a faint lean toward the holder for a week afterward. Look: A boathook with a starsteel head the colour of a dark star-chart, its wooden shaft draped in tarred cord worn smooth where hands have gripped it.

## Reward skill (1)
- **Bear The Wall** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — It lets the user take on the load of a failing structure and carry it as if it were their own body. Best used when: A roof, a bridge or a crowd is about to give way and there is no time to brace it properly. The catch: The user cannot lie down until the structure has been repaired by someone else. Look: A user standing braced with arms raised, a faint translucent masonry pattern spreading from their shoulders up through the sagging beam above.
