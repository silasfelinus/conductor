## The idea
A baking city rises on nanite-leavened dough that drifts down as weather, and a minotaur baker is negotiating how far the rising should be allowed to change him.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Leavenfall** — Warm flour-coloured weather settles on the city and everything it touches slowly begins to rise.
Art: A sky of marbled acrylic-pour swirls in cream, copper and teal, soft drifting flour-fine dust falling in sheets over domed bakeries, lunar-resin lamps glowing through the haze.

## Dream location (1)
- **Proofmere** — This is a sunken bakery basin where loaves are left to rise in open lunar-resin troughs while nanite-laden dust drifts down from the sky. Local rule: Everyone must stand clear of a trough once its crust begins to crackle, because nothing that is rising may be hurried. Best scene: A wall of warm cream-coloured dust rolls across the basin and every trough swells at once, lifting loaves, lamps and bakers' hats a hand's breadth into the air. Art: a wide flat sunken basin of pale lunar-resin troughs, domed copper ovens at the rim, a rolling wall of warm cream dust in a marbled teal and copper sky

## Character (1)
- **Dominic Ho-Laurent** — He is trying to keep his oldest friend safe while the weather changes what that friend is made of. A quilted indigo apron sewn in a Pearl River factory stitch holds a tin tiffin of cold sesame rolls that he refills for every long shift. Complication: His tongue tells him a bad storm is coming whenever he leans toward the oven, and he cannot tell if he is tasting the weather or his own fear. Look: Thirty-odd, broad and soft through the middle, with a towering, impractical braid of dark hair coiled and pinned up under a flour-dusted cap, wearing a quilted indigo apron over a patched tunic, and a worried, gentle face whose brow stays creased even when he smiles, tongue nudging at one cheek as if tasting the air.

## Reward item (1)
- **Proofing Bell** (ITEM, UNCOMMON) — It marks the exact moment a changing thing has finished changing. Best used when: Nobody can agree whether a transformation is complete or only paused. The catch: It rings for every transformation nearby, including the ones nobody wanted to notice. Look: a squat bell the size of a fist cast from pale moon-grey resin, its rim stained with old flour, a mended gold seam running down one side

## Reward skill (1)
- **Slow Rise** (SKILL, RARE) — It lets the user slow a change in something living to a patient crawl. Best used when: A transformation is moving faster than the one undergoing it can bear. The catch: The slowed change waits, and waiting shows on the face of whoever slowed it. Look: a pair of floury hands pressed flat to a swelling surface while swirling cream-and-copper marbling in the air slows to a near standstill

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **Risen** — In Leavenfall at Proofmere, Dominic Ho-Laurent kneels beside the minotaur baker Brannoch in a trough as the warm dust thickens, both of them negotiating how much of his old shape the rising will leave.
