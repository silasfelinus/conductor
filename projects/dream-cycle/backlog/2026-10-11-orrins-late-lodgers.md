## The idea
In a dry frontier town where the buried walk home at suppertime, a sexton runs a sunlit boarding house for the polite, hungry dead and keeps a seat at the long table for every one of them.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Everybody Walks Home** — The dead are only late, and the table is already set.
Art: A buttermilk-painted boarding house on a dusty street in hard noon sun, persimmon shutters, a porch hung with drying apples and quilts, pale grey-skinned lodgers in Sunday clothes crossing the yard toward the door.

## Dream location (1)
- **Pike House** — A tall buttermilk-white boarding house whose porch rail and gutters are cut in pale platinum filigree, and where the long table seats every resident the town ever buried. Local rule: Every lodger sits in their own chair at supper, and no chair is ever moved. Best scene: A morning when sunlight crosses the long table and every empty-looking chair turns out to hold a person eating quietly, steam rising from each plate. Art: A three-storey wooden boarding house painted buttermilk with persimmon shutters, platinum filigree lace along the porch rail and gutters, a long scrubbed table visible through open double doors, dust and noon light in the yard.

## Character (1)
- **Orrin Sutter-Hale** — They buried half the town and now cook for every one of them, keeping the house the way a parent keeps a table full. A hand-split kindling hatchet, a dented enamel stockpot, and a golden lion tamarin named Biscuit riding on one shoulder. Complication: The living townsfolk think Orrin is feeding strays, and the fence they sing back together is the only thing that keeps the two sides from meeting. Look: Late middle age and thick through the middle in a faded flour-dusted apron over a company-town work shirt, thinning grey hair left plain and uncovered, forearms scarred from the woodstove, a quilted shoulder pad stitched in hollow-country squares, and an easy open smile at rest, eyes already counting heads at the table.

## Reward item (1)
- **Welcome Spoon** (ITEM, UNCOMMON) — Whatever it stirs is eaten gladly by anyone, living or not, who sits down to it. Best used when: A guest everyone fears will not come to the table. The catch: The cook tastes every guest's last meal in a single mouthful. Look: A long-handled spoon of platinum filigree, the handle pierced into hawthorn leaves, its bowl smoked pale from the stove, hanging from a loop of blue cotton twine.

## Reward skill (1)
- **Settling Tune** (SKILL, COMMON) — A low hum that makes restless walking dead sit down and fold their hands. Best used when: A crowd of the hungry dead has come through the door at once. The catch: The hum settles the hummer too, and tempers you may have needed go quiet. Look: A ring of dust smoothing flat outward from a hummer's boots while a row of grey hands loosens around plates.

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **Biscuit's Extra Chair** — In Everybody Walks Home at Pike House, Orrin Sutter-Hale sets an extra place at the long table for a stranger in a drover's hat who was never buried here, and Biscuit the tamarin is already climbing onto the stranger's knee.
