## New Facets (invented for this dream)
- **Chorus Conscience** (`chorus-conscience`, ALIGNMENT) — A disposition whose sense of right is the running agreement of many smaller selves, such as limbs, voices, or the members of a colony, rather than a single will. Once its parts agree it acts with enormous conviction, and whenever they disagree it stalls in plain view of everyone watching.
- **Roofed Sky** (`roofed-sky`, SETTING) — A world whose sky is a lid, such as an ice shell, a cavern vault, or the inside of a vast hull, under which weather forms and falls back down. Nothing that rises can ever leave, so smoke, heat, and every stray balloon eventually comes back on the wind.

## The idea
Beneath the ice shell of a frozen moon, where lightning reaches the seabed and fuses the silt into glass, a reef that thinks as one community sends the creature it secretly fears most, an enormous adolescent crown-of-thorns starfish, to greet a pale lander that has just come down through the drifting white dark.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Nine Kilometres of Snowfall** — Under the ice the storms fall all the way to the floor, and this time a visitor fell with them.
Art: A dense blizzard of white marine snow drifting down through black water beneath a cracked ice ceiling nine kilometres overhead, a forked bolt of blue-white lightning dropping the whole height of the frame and lighting a seabed forest of branching glass tubes from inside, a small pale lander with round portholes sinking through the flakes, a huge spiny violet starfish silhouette rising on its arm tips below, the snow swirling in visible eddies around everything.

## Dream location (1)
- **Strikeglass Reef** — A reef that grows on branching tubes of lightning glass, fused from the seabed silt each time a storm under the ice shell throws a bolt all the way to the floor. Local rule: Nothing on the reef acts alone: polyps, sponges and shrimp ripple a slow vote of light across the glass, and whatever the reef agrees on, every creature does at once. Best scene: A snowfall thickens until the whole reef goes dark, and then a bolt from the ice ceiling lights every glass branch at once, showing a stranger's lander half-sunk among them. Art: A wide seabed forest of hollow branching glass tubes in smoke-green and ash-grey, rough and bubbled like melted sand, rising from black silt and crusted with orange fan corals, white glass sponges and pink shrimp, a thick blizzard of white marine snow drifting through the water and banking in soft drifts against the glass, a forked lightning bolt dropping from a cracked ice ceiling far above and lighting the branches from inside, a small pale lander half-buried among the tubes, ripples of amber and blue light running across the reef in waves.

## Character (1)
- **Tumbo** — They were chosen as the reef's hero by a slow vote of light across every polyp, and they narrate the whole errand under their breath in the second person, like a story waiting for somebody else to type the next move. A long fringe of knotted wool cords swings from their striped carrying-cloth, one knot tied for every wrong turn they have watched the reef take, and the newest knot is still loose. Complication: Their kind eat coral, so every polyp that cheered them out toward the lander is also quietly afraid of them, and their twenty-one arms will not all move until every one of them agrees. Look: An enormous adolescent crown-of-thorns starfish the size of a farm cart, tall and heavy, reared up on the tips of a dozen of its twenty-one thick arms so the broad disc sways high above the silt, the arms of slightly uneven length where new ones are still budding, skin a bruised plum-violet fading to slate grey at the tips, every surface bristling with long venomous spines still soft with youth, the crest of spines along the middle of the disc raised stiff and dyed cochineal crimson, a body that reads as neither he nor she and wears that easily, a handwoven carrying-cloth in bright magenta, orange and green bands knotted across the disc and patched at one corner with a dull blue weave nobody on the reef recognises, a long fringe of knotted wool cords swinging underneath, small red eyespots at every arm tip curled slightly inward in a permanent wince, as if already bracing for the next thing to go wrong, white marine snow settling on the spines.

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **You Are on the Hull** — In Nine Kilometres of Snowfall, the eldest glass sponges of Strikeglass Reef have spent weeks lecturing one another in the dark about the visitors, arguing over every flicker from the lander's portholes, and tonight they send Tumbo climbing its hull with a fresh knot in the cord and a thesis to deliver. Tumbo murmurs "You are on the hull. It is snowing. There is a window here." as the next bolt drops from the ice ceiling and lights the whole reef, then presses one red arm tip to the glass, and something on the other side presses back.

  - Setup: On the crest of Wakeful Dam, an aged anthropomorphic bighorn ram swordsman with trembling hands draws steadiness from a hexstone tied to his hilt and challenges Zuzu to one honest duel while the beaver clan watches from their lodges.
  - **Choice Accept the duel and fight in earnest**: It honours the old ram's wish, but a true blade against a failing swordsman can maim him in front of the clan. Immediate: Zuzu disarms him in a single stroke, the hexstone cracks, and the ram bows with a cut across his forearm. Later: Sets the flag RAM_HONOURED: his former students guide Zuzu through the high pass in a later chapter, but the cracked hexstone's curse follows whoever picked it up.
  - **Choice Cut the hexstone from his hilt instead**: It frees the ram from a cursed crutch but robs him of the steady hand and the dignified last fight he asked for. Immediate: The stone drops into the pond, the ram's sword arm shakes, and he sheathes his blade without a word. Later: Sets the flag RAM_SHAMED and teaches the skill Steady Without Stone, but the dam springs a leak where the hexstone sank, and the beavers ask Zuzu to dive for it on a replay.
  - **Choice Share tea with him and decline the duel**: It keeps everyone unhurt, yet the ram reads refusal as pity and the clan reads it as caution. Immediate: The ram tells a long story about the roads he used to walk, and leaves at dawn with the hexstone still tied on. Later: Unlocks a later branch where the ram, much weaker, turns up in a rival's gang and the duel happens on worse ground.
- **The Raft City Offers to Carry the Sick** [emperor-of-dust / illustrious]
  - Setup: A drifting city of balloon-lifted salvage rafts, once a commune of equals and now split into two feuding quarters, moors its cables to the Keel of the Fallen Colossus and offers to carry the Horde's injured over the ash flats, for a price each quarter names differently.
  - **Choice Pay the upper quarter in brass**: It moves the injured fastest and keeps every follower on the road, but drains scarce brass and strengthens one side of the feud. Immediate: Forty brass leave the strongbox, and four injured followers rise into the sky with their recovery cut by two days. Later: The upper quarter becomes a reliable ally for ferrying supplies, while the lower quarter quietly sells the Horde's route to a rival faction.
  - **Choice Lend Gorsa Tuskwell to mend the lower quarter's moorings**: It costs no brass and wins the poorer rafters, but puts the Frontfacer on frayed cables and leaves the column without its front for days. Immediate: The lower quarter carries the injured for free, and Gorsa has a two-in-six chance of a three-day injury from a snapping line. Later: The lower quarter shelters stragglers on later days and reveals Gorsa's clouded eye to the leaders, while the upper quarter raises its prices.
