## The idea
A cramped brass foundry that casts and rehomes the tiny household gods nobody
remembers to thank: the god of lost socks, the spirit of the last stair, the
small deity who keeps the fridge light honest. When a household forgets one, it
comes here to be re-poured, polished, and placed with someone who needs it.
Coal-warm, clangy, reverent about very small things.

## Location dream
**The Foundry of Small Gods** — LOCATION. A soot-dark workshop of tiny crucibles
and thimble-sized molds, shelves of brass householders waiting for a home, a
ledger of every hearth that ever forgot one. Flavor: "No god too small to be
missed." Art direction: warm forge-orange glow on brass and coal-dust, rows of
palm-sized figurines, ledgers and tongs, cozy-industrial amber palette.

## Characters (2-4)
- **Foundry-Master Brannock** — broad, singed apron, speaks to each figurine
  before he pours it; insists a god half-remembered is worth twice the care.
- **Tare, the Sorting Sprite** — a nervous small god of misplaced keys who works
  the shelves and keeps almost-losing herself; deeply competent when she forgets to worry.
- **The Unclaimed One** — a beautifully cast little god whose domain no one can
  name; it waits on the top shelf for the household that will finally need it.

## Rewards (3-6)
- **Thimble of the Last Stair** (COMMON) — you never miss a step in the dark again.
- **Honest Fridge-Light** (UNCOMMON) — one appliance in your life stops lying to you.
- **Brannock's Second Pour** (RARE) — recast one small thing you lost as something that stays.
- **The Unclaimed One's Domain** (LEGENDARY) — name what you most need watched over, and a god takes up the post.

## Scenarios (1-2)
- **The Forgotten Hearth** — a whole household's small gods have gone cold at
  once; the player and Brannock read the ledger to find what grief made the family stop noticing.
- **What Tare Keeps Losing** — the sorting sprite keeps almost-vanishing; the
  player helps her find the one key she was cast to guard — her own.
