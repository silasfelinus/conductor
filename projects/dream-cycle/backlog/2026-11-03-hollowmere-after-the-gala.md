## The idea
The morning after an undead family's last great banquet, a freshly retired scullion and a murder of crows must decide what to do with a house full of dishes that remember the guests.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Hollowmere After the Gala** — The party that ended the old order is over, and the crows are the ones left to clean up after it.
Art: A cutaway view of a grand dim-lit manor on a cold shore, its long banquet hall in grey dawn with tipped chairs and smeared glasses, a cluttered stone scullery below with a row of black crows perched along the sink and a broad old cook in a floury apron, wet plates stacked on every surface.

## Dream location (1)
- **Hollowmere Scullery** — A low stone scullery under a drowned-looking manor, crowded with enamel basins, wooden drying racks, tarnished spoons and a long row of glass tumblers that look empty but are not, where the crows do the washing. Local rule: No glass leaves the sink until it has been rinsed three times, because a tumbler that is not rinsed still pours the last drink it held. Best scene: A crow drops a faintly misted tumbler into the basin and a dead guest's laughter spills out across the floor tiles and rattles every spoon on the rack. Art: A low whitewashed stone scullery with chipped enamel basins and wooden drying racks, rows of faint misty phantom-glass tumblers on a long scarred table, flour on every surface, black crows perched on the pipes, a salt-stained window with cold grey dawn.

## Character (1)
- **Aaju Osei-Brandt** — They are the retiring head of the scullery who knows every dish in the manor by touch, and they want to rinse the last gala's glasses clean before the crows start drinking from them. Complication: The crows follow their humming through the dark like a tide and will not work for anyone else, so the scullery cannot go on without them, and they are tired.
