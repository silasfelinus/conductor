## The idea
Aboard a generation ship whose living skeleton is grown from Spire Crystal, the crystal spine doesn't just hold memories the way tree rings hold seasons -- it's started growing rings for things that haven't happened yet. A cheerfully stubborn goblin investigator has to figure out whether the ship is remembering the future or quietly writing it.

## Dream vibe (1)
**The Spire That Remembers** — The ship's crystal spine keeps growing new memories -- by eating the crew's old ones.
Art: A towering faceted crystal spire growing straight through the generation ship's core decks, veins of nanite light pulsing upward like sap, goblin engineers dwarfed and glowing in its reflected light.

## Dream location (1)
- **The Hollow Reliquary** — known for goblin engineers who read the spire's crystal growth rings like tree rings, each one holding a season's worth of the ship's memories. Local rule: no one splices new crystal into the spire without an Investigator present to log what memory gets grown over. Best scene: an engineer presses a palm to warm crystal and hears, faintly, a dead crewmate's voice. Art: A fantasy-tinged grotto deck where the spire's roots fan out like a tree's through nanopunk scaffolding, goblin-sized catwalks strung with lantern light between crystal buttresses.

## Character (1)
- **Vex Thistlewick** — cheerfully investigate every misremembered crystal ring before the ship's official history quietly rewrites itself. Carries a notebook of hand-copied growth-ring rubbings, refusing to trust the digital record. Complication: the newest ring matches a memory nobody aboard has actually lived yet. Look: patchwork nanopunk coveralls, an oversized brass loupe, crystal dust permanently under every fingernail

## Reward item (1)
- **Aria Shard** (ITEM, RARE) — a sliver of Spire Crystal wound through with clockwork gearing that plays back any memory grown into it as a sung aria. Best used when a buried memory needs to come out gently, in a form people will actually stop and listen to. The catch: it only ever sings the memory as the spire chose to remember it, never as it actually happened. Look: a palm-length sliver of pale crystal threaded with fine brass clockwork, tiny gears sunk into its facets, light pulsing along its internal fractures

## Reward skill (1)
- **Ghost-Ring Reading** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — read the emotional residue left in a freshly grown crystal ring like tea leaves, dry commentary included. Best used when a haunting needs to be laughed at instead of run from. The catch: the jokes get bleaker the closer the ghost is to the truth. Look: a freshly split crystal growth-ring lit from behind, its concentric bands smeared with faint coloured residue like oil floating on water
