## The idea
On a generation ship whose air is grown rather than pumped, a flower nobody planted has begun opening in a line across the farm decks — and the line traces exactly where a missing crewmate walked on the night she vanished.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Bloomline** — Something is planting evidence in the air we breathe.
Art: A curved agricultural deck lit by grown crystal instead of lamps, one narrow line of white flowers opening across otherwise green rows, pale violet light raking low between the planting beds.

## Dream location (1)
- **Greenhold** — Greenhold is a farm deck lit entirely by grown crystal instead of lamps. Local rule: Nobody prunes what they did not plant, so the unclaimed white flowers are left untouched no matter where they spread. Best scene: An entire row flowers at once, tracing a person's path from door to hull wall. Art: Long curving planting beds under a ceiling of clustered violet crystal, wet black soil, condensation beading on cold metal ribs, one line of white blossoms cutting across the green.

## Character (1)
- **Yusra Panek** — She means to find out who the flowers are following before the line reaches the nursery deck. A pocketful of seed envelopes, always refilled and never once opened to plant anything. Complication: She is relentlessly cheerful, and the crew has stopped believing anything she says. Look: A stocky goblin in patched solar-weave coveralls, green skin dusted yellow with pollen, wide flat nose, magnifying spectacles pushed up into wiry grey hair, soil worked deep into her knuckles.

## Reward item (1)
- **The Overture Fork** (ITEM, RARE) — It hums when it passes air that the wrong plant has breathed. Best used when: A room smells ordinary and should not, its air too clean for the growth that supposedly filled it. The catch: It hums for the plant that was there, never for the person who carried it. Look: A wind-up tuning fork of brass and clouded crystal, its winding key worn smooth, a hairline fracture running up one tine and catching the light.

## Reward skill (1)
- **The Long Pause** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — It holds a silence in place until the other person fills it with something true. Best used when: An answer arrives too quickly and too completely to trust, smoother than the truth ever comes. The catch: It works just as well on whoever is holding the silence, including you. Look: Air gone visibly still between two figures, dust hanging motionless in a shaft of violet light, one lamp guttering low and steadying again.

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **What Flowered on Deck Nine** — In Bloomline, down in Greenhold, Yusra Panek follows the opening flowers from the missing woman's bunk to a hull seam nobody has cracked in forty years, and finds the next bloom already unfolding on the inside of her own wrist.
