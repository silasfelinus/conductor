## The idea
On a flooded oil platform that has been turned into a floating feast-hall, a young cook who greets every door before opening it must invent a dish in front of a hungry crowd that includes the politely returned dead, while a golden tamarin sous-chef keeps stealing the best ingredient.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Ladlemoot** — The whole lagoon sits down at once, and a new dish decides who is called a hero.
Art: A vast rusted drilling platform standing in a turquoise atoll lagoon at noon, its decks hung with bunting, steaming cauldrons and long tables, swarms of small boats tied alongside, a tamarin in a tiny apron on a chimney, bold daylight in a digital matte painting.

## Dream location (1)
- **Gantry Feastdeck** — A decommissioned oil platform in a warm lagoon where every deck has become a kitchen, and where velvet curtains the colour of dried plums hang over the serving hatches. Local rule: Every door on the platform is greeted aloud and answered by whoever stands behind it before anyone walks through. Best scene: A hundred cooks lift their lids at the same moment while a cutaway of steam rises through every floor of the platform and the tamarins cheer from the cranes. ## Character (1)
- **Ione Tavita** — They are the youngest cook ever to enter the Ladlemoot and mean to invent a dish no one on the lagoon has ever tasted, because it is the only way to feed the whole crowd at once. A well-used ladle carved from coconut shell, a roll of oilskin pockets full of dried sea peppers, and a tiny apron for the tamarin who rides on their shoulder. Complication: They greet every door, hatch and lid before opening it and wait for an answer, which makes them slow in a contest that is judged on speed.
