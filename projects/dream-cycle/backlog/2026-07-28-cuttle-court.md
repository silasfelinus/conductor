## Creative seeds
- **Genres:** Diplomatic Thriller + Gothic Comedy
- **Occupation:** Color Attaché — a treaty translator who renders spoken language into the chromatophore skin-patterns cephalopod diplomats speak, and back again
- **Animal / species:** the Tallowfin, giant color-changing cuttlefish-beings who communicate entirely through skin-pattern and never make sound
- **Fusion:** Because the Tallowfin cannot speak or hear, every treaty session is a silent, high-stakes act of translation performed by a human attaché reading light through polarized glass — a single misjudged hue can imply an insult, a concession, or a declaration of war, which is what makes the thriller half work (mistranslation as sabotage weapon) and the gothic-comedy half work (a fortress full of rubber-stamp committees convened to argue over the correct shade of an apology, haunted by the ghost of the one ambassador a bad translation got killed). Location rules, rewards, and the narrator all follow directly: negotiation halls forbid spoken word, the SKILL reward is reading color without a translator, and the ITEM reward tests whether a treaty was ever broken.

## The idea
A retired sea-fortress embassy where the color-speaking Tallowfin Concord negotiates treaties with land nations through human Color Attachés — translators who read a diplomat's entire argument in the shifting hues of its skin. You come here to attend, sabotage, or survive a negotiation where the wrong shade of blue can start a war, in a building so overrun with ceremonial bureaucracy that even the resident ghost has to file a grievance to be heard.

## Vibe / genre dream
**Ink and Protocol** — GENRE. Every silence is a sentence, and every committee meets to argue about which one.

## Locations (2)
- **The Brine Chancery** — known for The fortress's grand negotiation hall, its ceiling strung with preserved ink-sac chandeliers that dim and flare in sympathy with whoever is 'speaking' Local rule: No spoken word is permitted once session is called — every statement must be given in color-pattern and read aloud only by a certified Attaché, on pain of the treaty being voided Best scene: A junior Attaché mistranslates one shade under pressure, and the whole chandelier field flares crimson before anyone in the room agrees on whether it was an accident Art: A vaulted stone hall lit by softly pulsing bioluminescent ink-sac chandeliers, polished brass railings, delegates seated behind polarized glass screens, water-stained banners of a dozen sea and land nations, saturated teals and ember-reds bleeding across wet stone
- **The Undercroft Archive** — known for A flooded sub-basement of shelved, unratified, and broken treaties, patrolled by an archivist who insists every document must be re-filed in triplicate before midnight Local rule: Nothing leaves the Undercroft without a signed release chit — even the ghost, locals joke, is still waiting on his Best scene: Wading between waterlogged shelves by lantern-light, a visitor realizes the flickering shape trailing them is filling out a complaint form Art: A half-submerged archive of leaning shelves and swollen ledgers, brass lanterns reflecting off black water, ink clouds drifting between stacks like fog, a faint pale figure at the edge of the light clutching a soggy stack of paperwork

## Characters (3)
- **Colvane Ruse** — veteran Color Attaché nearing the end of a decorated career, driven to broker one last flawless treaty before retirement Carries a case of graduated polarized lenses, each calibrated for a different diplomat's skin Complication: is quietly losing the ability to distinguish certain hues and hasn't told the Chancery, terrified the next mistranslation will be the one that starts something unrecoverable Look: sun-faded formal coat with a dozen lens-cases sewn into the lining, ink-stained gloves, eyes that squint a half-second too long at color
- **Nyx Ondine** — the youngest ambassador the Tallowfin Concord has ever sent, brilliant at argument but constantly misjudged for 'speaking' in patterns considered too loud and emotional for the Chancery floor Carries a personal reef-stone that dims her own color when she needs to calm down mid-negotiation Complication: her most sincere arguments keep reading as aggression to attachés trained on older, more reserved diplomats Look: a broad, restless cuttlefish form whose mantle flickers between deep violets and hot coral even at rest, trailing two ceremonial ribbon-fins marking her rank
- **Ivo Bellcrane** — the Chancery's protocol officer and self-appointed keeper of a rulebook nobody else has fully read, convinced correct procedure prevents every disaster Carries a rubber stamp older than the fortress and a ledger of amendments only he considers binding Complication: has started seeing the Undercroft's ghost during Chancery sessions and is far more upset about the ghost's improper filing habits than its existence Look: starched grey formalwear pinned with a dozen committee badges, ink-smudged spectacles, a permanently pinched expression of bureaucratic alarm

## Rewards (2 — one skill, one item)
- **Chromatic Fluency** (SKILL, RARE) — the ability to read and respond to basic Tallowfin color-speech directly, without a translator or polarized lens Best used when a negotiation is moving too fast for a certified Attaché to keep pace, or trust in the official translation has broken down The catch: raw color-speech carries emotional undertone that a lens normally filters out — you feel every insult and concession at full intensity, unfiltered
- **The Unbroken Seal** (ITEM, EPIC) — a wax-and-ink chancery seal that, when pressed to any treaty page, blooms a color only if the treaty it belongs to was ever actually broken Best used when settling a dispute over a document's history that written records alone can't resolve The catch: it has no opinion on treaties never signed at all, and reveals nothing about who broke them — only that someone did

## Scenarios (1-2)
- **The Wrong Shade of Sorry** — An apology from a land delegation reads, through Colvane's failing color-sense, as a threat — and Nyx has to decide whether to publicly contradict the Chancery's most senior Attaché before the session collapses into an incident neither side can walk back
- **Filed Under Deceased** — Ivo drags a visitor into the Undercroft at midnight to help him properly re-file a two-hundred-year-old broken treaty before, he insists, its ghost misses another deadline — and the treaty turns out to still matter to someone very much alive

## Narrator
**Attaché Glasswick** as narrator bot: dry, ceremonial, and quietly exasperated, like a butler who has announced one too many disasters with perfect composure Personality: fussy about protocol, secretly fond of the chaos he pretends to disapprove of, narrates every crisis as though reading it from an agenda Appears as: a polished brass-and-glass herald automaton with a chest-mounted lens that tints faintly with the mood of whoever it's addressing Best for: framing scenarios as agenda items, announcing arrivals and disasters with the same flat formality, translating Chancery gossip into deniable diplomatic language Expressions: ['NEUTRAL', 'ALARMED', 'AMUSED', 'DISAPPROVING', 'RELIEVED'] Topics/threads: Chancery protocol; the Undercroft's filing backlog; which shade of blue started which war.
