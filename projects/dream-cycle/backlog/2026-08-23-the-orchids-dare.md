## The idea
In a brimstone-vented greenhouse where bred orchid mantises guard a black market of grafted cacti, a duelist-wrangler falls for someone she keeps testing with a blade she never quite means to use.

## Dream vibe (1)
**The Orchid's Dare** — Every flower here is armed, and so is every heart that lingers too long among them.
Art: Warm sulfur-yellow light through fogged glass, deep red brimstone glow pooling at the floor, pale mantis-orchid blooms caught mid-sway with thin rapier-glints reflected in dew on their petals.

## Dream location (1)
- **The Brimstone Conservatory** — known for a domed greenhouse where orchid mantises are bred as living security, camouflaged among cactus grafts fed on brimstone runoff. Local rule: no open flame past the vapor line — the mantises read heat blooms as prey and strike without warning. Best scene: a duel fought down a narrow aisle of grafted cacti while unseen mantises shift color along the stems, tracking the blades. Art: Curved glass panes streaked with mineral soot, brimstone vents glowing dull orange along the floor grates, rows of spined cacti fused with pale orchid blossoms, mantis-shaped shadows folding and unfolding among the petals.

## Character (1)
- **Pecos** — keeps the Conservatory's grafted cactus stock alive and in line, testing every visitor's nerve before she'll trust them near her rows. Carries a cured cactus-spine rapier, its blade ribbed like a dried stem, hilt wrapped in brimstone-cured leather. Complication: she picks fights with the one person she wants to keep, mistaking every soft moment for a dare she has to win. Look: Sun-cracked hands stained rust-orange from brimstone dust, a canvas duster patched with cactus-fiber thread, wide-brimmed hat tilted low over a squint, twin scars along one forearm shaped like old spine marks.

## Reward item (1)
- **The Sulfur-Kissed Locket** (ITEM, RARE) — opens to reveal a single pressed orchid petal that never wilts, said to mark its wearer as promised to someone, for better or worse. Best used when sealing a bargain, a courtship, or a truce that both parties intend to actually keep. The catch: the petal darkens to brimstone-black if the promise is ever broken, and it cannot be pried loose or replaced. Look: A tarnished brass locket the size of a thumbnail, its hinge crusted with fine yellow sulfur crystals, a single waxy white petal pressed flat behind a scratched glass window.

## Reward skill (1)
- **The Deadpan Draw** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — the ability to lasso, wrangle, or disarm anything without changing expression, which somehow makes the feat funnier and more effective. Best used when a situation has become too dangerous to take seriously and needs to be defused through sheer nerve. The catch: the completely flat expression is involuntary once triggered — it cannot be turned off mid-technique, even for tears. Look: A coil of braided rope snapping taut mid-throw, spines and thorns bending harmlessly away from the loop as it tightens, dust kicked into a slow comic arc that hangs a beat too long in the air.

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **The Petal That Wouldn't Darken** — Under The Orchid's Dare, inside The Brimstone Conservatory, Pecos finds a locket left coiled in her cactus rows overnight, its petal still stubbornly, impossibly white, and has one shift to decide whether that means someone kept a promise she doesn't remember making.
