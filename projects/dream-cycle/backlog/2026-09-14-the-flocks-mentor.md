## New Facets (invented for this dream)
- **Mistkin** (`mistkin`, SPECIES) — A human lineage born generations into a slow apocalypse, their throats grown thin gill-like folds that filter the ever-present mist their world breathes. They taste a storm changing hours before it arrives, but the same folds leave them unable to raise their voice above a whisper in dry air.
- **Flockbound** (`flockbound`, ALIGNMENT) — A moral compass measured by whether an act keeps the group moving as one, not by what it costs any single member. Loyalty to the flock overrides personal survival, even when the flock is wrong.

## The idea
In a pastoral-apocalypse valley generations after the old world's quiet end, a woman convinced she is psychic mentors a bonded cassowary flock whose collective moods actually predict the valley's weather, and the settlement is slowly realizing the birds are the ones truly reading the future.

## Dream vibe (1)
**The Flock's Mentor** — The cassowaries read the mist long before she ever could.
Art: A dew-slicked flock of blue-necked cassowaries moving through drifting mist over furrowed fields at first light.

## Dream location (1)
- **The Roost Commons** — A terraced roosting ground where the valley's bonded cassowary flock nests among the crop rows, its old growth mist-catcher trees hung with condensation nets first strung by the Mistkin who built the place. Local rule: No one may separate a nesting cassowary from its chosen human while the chick is still unfledged, not even the mentor who raised the bird from an egg. Best scene: A fledgling cassowary chick, down still wet, refuses to leave a stranger's lap during the welcome ceremony, forcing the whole gathering to restart around the bird's opinion instead of the mentor's. Art: Terraced fields wrapped in low mist, blue-necked cassowaries roosting among condensation-strung nets, feathers slick with damp, roots of the old mist-catcher trees cracking through the terrace stone, hard morning light breaking through the haze.

## Character (1)
- **Yara Fenn** — She mentors the settlement's bonded cassowary flock, insisting her weather-sense comes from second sight when it is really the same gill-folds in her throat that let every bird in her care read the mist. A cracked hand mirror, its glass fogged permanently along one edge from her own breath, that she taps twice before any reading whether anyone is watching or not. Complication: The flock trusts her only because she smells right to them, and the day her gill-folds finally heal shut with age is the day every cassowary in the roost stops answering when she calls. Look: silver-streaked mentor with faint gill-fold scars beneath her jaw, mist beading on a weathered field coat

## Reward item (1)
- **Fledgling's Primer** (ITEM, UNCOMMON) — It carries one hard-won cassowary-training trick per notch cut along its spine, releasing them strictly in the order they were carved. Best used when: A fledgling refuses every gentler correction and the flock is starting to lose patience with it. The catch: The notches were cut by removing a bone from a bird that failed to learn that same trick, and the primer will never say whose. Look: a slim rod of yellowed bone, a dozen shallow notches filed down one side, the grain gone glassy smooth from handling

## Reward skill (1)
- **Performance Roost Review** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — It lets a mentor read a flock's entire pecking hierarchy at a glance, right down to which bird is quietly failing and hiding it from the rest. Best used when: A once-reliable bird starts missing its rounds and the rest of the flock is closing ranks to cover for it. The catch: It works by making the wearer feel every bird's private dread of disappointing them, all at once, whether the flock actually feels that way or not. Look: the wearer's shoulders draw inward and their eyes track the flock left to right in a fixed, unhurried sweep, like scanning a ledger

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **What the Flock Decided** — In The Flock's Mentor, at The Roost Commons, Yara Fenn watches her bonded cassowary flock turn its collective judgment on a rival mentor's fledgling, and must decide whether Flockbound loyalty to her own birds outweighs what she already knows is the fairer verdict.
