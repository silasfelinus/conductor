## New Facets (invented for this dream)
- **Pollenblood** (`pollenblood`, SPECIES) — A human-derived lineage whose blood carries symbiotic pollen that lets nearby plants trade chemical signals through their bodies. They heal quickly in living landscapes, but sterile ground leaves them exhausted and half-sensory-blind.
- **Mutualist** (`mutualist`, ALIGNMENT) — A moral compass that treats survival as a reciprocal exchange among people, creatures, and places rather than a hierarchy of who deserves saving first. It resists sacrifice without consent, even when a one-sided choice would be faster.

## The idea
A wandering post-apocalypse orchard and the cassowary flock that pollinates it choose a new home without consulting the farming community that raised both, forcing their mentor to learn what care means once the cared-for choose another home.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Ansa's Borrowed Orchard** — She taught the flock. The orchard taught everyone else.
Art: A broad orchard moving through pale morning mist on exposed, root-laced mounds of soil, blue-necked cassowaries striding between flowering trees while a lone human figure stands small at the edge.

## Dream location (1)
- **Featherroot Vale** — A long green basin where a migrating orchard settles only when the cassowary flock chooses to scratch, nest, and scatter seed among the exposed root-mounds. Local rule: Anything able to travel under root, hoof, wing, or foot remains a neighbor rather than property. Best scene: Hundreds of residents stand along an old stone wall while the orchard slowly uproots itself beyond them, roots flexing through wet soil as the flock calmly walks beside it. Art: A wide misty valley with enormous exposed tree roots lifting dark soil in slow ridges, flowering branches bending over cassowaries with cobalt necks and black feathers, tiny people gathered far behind a low stone wall.

## Character (1)
- **Ansa Marlowe** — She mentors the cassowary flock by reading tiny changes in posture and calls that she insists are psychic impressions, and has quietly built her adult identity around being the human they choose to follow. A plaited grass whistle threaded with shed cassowary quills, worn smooth where her thumb worries the braid whenever the birds ignore her. Complication: Her Pollenblood senses can feel the orchard exchanging signals with the flock directly, which means every successful prediction is also evidence that the two living systems have been speaking directly all along. Look: A weathered field mentor with cropped dark hair, gold-pollen veins faintly visible at her wrists, a quill-braided whistle at her throat, mist dampening a patched green coat.

## Reward item (1)
- **Reciprocity Seed** (ITEM, RARE) — Lets two living groups share one useful adaptation for as long as both continue benefiting from the exchange. Best used when: Two communities need partnership without becoming permanent resources for the other. The catch: The exchange ends the instant either side keeps taking after it has stopped giving, and the lost adaptation cannot be borrowed from that partner again. Look: A walnut-sized translucent seedpod veined with silver mist, one half wrapped in fine root hairs and the other in short blue-black feathers.

## Reward skill (1)
- **Flock Listening** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — Reveals the direction a group is leaning before any individual has committed, through shifts in spacing, breath, posture, and attention. Best used when: A crowd, herd, or team has reached a decision no one person can honestly claim to have made. The catch: It reads collective motion, not correctness; a whole group can be confidently wrong together. Look: A loose ring of pale mist ripples outward at ankle height while nearby heads, wings, leaves, and shoulders all tilt along the same invisible line.
