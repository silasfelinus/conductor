## The idea
In Warren Wake, a limestone-quarry city built during the Long Blackout and never resurfaced, naked mole rats bred for wartime tunnel labor now serve as structural sentries and social glue for a permanently underground society. A harried wake broker navigates chronic body-storage overcrowding, a corner-cutting black-market rival, and the ever-present risk that a collapse warning arrives mid-funeral. It's a place to visit for pitch-black comedy, communal warmth, and the strange dignity of death admin when the city itself might come down around you.

- **The Chirp Exchange** — known for A tunnel-junction market and social hub where mole rat wardens broadcast structural warnings in chirp-code and townsfolk trade 'warmth debts' for huddling favors Local rule: On three sharp chirps, all trade and talk stops instantly and everyone huddles together against the nearest wall until the wardens signal clear Best scene: A bustling trade day interrupted by a three-chirp collapse warning, the whole market folding into one shivering, gossiping huddle mid-transaction Art: A wide cross-tunnel junction lined with lamplit market stalls carved into rock, strings of tin warning-bells overhead, mole rats nested in wall-niches at knee height, condensation glistening on stone, a crowd mid-huddle lit by scattered dropped lanterns

## Characters (3)
- **Hesper Doyle** — Keep every family's grief dignified despite a body-storage queue that never shrinks Carries A dog-eared ledger tracking who's waiting for a burial niche and for how long Complication: Her own sister has been in the storage queue for three weeks and Hesper keeps bumping the line for strangers instead Look: Soot-smudged black coat, brass spectacles perpetually fogged from tunnel damp, ink-stained fingers
- **Cricket Voss** — Keep the tunnels from coming down, one chirp at a time Carries A brass stethoscope-horn for translating mole rat chirp-code into words Complication: The wardens' chirp-code is degrading generation over generation from captive breeding, and Cricket is the only one who's noticed Look: Perpetual kneeling posture with an ear pressed to stone, two mole rat pups asleep in her coat pockets
- **Ambrose Kell** — Undercut Hesper's prices by cutting every corner a wake broker shouldn't cut Carries A stack of forged 'priority burial' chits Complication: One of his shortcuts nearly buried a still-living man last month and he's been quietly covering it up since Look: A coat too clean for underground living, an oily smile, a gold tooth that catches every lamp

## Rewards (2 — one skill, one item)
- **Warren Hearing** (SKILL, RARE) — The ability to read vibrations through stone or skin the way the mole rats do, sensing structural danger and buried emotion alike Best used when Checking whether a tunnel is safe to enter, or whether someone is telling the truth The catch: Heavy use leaves the user disoriented and overwhelmed in open, non-tunnel spaces
- **Cricket's Spare Stethoscope-Horn** (ITEM, UNCOMMON) — Translates mole rat chirp-code into audible warning patterns for anyone, not just trained wardens Best used when Traveling unfamiliar tunnels alone The catch: Parts are scarce and it wears out fast, and it mistranslates badly in echo-heavy chambers

## Scenarios (1-2)
- **The Triple Wake** — Backlog forces three unrelated families into the Sighing Parlor at once, and Hesper must run all three services simultaneously while one of Ambrose's under-the-table clients turns out to not actually be dead.
- **Three Chirps** — Cricket hears the three-chirp collapse warning mid-wake at the Chirp Exchange, and the whole market must evacuate and huddle while a body mid-transport risks being lost in the scramble.
