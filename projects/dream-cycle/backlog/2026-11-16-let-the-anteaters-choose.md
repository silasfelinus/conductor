## The idea
In a cheerful post-scarcity college town, the benevolent Kindly Engine dreams each night in swarms of glowing spectral-quartz mites that raise new mounds of unwritten knowledge across the gothic quads. Every Opening Day the whole town turns out with banners and food while the giant anteater fellows lick the mounds open, and whatever they taste becomes the year's curriculum. Nobody, including the Kindly Engine, can say what the subjects will be.
