## Creative seeds
- **Genres:** space opera + absurdist comedy
- **Occupation:** interstellar customs inspector / cargo appraiser
- **Animal / species:** giant sapient-adjacent void-dwelling Cargo Slugs used as living customs holds
- **Fusion:** Interstellar customs work supplies the paperwork-and-empire scale of space opera plus a deadpan procedural comedy engine; giant Cargo Slugs turn every inspection into a physical, biological ordeal -- inspectors must enter a living creature's gut to verify freight, read its peristaltic 'body language' instead of trusting labels, and treat a slug's mood, molt cycle, or startle reflex as a literal supply-chain risk. Remove the species and it's just bureaucracy jokes; remove the occupation and it's just a gross monster; together they produce a very specific comedy of trying to legislate biology.

## The idea
Every ship crossing the Ninth Wormhole gets processed the same way it has for two centuries: swallowed whole by a customs slug, held in its gut for exactly one shift, and stamped back out the other end with a manifest the empire actually trusts. Nobody chose this system because it made sense -- they chose it because Cargo Slugs can't be bribed, can't be hacked, and mostly can't be lied to. You come here to watch two very different bureaucracies, one made of forms and one made of flesh, argue over the same crate.

- **Odalys Kreft** — Thirty-year veteran senior inspector who treats every shift like the punchline of a joke only she's heard the setup for; wants one clean retirement without a scandal on her record Carries A battered ledger of hand-annotated slug-glow-pattern translations nobody else on station can read Complication: She's the only inspector who can definitively read Vellum's moods, so she can never actually retire -- the station keeps finding reasons to extend her contract Look: Weathered dark skin, a silver undercut, permanently ink-stained fingers, a heavy waxed coat worn over standard inspector coveralls, and one cybernetic eye that overlays tariff codes on everything she looks at
- **Teo Marsh** — Idealistic rookie inspector fresh from the Customs Academy who genuinely believes fair tariffs can hold an empire together, and hasn't yet learned the job is ten percent law and ninety percent negotiating with a giant mollusk's digestive system Carries A laminated pocket copy of the Interstellar Tariff Code, already falling apart at the spine Complication: Chronically seasick inside Vellum's gut, which the veteran inspectors find hilarious and Teo finds professionally mortifying Look: Young and freckled, swimming in oversized standard-issue waders, with a nervous habit of double-checking their stamp is held right-side up
- **Sable Nune** — Vellum's dedicated rancher and handler, part veterinarian and part diplomat, fiercely protective of the slug against bureaucratic overwork and rough handling Carries A set of hand-carved bone calipers used to measure the depth and tension of Vellum's wall-ripples Complication: Is quietly stockpiling evidence that the station is running Vellum past safe digestion cycles to hit quota, and hasn't decided whether to blow the whistle or protect the slug by staying silent Look: Broad-shouldered with sleeves rolled to the elbow, forearms scarred from old digestive-acid burns, a battered station-service jacket over sea-worn overalls, and a jar of bioluminescent calming gel always within reach

## Rewards (2 — one skill, one item)
- **Gut Reading** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — The ability to read a Cargo Slug's peristaltic wall-ripples like tariff codes, spotting mislabeled or contraband freight by touch and smell from inside the gut chambers Best used when Paperwork looks clean but something about the shipment still doesn't sit right The catch: Only works on a calm slug -- a startled or nauseated slug's signals turn into false positives, and misreading them can trigger a reflex that reroutes the whole chamber's cargo into an emergency purge chute
- **Self-Notarizing Stamp** (ITEM, RARE) — A tariff stamp that keeps a manifest legally current in real time as goods biologically change composition mid-transit, instead of going stale the moment it's pressed Best used when Cargo is known to transform inside the gut and you need paperwork that stays valid through the whole digestion cycle The catch: The stamp is bonded to whichever slug witnessed its first use -- if that slug ever molts, dies, or gets reassigned, the stamp goes blank and every manifest it ever touched becomes legally void retroactively

## Scenarios (1-2)
- **The Reflex Audit** — An anonymous tip claims a smuggler routed a shipment of stolen war-relics through Vellum's gut disguised as diplomatic gift baskets -- Odalys and Teo have to get inside and verify before Vellum's scheduled digestive reflex purges, and legally erases, the evidence
- **Molt Season Shutdown** — Vellum goes into molt right as a fleet supply convoy is due through the checkpoint, and Sable has to keep the chain of command from panicking while the inspectors improvise an emergency inspection protocol on an uncooperative, hormonal, quarter-mile-long living cargo hold
