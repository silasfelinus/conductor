## New Facets (invented for this dream)
- **Former Mascot** (`former-mascot`, BACKSTORY) — A person, creature or place that once served as the public face of something larger and has since been retired from the role. Everyone still recognizes the symbol, and almost no one has met what stands underneath it.
- **First Greeter** (`first-greeter`, ROLE) — The one whose place is to be the first face any newcomer meets, setting the terms of welcome before anyone else can speak. Whatever the greeter gets wrong becomes the stranger's first impression of everyone behind them.

## The idea
A year after first contact, a Weird West company town has rebuilt its whole daily life around visitors who are kind only to whoever stands nearest them.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Stand Closer, They're Friendly** — The visitors landed and said hello a year ago, and now a whole desert company town is learning to live at arm's length from kindness.
Art: A bright, clean Weird West desert town remade around a first contact: smooth white ceramic and pastel polymer modules stacked beside weathered clapboard storefronts, translucent glowing silk awnings over the boardwalks, a huge pale seed-shaped lander resting in a red dry wash, a faded ocelot painted across the sandstone cliff, miners and shopkeepers in dusters standing close together, crisp high-desert light with long violet dusk shadows.

## Dream location (1)
- **Settledown Wash** — A white ceramic lander rests in this dry red wash beneath the cliff where the mining company once painted its ocelot logo, ringed now by printed-polymer stalls under glowing silk canopies. Local rule: The visitors help only whoever stands nearest the hull, so people queue in a tight ring and trade places on the hour. Best scene: At dusk the silk canopies brighten, the faded ocelot on the cliff glows through them, and a dozen people shuffle one step closer to the hull at once.
