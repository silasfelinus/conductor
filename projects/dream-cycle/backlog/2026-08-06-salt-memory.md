## The idea
A nanopunk dry-dock built from self-healing pulse-metal keeps regrowing crew that never really died — or so an axolotl-descended engineer believes, chasing the mystery through coral-carved gearworks steeped in oceanic myth.

## Dream vibe (1)
**The Salt Diagnostic** — Every drowned machine remembers the god that built it.
Art: A bioluminescent axolotl-descended engineer wades through a flooded gearworks temple, myth-carved coral pipes glowing along the pulse-metal walls.

## Dream location (1)
- **The Regrowth Yards** — Hulls here regrow themselves overnight, knitting fresh pulse-metal flesh over gashes as if the ships refuse to admit they were ever wounded. Local rule: No one repairs what the metal can mend itself, for the pulse-metal is trusted to heal its own wounds without human hands interfering. Best scene: A scar in the hull hums, flushes pink, and closes over a hidden compartment, sealing away whatever secret the ship has just decided to keep. Art: coral-pink nanopunk dry-dock, pulse-metal veins pulsing like a tide

## Character (1)
- **Coral Vey** — She chases the myth that her missing crew is still regenerating somewhere in the yard, refusing to believe they were truly lost. A socket wrench has fused permanently into her regrown forearm, grown back into her flesh like just another part of the ship. Complication: Every graft she recovers carries someone else's memory with it, so piecing her crew back together means reliving lives that were never hers. Look: gill-frilled daredevil engineer in a patched pulse-metal diving suit

## Reward item (1)
- **The Drowned God's Bolt** (ITEM, RARE) — Severed pulse-metal fuses back to living tissue, knitting wound and metal into a single healed whole. Best used when: Something vital has been cut away that never should have been severed in the first place. The catch: It remembers everything it reattaches, and afterward it keeps pulling toward whatever it was torn from, restless to return. Look: a heavy corroded bolt of dark pulse-metal, barnacle-scarred, its threads still glowing faint red from within, salt crust flaking from the head

## Reward skill (1)
- **Tide Telemetry** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — A hull's stress lines become readable as luminous constellations, the way sailors once read starcharts, revealing the ship's hidden intentions. Best used when: A structure looms that no living engineer ever designed, and instinct alone cannot be trusted to navigate it. The catch: The readings have a way of drifting toward wherever the old myth wants its holder to go, whether that path is chosen or not. Look: a hull's stress lines rendered as luminous starchart constellations laid over curved coral-pink plating, faint measurement arcs bridging the cracks
