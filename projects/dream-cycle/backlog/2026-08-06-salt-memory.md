## The idea
A nanopunk dry-dock built from self-healing pulse-metal keeps regrowing crew that never really died — or so an axolotl-descended engineer believes, chasing the mystery through coral-carved gearworks steeped in oceanic myth.

## Dream vibe (1)
**The Salt Diagnostic** — Every drowned machine remembers the god that built it.
Art: A bioluminescent axolotl-descended engineer wades through a flooded gearworks temple, myth-carved coral pipes glowing along the pulse-metal walls.

## Dream location (1)
- **The Regrowth Yards** — known for hulls that regrow themselves overnight. Local rule: no one repairs what the metal can mend itself. Best scene: a scar in the hull hums, flushes pink, and closes over a hidden compartment. Art: coral-pink nanopunk dry-dock, pulse-metal veins pulsing like a tide

## Character (1)
- **Coral Vey** — chase the myth that her missing crew is still regenerating somewhere in the yard. Carries a socket wrench fused into her regrown forearm. Complication: every graft she takes back brings someone else's memory with it. Look: gill-frilled daredevil engineer in a patched pulse-metal diving suit

## Reward item (1)
- **The Drowned God's Bolt** (ITEM, RARE) — reattaches severed pulse-metal to living tissue. Best used when something has been cut away that shouldn't have been. The catch: it remembers what it reattaches, and it keeps asking to go back

## Reward skill (1)
- **Tide Telemetry** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — reads a hull's stress lines like starcharts. Best used when navigating a structure no living engineer designed. The catch: the readings drift toward wherever the myth wants you to go

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **What the Hull Remembers** — In The Salt Diagnostic at The Regrowth Yards, Coral Vey follows a regenerating scar through the myth-drowned nanopunk dry-dock, chasing the crew the pulse-metal insists it can still save.
