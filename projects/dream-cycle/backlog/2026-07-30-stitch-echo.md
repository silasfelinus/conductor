## The idea
A nanotech engineer pulled out of her own world wakes as the boiler-imp's apprentice in a mountain bathhouse, and the town's bridge — built in a century that has not happened yet — has started coming apart.

## Dream vibe (1)
**The Bridge From Next Century** — Everything useful here was built by someone who has not arrived yet.
Art: A steaming wooden bathhouse under snow at night, pale blue mineral light leaking from its boiler vents, and beyond it a slender bridge of impossibly thin material glowing faintly across a black gorge.

## Dream location (1)
- **The Moonstone Bathhouse** — Nobody in this century knows how the boiler was built, and the bathhouse runs on it anyway. Local rule: The house rule is simple and absolute: feed the boiler before you feed yourself, or the pale blue fire goes quiet for good. Best scene: The boiler door swings open and the light inside is the wrong color for fire, cold and blue-white against the steam. Art: A cramped wooden boiler room thick with steam, brass valves furred with mineral scale, a small horned figure silhouetted against a cold blue-white glow that throws no heat shimmer.

## Character (1)
- **Rina Oyelaran** — She means to keep the bridge standing long enough to understand what built it. A cracked lab slate and a vial of dormant repair-motes travel with her, the motes rationed out by the grain. Complication: She keeps stopping mid-repair to ask what the bridge is for, and the imp keeps not answering. Look: A tired engineer in a borrowed cotton work robe over lab trousers, soot to the elbow, twists of hair tied back with wire, a cracked slate tucked under one arm.

## Reward item (1)
- **The Patient Seed** (ITEM, RARE) — It mends a single object completely, restoring it grain by grain until nothing is out of place. Best used when: A bad fast fix would hold just long enough to kill someone. The catch: Slowly means seasons here, and the repair cannot be hurried along. Look: A single moonstone bead the size of a pea, milk-pale with a drifting blue sheen, warm to the touch and faintly gritty, kept in a folded paper packet.

## Reward skill (1)
- **Ask the Small Things** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — It persuades a machine's smallest parts to hold together for a little longer than they should. Best used when: The whole structure is going and only a minute more is needed. The catch: Whatever it holds longer now gives way all the harder later on. Look: A haze of silver motes settling into a hairline crack and glowing steadily along its whole length like a filament.

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **The Night the Bridge Asked for Help** — In The Bridge From Next Century, out from The Moonstone Bathhouse in a snowstorm, Rina Oyelaran is halfway across the failing span when its surface writes a request under her feet in her own handwriting, in a notation she has not invented yet.
