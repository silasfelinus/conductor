The Occupation (taxidermy/resurrection) turns this into tactile, morbidly comic craft: stitching, mounting, and the risk of a half-finished mount waking up mid-stitch to argue about the wrong century. The Species (bats) supplies the only reliable way to date an unstuck specimen — their echolocation clicks reveal not just distance but *when* something belongs, so a colony's chorus becomes both scientific instrument and comic-chaotic oracle, shaping the location rules, the reward set, and every scene's central problem.

## The idea
In a fog-bound port town, time doesn't always keep its rooms in order — moths, foxes, and the odd pocket watch alike slip loose from their proper decade mid-breath. A gothic-comic taxidermy parlor stitches these 'unstuck' specimens back into a single moment, but only once a resident bat colony's echolocation clicks confirm exactly when they belong. It's morbid, absurd, and strangely tender work — half funeral parlor, half emergency room for time itself, and worth visiting for the specific pleasure of watching a civil servant argue with his own future paperwork.

- **Mortlake Vane** — Give every unstuck creature a proper, dignified place in time before it's lost for good Carries A stitching kit threaded with chronal gut — thread that only holds if a specimen's time has been correctly set Complication: He came briefly unstuck himself once, mid-stitch, and has been quietly terrified of it happening again ever since Look: Tall and ink-stained, a leather apron over mourning clothes, one eye faintly clouded from old temporal-fog exposure
- **Fen Corrigan** — Translate the bat colony's echolocation into usable, trustworthy 'when' readings for the parlor Carries A wax-cylinder recorder strapped to her chest to capture every click-chorus in full Complication: Years of living on strict dusk-and-dawn bat-time have left her own sense of 'now' slipping, and she sometimes answers questions from the wrong decade entirely Look: Soot-smudged coat, ear trumpets rebuilt into a jury-rigged bat-hearing rig, always a half-beat out of sync with the room
- **Bailiff Pell** — Certify every stitched specimen's timeline before it can be released back into the town, terrified of being blamed if a certification goes wrong Carries A ledger that quietly rewrites its own entries whenever a certified timeline shifts underneath it Complication: He was accidentally certified himself, twice, in two different years, and technically exists in triplicate somewhere in the filing system Look: An ill-fitting official coat, ink-smudged spectacles, perpetually flustered and one step behind his own paperwork

## Rewards (2 — one skill, one item)
- **Echo Reading** (SKILL, RARE) — The ability to interpret a chorus of bat clicks, or any faint temporal echo, to determine exactly when an object or creature belongs Best used when A specimen or character is behaving erratically or seems out of place in time and needs correct dating before anything else can proceed The catch: Misreadings are common and often comic — get the 'when' wrong and you may stitch or certify something into entirely the wrong decade. Look: a fan of overlapping sound-rings rendered as concentric brass-toned ripples rising from an open specimen jar, each ring a slightly different age of light
- **Chronal Gut Thread** (ITEM, EPIC) — Thread that only holds a stitch permanently once a specimen's correct time has been confirmed, finishing a preservation mount or repair for good Best used when Completing a taxidermy or preservation stitch right after confirming the correct when via Echo Reading The catch: Used before the timing is confirmed, the stitch holds only until the next dusk, then unravels and the specimen comes unstuck again — usually at the worst possible moment. Look: a spool of pale translucent gut thread on a turned bone bobbin, one waxed strand drawn taut and catching candlelight, a curved needle laid beside it on dark velvet

## Scenarios (1-2)

- **Triple Pell** — Bailiff Pell's own paperwork reveals he may exist in three different years at once; the party must use Echo Reading at the Loft Exchange to determine which Pell truly belongs in now, before all three arrive at the same meeting.
