## The idea
A nanotech engineer pulled out of her own world wakes as the boiler-imp's apprentice in a mountain bathhouse, and the town's bridge — built in a century that has not happened yet — has started coming apart.

## Dream vibe (1)
**The Bridge From Next Century** — Everything useful here was built by someone who has not arrived yet.
Art: A steaming wooden bathhouse under snow at night, pale blue mineral light leaking from its boiler vents, and beyond it a slender bridge of impossibly thin material glowing faintly across a black gorge.
