## The idea
A drifting greenhouse raft shelters people in the middle of changing bodies, and the story begins after the change has already been agreed to and cannot be undone.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Don't Look, It's Still Setting** — Everyone aboard is partway to someone else, and the polite thing is to let them finish.
Art: A wide daylight river of brown water carrying a long glass-and-timber raft crowded with waving neighbours, strings of paper lanterns, steaming pots, damp ferns, and shed husks hung like banners.

## Dream location (1)
- **Mudlark Chrysalis Raft** — A long enclosed raft of dark timber and fogged glass that drifts the delta carrying families through their slow changes, with a cot, a basin and a lantern for every resident. Local rule: Anyone mid-change is greeted by the name they had yesterday and the name they chose for tomorrow, both said aloud. Best scene: The whole raft gathers on the flat roof in bright noon to cheer a body stepping out of its own husk. Art: A flat-decked river raft with fogged glass walls, shadow-oak beams, damp moss, translucent husks pegged out to dry, steaming basins, and a crowd in saturated cloth under bright midday sun.

## Character (1)
- **Tobi Quang** — They tend the raft's changing residents as an apprentice molt tender, loyal to the person they will be next season more than to the one they are now, and their companion Ari is a Split-Kin dividing into two separate people. A folded paper grid-sheet and a stub of charcoal for sketching every patient from above at a fixed angle. Complication: Tobi is still growing themself, and the sketches they trust most show a future body they cannot yet fit through the raft's doors. Look: A fifteen-year-old who stands nearly head and shoulders above every doorframe, tall and heavy through the shoulders, androgynous, with faded rainbow hair in tight coils grown out to pale pastel tips, Lagos-patterned indigo wrapper over a Mekong-style woven hat hung down the back, and an easy, slightly tilted calm that holds the same angle whatever happens around them.

## Reward item (1)
- **Husk Calipers** (ITEM, UNCOMMON) — It measures exactly how far a body has changed from the shape it started in. Best used when: Someone insists they are the same as they ever were and nobody can say how to check. The catch: It always reads the holder's own change last, and never rounds it down. Look: Wooden calipers of dark shadow-oak the length of a forearm, pale brass pivot, thumb-rests worn bright, dusted with fine yellow pollen.

## Reward skill (1)
- **Isometric Hush** (SKILL, RARE) — It steadies a changing body by giving it one fixed angle to settle into. Best used when: A transformation is racing ahead of the person undergoing it and they need to slow down. The catch: For an hour afterward, the user can only be seen from the angle they chose. Look: Pale grid lines unfolding across the air and over bare skin, folded planes of soft light snapping into flat tidy angles around the subject.

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **We Split Over Breakfast** — In Don't Look, It's Still Setting at Mudlark Chrysalis Raft, Tobi Quang kneels beside Ari, whose shared shell is cracking down its seam, while the whole raft carries out bowls of warm river water.
