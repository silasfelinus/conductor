## The idea
A greenhouse where maps grow instead of plants. Coastlines climb trellises as
vines, mountain ranges bud along a stem, and unexplored territory blooms as a
blank white flower waiting to be filled in. Surveyor-gardeners tend the beds —
prune a vine and a river changes course somewhere real. Hushed, green-lit,
smelling of soil and pencil shavings; the quiet thrill of a place still being
drawn.

## Location dream
**The Cartographers' Greenhouse** — LOCATION. A vast Victorian glasshouse whose
beds grow living maps; watering cans full of ink, brass surveying instruments
hung like garden tools, panes fogged with the breath of continents. Flavor:
"Please do not deadhead the terra incognita — it is not finished blooming." Art
direction: sun-through-dusty-glass green light, ink-stained potting benches,
map-vines with contour-line leaves, soft botanical-illustration palette.

## Characters (2-4)
- **Head Gardener Ives** — a stooped surveyor-gardener with dirt under every
  fingernail and a monocle that reads latitude; believes a rushed map grows crooked.
- **The Grafting Apprentice, Wick** — young, eager, keeps trying to splice two
  countries together to make a shortcut; the greenhouse keeps quietly un-splicing them.
- **The Blank Bloom** — a single white flower that has never opened; whatever it
  becomes will be somewhere no one has ever been.

## Rewards (3-6)
- **Seed of Somewhere** (COMMON) — plant it and a small unmapped place grows for you alone.
- **Contour Shears** (UNCOMMON) — trim a coastline into a gentler shape (undo is not guaranteed).
- **The Fog-Pane Lens** (RARE) — read the map a place *wants* to be, not the one it is.
- **First Draft of the Blank Bloom** (LEGENDARY) — name a place before it exists, and it will remember you named it.

## Scenarios (1-2)
- **The Crooked River** — a map-vine has grown wrong and a real river is flooding
  its banks; the player must find where Wick's shortcut split the stem and coax it straight.
- **Opening Night** — the Blank Bloom is finally budding; Ives asks the player to
  decide, with him, what kind of place the world still needs.
