## The idea
Deep in a rain-fed backwater parish lives Kayal Marigold, a cryptid older than the canals, who sheds a skin of adamantium scales every monsoon. Every shed scale hums a single word that nobody has ever said, and the parish's whole way of speaking has grown out of those flakes the way rice grows out of mud. This year the shed is coming off in branching, ever-smaller pieces, and each smaller scale holds a smaller and stranger word.
