## The idea
A regional reforestation office was relocated into a drained quarry, and the forest it was meant to plant has already moved in around it, leaving staff to work out what an office is when the ferns are the senior occupants.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Ferns in the Open-Plan** — The forest was supposed to be the project, not the neighbour.
Art: A flat-roofed prefab office with fern fronds pressing against every window, hard white daylight, a crystalline coworker glinting at a desk.

## Dream location (1)
- **Fernwright Quarry Office** — A prefab regional forestry bureau parked on the floor of an emptied limestone quarry, where seedlings sprouted through the carpet within a week. Local rule: Nothing may be moved once a root has gripped it, which includes the water cooler, two desks, and the director's chair. Best scene: At noon the whole staff eats lunch standing in the aisle because the only clear floor left is the one strip of bare carpet. Art: A white prefab office on a pale limestone quarry floor, saw-cut walls behind, ferns and young birches growing through the doorway, hard noon sun and short crisp shadows.

## Character (1)
- **Teodora Kurian** — They are the office's longest-serving records clerk, rewriting the bureau's floor plan around the plants instead of against them, and they are the only one who has stopped being scared of the place. A folding bamboo stool, a screw-top jar of lime pickle, and a blunt pencil she uses to sketch where each new fern has come up. Complication: Her colleague Quillon, a Crystalborn field assessor, hums in a pitch that makes the ferns lean toward the desk, and the director wants the humming stopped. Look: Late sixties, soft through the shoulders and wrists, hair wrapped entirely in a faded blue-and-white block-printed cotton cloth knotted at the nape, reading glasses on a cord, hands dusted with chalk, face at rest in raised, delighted eyebrows over a mouth pressed shut to hold in a grin, standing on pale carpet gone green at the edges beside a faceted translucent quartz-bodied colleague.

## Reward item (1)
- **Herbarium Press** (ITEM, RARE) — It flattens any living thing placed inside into a perfect, harmless, labelled specimen that can later be unpressed intact. Best used when: Something wild must be carried through a place that forbids it. The catch: The label it writes names whatever you most recently lied about. Look: A hand-sized pressing frame of cream dragonbone porcelain plates held by brass wing screws, a brown paper tag tied to one corner, hard daylight on its glazed rim.

## Reward skill (1)
- **Root Etiquette** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — It lets you move calmly through crowded growth without breaking a single stem, and others nearby copy your gait. Best used when: A narrow space is full of living things that cannot be harmed. The catch: Afterwards you cannot walk across a lawn without apologising to it aloud. Look: A figure stepping sideways between fern fronds in hard daylight, every frond bending a hand's width away from the shoulder and springing back behind them.

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **Moss on the Photocopier** — In Ferns in the Open-Plan at the Fernwright Quarry Office, Teodora Kurian chalks a new floor plan on the carpet around the root-gripped furniture the morning after the relocation, while Quillon hums and an inspector's van is heard grinding down the quarry road.
