## The idea
A bog-punk frontier town settles land claims with a horn that senses lies. A coolheaded cybernetic hacker marked by unicorns has to out-code the marshal's ledger before the horn calls her bluff.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Marshlight Draw** — In this bog, every duel is judged by a horn that already knows who lied.
Art: A muddy frontier street at dusk, wooden storefronts leaning into black bogwater, gas lanterns strung between a crashed satellite dish and a hitching post, the pale glow of an orbital ring above the tree line.

## Dream location (1)
- **Sourmoss Landing** — known for adamantium claims buried under the mud. Local rule: no draw before the horn sounds. Best scene: a buried adamantium vein surfaces mid-duel. Art: A sunken frontier town built on stilts over black bogwater, corrugated-metal storefronts patched with salvaged satellite panels, a rusted horn-shaped beacon mounted at the crossroads, moss-slick boardwalks reflecting orbital ring-light.

## Character (1)
- **Tamsin Ruel** — crack the claims registry before the marshal's horn calls her out. Carries a forearm rig of adamantium fingers wired to a cracked data-horn. Complication: the horn only tells the truth about her own claim. Look: a lean figure in a mud-oiled duster, one arm sheathed in dull adamantium plating, a spiraled bone-white horn-shard fused into her temple, wires trailing from it into a cracked wrist terminal

## Reward item (1)
- **Revenant Derringer** (ITEM, RARE) — draws faster than the hand that holds it. Best used when the horn has already called the duel. The catch: it remembers every hand that's fired it, including the dead ones. Look: a stubby adamantium derringer, its barrel etched with faint glowing sigils, grip wrapped in bone-pale leather that shifts like breathing skin

## Reward skill (1)
- **Ghost Ledger Read** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — reads a claim's true owner from static alone. Best used when the paperwork has been forged. The catch: it also reads what you erased. Look: a flicker of green code bleeding across the user's vision, ledger lines rendering mid-air in cracked, archaic script
