## The idea
On Mars, a colony's only water source is a herd of unicorn-touched animals that seed the bog wherever their hooves fall, and they will only walk at dusk, so driving them across a dry basin in a single night decides whether a whole district fills its cisterns or goes dry until the next herd run.

## Dream vibe (1)
**The Dusk Drive** — The water goes where the herd goes, and the herd goes at dusk.
Art: A vast rust-red basin at last light, a moving mass of pale horned animals kicking up ochre dust, standing water spreading behind them in the hoofprints, mirror-bright sluice gates on the far ridge.

## Dream location (1)
- **Sump Nine** — The bog here exists only in the herd's footprints, a season's worth of water written into the ground one hoofstep at a time. Local rule: Never put yourself between the herd and the low ground. Best scene: A dry basin filling in behind ten thousand hooves in under an hour. Art: A terraced Martian basin of rust dust and black peat, mirror-bright adamantium sluice gates set into the slope, shallow water threading between churned hoofprints, thin cold haze under a violet sky.

## Character (1)
- **Tobiah Ncube** — He must walk the herd across the entire dry basin before dusk fades to full dark. A splice-cable runs from a coiled reel into his own jaw port, letting him hear exactly what the herd hears. Complication: The herd's signal has grown louder than his own thoughts, and he has stopped minding. Look: A calm, heavy-set man in a dust-caked drover's coat, a chrome splice port set into his jawline trailing a coiled cable, one eye replaced with a matte lens, pale horn-scar curling up his forearm.

## Reward item (1)
- **The Borrowed Grin** (ITEM, RARE) — It makes strangers trust the wearer exactly as completely as they once trusted its last owner. Best used when: There is no time left on the clock to earn that trust the honest way, only to wear someone else's. The catch: Its previous owner was trusted completely by everyone around him, right up until that trust got him killed. Look: A mirror-bright adamantium jaw plate shaped to a wide easy smile, a hairline crack running through two of the teeth, worn warm on the inside.

## Reward skill (1)
- **Old-Protocol Whisper** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — It makes obsolete machinery answer as though its original crew were still aboard and standing watch. Best used when: The only functioning machine for the job was built two centuries ago and no living crew remembers how to run it. The catch: The machinery answers back in the voice of whoever used it last, whether you want to hear it or not. Look: Amber legacy glyphs waking one panel after another down a dark corridor, each dying back as the next lights.

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **The Night Shift Reroute** — In The Dusk Drive, out on Sump Nine, Tobiah Ncube has the herd halfway across the dry basin when the smelter district's shift boss starts closing gates ahead of him, one at a time, to turn the water somewhere it will be worth more.
