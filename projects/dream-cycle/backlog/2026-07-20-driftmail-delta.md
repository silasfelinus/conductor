## The idea
In a river delta where six months of rain never truly stop, messages are folded into paper boats and sent downstream toward the sea. Some arrive; the rest are netted by a lighthouse keeper who reads every undelivered letter aloud before letting the paper go. It's a world about trusting your words to leave your hands.

## Vibe / genre dream
**Damp Devotion** — GENRE. Everything here is a little soaked, and that's how you know it's been loved.

## Locations (2)
- **The Fold House** — known for A stilted workshop where letters are folded into seaworthy boats using forty-three inherited creases, each said to change how the message travels. Local rule: No boat may be folded twice — a bad fold must be burned, not refolded, or the message arrives as someone else's memory. Best scene: A child folding their first boat under a lantern while an apprentice corrects one crease at a time, rain drumming the tin roof. Art: Warm lantern-lit wooden stilt-house interior, dozens of folded paper boats drying on twine lines overhead, rain streaking the windows, amber and teal palette, cozy clutter, painterly illustration style.
- **Driftlight Lighthouse** — known for A half-drowned lighthouse at the delta's mouth where the keeper nets undelivered boats from the tideline and reads every letter aloud before releasing the paper back to the water. Local rule: Once read aloud, a letter's wish is considered granted whether or not it was true — so the keeper chooses her words with more care than the senders did. Best scene: The keeper standing knee-deep at dusk, reading a stranger's letter to the open sea by the light of the tower's slow-turning lamp. Art: Weathered lighthouse leaning at the edge of a wide brown river delta meeting the sea, dusk sky in violet and rust, keeper silhouetted holding a lantern and a soaked paper letter, cinematic wide shot, muted watercolor tones.

## Characters (3)
- **Corla Wick** — The delta's last licensed boat-folder, trying to teach the forty-three creases before her hands stop remembering them. Carries A cedar box of pre-folded "emergency" boats for messages too urgent to fold properly. Complication: She folded a boat for her own unsent apology twelve years ago and has never let it leave the workshop. Look: Ink-stained fingers, salt-faded apron over patched wool, silver hair pinned with a fish-bone comb.
- **Ren Pell** — The lighthouse keeper who reads every undelivered letter aloud, half-convinced the sea is listening back. Carries A logbook of first lines from letters she's read but never finished remembering. Complication: She's begun writing replies she has no way to send. Look: Oilskin coat over a lamplighter's harness, wind-chapped cheeks, a voice worn soft from reading aloud in weather.
- **Tull** — A delta-born errand-runner who actually delivers the boats that survive the current, treating every successful delivery like a small miracle. Carries A waterproofed satchel with a hand-drawn map of every eddy that has ever eaten a letter. Complication: Doesn't know how to fold a boat himself, and is quietly ashamed of it. Look: Rolled sleeves, river-mud boots, a grin that shows up before good news does.

## Rewards (2 — one skill, one item)
- **The Forty-Third Crease** (SKILL, RARE) — The ability to fold one message so precisely it reaches its intended reader regardless of current, weather, or distance. Best used when The one time a message truly cannot afford to go astray. The catch: It only works once per person, ever — use it on the wrong letter and you'll know exactly what you wasted.
- **Ren's Waterproof Logbook** (ITEM, UNCOMMON) — A logbook that keeps anything written inside legible no matter how wet it gets. Best used when Recording something you need to survive the journey with you. The catch: Pages already used for a previous letter's first line can't be fully erased — new writing shares the page with someone else's opening words.

## Scenarios (1-2)
- **The Unsent Apology** — Corla finally decides to fold and send the boat she made twelve years ago, and the player has to help her survive letting it actually leave the workshop.
- **What the Tide Keeps** — A storm strands dozens of undelivered boats at Driftlight, and Ren needs help reading through all of them before the next tide carries them back out to be lost for good.
