## The idea
Everything that keeps a frontier station warm is asleep, armed, and coiled around the reactor — and the analyst who just sold its targeting codes to the wrong buyer has one shift to wake it politely.

## Dream vibe (1)
**The Reactor Dragon** — Everything keeping this station warm is asleep and still armed.
Art: An enormous scaled body coiled around a glowing reactor housing in a decommissioned docking bay, pink and cyan strip-light washing over dull plating, frost on the deck between the coils.

## Dream location (1)
- **Neon Bay Nine** — Neon Bay Nine is the only bay warm enough to sleep in, because of what sleeps in it. Local rule: Walk the painted line and do not run, whatever you hear. Best scene: The coils shift a metre and every strip-light in the bay flickers pink. ## Character (1)
- **Marisol Quintero** — She has one shift to get the sold codes back before the strike window opens. Carries: She carries an analyst's slate holding three days of targeting solutions she cannot explain. Complication: She believed the buyer, and she will probably believe the next one too. Look: A young woman in a station-issue jumpsuit with the sleeves cut off, cropped bleached hair, a cracked slate strapped to one forearm, blue mineral dust ground into her knees.

Look: A shard of pale blue angelite set in a cracked steel bezel, its surface fogging over with frost and clearing again in a slow pulse.
