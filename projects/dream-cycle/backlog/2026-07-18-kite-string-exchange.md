## The idea
A wind-scoured hillside town where wishes are flown, not spoken: you whisper yours to a kite-maker who builds it into paper and string, then fly it toward a cliff where hundreds of other people's lines cross and knot into fortunes nobody chose. Coming here means giving up control of your own wish the moment it leaves your hand, and maybe fishing a stranger's out of the sky in exchange.

## Vibe / genre dream
**Wind-Fortune** — GENRE. Every wish you fly gets tangled with a stranger's before it comes true.

## Locations (2)
- **The Spindle Workshop** — known for Hand-built wish-kites, one per customer, cut and knotted to carry a single spoken wish aloft — never a written one. Local rule: A kite can only be started once its wish has been spoken aloud to the spinner; nothing is ever written down first. Best scene: A nervous first-timer whispers their wish to old Mother Spindle while she cuts and knots colored paper by feel alone, eyes closed the whole time. Art: Warm lamplit workshop cluttered with spools of dyed string, half-finished kite skeletons hanging from the rafters like ribs, cut-paper scraps drifting on a plank floor, an old woman's weathered hands mid-knot, amber light through waxed-paper windows.
- **The Tangle** — known for The windward cliff where hundreds of kite lines cross and knot together over weeks into unclaimed 'fortunes.' Local rule: You may only pull down a tangle that is not attached to your own kite's line. Best scene: At dusk, a fortune-fisher climbs the rocks with a long hooked pole, reels in a snarl of string thick with fortunes, and unknots them one by one by lantern light while gulls wheel overhead. Art: Dramatic clifftop at dusk, sky crossed by hundreds of taut kite strings catching the last orange light, a silhouetted figure braced against the wind with a long hooked pole, snarled knots of string and paper fluttering like a net against the darkening sky.

## Characters (3)
- **Mother Spindle** — The town's kite-maker, who has flown ten thousand strangers' wishes and refuses to ever fly her own. Carries A pair of bone-handled shears, blunted from decades of cutting string instead of paper. Complication: She knows exactly which fortune on The Tangle is hers, thirty years unclaimed, and refuses to climb up and take it back. Look: Small, stooped woman with silver hair in a tight knot, fingertips permanently stained blue-black from dye, an apron stitched entirely from kite-scrap patches.
- **Cobb Fenwick** — A teenage fortune-fisher who climbs The Tangle nightly to sell unclaimed wishes back to the town, half-believing they're just wind noise. Carries A hooked pole twice his height, notched with a tally of every fortune he's ever pulled down. Complication: He pulled down a fortune last month written in his dead mother's handwriting, and hasn't told anyone. Look: Lanky and wind-chapped, salt-bleached hair, a coil of spare string looped over one shoulder, fingerless gloves worn through at the knuckles.
- **The Windreader** — A government wind-cartographer sent to explain the hillside's impossibly consistent thermals as geology, not magic. Carries A brass anemometer that has read the exact same 'impossible' number every day for a week straight. Complication: Is losing the argument with their own instincts and has started, quietly, to write wishes of their own. Look: Crisp gray coat out of place among patched wool, a notebook full of crossed-out explanations, a compass that keeps pointing at The Tangle instead of north.

## Rewards (2 — one skill, one item)
- **Blind Knotting** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — The ability to tie and read complex kite-string knots without looking, the way Mother Spindle does. Best used when Untangling a fortune-line or building a wish-kite in the dark, where looking or speaking would break the working. The catch: You can no longer tie a knot 'wrong' even when you want to — every knot you make now holds true, including the ones you didn't mean to make permanent. Look: a pair of weathered hands tying a complex dyed-string knot in mid-air with the eyes shut, loose ends trailing off into lamplight
- **Cobb's Hooked Pole** (ITEM, RARE) — Lets you pull down exactly one fortune from any tangle, guaranteed to be relevant to you specifically. Best used when You need an honest answer and are willing to let a stranger's wind-luck decide it for you. The catch: The fortune it pulls down is always true, but never phrased the way you wanted to hear it. Look: a long bamboo pole gone silver with handling, tipped with a hand-forged iron hook and a stub of frayed red string, leaning against a plank wall

## Scenarios (1-2)
- **The Thirty-Year Tangle** — Mother Spindle finally asks Cobb to climb up and bring down the one fortune she's never claimed, then has to decide whether to actually open it once he does.
- **The Windreader's Wish** — After a week of failing to find a scientific explanation for the hillside's thermals, the visiting official goes to the Spindle Workshop after dark to whisper a wish they'd never admit to believing in.

Personality: Incurably nosy, keeps everyone's secrets badly, genuinely soft-hearted underneath the gossip. Appears as: A paper-and-wire kite-bird patched together from a hundred old fortune scraps, tail feathers made of unclaimed kite string.
