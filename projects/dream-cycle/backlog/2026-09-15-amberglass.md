## New Facets (invented for this dream)
- **Nest-Bound** (`nest-bound`, ALIGNMENT) — A loyalty imprinted on a place rather than on any single person or cause: whoever holds that ground keeps the loyalty, even after they change entirely. It survives someone else's betrayal; it does not survive that ground being destroyed.
- **Name-Keeper** (`name-keeper`, ROLE) — A role entrusted to say who someone truly is once their own account cannot be trusted anymore, relying on nothing written down at all. Losing that person erases the only confirmation that ever existed.

## The idea
A vampire courier discovers her own reflection has begun confessing decades of secrets she buried to survive in daylight society.

## Dream vibe (1)
**The Undoing Light** — Dawn no longer burns the city's vampires — it undresses their reflections instead.
Art: Sunrise breaking across a rain-slick avenue, storefront windows doubling every passerby a half-second late.

## Dream location (1)
- **The Amberworks** — Rows of clockwork kilns melt vampire-harvested amber into lenses that never quite stop glowing. Local rule: No lens may leave the foundry floor before its amber fully cools. Best scene: A courier lens shatters mid-shift, and the escaping light replays everyone's reflection who ever touched it. Art: A cavernous foundry floor lit amber-orange, rows of clockwork kilns venting glowing dust, hanging lenses swaying overhead.

## Character (1)
- **Odalys Kestrel** — She has spent decades passing as human by keeping her reflection permanently averted from any polished surface. A cracked hand mirror, wrapped in oilcloth, rides in her courier satchel everywhere she works. Complication: The foundry's newest amber lens caught her reflection mid-delivery, and it has not stopped talking since. Look: A slight courier in a weatherproofed amber-brown coat, fangs barely visible, knuckles scarred from lens-glass nicks.

## Reward item (1)
- **Witness Locket** (ITEM, RARE) — Opening it in front of a stranger's own kin instantly settles who they truly are. Best used when: A person's own kin have started doubting whether they are still themselves. The catch: It only settles the doubt once; asking it to confirm the same person twice says nothing. Look: A tarnished brass locket the size of a thumbnail, its clasp worn smooth, both photograph slots left permanently empty.

## Reward skill (1)
- **Tarnished Sight** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — Staring into any reflective surface for a slow ten-count reveals whatever its owner has spent years hiding. Best used when: Someone insists a little too smoothly that they have nothing left to hide. The catch: Whatever surfaces stays visible to anyone else looking at that same reflection too. Look: A reflective surface briefly frosting over at the edges while a second, ghosted face rises beneath the glass.

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **The Empty Locket** — In The Undoing Light, Odalys Kestrel is asked to confirm a courier's true identity at The Amberworks using nothing but an empty locket and her own certainty.
