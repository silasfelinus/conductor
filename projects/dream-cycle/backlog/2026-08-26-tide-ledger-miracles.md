## The idea
Once each season, an impossible low tide exposes a glowing road across the seabed. A transformed river scout has six minutes to carry dawnfire to a cliffside commune before the sea returns, while giant sea cucumbers are the only creatures that know which parts of the road are about to vanish.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Starwake Scouts** — Transformation is a flare fired into weather, brief and blinding and gone.
Art: A pearl-armored magical scout running across mirror-wet seabed between black bone arches, luminous sea cucumbers curling along a safe path, with an immense returning wall of ocean on the horizon.

## Dream location (1)
- **The Boneglass Reach** — A seabed road that appears for six impossible minutes at the lowest tide of the season. Local rule: Follow the sea cucumbers when the horizon goes silent, or lose the path entirely. Best scene: The returning ocean rises into a vertical wall beyond the last hand-lit beacon. Art: Black demonic-bone ribs arch over mirror-bright wet sand, tide pools glow electric blue, and luminous sea cucumbers form a winding trail toward a distant cliff village.

## Character (1)
- **Tessa Nunes** — She must relight the far reef beacon before the returning sea cuts off the village for good. Carries: She carries a folding prism spear built from salvaged survey technology. Complication: Her tide-light transformation lasts only ninety seconds and attracts every deep-water hunter nearby. Look: A serious river scout in weathered field gear transformed into layered pearl-white tide armor, translucent fin-like cape panels streaming behind her, with a compact prism spear locked across her back.

## Reward item (1)
- **Nightheart Lantern** (ITEM, RARE) — It stores one burst of moonlight powerful enough to relight a dead beacon in an instant. Best used when: It works best when the coast has gone completely dark and no other light remains. The catch: Every nearby shadow keeps moving for a full minute after the light is spent. Look: A palm-sized lantern carved from glossy black demonic bone, its cage shaped from curved ribs around a cold violet flame that hangs without a wick.

## Reward skill (1)
- **River-Under-Sea** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — It lets her read hidden currents through subtle pressure changes underfoot as she runs. Best used when: It proves most useful when crossing exposed or flooded terrain with no visible trail. The catch: Living movement and rushing water feel exactly alike, making danger hard to distinguish from the tide. Look: Concentric pale-blue ripples spread from each footfall across wet sand and bend around invisible channels beneath the surface.

## Scenario (1, authored last)
