## The idea
A coastal scouting agency turns magical-girl transformations into audited fieldwork, and one scout has to solve why the upriver commune keeps filing its own miracles as clerical errors.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Low-Budget Miracles** — Every transformation comes out of somebody's paycheck.
Art: A dented enamel transformation badge half-buried in wet tidal mud, its paint chipped down to grey metal, a smear of translucent sea-cucumber slime drying across one edge, low grey coastal light.

## Dream location (1)
- **The Tide Ledger Station** — known for quietly refiling supernatural incidents as clerical errors. Local rule: no scout patrols past the bone markers alone. Best scene: the tide goes out and uncovers what the paperwork buried. Art: A low, salt-bleached wooden station raised on stilts of black demonic bone driven straight into grey tidal mudflats, ropes strung between the pilings hung with rows of drying sea cucumbers, a single oil lamp lit behind a warped window.

## Character (1)
- **Marisol Kade** — clear the station's backlog of misfiled miracle reports before the annual transformation audit. Carries a boxy retro-tech transformation compact patched together from salvaged circuitry, a thin bent antenna trailing from one hinge. Complication: her only defense is ejecting her own stored magic the way a sea cucumber ejects its organs, and it takes days to regrow. Look: a serious, weathered scout in a patched low-fantasy field vest worn over a scuffed magical-girl bodysuit, the boxy compact clipped to her belt, salt-stiffened hair tied back

## Reward item (1)
- **Compact of Borrowed Nights** (ITEM, RARE) — lets the wearer transform once without spending her own reserve. Best used when a scout has already emptied her own magic. The catch: the borrowed power is undead and it collects the debt back with interest. Look: a clamshell compact carved from polished black demonic bone, its hinge inlaid with a thin vein of dried red wax, a hairline crack running across the mirror set inside

## Reward skill (1)
- **Scout's Ledger-Sense** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — reads a place's old folk-warnings like fresh tracks. Best used when entering unmapped tidal ground. The catch: it only ever shows what to avoid, never what's safe. Look: faint chalk-white sigils bloom briefly across the ground underfoot, tracing old warning-marks in the mud before fading

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **The Commune's Miracle Audit** — In Low-Budget Miracles at The Tide Ledger Station, scout Marisol Kade discovers the upriver pastoral commune has been running stolen sci-fi salvage tech to fake their own magical-girl miracles, filing each one as a clerical error to justify seceding from the agency's tide tax.
