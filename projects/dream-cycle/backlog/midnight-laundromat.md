## The idea
An all-night laundromat on an empty corner that doesn't wash clothes — it washes
bad days. Feed a machine a rotten Tuesday and it tumbles out folded, warm, and
forgivable. The regulars are insomniacs, heartbreaks, and the occasional ghost
running one last load. Fluorescent hum, the smell of warm lint, 3 a.m. tenderness
between strangers who will never trade names.

## Location dream
**The Midnight Laundromat** — LOCATION. A humming corner laundromat that never
closes; rows of round glass doors, a change machine that only takes secrets, a
lost-and-found basket of things people were ready to let go of. Flavor: "Wash
gentle. Some days shrink." Art direction: fluorescent teal-white glow on speckled
linoleum, warm dryer-light circles, rain on the window, cozy-lonely night palette.

## Characters (2-4)
- **Marisol, the Night Attendant** — unhurried, fills the change machine with
  spare patience, keeps a chair warm for whoever needs it; has never once told
  anyone to hurry.
- **Denny the Regular** — washes the same grief every night and folds it a little
  smaller each time; swears he's almost done.
- **The Sock That Came Back** — a single argyle sock that returns to the
  lost-and-found no matter who claims it; may be waiting for a specific someone.

## Rewards (3-6)
- **Warm-Dryer Hour** (COMMON) — one hour where nothing can reach you; steam-soft and safe.
- **Fold of Forgiveness** (UNCOMMON) — press it flat and a small resentment stays flat.
- **The Change Machine's Secret** (RARE) — trade a secret for exact change to a place you can't afford.
- **Marisol's Kept Chair** (LEGENDARY) — a standing invitation; wherever you are, there is a warm seat waiting.

## Scenarios (1-2)
- **Last Load** — a ghost is running a final wash before it can move on, but the
  machine keeps stopping; the player and Marisol figure out what it isn't ready to let go.
- **The Sock's Someone** — the argyle sock won't stay claimed; the player traces
  its rounds to find the one person the laundromat has been holding it for.
