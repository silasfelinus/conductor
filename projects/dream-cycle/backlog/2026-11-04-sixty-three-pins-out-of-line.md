## The idea
On a canal-side tailor's barge the morning after a time-travelling burglary, an ancient leprechaun tailor finds that every garment in the workroom has quietly resized itself for a wearer from another year, and that sixty-three pins have been left standing in the cloth in a pattern nobody in the shop put there.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Sixty-Three Pins Out of Line** — The thieves from tomorrow have come and gone, and all they left behind is a workroom where every coat now fits somebody who has not walked in yet.
Art: A cluttered tailor's workroom on a flat barge among water-hyacinth, an orange polymer pegboard of scissors and tape, folded cotton wraps and indigo jackets heaped on stools, a dress form wearing a coat far too long for it, rows of bright pins stuck in a faded brown sleeve, fingerprints on the glass, soft grey morning light off the water.

## Dream location (1)
- **Kuttanad Fitting Barge** — A long flat-bottomed barge moored among water-hyacinth, roofed over into a crowded tailor's workroom of folded cotton, plastic bobbins, pegged paper patterns and mismatched stools. Local rule: Nothing leaves the fitting table until someone present has tried it on, because since the burglary a garment will fit only the person it is about to belong to. Best scene: A child's school jumper hangs on the line and, as the sun moves across it, slowly stretches until it would fit a stooped elder who has not yet come aboard. Art: A long flat barge among green water-hyacinth, its deck roofed with corrugated blue polymer sheeting, inside bolts of cotton and bright thread bobbins on a pegboard, a wide fitting table, a dress form in an oversized coat, a small coal brazier, a palm-leaf conical hat hanging on a nail, grey morning light on the water.

Complication: They answer questions before anyone has asked them aloud, which makes customers confess what they came in to hide and makes the one question they cannot answer, whom the garments are being resized for, all the more unsettling.
