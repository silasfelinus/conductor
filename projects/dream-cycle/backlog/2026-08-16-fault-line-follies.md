## The idea
A traveling circus stakes its big top directly over active fault lines, timing its bioengineered-cactus acts to the tectonic plates' slow, romantic collisions -- until a goblin wrangler feels the courtship reach its final beat mid-show.

## Dream vibe (1)
**The Continental Courtship** — The tectonic plates are getting married, and nobody warned the surface.
Art: A cracked desert horizon under a sky split by aurora-colored fault lines; a canvas circus tent glows amber against advancing dust while goblin silhouettes herd armored cacti along a seam of raw dragon-scale ore.

## Dream location (1)
- **The Suture Circus** — known for pitching its tents directly over active fault lines and performing in the gaps between tremors. Local rule: no show starts until the ground stops humming. Best scene: the big top's canvas rippling in sync with a distant quake while the crowd holds its breath. Art: A patchwork canvas big top staked into cracked red earth, guy-ropes strung with strands of iridescent dragon scale that hum and flex with each tremor, striped fabric lit gold from lanterns inside.

## Character (1)
- **Vex Thistlemaw** — keep her cactus herd calm through the quakes so the circus can perform. Carries a coil of dragon-scale rope worn smooth at the grip. Complication: her herd is bred from the last wild cactus line, and every hardened prod she uses risks becoming their apocalypse. Look: a squat, charismatic goblin in a patched leather vest studded with cactus spines, dragon-scale rope coiled over one shoulder, dust-caked boots, a gap-toothed grin

## Reward item (1)
- **Wrangler's Prod** (ITEM, RARE) — calms panicked bioengineered creatures by broadcasting a decommissioned first-contact greeting through a hidden speaker -- wildly wrong tone, herd-soothing anyway. Best used when a tremor spooks the herd right before showtime. The catch: overuse hardens the prod's tip into inert dragon scale and the speaker garbles into silence for good. Look: a forearm-length rod of bone-white cactus wood capped in a dragon-scale ferrule worn mirror-bright at the grip, a cracked speaker grille set into the haft, faint spiral grooves worked down the shaft
