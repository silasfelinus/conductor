## The idea
An all-ages coloring book of impossible garden-machines: contraptions where
horticulture and clockwork have grown into each other. A dandelion dirigible, a
watering-can locomotive trailing morning glories, a topiary automaton clipping
itself, a seed-drill that plants tiny brass suns. Whimsical, hopeful, and packed
with the kind of dense, clearly-bounded shapes that reward careful coloring. Clears
the coloring-book originality guardrails: every machine is an original invention,
no branded or protected designs.

## Book shape
Target ~24 solo/scene pages + 2-3 group "garden panorama" pages + a full-color
cover, modeled on projects/coloring-book/sets/monster-recast/README.md's
solo/group/cover structure. Rank the invention pool after rough studies; may grow.
All-ages.

## Cast / subjects
- **The Dandelion Dirigible** — a seed-head balloon with a woven-root gondola.
- **The Watering-Can Locomotive** — a spouted engine laying track of sprouting vines.
- **The Topiary Automaton** — a hedge-shaped robot forever pruning itself into new animals.
- **The Brass Seed-Drill** — plants rows of tiny wind-up suns that bloom at dawn.
- **The Greenhouse Walker** — a glass conservatory on insect legs, migrating for the light.
- **The Pollinator Choir** — clockwork bees with music-box wings.
- **The Root-Cellar Submarine** — a copper vessel that tunnels through soil like water.
- **The Compost Kiln-Beast** — a gentle furnace-creature that turns scraps into flowers.
- (enough invention seeds to fill the page target; expand in the home set)

## Style direction
Thick confident black contours, flat bounded color in the colored master, then a
faithful B/W conversion — the paired-master workflow adapted from Monster Recast's
STYLE-GUIDE but with a bright, sunlit botanical palette and none of the horror
texture. Many enclosed shapes (gears, petals, rivets, leaves) that stay fun to
color; readable silhouette at thumbnail size.
