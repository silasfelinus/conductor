## New Facets (invented for this dream)
- **Shared Authorship** (`shared-authorship`, ALIGNMENT) — A refusal to be named as the sole author of anything, even work done entirely alone. Credit is distributed on principle, and the principle sometimes protects people who don't deserve it.
- **Sun-Bleached Salvage** (`sun-bleached-salvage`, STYLE) — Nothing here hides its repairs. Every surface is patched in whatever material was on hand, faded unevenly by years of open sun, and repair work is worn as proof rather than hidden as failure.

## The idea
A boatyard on a delta towpath repairs everything in visible patches instead of hiding the damage, and when a pirate skiff runs itself aground trying to steal a barge, the yard's answer is to patch the wreck into the fleet instead of scrapping it.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Towpath Bloom** — The delta doesn't flood on the old calendar anymore, and the plants along the towpath have opinions about the new one.
Art: A wide river delta towpath under hard daylight, mismatched patched boats moored along mudbanks, ordinary real materials -- no glow, no haze.

## Dream location (1)
- **Shadewood Landing** — A patched boatyard on the delta's towpath where salvaged river barges are rebuilt hull-first from shadewood scrap, every repair left visible instead of sanded smooth. Local rule: No repair gets one name painted on it; whoever touched a hull all sign the same patch. Best scene: A pirate skiff runs aground trying to steal a moored barge at low tide, and half the boatyard turns out to help drag it off the mud before arguing about whether to report it. Art: Mismatched patched hull planks in hard daylight, tar and raw shadewood grain visible at every seam, no haze, no glow.

## Character (1)
- **Rill Varga** — Rill rebuilds barge hulls at the Shadewood Landing yard, and refuses to let any repair go out signed with just her own name, no matter how much of it she did alone. A Baltic caulking iron with a Mekong-style woven rattan grip lashed on where the original handle split. Complication: She left a river cult that demanded every finished work be credited to the cult's single Voice; three years out, she still can't sign anything with just her own initials. Look: A young, fox-featured creature, lean and quick-eared, junior in title only -- they move through the yard with a curved, deliberate economy like someone who has already run it for years. Their ruff is dyed a flat, matte violet no fox ever grew naturally. They wear a tar-stained work wrap pinned with a Baltic timber-guild clasp over Mekong-style rolled trouser cuffs, features kept deliberately neutral rather than styled toward either usual pole. Default expression: a flat, unimpressed stare, mouth already half open on a correction nobody asked for.

## Reward item (1)
- **Spite Paddle** (ITEM, COMMON) — Rows any boat in a dead straight line regardless of current, wind, or how badly it's being steered, purely out of spite. Best used when: Someone insists a boat can't be rowed there and you'd very much like to prove them wrong. The catch: It only works while someone is actively doubting you; row it alone with nobody watching and it wanders like any ordinary oar. Look: A single sun-bleached shadewood oar, blade patched with three mismatched wood scraps bolted flush, handle worn smooth and pale from gripping.

## Reward skill (1)
- **Root Rebuttal** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — Gets a blunt, one-line opinion out of any rooted plant or waterlogged wood, whether anyone wants to hear it or not. Best used when: Everyone nearby is confidently wrong about something growing close enough to answer back. The catch: The opinion is always rude, frequently correct, and never something you can un-hear. Look: A tangle of exposed root hairs stiffening upright and quivering along a waterlogged plank the moment it 'answers back.'

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **Low Tide Standoff** — In Towpath Bloom at Shadewood Landing, a pirate skiff runs itself aground trying to steal a barge mid-repair, and Rill Varga has to talk the whole yard into patching the wreck sun-bleached-salvage style instead of scrapping it, sharing the credit the way every hull here already gets signed.
