## New Facets (invented for this dream)

- **Kindly Scavenger** (`kindly-scavenger`, ALIGNMENT) — Someone who helps everyone they meet warmly and honestly, and who also takes whatever is left unclaimed behind them. Nobody is ever robbed, yet nobody ever finds quite what they put down.

## The idea
A moor of glowing stone where a choir of hollow singers keeps a harvest-rite going, and a sky of ash-storm and pale fog decides, hour by hour, which voices are still alive.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Glowmoor** — The whole moor sings with the weather, and the weather has started singing back in the wrong key.
Art: A wide upland moor of black heather studded with knee-high glowstone boulders giving off a warm amber light, a slow wall of ash-grey fog rolling across it, tall hollow-bodied singers standing in a ring with open throats, scarecrows of bone and straw leaning in the wind, sheets of pale rain slanting through the light, dusk sky bruised violet.

## Dream location (1)
- **Embertarn** — A bowl of moorland ringed by glowstone boulders where a harvest rite is sung every autumn, and where the fog bank that rolls in each evening takes the shape of whoever sang last. Local rule: Nobody may speak above a whisper once the fog is knee-deep, because every raised voice is answered by the fog in the same voice. Best scene: The fog climbs the glowstone ring and the whole moor lights up from underneath, each boulder humming one note of a chord nobody sang. Art: A round bowl of black heather moorland ringed with knee-high amber glowstone boulders, a thick grey fog bank climbing the inner slope in slow curls, wind-bent hawthorn, straw figures on poles, thin pollen-gold haze in the air, warm light under cold violet dusk.

## Character (1)
- **Verity Aldecott** — They run pollen between the scattered moor farms through ash-storms, because the glowstone fields yield a crop after fresh pollen arrives, and no other runner will cross the choir's ground. A lidded clay pollen jar the size of a loaf rides high on the back in a harness of Oaxacan woven palm and Lisbon-tiled cord, warm to the touch and faintly glowing at the seams. Complication: The jar's pollen wakes the dead plants it touches, so every farm they save grows a few stems that have no business standing, and some of those stems have begun to follow them home. Look: A tall heavy hollow-bodied singer, a long hollow torso of weathered pale reed-wood with an open resonant throat and slow low breath whistling through knotholes, aged-out-of-order grey bark patched with fresh green shoots at the shoulders, salt-and-pepper moss hanging unevenly from the crown, wrapped in a woven-palm poncho dyed cochineal red and tied with blue-and-white tile-pattern cord, a clay jar harness across the chest, a calm half-smile in the open throat as if just about to hum, amber glowstone light on one side, fog behind.

## Reward item (1)
- **Glowstone Gleaner** (ITEM, RARE) — A palm-sized lantern of amber glowstone that lights only what has been left behind, revealing every dropped, lost or forgotten object within a long stone's throw. Best used when: A storm has scattered a caravan's goods across open ground and there is no time to search. The catch: It lights the wearer's own pockets too, and any item in them that was never theirs glows brightest. Look: A palm-sized lantern of amber glowstone in a cage of blackened iron wire, a worn leather loop for the thumb, a warm glow strongest at the cracks, scuffed from being dropped often, a few straws caught in the wire.

## Reward skill (1)
- **Sing the Fog Shut** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — Standing in thick fog and singing one low sustained note draws the fog around you into a still wall that that no one can walk through. Best used when: Something is following a group across open moor and they need a hiding place that does not exist. The catch: The wall holds only as long as the note does, and the singer cannot breathe in without dropping it. Look: A figure standing with open mouth and lifted chin in a drift of grey fog, the fog curling into a smooth vertical wall around them, soft ripples spreading outward from the open throat like rings on a pond, amber light glowing through the mist.
