## New Facets (invented for this dream)
- **Batch-Made** (`batch-made`, BACKSTORY) — A backstory of having been built or born as one of a large identical batch, so individuality is something claimed afterward rather than given at the start. Somewhere, right now, another one of you is making a completely different choice.
- **Unclaimed Freight Handler** (`unclaimed-freight-handler`, OCCUPATION) — A job of receiving whatever arrives without a matching order, invoice, or sender, and deciding on the spot whether it's cargo, a guest, or a problem. Nobody trains for this; everyone who does it long enough develops their own rules.

## The idea
A whole batch of newly finished living dolls launches into daylight at once from a solar-glass dome, and the neighboring town mistakes the celebration for a full-scale alien invasion.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Three Hundred Dolls Launch at Once** — Every one of them looks exactly like an invasion, and every one of them is just excited.
Art: A sky filled with faceted, low-poly-jointed figures rising all at once against bright blue daylight, sunlight glinting off hundreds of identical glass-paneled bodies.

## Dream location (1)
- **Sunlight Floods the Launch Yard** — A fairground-sized solar-glass dome where a whole batch of newly finished Living Dolls charges together in the sun before their first launch, one identical bright-eyed face after another under the glass. Local rule: No doll may launch before every other doll from its own batch is charged and standing; nobody leaves alone. Best scene: A late straggler from the batch scrambles onto the charging platform just as the launch horn sounds, and the entire line turns to cheer instead of complaining. Art: A vast geodesic dome of gold-tinted solar glass, panels catching full daylight, hundreds of identical doll-shaped figures standing shoulder to shoulder on a wide platform beneath it, banners strung between the dome's ribs, the whole scene sun-bright and crowded with motion.

## Character (1)
- **Ayla-92** — Fresh out of the charging line and instantly promoted to Launch-Day Floor Manager because they're annoyingly good at reading the charge gauges — a job three grades above what any doll their age should have, and they know it. A dented brass whistle on a woven cord tied in an exact Baltic timber-port rigging knot, wound by the doll-maker before Ayla-92 was even switched on. Complication: That knot pattern only ever comes from finished dolls, and Ayla-92's own batch number puts them three dolls short of the count that's supposed to exist. Look: A doll built long and spare, joints faceted like cut glass rather than rounded, hair a flat, impossible violet that shifts blue at the edges depending on the light. Skin the dull matte of unpolished low-poly resin, seams visible at every joint like a kit not quite sanded smooth. A fitted work coverall, plain and unfitted to any one shape. A wide, delighted, gap-toothed grin, permanently a little too big for the moment.

## Reward item (1)
- **Sunstamp Seal** (ITEM, RARE) — Pressed onto anything — a crate, a doll, a stranger — it declares the object formally received, at which point every rulebook in the building treats it as accounted for, wanted or not. Best used when: Something has arrived with no paperwork, no sender, and no category anyone's willing to argue about out loud. The catch: It only works once per item, and once stamped, a thing can never be sent back, refused, or un-received — only reassigned to someone else's dock. Look: A palm-sized glass block the warm gold of sun-heated solar panel, one face etched with a sunburst seal, warm to the touch even in shade.

## Reward skill (1)
- **Faceted Reading** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — The user can pick out one specific individual from an identical crowd, batch, or swarm at a glance, by the way light catches their particular facets differently from everyone else's. Best used when: A crowd, batch, or fleet that's supposed to be interchangeable actually isn't, and it matters which one is which. The catch: It only works face-to-face, in direct light; a photograph, a list, or a description is just as anonymous as it was before. Look: Their eyes track very slightly ahead of their head, like they're already following the one true angle of reflection off a single face in a crowd of matching ones.

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **Neighboring Town Mobilizes Against the Launch** — During Three Hundred Dolls Launch at Once, the solar-glass dome at Sunlight Floods the Launch Yard finally reaches full charge — and the neighboring hill town, watching three hundred identical glass-jointed figures rise into daylight at once, mobilizes its entire brass-plated volunteer defense company under the honest belief they're being invaded. Ayla-92, freshly promoted Launch-Day Floor Manager, cannot talk down an armed town alone; it's the batch itself — all three hundred dolls, still mid-launch — who settle it, breaking formation to spell out a greeting banner in midair with their own bodies before anyone below fires a shot. What's left afterward is an unmarked cart of confiscated defense-company gear sitting on Ayla-92's dock with no paperwork attached, which is technically now their problem as the yard's Unclaimed Freight Handler.
