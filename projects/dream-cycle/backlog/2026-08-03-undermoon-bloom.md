## The idea
The moon has drifted close enough to seed the world every night, and the last orc settlement survives by wrangling drones through the bloom.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Undermoon Bloom** — The moon hangs low enough to touch, and every night it seeds the world with spores.
Art: A colossal, close moon dripping luminous fungal threads over a broken skyline.

## Dream location (1)
- **The Sporeline Reclamation Yard** — known for salvaging drone parts out of the fungal wastes before the nightly bloom. Local rule: never touch a bloom that pulses in time with your own heartbeat. Best scene: a drone comes home coated in a spore pattern shaped like a face. Art: heavensteel scaffolding overgrown with glowing fungal lattice under a moon that fills half the sky

## Character (1)
- **Grudge Fenrik** — keep the reclamation yard's drone fleet flying and the settlement fed. Carries a cracked heavensteel whistle that calls the drones home. Complication: the whistle also calls the spores. Look: a broad-shouldered orc in a patched jumpsuit, sharp deadpan grin, moon-glow caught in one tusk

## Reward item (1)
- **Heavensteel Spore Jar** (ITEM, RARE) — a sealed jar that stores one breath of bloom-air, safe to study up close. Best used when you need proof of the bloom without exposure to it. The catch: shake it, and it remembers what it heard

## Reward skill (1)
- **Mythroot Whisper** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — speak the old ancestor-names that calm a spreading bloom. Best used when a spore-field is about to bolt past the reclamation line. The catch: the bloom answers back, in your grandmother's voice

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **The Night the Moon Leaned In** — In Undermoon Bloom at The Sporeline Reclamation Yard, Grudge Fenrik races the last drone home before the too-close moon's nightly bloom swallows the salvage route -- an existential swashbuckling dash across a post-apocalyptic wasteland gone quiet with the hush of an ordinary evening chore, where losing means the whole yard goes dark for good.
