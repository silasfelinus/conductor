## The idea
The moon has drifted close enough to seed the world every night, and the last orc settlement survives by wrangling drones through the bloom.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Undermoon Bloom** — The moon hangs low enough to touch, and every night it seeds the world with spores.
Art: A colossal, close moon dripping luminous fungal threads over a broken skyline.

## Dream location (1)
- **The Sporeline Reclamation Yard** — Salvagers pick drone parts out of the fungal wastes here, racing to beat the nightly bloom before it swallows the route. Local rule: Never touch a bloom that pulses in time with your own heartbeat. Best scene: A drone comes home coated in a spore pattern shaped like a face, and for a moment nobody in the yard says a word. Art: heavensteel scaffolding overgrown with glowing fungal lattice under a moon that fills half the sky

## Character (1)
- **Grudge Fenrik** — Grudge Fenrik keeps the reclamation yard's drone fleet flying and the settlement fed. He carries a cracked heavensteel whistle that calls the drones home. Complication: Unfortunately, the same whistle that calls the drones home also calls the spores, so every rescue risks inviting the bloom straight to his door. Look: a broad-shouldered orc in a patched jumpsuit, sharp deadpan grin, moon-glow caught in one tusk

## Reward item (1)
- **Heavensteel Spore Jar** (ITEM, RARE) — It grants a sealed jar that stores one breath of bloom-air, safe to study up close. Best used when: Study the bloom up close without ever stepping into the spores yourself. The catch: Shake it, and it remembers what it heard. Look: a squat heavensteel canister with a bolted glass window, a slow drift of luminous green spores turning inside, condensation beading along the seal

## Reward skill (1)
- **Mythroot Whisper** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — It grants the ability to speak the old ancestor-names that calm a spreading bloom. Best used when: It works best when a spore-field is about to bolt past the reclamation line. The catch: The bloom answers back, in your grandmother's voice. Look: a spreading fungal lattice pulling back on itself, its glow cooling from hot green to soft silver where breath passes over it, faint concentric rings hanging in the air

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **The Night the Moon Leaned In** — In Undermoon Bloom at The Sporeline Reclamation Yard, Grudge Fenrik races the last drone home before the too-close moon's nightly bloom swallows the salvage route — an existential swashbuckling dash across a post-apocalyptic wasteland gone quiet with the hush of an ordinary evening chore, where losing means the whole yard goes dark for good.
