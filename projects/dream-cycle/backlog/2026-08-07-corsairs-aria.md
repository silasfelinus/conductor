## The idea
Space pirates raid an opera house sunk into a singing nebula, hunting the ghost-friend who can finish the aria that ends fleets.

## Dream vibe (1)
**The Aria That Sank a Fleet** — Every note sung here bends the tide of stars.
Art: A coral-glass opera house adrift in a bioluminescent nebula-sea, pirate skiffs circling like sharks in the current.

## Dream location (1)
- **The Drowned Overture** — The last note ever sung here silenced an entire fleet, and the coral walls have not forgotten its shape. Local rule: No performance is allowed to finish the final aria, and every conductor bold enough to attempt it has vanished before the last measure, leaving only the coral walls to remember the notes they never got to play. Best scene: A translucent figure, long rumored to haunt the opera house's ruined stage, hums the missing note back into the hall, and the chandeliers shiver with chrono-dust as the sea of stars leans in to listen. Art: a gothic-horror coral opera house half-submerged in glowing nebula tide, lit by drifting chrono-dust chandeliers

## Character (1)
- **Thess Marrow** — She is driven to recover her mother's stolen voice, torn away and trapped somewhere inside the opera's haunted acoustics. A jar of singing cactus spines rides at her belt, each one humming faintly with a note it refuses to let go of. Complication: The imaginary friend she invented as a lonely child turns out to be real, and it is his voice, not her mother's, that answers when she calls into the haunted acoustics. Look: a noir privateer in a thorn-stitched coat with a coral cutlass at her hip

## Reward item (1)
- **The Corsair's Encore** (ITEM, RARE) — It replays any sound it has captured exactly once, rendering the moment back with perfect, uncanny fidelity. Best used when: A vital clue was spoken aloud only once and can never be asked for again. The catch: The instrument exacts a price, stealing away the very next sound its user makes after the replay ends. Look: a spiral coral horn banded in tarnished silver, its throat packed with glowing chrono-dust, one captured waveform hanging frozen in its mouth

## Reward skill (1)
- **Thorn-Tide Boarding** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — It lets a crew slip silently through fields of singing coral, leaving the resonant thorns undisturbed and mute. Best used when: A raid depends on total silence, with no note allowed to betray the approach. The catch: The coral itself keeps score, memorizing every footstep and singing the pattern back to any listener much later. Look: a field of thorned singing coral parting around an invisible passage, glowing resonance rings blooming from the tips left untouched, a single boarding-line drawn taut through the gap
