## The idea
A ghostwriter in a mythic African-cyberpunk city trades finished endings for other people's unpaid debts, guided by a chinchilla familiar and a market that never forgets an unfinished story.

## Dream vibe (1)
**The Chittering Archive** — Every story you finish becomes a debt someone else must pay.
Art: A rain-slicked megacity market stall glowing with bioluminescent ink-vines, chinchilla-fur talismans strung from the awnings, neon sigils in Adinkra-inspired patterns reflected in wet cobblestone.

## Dream location (1)
- **The Undermarket of Nine Debts** — known for trading unfinished stories. Local rule: no manuscript leaves unsigned. Best scene: a merchant's chinchilla familiar unravels a silk ledger that rewrites itself as you read. Art: A subterranean bazaar strung with radiant gold silk banners, stalls carved from fossilized loom-wood, chinchilla-fur charms dangling from every awning, ankle-high mist lit by low copper lanterns.

## Character (1)
- **Yewande Okoro** — finish other people's stories before the Undermarket collects what's owed. Carries a cybernetic quill spliced from a chinchilla whisker and copper wire. Complication: every ending she writes for someone else erases one of her own memories. Look: a warmly weathered woman in a patched synth-leather coat lined with silver chinchilla fur, one brass optic glowing dim blue over her eye, ink-stained fingers

## Reward item (1)
- **The Unfinished Ledger** (ITEM, RARE) — reveals the one sentence someone left unwritten in their life. Best used when a choice has stalled and no one will name it. The catch: writing the missing sentence for them makes it true, whether they wanted it or not. Look: a slim book bound in radiant gold silk, its pages half-blank, a single loose thread trailing from the spine like a lit fuse

## Reward skill (1)
- **Debt-Binding Prose** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — writes a promise into being, sealing it into whoever hears it read aloud. Best used when a spoken deal needs to survive betrayal. The catch: the words bind the speaker as tightly as the listener. Look: looping brass-bright glyphs crawling up the speaker's forearm like living calligraphy while they read

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **The Ninth Debt** — In The Chittering Archive, deep in The Undermarket of Nine Debts, Yewande Okoro is handed a ledger with only one blank page left, and the client wants the ending written before the mist rises to collect.
