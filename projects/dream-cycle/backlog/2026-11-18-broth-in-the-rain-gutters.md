## The idea
Last season a crew cracked the heavensteel larder where the city's crime syndicate had locked away every good thing to eat, and by morning the rain gutters were running with broth. That was the climax and it is over, so the bosses are washing dishes, the detectives have no case, and everyone in town is full for the first time in their lives. What remains is a noir city of honest appetites, where kiwi birds sniff out what people truly crave and nobody is quite sure what to want next.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Broth in the Rain Gutters** — A rain-slick noir city is still digesting the heist that emptied its crime syndicate's larder into every kitchen, and its kiwi birds can smell exactly what each well-fed citizen is hungry for now.
