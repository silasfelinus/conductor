## The idea
Every ship stranded past the outer reef gets towed home by the one pilot whose arrival always drags a fresh storm in behind him.

## Dream vibe (1)
**The Slow Squall** — A single fraying towline outruns the storm it wakes every time.
Art: A barrel-bodied manatee pilot hauling a battered tugboat through a drifting reef of glowing void-crystal shards, lightning forking silently overhead.

## Dream location (1)
- **The Drift Reef** — Ships that stop moving here get folded gently into the reef's own drifting crystal formations. Local rule: No engine may run past the reef's outer shard ring after dark. Best scene: A grounded freighter drifts free at last as the crystal beneath it hums itself back to sleep. Art: A sprawling reef of jagged void-crystal spires jutting from black water, wrecked hulls half-swallowed between the glowing shards.

## Character (1)
- **Denner Cole** — He tows every stranded hull back to safety alone, refusing all offers of a second engine. A cracked brass compass, its needle frozen mid-spin, hangs from a cord around his neck. Complication: Every storm he calls to clear a channel also wakes something sleeping in the reef's underground caverns. Look: A broad, barrel-bodied figure with slate-grey manatee hide, a patched diving coat slung over one shoulder, knuckles scarred from towline burns.

## Reward item (1)
- **Driftline Anchor** (ITEM, RARE) — A taut length of glowing cable locks onto the nearest drifting hull the instant it is thrown. Best used when: A stranded hull has already drifted past the reach of every other line aboard. The catch: Once thrown, the cable will not release until the hull it caught reaches open water. Look: A coiled length of void-crystal cable veined with faint violet light, one end fused into a barbed anchor head streaked with rust.

## Reward skill (1)
- **Squall Call** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — A short, localized downpour gathers directly overhead within seconds of being summoned. Best used when: A grounded hull needs the tide raised just enough to float free again. The catch: The rain follows old grudges too, and old enemies tend to be standing nearby. Look: A tight grey cyclone spinning above an open palm, rain already darkening the sleeve beneath it.
