## The idea
A marriage between a mountain town and an armoured saurian clan has to be priced, witnessed and agreed in one night — and in this valley the dead have standing to object.

## Dream vibe (1)
**The Dowry of Stones** — The dead get a say in every marriage here, and they always come.
Art: A long stone stair climbing a mountainside at night, dusted with glittering pale powder, silent grey figures standing shoulder to shoulder on every step, lantern light climbing between them.

## Dream location (1)
- **The Bride's Long Stair** — This is the wedding stair the dead line shoulder to shoulder from bottom to top, silent witnesses to every marriage that climbs past them. Local rule: Never thank one of the grey figures by name, or you invite it to speak again and claim a stake in the marriage. Best scene: The whole stair turns its head at once toward a single spoken objection, a thousand grey faces swiveling in perfect unison to hear who was named. Art: A thousand worn stone steps rising into cold mist, drifts of glittering starlight powder banked against the risers, grey unmoving figures on either side, one warm lantern halfway up.

## Character (1)
- **Ilkay Demirci** — He has to get the dowry agreed and the couple up the stair before dawn. A folding brass scale hangs at his wrist, a bag of counting stones worn smooth rides one pocket, and the night's whole accounting stays kept entirely in his head. Complication: He keeps bending the terms to make the night work, and the dead are literalists. Look: A broad ankylosaur in a good coat cut to fit over armoured back plates, club tail bound in ceremonial cord, a brass scale hanging from one wrist, reading lenses perched on a blunt snout.

## Reward item (1)
- **Sieve of Small Grievances** (ITEM, RARE) — It separates a real objection from a petty one. Best used when: A room is full of people who all feel wronged. The catch: Petty grievances are not thereby made to go away. Look: A shallow brass sieve worn bright at the rim, caked in glittering pale powder, its mesh sagging in the middle from long use.

## Reward skill (1)
- **Standing to Object** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — It gives a silent party the floor for as long as it takes. Best used when: The loudest person in the room is not the injured one. The catch: It cannot be revoked once the floor is given. Look: A cold blue seam of light opening down the middle of a stone floor and widening into a clear standing gap, dust drawn back from both its edges.

## Scenario (1, authored last)
