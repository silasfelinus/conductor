## The idea
Deep beneath a quiet seaside town sits a library that doesn't catalog books — it catalogs marrow, the condensed last-feeling of things that are dying or already gone. Donors surrender a memory in the courtyard above, and it settles into the stacks below as a jar of warm, faintly glowing residue anyone can borrow and feel. You come here to remember something you never lived, or to finally let go of something you did.

## Vibe / genre dream
**Elegiac Wonder** — GENRE. grief, catalogued and kept warm.

- **The Marrow Stacks** — known for shelf after shelf of sealed jars, each one holding a single borrowed feeling Local rule: you may only check out a marrow-jar if you leave one of your own feelings as collateral, returned unopened when the jar comes back Best scene: a visitor cracking open a jar labeled 'first snow, 1962' and staying very still while it plays through them Art: a cavernous underground archive, towering wooden shelves lit by hanging jars that glow faint amber and blue, dust motes drifting in shafts of light from grates far above, warm and reverent like a cathedral built for feelings

## Characters (3)
- **Undersecretary Wrenna Vail** — the library's head archivist, who believes every feeling deserves to be kept rather than wasted Carries a ring of brass keys, each one etched with a donor's initials Complication: she has never once surrendered a memory of her own, and the stacks are starting to notice Look: tall, silver-streaked hair pinned with a cataloging needle, a long coat lined with small labeled pockets
- **Corvin Pell** — a grief-broker who sells guided access to the stacks for a price, and hates himself a little for it Carries a battered ledger of every jar he's ever brokered, and who cried over what Complication: he's quietly saving to buy back the one memory he sold when he was young and desperate Look: rumpled velvet vest, ink-stained fingers, a smile that never quite reaches his eyes
- **Iso** — a silverfish-quick courier who ferries fresh marrow down from the Molting Yard before it spoils Carries a satchel lined with cool wet moss to keep the jars from warming too fast Complication: is faster and stranger than anything else in the library, and privately fears they've absorbed pieces of memories they were only supposed to carry Look: wiry, silver-grey skin with a faint iridescent sheen, quick darting eyes, always slightly damp

## Rewards (2 — one skill, one item)
- **Marrow-Reading** (SKILL, RARE) — the ability to hold a sealed jar and read the shape of the feeling inside without opening it Best used when you need to know what a memory costs before you agree to borrow it The catch: read too many jars in one sitting and their feelings start to blur into your own. Look: two bare hands cupped around a sealed brass-lidded jar, the glass fogged from the inside, a soft amber shape pressing against it as though breathing
- **Molt-Jar** (ITEM, UNCOMMON) — an empty brass-lidded jar that can catch and preserve one memory before it fades for good Best used when you're about to lose someone or something and want to keep one true feeling intact The catch: once sealed, the jar can never be reopened by the person who filled it — only by someone else. Look: an empty wide-mouthed glass jar with a screwed brass lid and a blank paper label, moss greening the outside of the glass, dusk light bending through its curve

## Scenarios (1-2)
- **The Debt Iso Can't Repay** — Iso begs Wrenna to let them check out a jar using someone else's collateral, convinced a piece of a memory they carried down from the Yard got stuck to them on the way — and they need to know whose it was before it changes them further.
- **The One Corvin Wants Back** — Corvin finally has enough saved to buy back the memory he sold as a young man, but the only way in is to convince Wrenna to break her own rule about collateral for the first time.
