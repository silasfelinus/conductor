## The idea
In the kingdom of Verdane, royal legitimacy has been decided for three centuries by a single silver scar: whoever a unicorn touches during the ritual Scarring Season is fit to sit the throne. Dr. Amara Voss, the court's alien biologist -- fallen from the sky and quietly kept on as royal physician -- has spent a decade trying to prove the "blessing" is a biological process she can name, and the spring ball may be her last chance to say so before the crown prince's own scar finishes failing in front of the whole court.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Cinderhorn Court** — The next king is chosen by a wound the whole court can see healing wrong.
Art: A vaulted stone throne hall lit by hanging iron braziers, silver processional banners stitched with unicorn silhouettes, a bare-shouldered young heir kneeling on a raised dais with a fresh silver-white scar arcing across his collarbone, and beyond an open archway a forge chamber glowing molten orange where smiths hammer engagement rings.

## Dream location (1)
- **The Cinderhorn Forge-Chapel** — known for sealing courtship vows in filings of molten iron mixed with ash swept from old unicorn bones. Local rule: no vow is sealed here unless one partner already bears a scar or swears before witnesses to earn one. Best scene: a queue of paired couples waiting their turn at the quenching trough while the head smith reads their joined names off a scar-ledger before tipping the glowing filings in. Art: A low stone chapel built around an open forge pit, its molten core glowing orange-white beneath a black iron grate, walls hung with hundreds of small twisted rings on hooks, a stone quenching trough streaked rust-red and silver, couples in formal court dress standing in a candlelit line waiting their turn.

## Character (1)
- **Dr. Amara Voss** — prove, before the crown prince's scar finishes failing in public, that the unicorn's touch is a biological process she can name rather than a blessing the throne depends on. Carries a physician's leather roll of apothecary tools that are actually diagnostic instruments, and a logbook of scar measurements written in two alphabets. Complication: her own borrowed body reads the unicorn's field unpredictably -- some nights her instruments spike for no one, other nights they stay silent around a scar that should register, and she can't yet tell which readings to trust. Look: A tall, precise woman in a court physician's grey coat, her hands faintly too long at the knuckle, pale eyes with a fine star-flecked ring around each pupil kept hidden behind wire-rimmed lenses, a leather instrument roll slung across one shoulder.

## Reward item (1)
- **The Bog-Iron Signet** (ITEM, RARE) — marks whoever wears it as already-vowed, letting them pass any courtship or bloodline rite in Cinderhorn Court without being scarred first. Best used when a suitor needs the court's trust before they've earned, or survived, an actual unicorn's touch. The catch: the signet only holds the lie for one full moon; after that it tarnishes black and the wearer's original unscarred hand is exposed to everyone watching. Look: A thick iron ring pulled dark and pitted like bog-iron, its face inlaid with a thin vein of still-glowing molten metal that never quite cools, warm enough to fog cold glass.

## Reward skill (1)
- **Xenoscan Diagnosis** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — reads the true biological state of whatever the caster's hand rests on -- scar, wound, or lie -- independent of what it's dressed up to look like. Best used when someone's claimed scar or bloodline needs confirming and a courtier's word alone can't be trusted. The catch: the reading only works through skin-to-skin contact held for a slow ten-count, which is exactly long enough for a room full of court to notice. Look: A faint grid of pale blue light traces itself just beneath the skin of whatever is being read, pulsing twice before fading, leaving no mark once it's gone.

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **The Ball Before the Scar Fails** — At Cinderhorn Court's spring ball, with the crown prince's unicorn-scar visibly dimming under the chandelier light, Dr. Amara Voss slips away from the dance floor to The Cinderhorn Forge-Chapel to run one last Xenoscan Diagnosis before midnight, only to find a line of hopeful suitors already queued at the quenching trough, each one betting on whether he'll finish the night still fit to rule.
