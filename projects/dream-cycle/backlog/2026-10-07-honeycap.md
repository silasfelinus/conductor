## The idea
A midsummer fair blooms with a gentle fungus that crowns whoever the crowd applauds, and no one can say whether it chooses them or the cheering does.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Honeycap** — A fair so loved that the mushrooms came to the party.
Art: Bold noon light on a packed fairground, oversized banners, trestle tables heaped with pies and plums, honey-gold mushroom caps blooming through the bunting, a wheeled chair carried above the crowd.

## Dream location (1)
- **Applause Field** — A fairground of ghost-iron tent poles and long feast tables where a honey-coloured fungus blooms overnight along every rope and rail. Local rule: The fair chooses its mayor by applause, and the one cheered loudest must wear the living fungus crown until the next bloom. Best scene: Hundreds cheer under the noon bunting as the crown's caps open slowly and turn toward whoever is speaking. Art: A wide sunlit meadow of striped awnings, pale ghost-iron poles beaded with dew, heaped fruit stalls, honey-gold fungus swelling along ropes and bunting, a dense cheering crowd.

## Character (1)
- **Ysolde Quispe-Marek** — They roll through the fair each morning reading every stall aloud to its empty chairs, certain a session is about to be called to order. A battered gavel clipped to a wheelchair arm and a tin of smoked whitefish they share with a goblin rival. Complication: The crowd keeps trying to crown them, and they have refused so long that the fungus has started to grow on the chair instead. Look: An impossibly old person with long hair white since youth, in a wheelchair with a bright woven aguayo cushion, a patterned wool chullo on their head, a paper party hat clipped over it, a gavel clipped to the armrest, wearing a wide stubborn grin.

## Reward item (1)
- **Choir Ladle** (ITEM, UNCOMMON) — It hums a different chord for every ingredient stirred with it. Best used when: A pot is being shared among people who have not yet agreed on anything. The catch: The chord stays in whoever tastes it and hums for a full day. Look: A long pale ghost-iron ladle, faintly see-through at the bowl, a grey heron feather knotted at the handle, steam rising off a bright stew.

## Reward skill (1)
- **Raise the Banner** (SKILL, RARE) — It holds a crowd still long enough to hear one sentence. Best used when: The noise of a festival has grown too loud for anyone to say what matters. The catch: Everyone remembers the sentence exactly as spoken, mistakes included. Look: Two arms hoisting a huge saffron banner overhead, midday light glowing through the cloth, hundreds of upturned faces below it.
