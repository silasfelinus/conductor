## The idea
A garden that grows in the gaps between radio stations. Antenna trellises, flowers
that bloom when tuned just right, hedges of white noise. Somewhere between an
abandoned broadcast tower and a greenhouse, tended by machines that treat frequency
like sunlight. Retro-futurist calm; the loudest thing here is the hum.

## Location dream
**The Static Garden** — LOCATION. Overgrown broadcast station, dials for irrigation
valves, dish antennas full of rainwater and lilies. Flavor: "Please do not prune
between 88.1 and 91.5 — something is nesting." Art direction: 1970s NASA-brochure
palette, cream and orange against green, soft CRT glow.

## Vibe / genre dream
**Analog Reverie** — GENRE (create; check for an existing retro-futurist/solarpunk
GENRE first). Unhurried wonder, machines as caretakers, nostalgia for futures that

## Characters (2-4)
- **Gardener Unit 7 ("Sevens")** — a patched-up horticulture robot who tunes each
  plant daily and hums along with the carrier wave.
- **DJ Nightjar** — a reclusive bird-woman who broadcasts a show nobody remembers
  subscribing to; her setlist changes what blooms.
- **The Signal Cat** — appears only on clear frequencies; may be a cat-shaped hole
  in the static.

## Rewards (3-6)
- **Seed Packet, Unlabeled** (COMMON) — grows into whatever song you were humming.
- **Crystal Tuner** (UNCOMMON) — reveals hidden frequencies in any location dream.
- **Nightjar B-Side** (RARE) — a song that only plays once, for you.
- **The Test Pattern Rose** (LEGENDARY) — blooms in colors that don't broadcast.

## Scenarios (1-2)
- **Dead Air** — a frequency band has gone silent and its flowerbed is wilting; the
  player and Sevens trace the break through the garden's oldest wiring.
- **Request Hour** — DJ Nightjar takes exactly one request a night; the player must
  find her booth before the hour ends, guided only by what's blooming.
