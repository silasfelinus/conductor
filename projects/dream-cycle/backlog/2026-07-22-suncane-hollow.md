## The idea
In Suncane Hollow, martial artists cultivate solar-charged bamboo groves and duel each dawn to harvest banked qi-light, while the village's ancient looms weave every debt ever owed into thread that never quite forgets who owes what. It's a place where a promise can be as literal as a swatch of glowing cloth, and paying it off might mean giving up the fight you finally learned to win. Perfect for players who want martial-arts dueling with genuine consequence -- every strike banks light, every unpaid debt eventually gets called.

## Vibe / genre dream
**Ledger Light** — GENRE. Every dawn you win banks light -- and somewhere, a loom is already weaving down what it cost you.

## Locations (2)
- **The Cane Terraces** — known for Solar-charged bamboo groves tended by cultivators who fight dawn duels to harvest condensed qi-light from the bamboo's glowing joints. Local rule: Qi-light must be cut in the 'gold minute' -- the first full minute of direct sunrise. Harvest a joint before or after and the light voids, leaving only dead cane. Best scene: A duel between two terraces at first light, both fighters swinging bamboo blades that visibly dim with every parry as their banked charge spends down. Art: Sunrise through vertical terraced bamboo groves, golden particulate light drifting between stalks, wooden aqueducts catching reflections, duelists in indigo-dyed cotton with bamboo-fiber armor plates that glow faintly at the joints.
- **The Ember Loom Hall** — known for A long wooden hall of solar-thread looms that weave the village's debts into wearable battle sashes -- and, once a year, must be fed a name before Longest Light or the looms start weaving on their own after dark. Local rule: No name may be spoken into the Loom twice in one year. Repeat one and it summons whatever the Loom already wove for them. Best scene: At dusk, the looms humming faintly to themselves, and a half-finished debt-slip found bearing the name of someone standing very much alive in the doorway. Art: A dim hall lit by hanging solar-glass lanterns, looms threaded with faintly glowing filament, shelves of folded sashes, and at the far end older looms sitting untouched and unsettlingly still.

## Characters (3)
- **Kess Amaranth** — The terrace's youngest sanctioned duelist, fighting dawn bouts to bank enough qi-light to buy her grandmother free of an old debt owed to the Ember Loom. Carries A hollow-cored bamboo practice sword she's secretly filling with her own harvested light instead of trading it in at day's end. Complication: Every dawn she wins pushes her closer to freedom, but hoarded qi-light is exactly the kind of debt the Loom notices and comes looking for. Look: Sun-browned forearms wrapped in bamboo-fiber vambraces, a solar-thread hair cord that visibly dims when her reserves run low.
- **Elder Fen Ashgrove** — Keeper of the Ember Loom Hall, the only one left who still reads the old debt-slips aloud and remembers which names were fed to the looms generations back. Carries A ring of brass keys that don't open anything anymore, kept only because removing one from the ring once made a loom start weaving again. Complication: Loves the village enough to keep its oldest secret sealed, but this year's debt-ledger is one name short of balancing, and Elder Fen knows exactly whose name is missing. Look: Silver braid threaded with the same glow-fiber as the loom's oldest threads, ink-stained fingers, a permanent squint from decades reading by lantern-thread light.
- **Warden Iska Dray** — A Sunward Assembly quota inspector sent to audit the terrace's qi-light output, secretly hoping the posting lets her learn what happened to the mother who vanished into the Loom's ledger before she was born. Carries An Assembly-issue tally-lantern that's only supposed to count light, but hums differently near the Loom's older shelves. Complication: Her official audit and her private search are the same trip, and asking the wrong villager the wrong question could get the ritual noticed by the Assembly -- which would 'fix' it by erasing it entirely. Look: Practical gray-and-copper Assembly uniform, a tally-lantern strapped across her chest, eyes that keep drifting toward the loom hall's far wall she's not cleared to enter.

## Rewards (2 — one skill, one item)
- **Gold-Minute Draw** (SKILL, RARE) — The ability to land one strike so precisely timed to dawn's first full light that it harvests a burst of banked qi mid-swing, turning an ordinary hit into a light-charged one. Best used when Opening or closing a dawn duel, or any fight fought at first light. The catch: Only works in the true gold minute -- miss the window and the strike lands as an ordinary, uncharged blow, wasting the opening.
- **Debt-Slip Thread** (ITEM, EPIC) — A single spool of loom-thread pre-woven with someone's name; tying it around a wound, tool, or promise binds that name's luck to the outcome until the thread frays through. Best used when Sealing a bargain you need kept, or protecting something you can't watch over yourself. The catch: The loom always knows whose name got spent, and it adds the spending to that person's ledger whether they agreed to it or not.

## Scenarios (1-2)
- **The Gold Minute** — Kess needs one more dawn win to finish buying her grandmother free, but Elder Fen has just discovered the ledger is one name short -- and it might be Kess's own family debt, not her grandmother's, that the Loom has actually been tracking.
- **The Quota and the Question** — Warden Iska's audit puts her in the Loom Hall the same evening Elder Fen is due to feed this year's debt-slip to the looms, forcing a choice between filing an accurate Assembly report and letting a village keep its oldest secret.
