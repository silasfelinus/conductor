## New Facets (invented for this dream)
- **Honest Hunger** (`honest-hunger`, ALIGNMENT) — A disposition that holds appetite to be the one truthful thing in a pretending world, and trusts anyone who admits what they want. It forgives theft and rough manners far sooner than flattery, which makes it a poor guest at polite tables.
- **Drift Shepherd** (`drift-shepherd`, OCCUPATION) — A keeper of herds that travel by air or current, such as lantern jellies, spore clouds or kite-fish, who walks beneath them for seasons at a time steering by sound and shade. The herd goes where it likes; the shepherd's work is to be somewhere under it when it lands.

## The idea
A star that fell long ago has grown into a vast spotted jaguar who herds the drifting lantern-animals across a pink-skied chrome desert, and this year a small stranger from beyond the sky has arrived with a herd of her own.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Kasso's Long Pasture** — The herds cross the chrome dunes under a painted sun, and the old cat who guides them has never had to share the sky.
Art: A vast pastel desert of rose-pink and teal dunes under an enormous gridded setting sun fixed on the horizon, rows of chrome palm trees and half-buried marble statue heads, a slow herd of glowing lantern-bodied jellyfish drifting overhead trailing long soft tentacles, one colossal spotted jaguar walking tiny and distant across the sand beneath them, wide empty sky, very little clutter.

## Dream location (1)
- **Gridsun Basin** — A dry lake basin the size of a county where a gridded sun hangs fixed at the horizon, chrome palms stand in the dust, and drifting lantern-jellies gather each season to feed on the chrono dust that blows off the dunes. Local rule: Nothing may be hunted, picked or carried off within sight of the sun, because the dust reshows the last person who tried. Best scene: The whole herd lifts at once as the sun flickers, and a hundred lanterns rise over the basin like a second, smaller sunrise. Art: An enormous dry lake basin of rose-pink sand and teal shadow, a huge gridded neon sun sitting on the horizon behind rows of chrome palm trees, half-buried marble busts leaning in the dunes, a pale haze of golden dust drifting up from the sand, a herd of glowing jellyfish-bodied animals floating over the basin, a lone spotted jaguar far away at the dune crest for scale, wide empty sky.

## Character (1)
- **Kasso** — They walk the herds across the basin every season, steering them with slow low calls, and have never needed to be anywhere before the herd was. A long cord of indigo cotton strips knotted with cowrie shells trails from Kasso's neck, and each knot holds a tiny wandering light that rides the cord until it settles. Complication: Every light Kasso binds adds its small weight to the cord, and after centuries the cord is heavier than Kasso can easily carry, which they have not told the herd. Look: A truly ancient jaguar the size of a barn, massive and slow, shoulders like boulders, a heavy blunt head carried low, fur in piebald patches where the golden coat has faded to cream and the old rosettes survive only in scattered dark smudges, one pale star-shaped scar across the left flank, a madras-check cotton wrap tied over the shoulders and a long cord of indigo-dyed cloth strips and white cowrie shells looped round the neck, amber eyes half-closed and steady, jaw slightly open as if tasting the air with unhurried pleasure, golden dust clinging to the paws.

## Reward item (1)
- **Dustglass Crook** (ITEM, RARE) — Planted in the sand, it makes any drifting herd within earshot turn and slowly follow its bearer for as long as the grains run. Best used when: A herd is leaving its pasture on a wind that will carry it somewhere it cannot feed. The catch: The glass runs on the herd's own hours, so every animal that follows the crook ages a day for each minute it is led. Look: A tall shepherd's crook of pale bleached driftwood with an hourglass of pink glass hung in the curl of its hook, glowing golden dust trickling slowly through the waist, a faded indigo cord wrapped round the staff, scuffs and sand grit worn into the grain.
