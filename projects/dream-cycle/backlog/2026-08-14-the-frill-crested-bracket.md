## The idea
A tournament grown from living vine and solar fire, where a challenging void scout must out-flare a frilled lizard champion inside a coliseum that breathes.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Solar Bracket Bloom** — Every round the arena grows a new ring of thorns, and every fighter carries a piece of the sun.
Art: Saturated orange-gold light bleeding through green foliage, vine-wrapped stone arches, drifting embers caught in humid air, high contrast between molten highlights and deep jungle shadow.

## Dream location (1)
- **The Verdant Coliseum** — known for an amphitheater grown rather than built, its tiered seating formed from petrified banyan roots wound around a core of slow-burning solar rock. Local rule: no weapon may be carried in that was not grown or forged on the coliseum grounds that same morning. Best scene: the final bracket match beneath the collapsing ring of vines as the solar core flares white at dusk. Art: Massive curved root-terraces stacked in rising rings, cracks glowing amber like a low fire beneath bark, drifting spores lit gold, distant open sky bruised orange at the horizon.

## Character (1)
- **Vareth Kol** — a void scout turned reluctant tournament fighter, driven to win passage off-world by defeating a champion she does not believe deserves the crown. Carries a cracked void-sextant repurposed as a torch, its brass casing packed with a nub of captured solar flame. Complication: the flame inside her sextant is slowly consuming the instrument, and if it burns out mid-match she loses her only way home. Look: A tall gaunt figure in a patched void-suit stripped to the waist for the heat, scarred vacuum-tan skin, a frilled leather collar fanned stiff around her neck like the lizard she fights beside, dark hair shorn close, eyes reflecting a faint ember glow.

## Reward item (1)
- **The Barnacle Compass** (ITEM, RARE) — always points toward the nearest hidden passage, drowned wreck, or buried door within a day's walk. Best used when searching flooded ruins or coastal tunnels beneath the coliseum roots for a smuggled escape route. The catch: its needle only wakes near saltwater, and grows still and useless the longer it is kept dry. Look: A palm-sized brass compass crusted with fused barnacle shells around its rim, glass face fogged and salt-etched, a corroded green needle trembling faintly, chain of knotted rope wire looped through a worn eyelet.

## Reward skill (1)

Best used when facing a rigged match or a false champion whose advantage is not what it appears to be. The catch: the insight arrives only once per contest and cannot be recalled or repeated within the same match. Look: A thin seam of violet light briefly tracing the exact shape of a hidden weakness across a crowd or arena floor, visible for only a heartbeat before fading like ink dissolving in water.
