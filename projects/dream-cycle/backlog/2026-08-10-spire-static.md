## The idea
Every dusk, Maret Solvang flies a crop duster between the ribs of a slumbering giant curled beneath the bay, dusting it with spores to keep the whole coastal economy from waking to disaster, until the mainland orders her to let it stir.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Lullaby Run** — The whole town's work is keeping one enormous thing asleep.
Art: A small propeller plane threading between colossal barnacled ribs that arch out of a calm bay, violet crystal furring the bone, dusk light and a long trail of pale spore drifting behind the wing.

## Dream location (1)
- **The Ribbed Sound** — A bay entirely roofed by the ribs of something still breathing, its violet-crusted bones arching over the water like a cathedral built from a living thing. Local rule: Every boat cuts its engine between the third and fourth rib, out of respect for what sleeps below. Best scene: The ribs flex a handspan and every boat in the bay lifts at once, as if the whole town had inhaled. Art: Vast curved bone arches rising from flat green water, violet crystal growth crusting their undersides, fishing boats tied up small beneath them, low amber light and mist on the surface.

## Character (1)
- **Maret Solvang** — She flies the dusk spore run each evening, threading the ribs without ever waking what she is dusting. A canvas satchel of townsfolk's letters rides against her hip, and she reads them aloud over the open radio on every run. Complication: The town has started listening for her voice more than for the all-clear, and she has no idea how to stop being the thing they tune in for. Look: A lean sharkfolk pilot in a cracked leather flight jacket, slate-grey skin, gill lines dark along her throat, goggles pushed up on a smooth crown, one webbed hand taped at the knuckles.

## Reward item (1)
- **Hushspore Canister** (ITEM, RARE) — It releases one dose of sleep-spore in a wide drifting cloud across the sky. Best used when: Something colossal beneath the water has begun to stir, and the tremor needs to be smoothed over before it reaches the surface. The catch: Everything beneath the cloud falls asleep, including whoever was the one who dropped it. Look: A dented copper canister the length of a forearm, its little glass window furred with pale violet crystal, a rope handle bound around with faded red thread.

## Reward skill (1)
- **Breath Timing** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — It matches the rhythm of a run to the breathing of something enormous and half-asleep below. Best used when: The gap you need only opens and closes on its own unpredictable rhythm, never on command. The catch: Whoever uses it starts to feel the next breath coming in their own chest, whether they want to or not. Look: A long white contrail bending in slow unison with a vast rising surface, spray rolling outward from it in a widening ring.

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **The Night They Cut the Spore Line** — In Lullaby Run, out over The Ribbed Sound, Maret Solvang finds the dusk supply barge gone and mainland launches anchored where it should be, and has to decide on the radio, in front of the whole listening town, whether to fly the run empty or land.
