## The idea
A ghost airfield where the runway lights never go out and the spires remember every flight that didn't land. A sharkfolk pilot writes letters to her dead wingmate and starts hearing them answered. When a shadowy council orders the field grounded, she has to decide whether the last flight out is escape or exactly what the spires have been waiting for.

## Dream location (1)
