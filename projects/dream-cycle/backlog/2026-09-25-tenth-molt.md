## New Facets (invented for this dream)
- **Living Hull** (`living-hull`, SETTING) — A structure that is not built once but grown continuously by the bodies of everyone who has ever lived or died inside it, so its layout changes shape as its population does.
- **Claimed By Use** (`claimed-by-use`, ALIGNMENT) — Ownership is not a fact of the past but a continuous act: whoever is actually using and maintaining a thing owns it, and stopping, even briefly, opens it to anyone else's claim.

## The idea
A newly-arrived gang of dockside grifters is walked through the Tenth Molt's Handover night, where duties are not assigned but chosen by touching silver-grown walls and deciding, hand over hand, what to keep, while a colony of resident rock hyraxes screams warning at every seam nobody has claimed yet.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Silverbond** — A ship's oldest crew do not retire; they fuse into the hull and keep steering.
Art: A pirate vessel's silver-veined interior wall, human-shaped ridges half-emerged from the metal, hard daylight through a viewport, no soft glow.

## Dream location (1)
- **Tenth Molt** — Having gone through its silver-bonding ten separate times, once per generation of hull-elders, without ever losing a crew member to the merge, only gaining new ones. Local rule: No one may scrape, sand, or repaint a wall once a crewmate's face has started to surface in it, no matter how slow the process or how much cargo space it costs. Best scene: A boarding party freezes mid-heist in the cargo hold when they realize the decorative silver ribbing along the ceiling is breathing in time with the ship's engine hum. Art: A cargo hold's silver-ribbed ceiling folding into rounded knuckle and rib shapes at handhold height, hard daylight spilling through an open hatch, dust motes visible in the beam, no soft glow.

## Character (1)
- **Thara Otsu** — She is trying to learn the ship's real shape by mapping which fused crew-elders now form which wall, stair, or spar, not to fix anything, just because the ship refuses to hold still in her head until she does. A fist-sized shard of raw Arcane Silver, never machined, kept wrapped in oiled fish-skin so it cannot touch her palm directly. Complication: Her own hands have started matching the cold, silver-veined texture of the hull in patches, and she cannot tell yet if that is the beginning of her own bonding or just years of handling raw ore. Look: A gangly, soft-shouldered teenager who has not grown into their hands yet, box braids gathered loose at the shoulder and threaded through with a strip of coir rope gone silver-grey with wear. A fish-skin leather satchel, indigo-dyed and cracked white along the fold, rides against one hip, patched more than original. Knuckles permanently ink-stained from close reading in bad light. At rest the mouth sits slightly open in the unguarded, already-listening look of someone raised talking to a machine that always answered back.

## Reward item (1)
- **Three-Hand Wrench** (ITEM, RARE) — It fits exactly one fastening, bolt, or seam per person who holds it, never the same one twice, and works cleanly only for whoever used it last. Best used when: A ship's systems have drifted out of any single person's expertise and the fix needs several different specialties applied in sequence. The catch: Set it down for even a shift and the next hand to pick it up inherits the fit completely; you do not get it back by asking. Look: A stubby silver wrench, its head worn into a shape no catalog lists, handle wrapped in three visibly different eras of grip-tape layered without ever removing the one beneath, the newest strip still bright, the oldest gone black and glassy.

## Reward skill (1)
- **Silverlisten** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — Pressed flat against any arcane-silver surface, a bonded hand can feel the shape of every sealed space on the other side of it, out to about a ship's width. Best used when: Planning a boarding route through a hull nobody has blueprints for anymore. The catch: It only works through silver that has bonded to a living body at some point; dead ore stays silent, so it is only ever as good as who is willing to lend a hand to the wall. Look: A bare palm pressed flat to a silver-veined wall, faint hairline cracks of paler silver spreading outward from the fingertips like frost, no light or glow, just a slow structural bloom under the skin of the metal.

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **Handover** — In Silverbond aboard the Tenth Molt, Thara Otsu runs the ship's traditional Handover: guiding a newly-arrived crew of dockside grifters through a night-long, wonderstruck initiation where they meet the ship's living hull face to face and decide, by hand and choice rather than by old rank, who will maintain what, while a colony of resident rock hyraxes screams warning calls at every arcane-silver seam nobody has claimed yet.
