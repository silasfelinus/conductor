- **Occupation:** harbor pilot (guides starships through the Fringe, a stretch of space that isn't reliably space)
- **Animal / species:** axolotl (neotenic, gilled, regenerative amphibian)
- **Fusion:** Axolotl pilots regenerate any Fringe-caused injury overnight, but the regrowth is never quite the original part — a new fin threaded with a sliver of Outside, an extra eye that reads non-Euclidean weather. That turns the piloting guild's licensing and insurance paperwork into real, load-bearing comedy over what legally counts as 'you' after a regrowth, while the horror underneath the forms stays completely genuine.

## The idea
Molt Harbor is the last docking complex before the Fringe, where axolotl pilots guide star-freighters through a stretch of space that doesn't reliably obey geometry. Every scrape a pilot takes out there regrows by morning — wrong, useful, and faintly Outside. Ships hire pilots for the specific regrowths they're carrying, a black market trades in shed limbs like rare parts, and everyone in the harbor has learned not to ask too many questions about what's growing back this time.

## Vibe / genre dream
**Regrowth and Red Tape** — GENRE. Every wound here heals into a shape, and every shape needs a permit.

## Locations (2)
- **The Gill Locks** — known for the pressurized dock and pilot ready-room where ships lock in before a Fringe run, and where every pilot gets checked over on the way back out Local rule: any visible regrowth must be logged with the Harbormaster before your next run, or your license is pulled on the spot Best scene: a rookie pilot getting inspected fin by fin while the Harbormaster pokes a still-tender regrowth with a stylus, utterly unbothered by the faint starfield glimmering just under its skin Art: a low, wet, pressurized dock bay lit in cold fluorescent green-white, condensation beading on steel walkways, axolotl pilots in translucent pressure suits queued at an inspection window, one pilot's newly regrown fin held up to the light showing something that isn't reflection
- **The Molt Bazaar** — known for a cramped market alley where retired and shed regrowths are traded, since Outside-touched tissue holds real value and stranger uses Local rule: nothing bought here may ever be reattached to the pilot it grew from — an old guild superstition about reabsorbing your own Outside-growth Best scene: a trader haggling over a jarred fin that shows a slowly rotating doorway when held up to a lantern, both parties pretending this is a completely normal transaction Art: a narrow covered market alley strung with dripping lanterns, shelves of cloudy specimen jars each holding a twitching or faintly glowing piece of regrown tissue, hand-lettered price tags, condensation fogging the glass

## Characters (3)
- **Marisol Fenn** — a veteran pilot chasing one more clean run before retirement, unnerved that her regrowths keep getting stranger and more useful the closer she gets to quitting Carries a battered logbook where she sketches the shape of every regrowth before the Harbormaster sees it Complication: her newest regrown eye keeps showing her routes through the Fringe that appear on no chart — and they're always the fastest way through Look: fringed gills gone translucent blue at the tips, one eye an ordinary black bead, the other a slow oil-slick swirl that never quite settles
- **Cordwin Vask** — the Harbormaster, who enforces licensing paperwork with obsessive precision because he watched an unlicensed regrowth cause a real disaster years ago Carries a clipboard that isn't strapped to his claw-hand anymore — it grew there, fused on by his own third regrowth Complication: he is quietly stockpiling banned regrowths in a locked office out of fear of what they might otherwise become, breaking the rule he enforces on everyone else Look: a heavily regrown body built from a patchwork of skin tones, small unplanned gill-fringes surfacing in odd places from years of licensed and unlicensed healing
- **Yevgenia Thist** — a Bazaar trader always hunting the one big find that could set her up for life, buying and reselling shed regrowths from pilots who need quick coin Carries a satchel of nested specimen jars, each padded and labeled in her own private code Complication: she's sitting on a shed tailtip that keeps regrowing overnight no matter how many times she cuts it away and rejars it, and she's too afraid of its value to sell it or study it Look: a soot-stained apron heavy with patch pockets, a jeweler's loupe strapped permanently over one eye from years of squinting at jarred goods

## Rewards (2 — one skill, one item)
- **Regrowth Charting** (SKILL, RARE) — lets you see, in the first hour after a Fringe injury, a faint preview of the shape the wound will regrow into Best used when right after taking Fringe damage, before the harbor's mandatory 24-hour healing quarantine ends The catch: the preview only ever shows the SHAPE, never what that shape will let you see, do, or sense — you find out the actual power of a regrowth the hard way, same as everyone else
- **The Ever-Tail** (ITEM, EPIC) — an endlessly-regrowing jarred tailtip you can shave tissue from to graft onto a fresh wound, cutting a full day off mandatory healing quarantine Best used when before a run you can't afford to be pulled from the roster for The catch: every graft taken from it regrows on the jar in a slightly different color than the last, and nobody in Molt Harbor knows what happens when it runs out of colors

## Scenarios (1-2)
- **The Uninspected Fin** — a rookie pilot's post-run regrowth doesn't match anything in the Harbormaster's logbook, and she has six hours to get it inspected, sold quietly at the Bazaar, or hidden before her license is pulled
- **What Cordwin Won't License** — someone at the Bazaar is selling a regrowth stamped with Cordwin's own old licensing seal — from a specimen he swore he destroyed years ago
