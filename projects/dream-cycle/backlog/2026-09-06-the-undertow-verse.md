## The idea
A hammerhead shark marked at birth to finish her reef's warding verse keeps getting driven off before she can complete it, and every unfinished recitation weakens whatever the reef is actually warding against.

## Dream vibe (1)
**The Undertow Verse** — Every charm that keeps the deep asleep must be finished precisely as it started, or something down there notices the gap.
Art: A broad hammerhead shark gliding under a canopy of drifting kelp-charms strung with bone beads, its hammer-shaped head haloed by a marbled swirl of poured, layered bioluminescent light.

## Dream location (1)
- **The Verse Reef** — A reef of coral-grown warning charms wound around the drowned hull of an old relay satellite, where reciting any carved verse only partway is said to wake what sleeps in the trench below. Local rule: No charm carved into the reef may be read aloud past its first half unless the reader intends to finish it completely. Best scene: The reef's charm-script lights fully end to end for the first time in memory, every line finished at once. Art: A crashed satellite hull fused into living coral, its solar panels replaced by sheets of pale calcified coral etched with looping charm-script, a dull red ember glowing faintly from a crack near its core.

## Character (1)
- **Coda** — She was marked at birth to finish the reef's warding verse every generation, and every attempt to swim away from that duty circles her straight back to it. A single loop of bone-and-glass beads knotted into her fin, each bead etched with one syllable of a verse she has never once said all the way through on purpose. Complication: Reef-folk read her marbled scars as an omen and drive her off before she can finish reciting, and each unfinished recitation weakens the wardings guarding the trench a little further. Look: A wide hammerhead shark with a marbled swirl of grey-and-rust scarring layered across her hammer like poured, pooled paint, one bead-strung charm cord snagged permanently around her left cephalofoil wing.

## Reward item (1)
- **Ember Seal** (ITEM, RARE) — Lit once, the feather flares bright red for one held breath, then goes dark until struck again — but never truly burns out. Best used when: A single unmistakable signal is needed the moment a boundary line is crossed, and nothing more. The catch: Every flare draws something closer; whatever sleeps in the trench below already reacts to this exact shade of red. Look: A single curled phoenix feather sealed inside a chipped glass vial, its cork capped with dribbled black wax shaped like a small closed eye.

## Reward skill (1)
- **Borrowed Ending** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — Lets the user finish any warding verse someone else left incomplete, sealing it shut as if they had spoken every word themselves. Best used when: Someone else's unfinished charm is about to fail and there is no time to find the original speaker. The catch: The verse remembers whose voice actually finished it, and the debt of that ending transfers permanently to the borrower. Look: A faint second shadow of moving lips overlaid translucent against the user's own mouth, mouthing the last words a beat before they are spoken aloud.

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **The Half-Finished Ward** — In The Undertow Verse at The Verse Reef, Coda has one tide to finish the warding verse the reef-folk keep driving her off before she can complete, after the old satellite ember at its core flares once, unlogged, for the first time in years.
