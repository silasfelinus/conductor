## The idea
A blacklisted ghostwriter, half-grafted into a stick insect's camouflage limbs, investigates fairy-tale wish-trading crimes for a noir agency that never forgets a debt.

## Dream vibe (1)
**The Ever After Files** — Every happily ever after has a body count nobody wrote down.
Art: A rain-slicked cobblestone alley under a gaslamp, gingerbread rooftops looming through fog, a long insectile shadow thrown across wet stone.

## Dream location (1)
- **The Godmother's Exchange** — known for trading fractional shares of broken wishes. Local rule: no wish trades below its opening curse price. Best scene: a ledger entry rewrites itself mid-sale. Art: A cramped shopfront of towering brass ledgers, gaslight glinting off insect-thin scaffolding, golden starfruit-fiber threads strung floor to ceiling like a stock ticker.

## Character (1)
- **Perpetua Quill** — ghostwrite a confession that will finally indict the godmother who blacklisted her. Carries a starfruit-fiber-bound notebook that only writes true words. Complication: the notebook also writes down her own petty grudges, poisoning the confession. Look: a gaunt woman in a moth-eaten trench coat, forearms replaced with jointed chitin-and-brass stick-insect limbs, ink-stained fingers, a coat lined with dead gaslamp moths

## Reward item (1)
- **Godmother's Ledger Quill** (ITEM, RARE) — writes a binding true confession. Best used when a suspect's testimony is incomplete. The catch: it also transcribes the writer's own secrets. Look: a slim quill fashioned from a starfruit-fiber stalk, its nib capped in tarnished gold leaf, a faint golden thread trailing from the barrel like unspooled twine

## Reward skill (1)
- **Ghostwriter's Tell** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — reads the true draft beneath a rewritten story. Best used when a witness's account has been polished for public consumption. The catch: reveals your own unspoken drafts too. Look: the air around a page ripples like heat haze, faint charcoal lines of a crossed-out sentence bleeding up through the visible ink

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **The Wish That Wouldn't Clear** — In The Ever After Files at The Godmother's Exchange, Perpetua Quill traces a wish-trader found drained of every unwritten word, the only clue a starfruit-fiber thread leading straight back to the godmother who ruined her.
