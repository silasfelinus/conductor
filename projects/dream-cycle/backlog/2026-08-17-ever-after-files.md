## The idea
In a city where happy endings are bought and sold as futures above the stage, a playwright who was written out of her own hit show goes back on opening night to take her ending off the actress currently wearing it.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Third-Act Money** — Somebody is trading your ending on a floor you will never be shown.
Art: A gilded theatre seen from the flies at night, brokers hanging motionless along the rafter beams, gold thread strung between them catching the stage-light rising from below, rain on the high windows.

## Dream location (1)
- **The Gilded Rafters** — Endings are bought and sold in the beams above a working stage. Local rule: No bidding is allowed while the curtain is up, so every trade freezes for the length of the scene. Best scene: The whole rafter goes still mid-bid because a real ending just walked in the door. Art: Dust-thick timber beams above a lit stage, brokers folded thin and motionless along them like branches, gold starfruit-fiber thread strung bead to bead, warm light leaking up through the boards.

## Character (1)
- **Ottoline Sarr** — She has come back to take back the ending that was cut out of her and sold on to someone else. She carries the only surviving copy of her original third act, folded into eight. Complication: She has waited so long to be owed this that she no longer wants it back gently. Look: A tall stick-insect woman in a soaked evening coat, limbs long and dry-brown and jointed twice too often, a narrow face lit hard from below, gold thread wound and knotted along one forearm.

## Reward item (1)
- **The Understudy's Shawl** (ITEM, RARE) — It makes the wearer read as whoever the room was already expecting to see. Best used when: The wearer's own face would get them thrown out of the room before they said a word. The catch: The room's expectation is what shows, not anything the wearer actually chose. Look: A loose knitted shawl of gold starfruit fiber gone soft with handling, one corner unravelling into a long loose thread, warm lamplight caught in the weave.

## Reward skill (1)
- **Rewrite On Your Feet** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — It changes how a scene ends while it is still being played out. Best used when: A scene has just locked into an ending everyone in the room already believes. The catch: Whatever it changes, it changes for everyone who was there, permanently. Look: Wet black ink blooming up through printed lines on a held page, letters sliding into new positions while the paper is still in someone's hand.
