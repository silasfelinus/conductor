## The idea
A coral-grown hulk drifts in slow orbit round a gas giant, its tide-pools kept alive by jellyfish-haired matriarchs and the long glass colonies that thread its halls.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Lanterns Over the Black Tide** — The whole station is one slow animal, and it is hungry for guests.
Art: A coral-encrusted orbital hulk, bioluminescent pools spilling light into starless dark.

## Dream location (1)
- **Nursery of Drowned Lanterns** — Flooded coral galleries where glowing jelly drift between the ribs of a ship that died and kept growing. Local rule: Nobody speaks above a whisper once the lanterns dim, because the walls remember sound as food. Best scene: A wrong step in the dark makes the whole gallery swell and exhale, and every lantern turns toward the doorway at once. Art: wet coral vault, jelly lanterns, slick ribbed walls, pale light

## Character (1)
- **Pip Anvilsong** — They want to find one patch of the station that stays still long enough to be called home. A knotted sealskin cord strung with fire opal beads, each one worn smooth by a tide they never saw. Complication: The fire opals brighten whenever the walls grow hungry, so the beads announce their nervousness to everything near. Look: Barely grown dwarf, long and angular where dwarves run broad, with dye-bright hair of a colour that has no name and falls in salt-stiff braids. Seal-gut parka stitched in atoll shell-thread, fire opal beads at the throat, patient grey eyes and a lopsided half-smile held steady while they weigh everyone who enters.

## Reward item (1)
- **Bell of the Long Chain** (ITEM, RARE) — It rings a clear tone that tells every part of a colony to move together. Best used when: A crowd, a swarm, or a divided crew must act as a single body. The catch: While it rings, you also move in step with them and cannot break away. Look: a hand-sized bell of clear glass-like jelly, trailing fine threads, pale blue light pulsing inside the dome

## Reward skill (1)
- **Tidebreath** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — It lets you hold your breath through a flooded passage by slowing everything in your body. Best used when: A drowned corridor lies between you and the way out. The catch: Waking from it feels like surfacing from a long dream, and you lose the last minute of memory. Look: a slow bloom of silver bubbles unfolding from the lips, chest barely moving, lashes beaded with water

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **Pip and the Hungry Halls** — Under Lanterns Over the Black Tide at Nursery of Drowned Lanterns, Pip Anvilsong must lead a lost trade crew past walls that have begun to breathe.
