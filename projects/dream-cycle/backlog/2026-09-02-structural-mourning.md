## New Facets (invented for this dream)
- **Compartmented Mourner** (`compartmented-mourner`, PERSONALITY) — Someone who has learned to grieve in scheduled, contained bursts because unregulated grief here has physical consequences. The discipline keeps the walls up; it also means the grief never actually finishes.
- **Ledger-Balanced Kindness** (`ledger-balanced-kindness`, ALIGNMENT) — Doing good only to the exact degree it can later be repaid, tracked as scrupulously as a debt. It reads as generosity from a distance and as accounting up close.

## The idea
A noir dream-district's tenements are held up not by steel but by scheduled human grief, and the cartographer who maps whose mourning is load-bearing is running out of her own before she can train a replacement.

## Dream vibe (1)
**The Load-Bearing District** — Stop grieving and the ceiling remembers you didn't.
Art: A canted tenement block held aloft by taut mourning-ribbons instead of steel beams, long noir shadows pooling in the sag.

## Dream location (1)
- **The Underprop** — A subterranean gallery of hollow steel conduits that carry mourners' scheduled grief downward like current, keeping the district's walls standing. Local rule: No conduit may run past its rated hour; a mourner who overruns their slot floods the line and something below drinks the overflow. Best scene: A junior tech traces a conduit gone cold to its splice and finds the steel warm from the inside, pulsing at a rate no clock in the room keeps. Art: a low concrete gallery of fused hollow-steel conduits overhead, faint blue condensation beading where the grief runs hottest, one long crack leaking a darker black than the tunnel around it

## Character (1)
- **Odalys Pryce** — She's trying to certify a new load-bearing mourner for the Ashgate tenement before her own decade-old grief — the one that's held it up till now — finally runs its course. A fold-out brass theodolite calibrated to grief-load instead of angles, its sighting glass permanently fogged at the edges. Complication: She built her career on scheduled, compartmented grief — the discipline that let her hold up Ashgate for ten years. She can no longer tell if she still feels anything outside its allotted hour. Look: a noir mourner in a rain-dark coat cut like a draftsman's, brass theodolite slung across one hip, cartoon-long shadow pooling a full step behind her actual feet

## Reward item (1)
- **The Undertow Ledger** (ITEM, RARE) — It tallies exactly how much grief a person still owes before their debt to a building is paid. Best used when: A mourner needs proof they've carried their share and can finally stop. The catch: The ledger only balances downward — entering a kindness without expecting return erases the entry instead of crediting it, so pure generosity here reads as if it never happened. Look: a slim hollow-steel ledger the size of a hymnal, pages that ripple like standing water when opened, ink that pools and drifts in slow aquatic currents instead of drying flat

## Reward skill (1)
- **Structural Confession** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — Speaking a grief aloud, precisely and completely, temporarily transfers its load-bearing weight into the nearest wall instead of the mourner's own frame. Best used when: A mourner is about to collapse under grief faster than the schedule allows. The catch: The wall remembers the confession exactly as spoken — a future crack will run in the shape of that sentence, readable by anyone who knows how to look. Look: a hairline crack spidering up plaster in the unmistakable shape of cursive handwriting, faint noir-blue light bleeding from inside the wall along its path

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **The Ashgate Vacancy** — In The Load-Bearing District at The Underprop, Odalys Pryce has three days to certify a new load-bearing mourner for Ashgate before her own decade of scheduled grief runs dry and whatever the conduits have been feeding rises to collect the difference — and the circus that just pitched its tents in the vacant lot next door keeps drawing candidates who grieve too loudly, too publicly, to ever pass inspection.
