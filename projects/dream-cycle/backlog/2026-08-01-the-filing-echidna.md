## The idea
A vast echidna-shaped kaiju has spent a century growing a living coral cathedral across her spine, and twice a year she surfaces so an unlicensed reef exorcist can renew the improvised paperwork that keeps her spirits, tides, and tenants from spilling into chaos. Officially, kaiju do not qualify for wilderness permits and neither does the exorcist who issues them — until the real Bureau finally notices.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Paperwork for a God** — Told from the kaiju's own drowsy, contented perspective — being worshipped less than being properly filed.
Art: A colossal sunlit echidna-shaped kaiju breaching a turquoise sea, coral cathedral spires and solar-veined greenery growing across her spine, glowing starlight-powder quills catching the noon sun, tiny boats and a lone exorcist skiff dwarfed at her flank; warm solarpunk color, epic scale, gentle comedic tenderness rather than menace.

## Dream location (1)
- **The Reef Cathedral of Her Spine** — Coral flying buttresses grown from the kaiju's own molted quills, tide pools doubling as baptismal fonts, and a ribcage of kelp-glass windows that filter sunlight into the coral beneath. Local rule: No permit, no passage — every migrating spirit, crab, or tourist must be logged in the wet, spine-bound ledger before crossing the transept arch. Best scene: At slack tide, a lone exorcist balances on a swaying quill-buttress stamping starlight-powder seals onto translucent kelp-paper while a procession of hermit crabs, each towing a tiny soul-light, waits patiently in line. Art: Sunlit coral flying buttresses rising from an echidna's molted spine, glowing tide pools, kelp-glass windows, a small robed figure stamping paper amid a patient queue of glowing crabs.

## Character (1)
- **Bramble Osei** — She is the reef's only unlicensed exorcist, too compassionate to turn away a single migrating spirit for lack of proper papers, even though the official Wildlife Spirit Bureau refuses to recognize kaiju habitats at all. A coral stamp kit rides alongside a ledger bound in old quill-leather and a jar of starlight powder for sealing permits, all she needs to make a crossing official. Complication: The Bureau has just discovered a decade of forged authority stamps and sent a real inspector on the very tide the kaiju surfaces. Look: Sun-bleached bureaucrat's coat over diving gear, ink-stained fingers, a battered clipboard lashed to her wrist with kelp cord.

## Reward item (1)
- **The Starlight Stamp** (ITEM, RARE) — Sealing a permit in glowing starlight powder, it is recognized as binding by every spirit and tide in the reef whether or not any bureau approves it. Best used when: A soul or creature needs to be legitimized on the spot before the tide turns and the crossing closes for good. The catch: The stamp only works on parchment made from the cathedral's own kelp-glass windows, so overuse means cutting into the kaiju's own living architecture. Look: a hand-carved coral stamp with a spiral-cut face and a handle of bleached branch-knuckle, powdered starlight caught glowing pale blue in its grooves

## Reward skill (1)
- **Wilderness Filing** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — It lets its holder draft, stamp, and cross-reference an entirely invented but internally consistent bureaucratic form for any creature, species, or spirit, no matter how undocumented. Best used when: An inspector or hostile authority is demanding proof of jurisdiction that officially does not exist. The catch: The more elaborate the invented paperwork, the more the reef's spirits start behaving as though the invented rules were real, whether or not its user intended them to. Look: a sheet of kelp-glass parchment mid-stamp, luminous starlight ink spreading outward through its ruled boxes, one pair of hands steadying the edge

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **The Equinox Inspection** — In Paperwork for a God, Bramble Osei is mid-stamp in The Reef Cathedral of Her Spine when the Wildlife Spirit Bureau's real inspector wades ashore on the same tide the kaiju always chooses to surface, holding a decade of Bramble Osei's forged authorizations. Bramble Osei must use Wilderness Filing to draft the reef's first legitimate charter before the queue of waiting spirits spills past the transept arch, while The Starlight Stamp is the only thing standing between an unlicensed decade of kindness and a bureau that has just discovered the kaiju was filing right under its nose the entire time.
