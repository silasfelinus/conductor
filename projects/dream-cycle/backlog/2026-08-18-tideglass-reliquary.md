## The idea
Once a year the horseshoe crabs come ashore in their millions, and the drowned come up with them. The woman hired to walk an old lord across the flats that night has not been told which of the two he intends to end up as.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Spawning Tide** — Once a year the sea gives everything back at once.
Art: A moonlit tidal flat carpeted edge to edge with armoured horseshoe crabs, a granite causeway running out into black water, pale figures standing further out than anyone should be able to stand.

## Dream location (1)
- **The Weeping Causeway** — Letters wedged into every joint of its stone by people who could not say the words aloud. Local rule: Read one letter aloud before you cross, whichever one your hand finds. Best scene: The tide comes over and takes a whole year of letters at once. Art: A low causeway of speckled violet granite running into dark water, folded paper jammed into every seam and fluttering, salt crust and weed on the stones, a drowned graveyard island at the far end under moonlight.

## Character (1)
- **Juno Ashcombe** — She has been hired to walk her client out to the island and back again before the tide closes over the causeway. A surveyor's tape, a hooded lamp, and a client's letter still sealed sit close at hand, the last of them unopened since the day it was pressed into her palm. Complication: She has taken the job at face value, and no one in the town will explain what it actually asks of her. Look: A young woman in a waxed storm coat too thin for the season, wet hair scraped back, a brass surveyor's tape clipped at her belt, mud to the knee, a hooded lamp swinging low.

## Reward item (1)
- **The Wrong-Door Stone** (ITEM, RARE) — It opens the door you actually needed instead of whichever one happens to be standing in front of you. Best used when: The true way out is not the direction you are currently facing, and the stone knows it even when you don't. The catch: It never opens the same door twice, and it never, under any circumstance, opens back. Look: A palm-sized wedge of violet granite worn smooth on one face, a keyhole-shaped void bored clean through it, cold and faintly wet to the touch.

## Reward skill (1)
- **Loss Adjustment** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — It calculates the exact price a loss is about to cost the person standing there carrying it. Best used when: Someone standing right in front of you flatly insists they are fine. The catch: The number it gives you is always correct and is never, ever welcome. Look: Thin amber survey lines crawling across wet sand and closing into a tight contour ring around one standing figure.

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **The Night the Crabs Came Up** — In Spawning Tide, out on The Weeping Causeway during the spawning, Juno Ashcombe walks her client toward the drowning graveyard through a moving carpet of shells, and realises the pale figures standing in the shallows are all facing the two of them.
