## The idea
A grief cartographer maps a vampire lord's four centuries of losses in horseshoe-crab-blue ink, unaware the archive's final map will draw itself in her own blood.

## Dream vibe (1)
**The Tideglass Reliquary** — Grief has a shoreline, and something ancient keeps the charts.
Art: A crumbling seaside vampire manor half-submerged at high tide, stained-glass windows the color of horseshoe-crab blood, driftwood scaffolding bracing a collapsing widow's walk.

## Dream location (1)
- **The Coagulate Archive** — known for storing every grief map the vampire lord of the Reliquary has ever commissioned. Local rule: no map may be redrawn once its ink dries to black. Best scene: the tide floods the archive floor and the maps start bleeding into each other. Art: A vaulted stone gallery of shelved scroll-cases, floor inlaid with tidal channels that fill with seawater at high tide, horseshoe-crab shells nailed above every doorway, ink-blue stains creeping up the plaster.

## Character (1)
- **Perpetua Voss** — map every grief the vampire lord of the Reliquary has caused, one shoreline visit at a time. Carries a horseshoe-crab-shell inkwell on a salt-cracked strap, its hinge worn glass-smooth. Complication: ignorant that the archive's final map will draw itself, in her own blood, once she finds the last name. Look: a gaunt cartographer in a coat the mottled black-and-silver of galactic granite, stiff with dried salt, fingertips stained permanent blue-black, an old brass compass fused shut around one wrist

## Reward item (1)
- **The Grief-Bled Compass** (ITEM, RARE) — opens a walkable path to wherever the bearer's dead now rest, as if that place were just around the next corner. Best used when the way forward is lost to grief, not geography. The catch: the path only opens while the grief is still raw. Look: a fist-sized compass housed in polished galactic-granite stone, its needle a sliver of horseshoe-crab shell that swings only toward the drowned

## Reward skill (1)
- **Intake Ritual** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — reads a newly turned zombie retainer's last memory straight off their skin, like scanning a manifest. Best used when a fresh retainer arrives and must be catalogued before they forget who they were. The catch: the memory transfers to the reader, one lost name heavier. Look: a palm pressed flat to a retainer's brow, faint blue veins surfacing under the skin like ink bleeding through paper, a nearby ledger page filling itself in looping handwriting

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **The Last Undelivered Letter** — In The Tideglass Reliquary at The Coagulate Archive, Perpetua Voss finds a decades-old letter addressed to herself in her own handwriting — proof the estate's zombie retainers have been trying to warn her since long before she arrived, as a rising tide threatens to drown the last unfinished map.
