## New Facets (invented for this dream)
- **Threshold-Keeper** (`threshold-keeper`, ROLE) — A person or creature formally entrusted with standing at the one crossing between two conditions and deciding what may pass. The role grants a refusal nobody else may override, and it is measured only by what was kept out.
- **Symbiotic Colony** (`symbiotic-colony`, SETTING) — A settlement built from the physical fusion of two or more living things too joined to survive apart, sharing tissue, structure, or circulation the way other places share streets. Independence is the one comfort this setting cannot offer any of its residents.

## The idea
In a subterranean society of gently undead bloodleech elders fused into one shared living colony, the reigning Threshold-Keeper draws an unclaimed skin at the annual matching festival and has to work out, skin by skin, who in the Warren it could possibly belong to.

## Dream vibe (1)
**The Molt Betrothal** — Every molt is graded, drawn blind, and bound into a lifelong hearth-pair.
Art: A shared amber-veined leech-colony commons lit by breath-blown brimstone bubbles.

## Dream location (1)
- **The Amberlung Nave** — Its ribbed brimstone-glass ceiling glows amber wherever the colony's shared vessels run close beneath the surface. Local rule: No molt may be redrawn once its skin has touched the communal urn. Best scene: A single discarded skin surfaces in the urn that matches no living Moot-Kin at all. Art: A vast ribbed chamber of translucent amber glass, wet leech-skin banners drying along bone racks, pooled molting water reflecting bubble-lanterns.

## Character (1)
- **Weft** — She has kept the Warren's one sealed gate for four molt-cycles running, and has never once let the city above look through it. A breath-blown bubble of brimstone-glass, no larger than an egg, rides tucked into the loose folds of her collar. Complication: The bubble was blown from her own first molt-skin, and cracking it open would count as leaving the Warren for good. Look: A stooped elder leech-woman fused waist-down into the chamber wall, iridescent skin scarred pale where old molts never fully released, breath fogging faint amber.

## Reward item (1)
- **Marrowbead** (ITEM, RARE) — It lets the holder taste one memory the Symbiotic Colony's shared flesh has stored nearby. Best used when: A shared-colony memory has gone missing from the communal wall and nobody living recalls it. The catch: Whatever memory the bead surfaces belonged to someone who is now part of the wall itself. Look: A pea-sized bead of amber brimstone-glass, one internal thread of translucent tissue suspended and slowly pulsing, surface fogged with fine scratches.

## Reward skill (1)
- **Moldstar Reckoning** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — It reads the ceiling's slow-drifting mould-light like a chart and predicts which tunnel will flood next. Best used when: A tunnel is about to be sealed and nobody has confirmed which route still drains. The catch: The mould only glows for a caster who has not slept in the Warren for at least a day. Look: Faint constellations of blue-green mould flare bright along the tunnel ceiling wherever a fingertip traces them, then fade in a slow spreading afterglow.

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **An Extra Skin in the Urn** — During the Molt Betrothal in the Amberlung Nave, Weft draws a molt-skin from the communal urn that matches no living Moot-Kin in the Warren, and must decide whether to open the Symbiotic Colony's own shared memory to learn whose skin was left behind.
