## The idea
A harbor town where broken storm-glass barometers are brought in to be mended — and every repair means re-living the exact weather the glass once measured. Down the quay, a leaning lighthouse called the Squall Archive keeps entire storms corked in jars, one per name, one per grief. People come here less to fix instruments than to finally listen to what a storm was trying to tell them.

**Salvaged Weather** — GENRE. A cozy nautical mystery where mending an instrument means reliving the storm it once measured.

## Characters (3)
- **Nell Corrigan** — Apprentice glazier racing to repair her late mother's storm-glass before she forgets the exact sound of her mother's laugh Carries Her mother's cracked storm-glass, wrapped in a salt-stiffened fisherman's sweater Complication: She keeps repairing glasses too well — sealing the weather in so precisely that she can't stop hearing the storms trapped inside them Look: Soot-smudged canvas apron, sea-glass-blue eyes, hands crosshatched with fine scars from glasswork
- **Old Bartholomew "Bart" Quills** — Retired storm-chaser turned Archive keeper, cataloguing every jar by feeling instead of date Carries A waterlogged logbook whose ink only resurfaces when breathed on Complication: He's hoarding one uncatalogued, unopened jar — from the night his ship went down — and refuses to index it Look: Weathered oilskin coat, one glass eye that fogs faintly before real storms arrive, a gull perpetually asleep on his shoulder
- **Tansy** — An unusually large, unusually opinionated hermit crab who adopted a broken barometer as her shell and can feel weather three days out Carries Nothing — but her shell-barometer visibly clouds and clears with what's coming Complication: She's outgrowing her barometer shell and refuses every replacement offered, terrified of losing her forecasting sense along with it Look: Sea-green shell fused seamlessly with a brass barometer casing, dial needle twitching like an antenna

## Rewards (2 — one skill, one item)
- **Weatherreading Hands** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — The ability to read the coming emotional weather of a scene or conversation by touching any glass surface — a window, a jar, a pair of spectacles Best used when Before a difficult conversation or decision, to know what's coming The catch: It only reveals what's coming, never what to do about it — and touching too many glasses in one day leaves your own mood as foggy as a storm-glass mid-change
- **A Jar of Someone Else's Calm Weather** (ITEM, RARE) — Opening it fills the room with a single perfect afternoon someone else once lived through — total, borrowed calm Best used when After a real storm, when you need ten stolen minutes of somebody else's ordinary good day The catch: It can only be opened once, ever, and you never get to choose whose calm it is until the cork is already out

## Scenarios (1-2)
- **The Glass That Wouldn't Clear** — Nell brings her mother's cracked storm-glass to Bart, hoping a matching recorded storm jar will tell her which storm it was calibrated against — but the only jar with a matching date is the one Bart refuses to open.
- **Tansy's Third Molt** — Tansy's barometer shell is cracking under growth pressure just as an unrecorded, unnamed storm starts brewing off the coast — one only she can feel coming, and nobody at the Infirmary trusts a hermit crab's forecast over their instruments.
