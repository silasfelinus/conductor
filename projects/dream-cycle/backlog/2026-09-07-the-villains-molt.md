## New Facets (invented for this dream)
- **Debt to No One** (`debt-to-no-one`, ALIGNMENT) — A vow to answer to no authority that did not earn the right first, sworn only after the authority someone used to answer to has already failed them. It frees its holder from oversight, and strips them of anyone who might have caught a real mistake before it cost something.
- **Wireroot Fae** (`wireroot-fae`, SPECIES) — A fairy lineage that regrew its wings from harvested fiber-optic filament after a botched biopunk graft, trading true flight for a nervous system that anything with the right port can plug into and read. What it senses, it also leaks.

## The idea
A biopunk fairy who scans other fairies for illegal forbidden-power grafts discovers the same graft growing wild inside her own body, and has to outrun her own unit's hunter-drones to reach a sanctuary chrysalis before they catch her.

## Dream vibe (1)
**The Villain's Molt** — Every year she sheds the version of herself that still asked for permission.
Art: A fairy mid-molt, an old translucent wing-husk peeling away in a papery shell behind her, the new wings underneath laced with faint amber circuitry-veins.

## Dream location (1)
- **The Unlicensed Chrysalis** — A living chrysalis that fairies flee into once their own scanning grafts have marked them for capture; whoever crosses the threshold has the graft physically dissolved by the chrysalis's own silk. Local rule: No one may cut their own graft out before crossing the threshold; the chrysalis only trusts a graft it dissolves itself. Best scene: A newcomer's scanning graft flares white-hot and then liquefies mid-arm, and by morning the chrysalis wall has grown a faint amber vein where it absorbed the metal. Art: A biopunk chrysalis-shaped structure grown from radiant silk in a fairy-tale forest clearing, its walls glowing faint amber, old scanning grafts fused into the outer membrane like insect husks.

## Character (1)
- **Talis Okoro** — She used to scan every fairy that crossed the border for illegal forbidden-power grafts, until the same graft took root inside her own body and her own unit turned their scanners on her. A biopunk scanning graft fused into her palm, its lens cracked down the middle from the day it first read her own signal instead of someone else's. Complication: Her own scanning graft still broadcasts her location to every unit she used to run with, so the only way to go dark is to cut it out herself, mid-flight. Look: A small fairy with wings scarred by close-range scanner burns, a cut-down uniform jacket with the insignia torn free, an illegal jump-route map tattooed up one forearm in slow-fading bioluminescent ink.

## Reward item (1)
- **Wireroot Cocoon** (ITEM, RARE) — It regrows a single severed connection — nerve, wire, or graft — as living wireroot filament, splicing back exactly what was cut. Best used when: A graft, a limb, or a signal line has just been severed and something still needs to pass through where it used to run. The catch: Whatever it regrows answers first to a wireroot signal, the same way a scanning graft never stops broadcasting to whoever built it. Look: A fist-sized cocoon of woven radiant silk grown wild at a chrysalis threshold, one end split open to show amber wireroot filament threading out like exposed nerve fiber, faintly warm to the touch.

## Reward skill (1)
- **Second Molt** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — It sheds the user's outward self in one motion, leaving behind a hollow, wing-shaped husk that still carries the shed scanning signal. Best used when: A tracked identity needs to visibly die so a forbidden new one can be believed. The catch: The husk keeps broadcasting the old signal for hours after it is shed, luring anyone still hunting straight to wherever it was left. Look: A papery, wing-shaped husk translucent enough to see through, its inner surface still faintly pulsing amber where a scanning graft used to sit.

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **The Last Scan** — In The Villain's Molt at The Unlicensed Chrysalis, Talis Okoro races her own former unit's hunter-drones to the threshold, one wireroot-grafted fairy already dissolving her tracking graft ahead of her.
