## The idea
An all-ages coloring book about the gentle trade of herding and grooming weather.
Sky-shepherds comb tangles out of storm-fleece, shear rainbows for market, bottle-
feed orphaned drizzle, and walk their cloud-flocks across the evening. Cozy,
pastoral, a little surreal — the pages are full of billowing, curling, colorable
shapes (fleece, fog, feathers, rain-ribbons). Original throughout; no protected
designs — it clears the coloring-book originality guardrails.

## Book shape
Target ~24 solo/scene pages + 2-3 group "flock at dusk" panoramas + a full-color
cover, structured like projects/coloring-book/sets/monster-recast/README.md.
All-ages. Rank the concept pool after rough studies.

## Cast / subjects
- **The Cloud-Shepherd** — crook made of lightning-rod and ribbon, herding a woolly cumulus flock.
- **Storm-Fleece Sheep** — thunderhead ewes that must be combed before they tangle into a squall.
- **The Rainbow-Shearer** — trims spectra into skeins for the sky-market.
- **The Drizzle Lamb** — an orphaned little raincloud raised by hand.
- **The Fog Sheepdog** — a mist-bodied herder that rounds up strays by rolling downhill.
- **The Weather-Vane Rooster** — brass, cardinal-direction proud, wakes the whole sky.
- **The Market of Bottled Weather** — stalls of jarred sunbeams, breezes, and first-snow.
- **The Windmill Shearing-Barn** — where the flock is groomed and the fleece is spun to rain.
- (enough seeds to fill the page target; expand in the home set)

## Style direction
Paired-master workflow adapted from Monster Recast's STYLE-GUIDE: thick black
contours and flat bounded color in the master, faithful B/W conversion for the
page. Soft pastoral-sky palette — dawn peaches, dusk lavenders, storm greys — with
lots of curling fleece/fog/ribbon shapes that stay enjoyable to color and read
clearly at thumbnail size.
