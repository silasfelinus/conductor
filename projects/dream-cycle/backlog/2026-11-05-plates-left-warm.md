## The idea
The day after a glamorous banquet for the undead, a broad, soft-spoken keeper of a leaning hall finds that a murder of crows has moved into the dining room and is finishing every dish the guests could not.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Plates Left Warm** — The party is over, and its leftovers are still making decisions.
Art: A banquet hall sliced open along one side like a dollhouse, a long table of half-eaten dishes and tipped chairs, a wall of faintly glowing phantom glass, crows perched along the candelabra, fingerprints and grease on every surface, flat grey morning light.

## Dream location (1)
- **Gannet Row Banquet House** — It is a tilted dining hall on a leaning slope, one whole side cut away and closed with phantom glass so that the street can watch every meal. Local rule: No dish is set down on the long table without a wedge under it, because everything slides toward the glass wall. Best scene: At sunrise a crow walks the full slope of the table, nudging each plate back uphill with its beak, and the glass behind it fogs with a hundred old breaths. Art: A long timber hall with its street-facing wall replaced by pale, faintly luminous glass, the floor visibly tilted toward it, a banquet table propped on wooden wedges, tipped chairs, tallow candles guttered to stubs, crows on the rafters, grey light.

## Character (1)
- **Tove Brandvold** — They have kept this hall level for forty years and want one morning to clear the table without any of the dead guests coming back to finish their courses. A seal-gut apron, stiff and translucent as old paper, with a pocket of wooden wedges sewn along the hem. Complication: The crows will only leave if every plate is eaten, and the dead guests will only stay gone if no plate is. Look: Sixty-odd, wide through the chest and soft with it, a demi cook in a stained seal-gut apron, white hair piled in a towering braided arrangement that sways when they lean, a mouth set in a patient half-smile and eyes that keep checking the ceiling, powdered flour on both forearms.

## Reward item (1)
- **Wedge-Spool Tether** (ITEM, UNCOMMON) — It pays out a line of cord that holds any one object steady against wind, slope or a determined hand. Best used when: Something is sliding toward the edge and you need it to stay exactly where it is. The catch: Whatever it holds stays held for a full day, even if you change your mind. Look: a squat wooden bobbin the size of a fist, wound with waxed grey kite cord, a brass crank worn bright, a small hardwood wedge tied to the loose end
