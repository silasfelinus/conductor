## The idea
A bureaucracy of minor gods processes every prayer as paperwork, and the tiniest, fiercest clerks are the ones who actually get anything approved.

## Dream vibe (1)
**The Long Blue Docket** — Every prayer is a paper boat, and the tide never truly answers to anyone but the clerks.
Art: An endless indigo ocean-sky where thousands of folded paper boats drift under a horizon-spanning brass stamp hanging like a second moon.

## Dream location (1)
- **Far Window Customs** — It is the only intake post built on the outer edge of the Long Blue Docket, where the tide occasionally delivers a petition from outside the pantheon's own paperwork entirely. Local rule: Any petition not written in a recognized tongue must be stamped 'pending translation' rather than refused, no matter how strange its author. Best scene: A silver squid-shaped emissary surfaces through the floor with a petition nobody in the office can read, and the entire counter staff argues about whether that counts as First Contact or just bad filing. Art: A low customs booth built from reclaimed ship timber and pulse-metal piping that visibly throbs with faint light, facing an open horizon of deep blue water under a paper-boat sky.

## Character (1)
- **Prue Ashdown** — She trains every new clerk who washes ashore at Far Window, the same way she once led the first six petitioners into an empty red dust-realm nobody else wanted to claim. A black-footed cat rides in the crook of her arm, ears swiveled toward the counter, its claws already extended over any petition it decides is a lie. Complication: She only ever bends a rule by its own precise wording, and helping the red realm's independence petition through legally is taking three times as long as breaking policy would. Look: A stocky woman in a patched customs coat with pulse-metal cuffs that flicker faintly, a small black-footed cat perched on her shoulder with its ears pinned flat.

## Reward item (1)
- **The Spite-Bright Seal** (ITEM, RARE) — It approves any petition the holder can honestly call unfair to themselves, no matter how small the unfairness. Best used when: A grievance is real but too petty for anyone else to file paperwork over. The catch: It only stamps once a day, so it has to be saved for the grievance that actually matters. Look: A bog-iron stamp inlaid with a sliver of pulse-metal that pulses brighter with every petty complaint pressed into it, its handle wrapped in tarnished ribbon like a faded pageant sash.

## Reward skill (1)
- **Datestamp Sense** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — It lets the user feel exactly which decade, era, or pantheon a petition's phrasing actually belongs to, whatever era it claims. Best used when: A petition's story doesn't quite match the paper it's written on. The catch: The user can't turn it off, and modern conversations start sounding four centuries out of place. Look: A faint marginal shimmer that creeps across the edges of a document, aging or de-aging in real time until it settles on the truth.

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **The Ex-Empress's Grudge** — In The Long Blue Docket at Far Window Customs, Prue Ashdown must process a glamorously undead ex-empress's petition to have her own funeral re-catered, four centuries late and dressed for an audience that no longer exists.
