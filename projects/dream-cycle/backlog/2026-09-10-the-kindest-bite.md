## New Facets (invented for this dream)
- **Double-Counts Everything** (`double-counts-everything`, QUIRK) — A compulsive habit of tallying anything countable exactly twice before trusting the number, born from one long-ago ledger error that cost someone their life. The second count is never faster than the first.
- **The Undertow Market** (`the-undertow-market`, SETTING) — A market that only opens where some imbalance is large enough to need correcting, a town-sized debt or a season's missing harvest, and closes the instant the books balance again. It never appears in the same place twice.

## The idea
In a cozy vampire-gothic pantry ruled by strict fairness, a middle-manager vampire has to track down a debt-market that only appears where the numbers do not balance, and settle her hollow's account before the coven forecloses.

## Dream vibe (1)
**The Kindest Bite** — Every vampire here would rather ration than waste, and calls that mercy.
Art: A moonlit hollow beneath twisted briar trees, a flightless nocturnal bird perched atop a cracked marble fountain that drips something dark and thick instead of water.

## Dream location (1)
- **Hollowbrew Pantry** — A cozy tea-and-blood pantry tucked inside a hollow oak, its counter forged from a single slab of voidsteel that never needs cleaning and never quite warms up. Local rule: No patron may be served more than the pantry's kiwi-bird taster has already tasted and approved as spare. Best scene: Every dusk the kiwi-bird hops the length of the voidsteel counter tasting each new patron's offered thumb-prick before the kettle is even put on. Art: A snug hollow-oak pantry glowing amber from oil lamps, a dark voidsteel counter reflecting no warmth, dried herb bundles hanging beside jars of something too red to be jam.

## Character (1)
- **Marjoram Voss** — She has to keep the pantry's monthly count balanced enough to satisfy what the coven expects, without ever taking one drop more than the kiwi-bird has approved. A pocket abacus strung with tiny voidsteel beads, each one worn smooth from being recounted twice every single night. Complication: Her habit of counting everything twice cost the pantry a full night's takings once already, and the coven has warned her a second miscount will cost her the hollow itself. Look: A slight, unnervingly neat figure in a flour-dusted apron over old-fashioned mourning black, fingers stained faintly grey from constantly worrying a string of counting-beads.

## Reward item (1)
- **The Coven's Overdraft Thimble** (ITEM, RARE) — It measures out the exact smallest amount of anything, blood, coin, or time, that will still settle a debt in full. Best used when: A debt too large to pay outright needs to be reduced to something survivable instead. The catch: It never measures generously, so whatever it grants is always the bare legal minimum, and never a drop of goodwill more. Look: A thimble-sized cap of dull voidsteel, dented as though something enormous once tried to wear it, cool to the touch even beside an open flame.

## Reward skill (1)
- **Exact Measure** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — It lets its user draw off exactly the amount of strength, breath, or blood needed to settle a debt, and stop the instant that amount is reached. Best used when: Taking too much would tip a fair trade into an outright theft. The catch: It cannot be used to take less than what is owed either, so mercy beyond the exact number is impossible while it is active. Look: A hand closing around a wrist with unnatural, mechanical precision, releasing the instant a faint chime only the user can hear finishes ringing.

- **High Noon at the Undertow Market** — In The Kindest Bite at Hollowbrew Pantry, Marjoram Voss has to track the Undertow Market to wherever it has surfaced tonight and settle the pantry's overdue account before the coven forecloses on the whole hollow.
