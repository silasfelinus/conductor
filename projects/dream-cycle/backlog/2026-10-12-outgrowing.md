## The idea
A weasel at a plateau bake-off swallowed an enchanted pastry and grew to the size of a barn for good, so the whole tournament reorganizes around one enormous, permanent and delighted judge.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Outgrowing** — A weasel ate the wrong pastry at a meadow bake-off, and now the tournament cooks for a guest the size of a barn who is never going to shrink.
Art: Hard noon daylight on a flat mown hay meadow, crisp black shadows under striped canvas tents and copper pans, wind lifting a long plume of flour and chaff, and a cream-and-chestnut weasel the height of a grain silo sitting upright at the meadow's edge.

## Dream location (1)
- **Mowfield** — This is a high plateau hay meadow that is cut once a year for a baking tournament, with a ring of pale blue angelite oven stones set into the stubble. Local rule: Every contestant is attended by a rival's second, who carries their trays and is also baking against them. Best scene: Hard noon light throws the tent shadows across the stubble as the barn-sized weasel sits up on its haunches, and forty seconds kneel at once to slide their trays toward it. Art: a flat mown hay meadow under hard noon sun with crisp black shadows, a ring of chalky sky-blue angelite oven stones, striped canvas judging tents, gusts of chaff, a barn-sized cream and chestnut weasel sitting upright at the far edge

## Character (1)
- **Ilkay Brodersen** — They are serving as rival's second to the baker whose weasel grew, and they intend to win the tournament without letting anyone pretend the weasel can be made small again. A brass piping gun built by the meadow's Afrofuturist solar-kitchen cooperative hangs from their belt beside a long thin yufka rolling pin from their grandmother's plateau village and a dented rig-town flask of strong black tea. Complication: The pastry the weasel swallowed was theirs and not their rival's, and they have not mentioned this to anyone yet. Look: Somewhere between twenty-five and seventy depending on where you look, with a smooth unlined face above knuckly, spotted hands, a scalp shaved to bare tan skin, leaning on a hickory stick wrapped in orange rig tape, wearing a kilim-patterned waistcoat over a flour-streaked oilskin smock, one eyebrow permanently raised and their gaze resting just past whoever is talking.

## Reward item (1)
- **Angelite Peg** (ITEM, UNCOMMON) — Pulled from the earth, it returns the patch of ground around it to exactly how it was before anyone camped there. Best used when: A gathering is over and the land it borrowed has to be handed back. The catch: It cannot tell a trampled flower from a burrow that was dug yesterday, so it undoes both. Look: a palm-long tent peg of chalky sky-blue angelite with a machined steel collar stamped with a survey grid, its head bound in frayed orange rope, grass stains up one side

## Reward skill (1)
- **Spun-Sugar Change** (SKILL, RARE) — It wraps the baker in a whirl of spun sugar that sets into a ceremonial costume fit for judging, serving or calming a giant. Best used when: A moment needs ceremony and there is no time to change clothes. The catch: The costume is real sugar, so every weasel in the meadow can smell it from a long way off. Look: a person turning inside a fast spiral of glassy amber sugar threads in hard daylight, the threads setting into a crisp layered costume with a piping-bag sash and a collar of brittle lace
