## New Facets (invented for this dream)
- **Dreamweight Kin** (`dreamweight-kin`, SPECIES) — A kind whose body gains mass and slows with every dream it has fed on, the years showing as accumulated weight rather than as years. Shedding that old dream-matter comes out through the coat, in patches, whether or not the kind is ready to let it go.
- **Afterglow Handler** (`afterglow-handler`, OCCUPATION) — The one who arrives once the feeding is done, to contain and cool whatever residual glow it left behind in the room or in the eater. The job ends only when the shimmer stops answering back.

## The idea
The dream-eating tournament ended hours ago, and the hyena who placed dead last is the only contestant still awake, molting last season's borrowed dreams out through his own coat while the afterglow handlers try to get him to hold still long enough to be swept up.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Molthollow** — The tournament ended hours ago; the losers are the only ones still awake enough to notice what it cost them.
Art: A slumped, heavyset hyena-beast shedding matted, faintly glowing fur in clumps under floodlights already being switched off, arena seating empty behind him.

## Dream location (1)
- **Barkhollow** — A stadium ring grown fresh each season from living Enchanted Bark for the annual dream-eating tournament, torn out again by the crowd as souvenirs before the last match photo is even taken. Local rule: Nobody sweeps the ring floor until every shed clump from a Dreamweight Kin has been picked over by the crowd, since a scrap of a champion's molted coat still carries a trace of whatever dream it finished eating. Best scene: The floodlights cut out mid-molt, and for one dark second every shed clump still on the ring floor keeps glowing on its own. Art: A stadium ring grown from pale living bark, wet floor littered with shed, faintly glowing fur clumps, empty stands beyond the light's edge.

## Character (1)
- **Bartol Yaghan** — He placed dead last in the tournament and is cheerfully trying to match every glowing clump still shedding out of his own coat to the sleeping stranger it belongs to, so he can return each dream personally. A coil of tarred barge-rope looped over one shoulder, the kind his family used to lash river cargo, now used to bundle his own shed fur before it can wander off glowing. Complication: Every bundle he ties keeps answering back in a different sleeping stranger's voice, and he can no longer tell which rope-wrapped clump holds which dream. Look: A spotted hyena built tall and heavy, shoulders gone thick and slow with age, coat coming away in dull, faintly glowing clumps everywhere he moves. A wide grin sits loose and easy on his face, eyes a little unfocused, like he hasn't quite caught up with his own bad news yet. A tarred barge-rope crosses one shoulder, and a coarse, windproof wool wrap knotted twice around his heavy waist.

## Reward item (1)
- **Handler's Gloves** (ITEM, RARE) — Worn, they lift a residual dream-glow off fur, floor, or skin without waking whatever the glow is still carrying. Best used when: A dream-eater has finished feeding and the leftover shimmer needs to come off something before it settles in for good. The catch: The glow they lift has to go somewhere; wear them twice in one sitting and your own hands start humming faintly by morning. Look: A pair of scorched canvas gloves gone glossy black at the palms, a faint warm shimmer caught permanently in the seams, hanging from a bark-fiber loop.

## Reward skill (1)
- **Coatreading** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — It reads which sleeper a shed clump of dream-matter belongs to just by the pattern the fur fell in. Best used when: A dream-eater's molt has scattered before anyone can tell whose dreams are whose. The catch: It only works on fur that has already come loose on its own; pull a live clump early to check and the reading comes back as somebody else's dream entirely. Look: A hand hovering an inch above a scattered clump of shed, faintly glowing fur, the clump slowly re-forming the ghost-shape of a sleeping face.

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **Barkhollow Morning** — In Molthollow at Barkhollow, the morning after the tournament, Bartol Yaghan tries to match every glowing clump of his own molted coat to the sleeper it belongs to before the arena crew starts hauling out the torn-up bark floor.
