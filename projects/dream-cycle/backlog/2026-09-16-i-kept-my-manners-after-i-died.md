## The idea
In a domed Martian mining colony, a vein of living Astral Ore slowly reanimates the recently dead into permanent, quartz-veined versions of themselves who keep every habit they had in life. Renata Voss, the colony's Threshold Warden, walks each newly grown colonist through their irreversible change — until the vein grows someone who won't wait to be walked through it.

## Dream vibe (1)
**I Kept My Manners After I Died** — Nobody in Cutbrace Hollow got faster after dying; they only got more careful with each other.
Art: A pastel-lit greenhouse dome under a rust-orange Martian sky, translucent quartz-veined figures tending trellised vines beside living colonists, everyone queued politely at a single hand pump.

## Dream location (1)
- **Cutbrace Hollow** — The reinforced airlock chamber between the colony's living quarters and the raw Astral Ore vein, where the newly reanimated complete their first growth before rejoining the community. Local rule: Anyone still growing through the veil is escorted, never rushed, until their new senses finish settling. Best scene: A line of newly grown colonists waits one after another at the inner airlock, translucent quartz veins still spreading up their forearms, each one patiently practicing the handshake they used to give. Art: A narrow pressurized stone corridor lit by embedded blue-veined ore seams, one wall rough unfinished rock and the other lined with reinforced glass, condensation beading over faint root-like growths threading through hairline cracks.

## Character (1)
- **Renata Voss** — As Cutbrace Hollow's Threshold Warden, she personally walks every newly reanimated colonist through their first days of growth, treating the job as equal parts triage nurse, etiquette coach, and structural inspector. A hand-grown astral-ore cane she swaps out every few weeks, endlessly convinced the next one will finally read a body's condition without lying to her. Complication: Her own hands have started growing the same quartz veining she inspects in others, and no cane she has tried yet will tell her how far along she already is. Look: A stocky woman in a scale-plated work coat, one forearm laced with fine blue-white quartz veins beneath faintly translucent skin, close-cropped grey hair, knuckles callused from gripping cane after cane.

## Reward item (1)
- **Verdigris Divining Cane** (ITEM, RARE) — Reveals which parts of a changing body, wall, or vein have finished settling into their new shape and which are still moving. Best used when: A transformation has stalled partway and no one can tell whether it is still progressing or already finished. The catch: It only reads change already underway; it cannot start, slow, or reverse a transformation it detects. Look: A slender astral-ore cane grown rather than carved, its handle a fused knot of pale root fiber, faint blue veining threaded through the shaft that brightens wherever the tip touches something mid-change.

Look: A steady hand slowly flexing a found stick along its length, ear tilted close to it, eyes half-closed while a light scatter of bark or mineral dust falls with each test.
