## New Facets (invented for this dream)
- **Standing Spare** (`standing-spare`, ARCHETYPE) — A person or post kept in readiness to replace one that has not yet failed. Being needed is the whole purpose, and the price is that being used means somebody else has already lost.
- **Sodium Linocut** (`sodium-linocut`, STYLE) — Chunky relief-print imagery in black and a single glaring orange, with visible gouges and off-register edges. It makes every light source look harsh and every shadow look carved, and it leaves no room for subtle colour.

## The idea
A dystopian city keeps a reserve sun locked in a starsteel tower, and the adolescent catalogued creature who is trained to replace its keeper has fallen in love with the one thing in the tower that must never be looked at.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Romance at the Reserve Lamp** — Everyone in the city is promised a sunrise, and the two people who tend the spare one would rather it never came.
Art: A narrow starsteel tower under a brown evening sky, one huge shuttered lamp glowing orange through the louvres, quicksilver-bright rain running down the walls, tiny figures on a catwalk holding hands in the glare, flat black and orange shapes like a linocut.

## Dream location (1)
- **Standby Sun Annex** — A windowless starsteel tower at the edge of the ration districts that houses the city's reserve sun, a lamp the size of a boiler kept polished and cold in case the official one ever fails. Local rule: The spare lamp is polished every day and may never be turned to face east, because the building's catalogue lists it as an object that changes anyone who watches it rise. Best scene: The keeper and the trainee slide the louvres closed together at dusk, and the lamp's reflection crawls across the mercurial puddles on the catwalk while they pretend not to be holding the same rope. Art: A tall windowless tower of blue-grey starsteel plates riveted in rows, a boiler-sized shuttered lamp glowing dull orange behind louvres, mercury-bright puddles on a rain-slick iron catwalk, a standing-spare's folding chair beside a closed door, low sodium light and hard black shadows.

## Character (1)
- **Ilse Zaitoun** — They are an SCP entity who is the standing spare for the annex's aging lamp keeper, trained for years to take over the day the keeper falters, and they want to find a reason to be needed that is not someone else's failure. A pair of oversized leather paw-guards oiled with olive soap, and a pocket-sized brass lamp key worn smooth at the bow. Complication: The glass-skinned hound the annex catalogues as Entry Nine has started to follow them home, and it only calms when they sing, which the annex has forbidden. Look: An adolescent catalogued entity, squat and dense as a cast-iron stove at knee height to a grown keeper, with paws already enormous for the frame and a matte black glassy hide that drinks the lamp light, a stiff crest of coarse fur raised in a comb and dyed rust orange, a soot-grey work vest mended at the seams with olive-green patches in careful ladder stitch, round dark eyes and a flat, unimpressed mouth that tilts slightly toward the lamp whenever it dims.

## Reward item (1)
- **Spare Key of Starsteel** (ITEM, RARE) — It relights anything that has gone dark for exactly as long as it was dark before, restoring each flame to its former strength. Best used when: A fire, a lamp, or a hope has gone out and the people around it have not yet decided to give up. The catch: Whatever it relights takes its brightness from the nearest warm thing, and that is usually the person holding the key. Look: A forearm-long key forged from blue-grey starsteel with a heavy ring bow, the teeth cut like a tiny skyline, a thin thread of orange light leaking from its keyhole end, hard sodium light on the scratches.

## Reward skill (1)

Best used when: A newcomer must cross a district that treats outsiders as a problem to be solved. The catch: At every doorway afterward the user forgets for a moment which gait was their own.
