## The idea
After the invasion stalled, two species share one wrecked warship, and peace holds because every promise between them is woven into a sash worn where everyone can see whether it is still being kept.

## Dream vibe (1)
**The Silk Bargain** — Peace here is a promise you have to wear in public.
Art: A vast wrecked hull opened to the sky and filled with hanging woven sashes glowing faint gold, two very different silhouettes standing close under them, dusk light through torn plating.

## Dream location (1)
- **The Wedding Hulk** — The Wedding Hulk is a downed warship where two peoples gather to make their promises out loud. Local rule: You must wear what you promised where everyone can see it. Best scene: Hundreds of sashes dim and brighten across one hall as promises are kept or dropped. Art: A cathedral-sized breached hull, rain coming through the tear, hundreds of woven sashes strung between girders and glowing pale gold, scorched deck plate below swept clean and laid with mats.

## Character (1)
- **Reyna Odum** — She holds every promise in the hulk straight in her head so nobody has to be taken at their word. A spare undyed sash rests folded over her arm, ready for anyone who arrives without one of their own, for she carries nothing written down. Complication: She is keeping one promise she has told absolutely no one about. Look: A steady, unhurried woman in a plain grey work coat, sleeves rolled, close-cropped hair going white at the temple, an undyed sash folded over one shoulder and no ornament of any kind.

## Reward item (1)
- **First-Light Sash** (ITEM, RARE) — It shows at a glance whether the promise woven into it is still being kept. Best used when: Two people need to trust each other faster than trust normally takes, with no time to prove themselves the slow way. The catch: It shows everyone, not only the two who made it. Look: A broad woven sash holding the colours of a sunrise in its threads, gold running to rose along its length, one end frayed and rewoven in a slightly different shade.

## Reward skill (1)
- **The Long Telling** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — It settles a quarrel by telling it back as a story both sides recognise. Best used when: Both sides are certain of their own account and neither one is lying, only remembering the same events differently. The catch: It takes all night, and everyone has to stay. Look: A ring of seated listeners lit only by the sash-glow of the one speaking, that light strengthening steadily as the telling goes on.

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **The Sash That Went Dark** — In The Silk Bargain, in the middle of The Wedding Hulk, the oldest promise in the hall goes dark during a crowded evening, and Reyna Odum has until dawn to tell it back correctly before both sides decide the other broke it first.
