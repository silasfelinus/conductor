## The idea
A fallen invasion ship becomes a commons where survivors of two species rebuild trust one recorded debt at a time.

## Dream vibe (1)

## Character (1)
- **Odalys Marsh** — keep the commons' shared ledger honest enough that both species still trust it. Carries a slim alloy tally-wand salvaged from the wreck, wrapped at the grip in aether-silk cord. Complication: she keeps quietly forgiving the debts of the one vendor she can't stop watching. Look: a compact woman in a patched grey overcoat lined with strips of shimmering aether-silk, a tally-wand tucked behind one ear like a pencil, ink-stained fingertips.

## Reward item (1)
- **The Debtless Sash** (ITEM, RARE) — reveals every debt and favor owed to whoever wears it. Best used when a community's trust is fraying and no one agrees anymore who owes whom. The catch: it reveals the wearer's own outstanding debts just as plainly. Look: a wide woven sash of shifting silver-white aether-silk, patterned with beadwork knots in indigo and rust that catch light like a starfield, a fringe of small bone tally-beads clicking at the hem.

Best used when the written records are gone and only memory can settle what's owed. The catch: the recitation leaves out nothing, including the reciter's own debts. Look: a speaker standing at the commons' center at dusk, one hand tracing invisible tally-marks in the air as the recitation unspools, fine motes of aether-silk fiber drifting up from their sleeve with each name spoken.
