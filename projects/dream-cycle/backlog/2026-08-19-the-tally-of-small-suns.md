## The idea
A fallen invasion ship becomes a commons where survivors of two species rebuild trust one recorded debt at a time.

## Dream vibe (1)
**The Tally of Small Suns** — Every debt from the invasion gets counted, and every count gets rebuilt into something worth keeping.
Art: A collapsed alien needle-ship's ribbed spine, the size of a cathedral nave, wrapped in overlapping sheets of silver-white aether-silk that catch dusk light like fish scales; strings of small paper lanterns hang between the ribs.

## Dream location (1)
- **The Driftspine Commons** — known for neutral trade between the two species who survived the landing. Local rule: no debt goes unlisted on the public chalk ledger strung along the spine. Best scene: a goblin-shark-jawed vendor forgives a debt out loud, and the whole commons hears it. Art: Market stalls hung from a curved alien hull rib on ropes of braided aether-silk, chalk ledger boards swinging between them, silt-grey light filtering through torn hull plating overhead.

## Character (1)
- **Odalys Marsh** — keep the commons' shared ledger honest enough that both species still trust it. Carries a slim alloy tally-wand salvaged from the wreck, wrapped at the grip in aether-silk cord. Complication: she keeps quietly forgiving the debts of the one vendor she can't stop watching. Look: a compact woman in a patched grey overcoat lined with strips of shimmering aether-silk, a tally-wand tucked behind one ear like a pencil, ink-stained fingertips.

## Reward item (1)
- **The Debtless Sash** (ITEM, RARE) — reveals every debt and favor owed to whoever wears it. Best used when a community's trust is fraying and no one agrees anymore who owes whom. The catch: it reveals the wearer's own outstanding debts just as plainly. Look: a wide woven sash of shifting silver-white aether-silk, patterned with beadwork knots in indigo and rust that catch light like a starfield, a fringe of small bone tally-beads clicking at the hem.

## Reward skill (1)
- **The Nightly Reckoning** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — recites an entire night's tally from memory, turning a list of debts and gifts into a story no one can dispute. Best used when the written records are gone and only memory can settle what's owed. The catch: the recitation leaves out nothing, including the reciter's own debts. Look: a speaker standing at the commons' center at dusk, one hand tracing invisible tally-marks in the air as the recitation unspools, fine motes of aether-silk fiber drifting up from their sleeve with each name spoken.

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **The Shark Who Kept Count** — In The Tally of Small Suns at The Driftspine Commons, Odalys Marsh is handed an impossible debt by the goblin-shark-jawed trader who runs the west rib stalls: not silk, not grain, but the truth of why she keeps forgiving him.
