## New Facets (invented for this dream)
- **Counted by Breath** (`counted-by-breath`, STYLE) — A record-keeping convention where duration, dosage, or distance is measured against a living thing's own exhalations rather than a clock. Precision follows whatever the creature or system in question is willing to do that day, not the other way around.
- **Failed Its Own Inspection** (`failed-its-own-inspection`, BACKSTORY) — Whatever holds this role once failed the very standard it now measures others against, and the failure was never expunged from the record, only outlived. It enforces the rule with total sincerity and no right to feel superior about it.

## The idea
A compliance officer who failed the same kind of fitness review decades ago now grades a colony organism's health from a checklist every shift, and the colony is about to demonstrate, mid-inspection, that its own rhythm was never actually subordinate to anyone's paperwork.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Gillstack** — A living reef stacked from thousands of breathing gill-membranes filters an alien moon's atmosphere on its own schedule, and no inspector's checklist has ever once made it hurry.
Art: A cliff-face reef built from overlapping translucent membrane sacs, each one visibly inflating and collapsing with its own breath, wet grey-pink surfaces catching low amber starlight, fine spore-dust drifting off the newest growth at the reef's crown.

## Dream location (1)
- **Spore Shelf** — Every membrane sac inflates and empties on its own rhythm, so the whole reef never breathes in unison twice. Local rule: No inspector may average two breath-counts into a single number. Best scene: A membrane sac that has stayed collapsed for three straight counts reinflates at last, and the whole reef's throb visibly changes pitch around it. Art: A cliff of overlapping translucent gill-membranes in wet grey-pink, each sac ringed with fine cartilage struts, spore-dust drifting off newer growth at the crown, hard amber starlight raking low across the whole wall.

## Character (1)
- **Bereg Voss** — They log the reef's breath-counts every shift and sign off on what passes, but the numbers Bereg writes down are only ever a lagging echo of a decision the reef has long since made for itself. A cattle-hide notebook stitched at the spine with sealskin cord, one material carried up from each half of a childhood split between two coasts, its pages dense with breath-count tallies and the odd cryptic proverb Bereg jots down between readings. Complication: Bereg's own faded ruff has started collapsing on the same ragged rhythm as the reef's failing sacs, and no inspection report has a column for an inspector's body agreeing with what it is only supposed to measure. Look: A spotted hyena well past prime, ribs and haunches showing lean beneath a coat gone patchy, ruff dyed a faded rainbow now bleached pale at the tips, ears carrying old notches from a life before this posting. A hand-braided cord of mixed-dye cattle-hair sits knotted around one foreleg where the outpost's binary crew tags would normally go, self-marked rather than issued. Jaw hangs a little open at rest, tongue lolling, the unbothered half-grin of something that stopped being surprised by anything it inspects long ago.

## Reward item (1)
- **Rejected Seal** (ITEM, RARE) — It stamps any inspection report as failed, permanently, even over an authority that outranks whoever is holding it. Best used when: An approval is about to be signed off on political pressure alone, with nobody in the room willing to be the one who says no. The catch: The seal was rejected once itself, badly, and using it again means the whole room learns exactly why, your own authority included. Look: A dented ghost-iron inspection seal, cold and faintly grey against the palm, its center panel stamped REJECTED in a typeface long out of production and overstruck with a second, newer approval stamp that never quite covers the first.

## Reward skill (1)
- **Proverb Reading** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — It turns one of a notebook's cryptic self-written proverbs into the one true instruction for whatever is happening right now. Best used when: A decision has to be made with no clean data, and the only thing left in reach is a stack of your own old handwriting. The catch: Every proverb was written by a slightly earlier version of you, so the instruction it gives is always honest and only sometimes still good advice. Look: A gloved hand runs down a page of cramped handwriting until it stops, unbidden, on a single underlined line, fingertip pressed flat against the paper as if holding it still.

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **Overbreath** — At Gillstack, on the Spore Shelf where Bereg Voss logs every membrane sac's breath-count each shift, the whole reef's rhythm shifts audibly out from under the last three inspection cycles, and the shelf's own throb, not Bereg's tally sheet, is what the rest of the outpost ends up trusting.
