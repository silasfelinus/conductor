## New Facets (invented for this dream)
- **Tide Notary** (`tide-notary`, OCCUPATION) — A licensed witness who presses a mantis-shrimp-etched runestone to a signer's palm, binding an oral oath into stone the sea itself is trusted to remember. The stone never forgets a broken promise, and neither does whoever it was pressed against.
- **Floodline Precinct** (`floodline-precinct`, SETTING) — A district whose legal jurisdiction changes with the tide, since the water itself redraws the official boundary twice a day. Whoever holds authority there only holds it until the schedule says otherwise.

## The idea
A sunken city rules its living heirs through oaths that only a rare shrimp's color vision can verify, and a Tide Notary must reopen the one deal that would erase her flooded home village.

## Dream vibe (1)
**The Drowned Compact** — Every political treaty in the compact is only as real as the color no ordinary eye can read.
Art: A council chamber built half below the waterline, robed petitioners kneeling before a submerged dais lit by carved runestones, a mantis shrimp drifting through a shaft of fractured light overhead.

## Dream location (1)
- **The Ebb Chancery** — A half-submerged chancery where Tide Notaries press runestones to petitioners' palms to seal treaties with the sea, open only during the two hours a day the water allows entry. Local rule: No treaty may be witnessed after the tide turns; a stone pressed even one minute past the ebb binds no one. Best scene: A runestone cracks mid-ceremony, and the mantis shrimp kept in the chancery's central tank scatter toward the one witness whose oath it never fully accepted. Art: A half-drowned stone hall, waterline chest-high at high tide, carved oath-stones stacked along dry upper shelves, a glass tank of mantis shrimp glowing faintly at the chamber's center.

## Character (1)
- **Isa Vey** — She is trying to reopen a treaty her own signature helped seal, one that will erase her flooded home village from the compact within the week. A dry ledger of every hiccup she's ever predicted mid-hearing, a private tally she uses to time when a nervous witness is about to slip. Complication: The only way to reopen the treaty is to admit, on record, that she mistimed a hiccup three years ago and missed the lie it was covering. Look: A tide-worn woman in a salt-stiffened notary's coat, oath-cord scars circling one wrist, head tilted in permanent listening posture, watching throats instead of faces

## Reward item (1)
- **Split Vow** (ITEM, RARE) — It marks the exact moment a promise between two people becomes legally binding within Floodline Precinct's shifting jurisdiction. Best used when: Sealing a personal vow you want the shifting tideline law to actually recognize as binding. The catch: It only holds while the jurisdiction it was sealed under is still in effect, and the same tide that makes it binding can turn it back into an ordinary rock. Look: A palm-sized grey stone split cleanly in half, each half warm and faintly damp, a hairline vein of blue light running along the fracture only when both halves touch

## Reward skill (1)
- **Threshold Hum** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — It lets the wearer feel, as a low hum behind the teeth, whether a room's occupants currently mean anyone inside harm. Best used when: Walking into any hearing, chamber, or gathering where you can't verify everyone's intentions ahead of time. The catch: The hum never distinguishes between one hostile stranger and a room full of people who are merely furious at you. Look: A faint vibration visible only as a ripple across the surface of any nearby standing water, syncing with the user's own held breath
