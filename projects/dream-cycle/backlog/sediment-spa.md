## The idea
A bathhouse carved into a slot canyon where you soak in the colored mineral
strata, and each band of rock soaks a different decade out of your bones. Bathe in
the red layer and shed a hard year; float in the pale-green seam and remember a
summer you thought you'd lost. Ancient, echoing, warm-water calm; the deep time of
stone made gentle enough to sit in.

## Location dream
**The Sediment Spa** — LOCATION. Terraced soaking pools cut into banded canyon
rock, steam rising between strata that glow faintly by their age, stone benches
smoothed by ten thousand quiet afternoons. Flavor: "Soak to the layer you can bear.
The canyon keeps the rest." Art direction: warm ochre-and-rose canyon walls,
glowing mineral seams, turquoise steaming pools, soft desert-dusk light, serene.

## Characters (2-4)
- **The Strata-Keeper** — a slow, lichen-cloaked attendant who knows which layer
  holds which year and never lets a bather soak deeper than they're ready for.
- **Pell, the Skipping Stone** — a restless young bather who keeps trying to reach
  the oldest pool at the bottom; the canyon keeps floating them gently back up.
- **The Echo** — a voice in the deepest seam that answers only questions you
  already know the answer to; possibly just the canyon, possibly a former bather who stayed.

## Rewards (3-6)
- **A Warm Red Hour** (COMMON) — soak one hard year loose; it floats off like silt.
- **Pale-Green Seam Water** (UNCOMMON) — a vial that returns one good memory whenever opened.
- **The Strata-Keeper's Reading** (RARE) — learn exactly which layer of yourself a feeling is buried in.
- **A Stone from the Oldest Pool** (LEGENDARY) — hold the long view in your palm; nothing feels like the end of the world again.

## Scenarios (1-2)
- **Too Deep, Too Soon** — Pell has slipped to a layer they can't handle alone;
  the player must coax them up one warm band at a time before the old cold sets in.
- **What the Echo Asks Back** — the deepest seam keeps returning the player's own
  question; the Strata-Keeper hints the only way out is to finally answer it.
