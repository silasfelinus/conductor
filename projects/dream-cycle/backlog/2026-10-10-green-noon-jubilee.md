## The idea
Under a green sky, a market town holds its yearly parade of glamorous dead and four thousand daylight moths, and nobody can say whether the garden they tend is one thing or many.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Green Noon Jubilee** — A festival where the dead dress up and the sky wears green.
Art: Bold green noon sky over a packed market square, glittering dead in sequined finery beside living stallholders, swarms of pale moths overhead, oversized paper banners, heaped fruit, saffron and magenta bunting.

## Dream location (1)
- **Ghostsilk Plaza** — A wide flat market plaza where travellers from other worlds arrive at noon among stalls hung with sheets of pale specter silk. Local rule: Every stall must keep a spare costume ready, because a dead guest may claim any seat at the table. Best scene: Thousands of moths drift through the green daylight while living and dead dancers share the same sunlit paving. Art: A wide flat sunlit plaza, striped awnings, translucent specter-silk banners lifting in the wind, heaped chillies, melons and bread, dancers in sequins, a green sky above.

## Character (1)
- **Ximena Brzezina** — They have memorised every move of the festival's glamorous dead queen and stand ready to take her place in the parade. A pair of steel-toed boots polished like mirrors, a hand-woven palm mat for kneeling, and a small tin of saffron-coloured face paint. Complication: Their copying is so exact that the crowd sometimes bows to them when they only meant to wave. Look: A young person, curved and deliberate in build, with dark hair cut badly and recently, uneven fringe and a shaved patch, wearing a sequined parade cape over blue steel-works overalls, a woven palm hat, serious careful eyes with a held, nearly-smiling mouth, a large pale moth resting on one shoulder.

## Reward item (1)
- **Bragging Sash** (ITEM, UNCOMMON) — It dresses its wearer in the full glamour of a beloved ancestor for as long as the music plays. Best used when: A celebration needs someone to stand in for a guest who could not come. The catch: Everyone praises the sash and nobody remembers the wearer's face. Look: A broad sash of pale translucent specter silk, saffron stitches along its edge, drifting upward in still air, a small paper rosette pinned to one end.

## Reward skill (1)
- **Other Street** (SKILL, RARE) — It tells a short story so well that the crowd briefly walks a street that went another way. Best used when: A market has fallen quiet and nobody can imagine anything different. The catch: Anything bought on the other street stays there when it fades. Look: A market street splitting into two translucent copies, one washed in green light and one in gold, stalls and awnings overlapping like layered glass.

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **Four Thousand Wings** — In Green Noon Jubilee, at Ghostsilk Plaza, Ximena Brzezina and the whole market watch four thousand moths settle over a garden no one can look at twice the same way, and the first stalls begin opening beneath it.
