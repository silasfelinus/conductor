## Creative seeds
- **Genres:** Dieselpunk + Comedy
- **Occupation:** zeppelin ballast-tank technician
- **Animal / species:** raccoons
- **Fusion:** Raccoons' small dexterous paws are the literal job requirement for reaching ballast-tank machinery no human hand fits, and their nocturnal vision makes them the yard's real, unofficial night shift; their hoarding instinct then becomes the world's central economic and comedic engine, since every spare bolt and gasket they instinctively cache turns routine maintenance into a black-market negotiation.

## The idea
Grease Paws is the night-shift world of a zeppelin dry-dock where nimble-pawed raccoon tank crews are the only ones small enough to fit inside the ballast machinery keeping every airship aloft. The whole operation runs on pilfered bolts, unspoken debts, and one exhausted human supervisor's creative paperwork.

## Vibe / genre dream
**Ballast & Bribery** — GENRE. Every zeppelin here flies on stolen bolts and favors nobody wrote down.

## Characters (3)
- **Tumble** — runs the night-shift ballast crews and keeps every zeppelin in dock actually airworthy, whether the company admits it or not Carries a bent brass ballast-valve wrench she's used so long it fits no other paw Complication: hoards "lucky" rivets so compulsively that she's sitting on half the yard's missing-parts crisis and won't admit it even to herself Look: silver-muzzled raccoon in a patched leather tank-crawling harness, one ear notched from an old valve blowback, headlamp goggles pushed up on her forehead
- **Odessa Crace** — keeps the daylight paperwork clean enough that corporate never has to learn the yard runs on an unofficial raccoon night shift Carries a ledger she deliberately fills with creative misdirection instead of facts Complication: has started leaving out "rodent deterrent bait" that both of them know is actually payment, and can't decide if that makes her complicit or just honest Look: soot-streaked overalls, a monocle-gauge for reading tank pressure, perpetually one strong coffee behind on sleep
- **Splice** — runs the Understack's parts trade and knows exactly where every missing bolt in the yard ended up Carries a jury-rigged brass scale for weighing trades that always seems to favor her Complication: will let a zeppelin sit grounded for hours if the trade offered for a needed gasket isn't interesting enough Look: lean raccoon with a chest harness of clinking trade-charms, one gold tooth traded rather than grown, permanently amused expression

## Rewards (2 — one skill, one item)
- **Tank-Crawl Reflex** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — the dexterity and nerve to move through machinery-tight spaces a human body shouldn't fit through Best used when a mechanical puzzle or tight passage blocks the only way forward The catch: you pick up the hoarding instinct along with the reflex -- small shiny objects start migrating into your pockets whether you meant to take them or not
- **Tumble's Bent Wrench** (ITEM, LEGENDARY) — loosens or tightens any valve, bolt, or fitting regardless of rust, size, or how badly it was over-tightened Best used when one impossible last fix stands between you and a working engine The catch: every raccoon in the yard recognizes it on sight and assumes you owe Splice a favor for having it -- whether you do yet or not

## Scenarios (1-2)
- **The Vanishing Valve** — a cargo zeppelin's ballast gasket has gone missing hours before a scheduled dawn launch, and Tumble's crew has to strike a trade with Splice in the Understack before sunrise while Odessa stalls the daylight inspectors with increasingly implausible paperwork
- **Audit Night** — corporate sends a surprise inventory auditor to the yard, and humans and raccoons have to quietly coordinate, for the first time semi-officially, to hide three shifts' worth of hoarded contraband before the auditor's morning walkthrough
