## The idea
A starship pilot demoted to a sea-otter admiralty's filing trench must out-bureaucracy an alien archive before the tide erases her file.

## Dream vibe (1)
**The Kelp-Ink Transfer** — One passive-aggressive memo away from mutiny, and nobody will say whose.
Art: A cramped orbital office where kelp fronds grow through the seams of steel desks, otters in tarnished brass epaulettes stamping forms under flickering starlight.

## Dream location (1)
- **The Low-Tide Ledger** — known for settling filing disputes by tide schedule. Local rule: no request outlives the tide. Best scene: a filed grievance floods the archive. Art: A glacial archive carved into a wall of cosmic ice, brass filing cabinets sunk waist-deep into blue-white ice, kelp fronds frozen mid-drift between the drawers, otter clerks perched along shelves gnawing sealing wax.

## Character (1)
- **Bristle Voss** — prove her downgrade from starship pilot to filing clerk was a clerical error, not a demotion. Carries a dented pilot's chronometer that now ticks backwards. Complication: it is counting down to something buried in the archive. Look: a sleek sea otter in a cracked void-suit collar over ink-stained fur, whiskers threaded with fine brass wire, one webbed paw permanently stained with kelp-ink.

## Reward item (1)
- **The Unclosed Requisition** (ITEM, RARE) — reopens any denied request once. Best used when a rejection was declared final. The catch: it reopens something else along with it. Look: a frost-rimed brass stamp, its face carved into a small open mouth instead of a seal, cold enough to smoke faintly in warm air.

## Reward skill (1)
- **Dead-Reckoning Filing** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — navigates any bureaucracy by feel alone, no map or index needed. Best used when the archive has rearranged itself. The catch: you stop trusting solid ground afterward. Look: a spread webbed paw trailing faint star-chart lines of light across a bare desk that has no business holding them.

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **The Tide That Reads Your File** — In The Kelp-Ink Transfer at The Low-Tide Ledger, Bristle Voss must refile her own transfer order before the next tide erases the archive's memory of her ever being a starship pilot.
