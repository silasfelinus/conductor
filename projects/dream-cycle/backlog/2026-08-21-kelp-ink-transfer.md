## The idea
A grounded starship pilot takes the worst job on a sea-otter kelp rig — the deep shift, alone under the canopy — and finds that the thing the crew will not discuss is already waiting at the bottom of her first descent.

## Dream vibe (1)
**The Deep Shift** — Everyone here is very calm about what happens on the night shift.
Art: A cramped underwater work rig lit by yellow deck lamps, dense kelp stems rising past its windows into black water, a single diver's lamp far below in the murk, frost creeping across the inside of the glass.

## Dream location (1)
- **Kelphold Rig** — A kelp canopy so dense nobody goes under it alone, except whoever draws the deep shift. Local rule: When the lamps go amber, you come up immediately, whatever you are holding. Best scene: Every lamp on the rig goes amber at once, and nobody moves toward the ladder. Art: A squat riveted underwater rig furred with growth, yellow lamps in cages, harvest cradles heaped with dripping kelp, cold ice-blue light filtering down through a canopy far overhead.

## Character (1)
- **Imogen Halvard** — She works the deep shift, alone under the canopy, for enough hazard pay to buy back her flight certification. She still wears her grounded pilot's helmet underwater, scarred and out of regulation, simply because it is hers. Complication: She keeps proposing better ways to run the rig, and the crew keeps quietly declining to explain why none of her ideas will work. Look: A rangy woman in a patched dive suit two decades out of date, scarred flight helmet under her arm, salt-bleached hair, forearms corded from hauling, a burn scar across one cheek.

## Reward item (1)
- **Coldwater Bell** (ITEM, RARE) — It rings once, everywhere on the rig at once, the instant something below has noticed you. Best used when: The water has gone quiet in a way that feels deliberate, not empty, the kind of stillness that means something is choosing not to move. The catch: The same ring that warns the crew also tells whatever is below exactly where the bell, and you, are standing. Look: A small hand-bell cast from cloudy blue ice-metal, rimed white at the lip, its clapper hanging perfectly still even when the water moves.

## Reward skill (1)
- **Dead-Reckoning Descent** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — It holds a true course straight down through lightless water with no landmarks to steer by. Best used when: Every instrument has failed and the dark offers nothing else to navigate by, only a straight line down through water with no landmarks left. The catch: It is honest only about the way down, and offers no guidance at all for finding your way back up. Look: A single lamp-beam falling in a perfectly straight line through churning dark water, silt and bubbles crossing it without bending it.

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **What the Amber Lamps Mean** — In The Deep Shift, under Kelphold Rig, Imogen Halvard is halfway down her first solo descent when every lamp above her turns amber at once, and the crew she has been arguing with all week starts hauling the ladder up.
