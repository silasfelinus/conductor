## The idea
A grounded starship pilot takes the worst job on a sea-otter kelp rig — the deep shift, alone under the canopy — and finds that the thing the crew will not discuss is already waiting at the bottom of her first descent.

## Dream vibe (1)
**The Deep Shift** — Everyone here is very calm about what happens on the night shift.
Art: A cramped underwater work rig lit by yellow deck lamps, dense kelp stems rising past its windows into black water, a single diver's lamp far below in the murk, frost creeping across the inside of the glass.

- **Kelphold Rig** — known for Kelphold Rig is known for harvesting a kelp canopy that nobody is allowed to go under alone, except on the deep shift.. ## Character (1)
- **Imogen Halvard** — work the deep shift long enough to buy back her flight certification. Carries a grounded pilot's helmet she still wears underwater because it is hers. Complication: she keeps proposing better ways to run the rig, and the crew keeps quietly not explaining why they don't. Look: A rangy woman in a patched dive suit two decades out of date, scarred flight helmet under her arm, salt-bleached hair, forearms corded from hauling, a burn scar across one cheek.

## Reward item (1)
- **Coldwater Bell** (ITEM, RARE) — rings once, everywhere, when something below has noticed you. Best used when the water has gone quiet in a way that feels deliberate. The catch: it also tells the thing below exactly where the bell is. Look: A small hand-bell cast from cloudy blue ice-metal, rimed white at the lip, its clapper hanging perfectly still even when the water moves.

## Reward skill (1)

The catch: it gets you down accurately, and says nothing about getting back. Look: A single lamp-beam falling in a perfectly straight line through churning dark water, silt and bubbles crossing it without bending it.
