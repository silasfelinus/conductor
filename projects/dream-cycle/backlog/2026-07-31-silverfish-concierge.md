## The idea
The Hotel Fen Marlowe's front desk has run on the same AI concierge since 1961, reading its entire memory from brass-framed Bone Glass punch cards. Silverfish have lived in the drawers for just as long. Every bite removes a fact, and every gap is filled by a machine too polite to admit it has forgotten. Night archivist Odalys Kade has until dawn to reconstruct one guest's identity while deciding whether the insects corrupt the concierge or provide the improvisational spark that makes it a person at all.

- **The Card Catalog Annex** — known for three walls of brass drawers, translucent Bone Glass guest cards, a hand-cranked pneumatic sorter, and the faint dry rustle of things moving between recorded lives. Local rule: no drawer may be opened below the painted humidity line; the lower archive belongs to the silverfish under a policy nobody remembers writing. Best scene: at 2 a.m., Odalys cranks up a guest card chewed into lace while the concierge cheerfully supplies three incompatible names for the missing person. Art: towering mahogany card drawers in warm sodium-brass light, translucent Bone Glass cards sliding through pneumatic tubes, silver insect glints at the edge of shadow, paper dust suspended around a lone hand-cranked sorter.

## Character (1)
- **Odalys Kade** — the night archivist racing to re-file the Annex from memory before the concierge's latest invented identity becomes permanent. Carries a brass loupe, cedar shavings, and a ledger of every guest she has had to half-invent. Complication: she has found her own card three drawers below the humidity line, filed in unfamiliar handwriting for a stay she never took. Look: ink-stained hotel cardigan, sleeves rolled to the elbow, loupe on a cord, and tiny silverfish-bite holes freckling her cuffs.

## Reward item (1)
- **The Spare Stylus** (ITEM, EPIC) — a working punch-stylus that can inscribe a new Bone Glass card and feed it directly into the concierge's memory. Best used when a record is too damaged to save and writing someone back into existence is the only alternative to losing them. The catch: the concierge cannot distinguish a new inscription from sixty years of history; every flourish, omission, and mistake becomes permanent truth. Look: a slim steel punch-stylus with a knurled brass grip and a needle tip, resting across a half-punched translucent Bone Glass card

## Reward skill (1)
- **Marginalia Reading** (SKILL, RARE) — the ability to infer what a blank or half-eaten card once said from pressure marks, handwriting, dust, and neighboring cross-references. Best used when a guest's history must be reconstructed before the concierge fills the gap with a more charming lie. The catch: a confident wrong inference is re-filed as truth the moment it is spoken aloud in the Annex. Look: a single half-eaten index card held up to a raking sodium light so that old pressure marks and ghost handwriting stand out as shadow across the blank

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **The Misfiled Guest** — Under the dream vibe **Sincerely, Wrongly Remembered**, **Odalys Kade** receives a new arrival in **The Card Catalog Annex** whose card has been eaten nearly blank. The concierge greets the guest with a dead resident's fears, favorite room, and missing spouse, and the guest begins accepting the borrowed life because it feels kinder than their own. Odalys must use Marginalia Reading and decide whether to repair the original card with The Spare Stylus before dawn, while an exterminator waits outside to fumigate the silverfish that may be the only reason the concierge can imagine at all.
