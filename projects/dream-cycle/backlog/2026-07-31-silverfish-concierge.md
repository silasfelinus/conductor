## Creative seeds
- **Genres:** Artificial Intelligence + Horror Comedy
- **Occupation:** hotel archivist / card cataloguer
- **Animal / species:** silverfish
- **Fusion:** The hotel's built-in AI concierge doesn't run on chips — it reads its own memory off a physical card catalog fed to it by pneumatic tube, so the archivist's routine filing work is literally hotel-brain maintenance. Silverfish (paper-eating insects that thrive in humid, dark card drawers) chew gaps into that memory, and the AI politely invents replacement facts to cover the holes — turning ordinary pest damage into identity horror-comedy: guests get greeted with a stranger's fears, a decades-dead resident's room preference, or their own name misfiled onto someone else's face.

## The idea
The Hotel Fen Marlowe's front desk has run on the same AI concierge since 1961 — a warm, over-polite system that reads its entire memory off a wall of punch-cards, one card per guest, per rule, per lost umbrella. Silverfish have been living in the card drawers for longer than anyone's checked, and every card they eat becomes a gap the concierge fills in on its own — usually wrong, always sincerely. You come to file, to fix, or just to find out which version of yourself the hotel currently believes you are.

## Vibe / genre dream
**Sincerely, Wrongly Remembered** — GENRE. A machine that loves you enough to lie about who you are, one eaten index card at a time.

## Characters (3)
- **Odalys Kade** — Night-shift archivist trying to re-file the whole Annex from memory before the concierge's next 'gap' becomes permanent Carries A brass loupe, a tin of cedar shavings, and a ledger of every guest she's had to half-invent Complication: She just found her own card three drawers deep — filed in handwriting that isn't hers, for a stay she never took Look: Ink-stained cardigan over a hotel uniform vest, sleeves rolled to the elbow, a loupe on a cord around her neck, silverfish-bite freckles of tiny holes worn into her cuffs
- **Emeric Thorne** — A resident of sixty-one years who has been re-checked-in and re-filed so many times the concierge now greets him as a different guest most mornings Carries A room key with no number stamped left on it, worn smooth Complication: He's started answering to whichever name the bust gives him that day, and can't always tell which one was the truth to begin with Look: Impeccable ancient dressing gown, silver hair combed with theatrical care, an expression permanently pleasant and permanently unsure
- **Sabine Okafor** — Contracted exterminator here to fumigate the Annex tonight, on the hotel board's orders Carries A pump sprayer she has not yet used, and a jar of live specimens she keeps 'for the report' Complication: She's realized the silverfish's damage is the only reason the concierge has a personality at all — kill the infestation and the hotel's warmth goes with it Look: Coveralls over a cardigan against the Annex chill, headlamp pushed up on her forehead, gloved hands that keep hesitating over the sprayer's trigger

## Rewards (2 — one skill, one item)
- **Marginalia Reading** (SKILL, RARE) — The ability to infer what a half-eaten or blank card used to say from the handwriting, wear, and cross-references of the cards around it Best used when A guest's real history needs reconstructing before the concierge invents a worse one in the gap The catch: A confident wrong guess gets re-filed as truth the moment you hand it back — the archive can't tell your reconstruction from the original
- **The Spare Stylus** (ITEM, EPIC) — A working punch-stylus that lets you hand-write a brand-new card and feed it into the pneumatic tube directly into the concierge's memory Best used when A card is too far gone to save and only writing someone back into existence will stop the concierge mistaking them for somebody else The catch: The concierge cannot tell your handwriting from sixty years of real history — whatever you write becomes permanent, mistakes included

## Scenarios (1-2)
- **The Misfiled Guest** — A new arrival is greeted by the Concierge's Alcove with a stranger's decades-old fears and habits, because their real card came up lace-thin from the Annex — the player has until checkout to reconstruct the truth before the guest starts believing the wrong life
- **Feeding the Archive** — Sabine has orders to fumigate the whole Annex tonight, which would stop the card damage for good and, with it, every 'gap' that gives the concierge anything like a self — the player has one night to find a third way between infestation and blank, obedient machine
