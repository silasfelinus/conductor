## New Facets (invented for this dream)
- **Hoards Every Discarded Self** (`hoards-every-discarded-self`, QUIRK) — Keeps physical proof of every stage it has already grown out of — a shed skin, an outgrown shell, a demolished wing, a discarded draft — refusing to let a finished version disappear. The hoard proves nothing is truly lost, but it also crowds out the present and keeps a change from ever fully finishing.
- **Witness-Bound** (`witness-bound`, ALIGNMENT) — A moral compass holding that a transformation is only trustworthy once someone else stood in the room and saw it happen. It treats an unwitnessed change as suspect rather than settled, which builds real trust through shared presence but can also trap a person in an old shape simply because nobody was there to see them leave it.

## The idea
On a Martian mining colony where Astral Ore leaches into the groundwater, settlers who breathe too much of it stop aging in the ordinary way and start molting instead, shedding whole bodies every few years while keeping every skill they've ever learned. A bathhouse keeper who just underwent her first elderly molt spends her days testing sticks against her own hide, hunting the one that can ease a shed without tearing the new skin, while quietly enforcing the one house rule she has never broken: nobody sheds alone.

## Dream vibe (1)
**You Come Out Somebody Else** — Every molt buries who you were and grows someone new inside the same skin.
Art: A steaming stone bathhouse interior at dawn, translucent shed skins draped like curtains from the rafters, mineral-blue light rippling off ore-veined water.

## Dream location (1)
- **Vein Nine Bathhouse** — The colony's oldest Astral Ore vein feeds this bathhouse's black mineral pools, and every settler whose blood has taken up the ore eventually comes back here to molt. Local rule: Every shed skin stays inside the hall until its owner claims or releases it. Best scene: Dozens of translucent old skins drift loose across the pool's surface as a fresh molt breaks the water gasping in an unfamiliar body. Art: A vast steaming stone bath lit blue-green by ore-veined water, torn translucent skins floating and draped over the rafters, condensation beading on rough-cut rock walls.

## Character (1)
- **Yem Okoro-Diallo** — She keeps the bathhouse's molt schedule and insists that at least one other person stay in the room for every shed. A bundle of forty-some scratching sticks, bone to driftwood to coral, rides slung across her back, each one notched with the molt it failed. Complication: She has carried the skills of a dozen prior molts into this one, but this elderly shed came early and unbidden, and she still doesn't know what her body is preparing to become. Look: Heavyset and steam-flushed, a two-spirit elder freshly molted into a wider, softer frame than she's worn before, a bony crest running scalp to nape dyed harbor-blue with ore paste, faint gill-like seams still healing at her throat, a faded indigo wrapper knotted harbor-style at the hip, beadwork banding both wrists, mouth set in a patient, gap-toothed half-grin.

## Reward item (1)
- **Witness Bottle** (ITEM, RARE) — It replays, ripple by ripple, exactly who stood in the room during a molt. Best used when: Someone insists their transformation happened while nobody else was in the room to see it. The catch: The water shows only who was present, never what anyone actually saw or understood, so a room of distracted strangers counts the same as one true witness. Look: A squat glass bottle of clouded bathwater that ripples faintly on its own, throwing brief silhouettes of whoever last stood over it.

## Reward skill (1)
- **Notch Sense** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — It finds the one tool already in reach that will ease this particular shed without tearing new skin. Best used when: A molt stalls half-finished and nothing anyone has tried loosens the last stubborn patch of old shell. The catch: The right tool works only once; whatever solved this molt is dead weight for the next one. Look: A steady hand drags a chosen stick along a half-shed spine, old skin curling back in one continuous ribbon without a single tear.

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **Let Me Go Under Alone** — In You Come Out Somebody Else, at Vein Nine Bathhouse, Yem Okoro-Diallo has to decide whether to honor a settler's request to molt alone, unwitnessed, against every custom of the baths, or insist that someone stay to see who he becomes.
