## New Facets (invented for this dream)
- **Fallen Titan Hull** (`fallen-titan-hull`, SETTING) — A single fallen mechanical-organic giant whose fused body has become a settlement's only architecture, load-bearing in every wall and roof. Nothing there can be repaired, expanded, or abandoned without reckoning with the fact that the town's whole structure is also, still, partly alive.
- **Proving Graft** (`proving-graft`, ROLE) — Whoever performs a body-merge that has never been verified survivable, so an entire community learns what it costs by watching only this one person attempt it. There is no ceremony once the graft actually takes, only whether the body accepts it or fails.

## The idea
In a post-apocalyptic river delta, an entire town lives inside the fused, coral-grown ribs of a mechanical kudu the size of a hill, and every child there eventually chooses whether to graft part of their own body into its slowly still-growing frame.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Bonehull Providence** — A fallen titan's skeleton is the only home this delta has left.
Art: A colossal fallen bronze-and-bone kudu-titan filling the horizon, sunlit ribs and spiral horn-spurs towering over tiny rooftop clusters built along its spine, wide open sky, minimal ornament.

## Dream location (1)
- **Bonehull Reach** — A fallen mechanical kudu the size of a hill, its ribs, plating, and spiral horn-spurs now load-bearing for the whole settlement built inside them. Local rule: No structure may cut through a horn-spur; every wall bends around the growth instead. Best scene: Sunrise floods straight down the spine walkway, turning every rooftop and grown coral plate gold at once. Art: Monumental fallen bronze kudu-titan the size of a hill, sunlit ribs and coral-grown horn-spurs as rooftops, tiny catwalks along its spine, wide open sky, little ornament.

## Character (1)
- **Adaeze Nwosu** — She tends the settlement's slow-growing horn-spurs, deciding each season which volunteers are ready to graft. A bronze grafting awl has fused into her right palm, its handle grown flush with her skin after the first small merge she performed alone. Complication: The graft she gave herself as a girl never fully stopped growing, and stopping it now would mean losing the arm entirely. Look: A stocky dark-skinned woman with a bronze grafting awl fused flush into her right palm, forearm ridged where old coral-metal growth has healed into the skin, practical work wrap, sunlit workshop light.

## Reward item (1)
- **Prismarine Main Gauche** (ITEM, RARE) — Parries a single incoming blow no matter its force, so long as the wielder names what they are protecting out loud before it lands. Best used when: A duel turns unfair the instant a second opponent joins from behind. The catch: Naming the wrong thing at the wrong moment turns the parry into a promise the blade holds you to instead. Look: A slim curved parrying dagger forged from layered prismarine shard and dark iron, blade edge faintly iridescent, hilt wrapped in salt-cured leather, small chips worn along the guard.

## Reward skill (1)
- **Ironbloom Calling** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — Calls every nearby loose scrap of iron into a single slow-orbiting ring around the caster's raised arm. Best used when: A collapsing structure is about to drop debris on people who cannot move fast enough. The catch: The ring keeps orbiting long after the danger passes, and dismissing it early always draws blood. Look: A slow ring of rusted scrap iron and bronze bolts orbiting a raised forearm, faint heat-shimmer where the metal grinds against itself, no visible weapon.

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **First Graft, Grown Wrong** — In Bonehull Providence, at Bonehull Reach, Adaeze Nwosu is asked to perform the settlement's first graft in a generation without a proven pattern to follow, on a volunteer half her age.
