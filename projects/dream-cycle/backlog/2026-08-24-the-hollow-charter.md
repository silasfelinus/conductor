## The idea
Two lineages tied their old agreement into a braided rope and sealed it inside the carcass of a dead terraforming leviathan. The hull engineer whose plated hands finally open that chamber walks straight into both of their claims at once.

## Dream vibe (1)
**The Ironback Braid** — Two lineages tied their agreement into a rope, and nobody alive can read a knot.
Art: A colossal rusted ribcage interior lit by flat blue emergency light, a thick braided rope of black grained wood and hull wire strung across a chamber, banded iron plates unrolling like a shell.

## Dream location (1)
- **The Ironback Hulk** — known for the gutted carcass of a kilometre-long terraforming leviathan, still sealed at its core. Local rule: the plates only unroll for a hand they have felt before. Best scene: the banded door unrolls scute by scute while gas hisses from vents shaped like gills. Art: A cavernous curved hull interior ribbed like a colossal rusted spine, brass conduit veins along riveted iron plates, an armadillo-banded door of overlapping scutes unrolling at the far end under flat blue light.

## Character (1)
- **Halden R. Boone** — get the chamber open without becoming the person who has to decide who owns it. Carries a wrist-mounted breach kit of magnetised picks on a short lanyard. Complication: he is entirely confident he can hand this off to somebody senior, and there is nobody senior. Look: Overlapping armadillo-banded grey-brown dermal scutes running down both forearms and across the knuckles, a cropped patchwork jacket with the sleeves cut away, breach kit strapped at one wrist.

## Reward item (1)
- **The Shadow Oak Spindle** (ITEM, RARE) — unwinds exactly one turn of a braid at a time and holds it there. Best used when everyone in the room wants the whole thing opened at once. The catch: one turn per person present, and it counts everyone. Look: A hand-length spindle turned from shadow-oak so dark it takes no highlight, its shaft polished by handling, a single strand of hull wire caught in the groove.

## Reward skill (1)
- **The Third Cup** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — buys one round of calm between people who arrived armed. Best used when both sides are still standing and neither has sat down. The catch: it lasts exactly as long as the tea does. Look: Steam rising in three straight columns from small cups on a low table, none of it drifting sideways however the air moves.
