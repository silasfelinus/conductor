## The idea
Two rival hull-clans wove an old pact as a braided cable of black wood and wire, then sealed it inside the gutted carcass of a dead terraforming leviathan. The engineer whose plated hands finally pry open that chamber collides with both clans' claims at once.

## Character (1)
- **Halden R. Boone** — He wants to get the chamber open without becoming the person who has to decide who owns it. A wrist-mounted breach kit of magnetised picks hangs on a short lanyard, worn smooth from constant use against stubborn seals. Complication: He is entirely confident he can hand this off to somebody senior, and there is nobody senior. Look: Overlapping armadillo-banded grey-brown dermal scutes running down both forearms and across the knuckles, a cropped patchwork jacket with the sleeves cut away, breach kit strapped at one wrist.

## Reward item (1)
- **The Shadow Oak Spindle** (ITEM, RARE) — One turn of a braid unwinds at a time and holds steady there, no matter how hard the whole length is pulled.
