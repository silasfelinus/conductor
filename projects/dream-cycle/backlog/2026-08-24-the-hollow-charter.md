## The idea
Inside the gutted, kilometer-long carcass of a derelict terraforming leviathan, colonists have found an armored vault holding a treaty that predates their own ship's records by centuries. Only a dermal-plated hull engineer's hands will trigger the seal, so when the vault finally opens, that engineer becomes the accidental diplomat standing between two delegations who both claim the same unread promise.

## Dream vibe (1)
**The Hollow Charter** — Every clause was sealed before anyone alive learned how to read it.
Art: Corroded brass filigree crawling across a curved iron ribcage vault door, cold blue emergency light falling in flat shafts through drifting dust, a segmented brass seal fused shut under centuries of green verdigris.

## Dream location (1)
- **The Ironback Hulk** — known for the gutted carcass of a kilometer-long terraforming leviathan machine, now used as the ship's deepest archive. Local rule: the vault chamber floods with inert gas the instant an unlisted hand touches the seal, so only a registered dermal signature may enter. Best scene: the vault door grinding open scute by scute as gas hisses from vents shaped like ribs. Art: A cavernous curved hull interior ribbed like a colossal rusted spine, brass conduit veins running along riveted iron plates, a domed vault door built from overlapping bronze scutes catching thin blue worklight.

## Character (1)
- **Halden R. Boone** — a hull-maintenance engineer grafted with nanothread dermal plating, pulled into negotiating the vault's contents purely because their hands are the only ones the seal will open for. Carries a wrist-mounted breach kit of magnetized picks clipped to a frayed harness strap. Complication: the factions now treat their plated hands as a bargaining chip rather than a body part, and they have no training for the negotiating table they've been shoved into. Look: Overlapping grey-brown dermal scutes running down both forearms and across the knuckles, a cropped patchwork jacket with the sleeves cut short to show them off, a thin nanothread scar glowing faint blue along one temple, calloused hands stained with rust and grease.

## Reward item (1)
- **The Shadow Oak Ledger Case** (ITEM, RARE) — unlocks to reveal a single page of the sealed treaty at a time, in an order it alone decides. Best used when someone needs proof of an old promise without revealing everything it cost. The catch: once a page is read it fuses shut and can never be reopened, so the order it chooses is final. Look: A flat black case carved from dense shadow-oak grain so dark it seems to swallow the light around its hinge, a single brass clasp gone dull green, one corner scorched pale from an old fire.

## Reward skill (1)
- **The Folded Concession** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — the ability to fold a spoken demand into a smaller, acceptable one without either side noticing the exchange. Best used when a negotiation has stalled because neither side can be seen to back down first. The catch: it only works once per conversation; try it twice and both sides notice exactly what was folded. Look: A slow, deliberate motion of both hands closing a paper fan patterned with dark ink-brushed strokes, the fan's ribs snapping shut in one continuous fold as the air around it goes momentarily still.
