## The idea
Retired villains throw a harvest fair on a tilted stone plain beside a temple older than the stars, and a patient virus the size of a barn is the only guest everyone agrees to trust with the oven.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Warm Bread at the Villains' Harvest** — The old villains have laid down their schemes for one bright afternoon, and the whole slanting fair leans together to keep the bread warm.
Art: A vast tilted plain of pale warped stone under a cloudless blue sky, hundreds of striped awnings in red, yellow and teal leaning uphill, long banners snapping, giant loaves and oversized pumpkins stacked on carts, great crowds of costumed villains in capes and horned helms passing trays down the slope, a huge weathered temple of fluted grey stone rising behind the fair, one tall curved capsid-headed creature standing small among the stalls, saturated daylight, motion everywhere.
