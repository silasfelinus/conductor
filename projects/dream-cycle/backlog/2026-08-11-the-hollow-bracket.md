## The idea
In a collapsed stadium veined with glowing ore, wounded fighters compete not for glory but for the right to be healed, while something that isn't quite dead watches from the dark beneath the bleachers, and an academic doctor tries to save them all without becoming one of the things that never really leaves.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Feigned Death Round** — Stay still long enough and the bracket forgets you were ever alive.
Art: Sodium-orange floodlight through cracked stadium roofing, smoke drifting low across a cracked concrete floor, everything rendered in high-contrast amber and bruise-purple with hard black shadow pools; motion blur on standing figures, total stillness on collapsed ones.

## Dream location (1)
- **Rustbracket Coliseum** — A gutted sports arena where seams of raw prismatic ore have grown up through the shattered seating, and survivors fight in scheduled rounds for medicine hoarded at the center ring. Local rule: Any fighter who falls must be left untouched and unmoved for three full minutes before anyone may check if they're breathing. Best scene: A collapsed fighter lying motionless at ring center while dozens of small grey shapes creep down through the broken bleachers, sniffing at the ore-light reflected in her open eyes. Art: Wide shattered oval of concrete seating collapsing inward, ore veins threading through rebar in shards of violet, green, and gold light, cast shadows long and multiplying from the flickering ore glow, dust hanging motionless in shafts of light.

## Character (1)
- **Dr. Perpetua Vell** — She works as the tournament physician, treating the fallen between rounds and keeping every fighter alive long enough to fight again, and she cannot stop even as the arena's watching things begin to recognize her by name. She slings a cracked leather medical satchel low against her hip like a marsupial pouch, its seams stitched shut with silver wire and its buckles replaced with small bones. Complication: She has learned to lie as still as her patients to move unseen through the arena after dark, and the stillness is starting to last longer than she means it to. Look: A tall woman in a stained white coat layered over patchwork robes stitched with faint sigils, close-cropped grey hair, one pupil permanently dilated, fine cracks like dry riverbed lines spreading from her left temple, ore dust ground into the creases of her hands.

## Reward item (1)
- **Vial of Second Spring** (ITEM, RARE) — Uncorking the vial releases a thick green fluid that closes wounds by growing living tissue over them within minutes, visibly rooting and pulsing as it works. Best used when: A fighter has collapsed past the three-minute rule and needs to stand again before the round is called. The catch: The regrowth pulls faint spectral shapes toward the wound, drawn to new warmth, and they linger nearby afterward. Look: A slim glass vial the length of a finger, filled with churning bottle-green liquid faintly glowing, stoppered with a knot of living root that has grown through the cork, condensation beaded on cracked old glass.

## Reward skill (1)
- **Cold Read** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — Its user can read a fighter's injuries and lies at a glance, seeing fractures and fear both laid bare in the same look. Best used when: Assessing a collapsed opponent before the three-minute rule expires, to know if they are truly down or waiting. The catch: Using it too often leaves the user's own eyes glassy and half-lidded, mistaken by others for someone about to fall. Look: A brief visible haze of grey static crossing a target's silhouette, outlining hidden fractures in stark white lines and dimming the color from their face for a heartbeat before it fades.

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **The Third Minute** — In the Feigned Death Round settling over Rustbracket Coliseum, a scavenger crew of half-drowned space pirates rappels down through the broken roof to strip the ore veins bare, arriving exactly as Dr. Perpetua Vell kneels over a fighter who has been down for two minutes and fifty seconds, unsure which of the shapes gathering in the dark are hers to save.
