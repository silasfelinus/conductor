## The idea
A fugitive former starship mechanic, now unmistakably enormous and still growing, spends every free hour refitting their own body in a scrap-panel backroom instead of fixing anyone else's engine.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Exactly Your Size** — Nothing fits until it is measured and rebuilt to match.
Art: Clean modular workshop of off-white polymer panels and brushed steel, warm utility lighting, a huge powder-blue butterfly perched on a resizing rig.

## Dream location (1)
- **Panel Nine** — Panel Nine gets rebuilt overnight for whoever needs new walls by morning, no questions. Local rule: Every panel taken must be replaced before the next shift arrives. Best scene: A whole back wall folds away in under a minute, hiding three days of work behind blank polymer. Art: A cramped modular backroom of interlocking off-white polymer panels and brushed steel struts, snap-lock seams glowing faint blue, a huge powder-blue butterfly resting on a folded resizing rig.

## Character (1)
- **Coco Linde** — They are quietly refitting their own body one panel at a time, the same way they used to refit engines. A magnetized multitool rides clipped to their belt, its handle re-gripped in scavenged shuttle tape. Complication: Every growth spurt cracks another weld from the last emergency patch-up, and the shop has no parts big enough left. Look: A towering, still-growing teenager half a head taller than any adult nearby, broad through raw new muscle and knuckle-scarred; one temple shaved to bare scalp, the rest bound in loose black locs threaded with recycled solder-wire; one whole forearm rebuilt in mismatched brushed-steel plating that doesn't quite match their skin tone; a faded Ruhr Werkstatt patch stitched beside a hand-beaded Windward cord at the collar; eyes narrowed in constant, restless calculation, mouth already half-open to ask the next question.
