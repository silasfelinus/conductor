## The idea
For ninety nights a year the cliffs light from inside, and whoever writes that season's rules decides who may glide the high pasture — a job everyone wants until the mountain starts enforcing the rules exactly as written.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Rulewriter's Season** — Somebody writes the rules each year, and the mountain enforces them to the letter.
Art: A vast cliff face lit from within by veins of pale green glowstone at dusk, dozens of small gliding figures launching from ledges, long banners of hand-lettered rules snapping in the updraft.

## Dream location (1)
- **The Glowstone Shelf** — known for cliffs that light from the inside for ninety nights a year. Local rule: no glide begins before the third stone kindles. Best scene: the last stone lights and a hundred gliders leave the ledge in one breath. Art: A wide stone shelf over a green-lit gorge, glowstone veins pulsing under the rock like lit wiring, chalked rule-boards leaning along the wall, updraft carrying loose paper into the dark.

## Character (1)
- **Oriel Tanaka** — write a season of rules nobody can twist, and be admired for it. Carries a chalk-stick and the rule-board she refuses to let anyone else letter. Complication: she is certain her wording is perfect, and the mountain has begun proving otherwise. Look: A small sugar-glider woman in a wind-scuffed flight wrap, patagial membrane stretched between wrist and ankle and chalk-dusted along its edge, huge dark eyes, chin up, ink stains at both cuffs.

## Reward item (1)
- **The Second-Chance Stone** (ITEM, RARE) — gives back the ten seconds you have just spent. Best used when you have already committed to the wrong line. The catch: everyone else remembers the ten seconds you took back. Look: A thumb-sized shard of green glowstone in a bent wire cage, its light guttering and recovering in a slow ten-count, warm as a held coal.

## Reward skill (1)
- **Clause Craft** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — writes one new rule into the season and makes the valley keep it. Best used when the existing wording is being obeyed and abused at once. The catch: the valley keeps it exactly as written, forever. Look: Letters scorching themselves pale gold into standing grass in a wide sweeping arc, thin smoke rising from each stroke as it finishes.

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **The Night the Rules Changed Mid-Air** — In Rulewriter's Season, off The Glowstone Shelf with a hundred gliders already in the air, Oriel Tanaka realises her own wording lets the leading team win by never landing, and has to letter a new clause before they cross the pasture line.
