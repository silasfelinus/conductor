## The idea
In a lantern-lit mountain hall whose long corridor runs on into a yellow-carpeted hall of its own, a lanky ex-detective entity and the keeper who adores them set out to shut every shutter as the corridor's weather rolls through.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Close the Shutters, Love** — The hall's corridor never ends, the weather inside it is moody, and somebody has to keep the paper windows from tearing.
