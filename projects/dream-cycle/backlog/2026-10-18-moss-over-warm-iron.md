## The idea
Years after an invasion fleet fell dead out of the sky, the slowest animals alive are the only ones patient enough to eat what it left behind, and one tallowkin knows exactly which hulls hold the dead.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Moss Over Warm Iron** — The invaders died on landing, and the slowest animals in the world are eating what they left.
Art: A salt flat in hard noon sun with black pitted alien hulls lying like beached whales, green moss creeping over their warm iron, grey cables rising to small tethered moons, a herd of sloths moving hand over hand along the cables, crisp black shadows, no glow.
