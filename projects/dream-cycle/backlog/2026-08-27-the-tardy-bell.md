## The idea
A boarding-school corridor keeps whatever decade you entered it in, and something dugong-shaped has been grading the exams from a year that hasn't happened yet.

## Dream vibe (1)
**The Tardy Bell** — Ring it late enough and you graduate somewhere else entirely.
Art: A brass school bell hanging inside a spiral stairwell that telescopes into fog, locker banks fading from scuffed bakelite at the top to smooth acrylic near the bottom, each landing lit by a different decade of fixture.

## Dream location (1)
- **Corridor Nine** — known for rooms that keep the decade you first entered them in. Local rule: never leave two decades' doors open at the same time. Best scene: a disputed exam key changes the room's furniture era mid-argument. Art: A school corridor that looks grown rather than built: locker banks fused from bone-pale resin veined with crystal, floor tiles cracked open over root-like tubing, the fluorescent tubes swapped for jagged spire-crystal shards glowing faint amber.

## Character (1)
- **Substitute Miss Sorrel Fenn** — find the missing decade before the corridor forgets to give it back. Carries a spire-crystal fountain pen that only writes the correct year. Complication: the pen has started writing next year instead. Look: a broad, placid-faced substitute teacher with small ivory tusks tucked under a tweed collar, slate-grey skin dusted in chalk, a crystal-nibbed pen clipped to a waistcoat pocket

## Reward item (1)
- **The Correction Slip** (ITEM, RARE) — rewrites one recent event as though it happened differently. Best used when a wrong answer has already been marked. The catch: the rewrite changes what everyone else remembers too, including you. Look: a hall-pass-sized chip of spire crystal, one edge chipped into a wolf's silhouette, a hairline crack pulsing warm amber down its center

## Reward skill (1)
- **Marginalia** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — writes a true annotation in the margin of any document, naming what its author left out. Best used when an official record looks too clean. The catch: the annotation appears in the reader's own handwriting. Look: gaslamp-amber ink blooming unbidden in a page's margin, spidering into looping cursive that wasn't there a breath ago

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **The Corrected Corridor** — In The Tardy Bell at Corridor Nine, Substitute Miss Sorrel Fenn finds a fossilized dugong skull grafted into the crystal wall, still grading exams from a year that hasn't happened yet.
