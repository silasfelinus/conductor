## The idea
On a generation ship gone frontier-lawless, a dugong-formed debt collector drifts the flooded cargo decks calling in debts owed to a dead economy, while something in the water keeps the books better than she does.

## Dream vibe (1)
**The Slow Bell of Ninth Ledger** — Out past the last audited star, the only law left is compound interest.
Art: Wide flooded corridor of a generation ship lit by failing amber strip-lights reflected in still black water, dust motes hanging in stale recycled air, a brass bell bolted to a bulkhead green with verdigris, distant silhouette of a heavy round body gliding just beneath the surface.

## Dream location (1)
- **Ninth Ledger Flood Deck** — known for a sunken cargo level of a generation ship where old debt contracts float sealed in waterproof pouches, and something large and gentle patrols the wreckage collecting them. Local rule: no debt may be called in until the bell above the waterline is rung three times by hand. Best scene: a rowboat threading between upright shipping crates half-submerged, dragon-scale patches welded over hull breaches catching lamplight, a slow wake fanning out where something unseen has just passed beneath. Art: Waist-deep black water filling a corridor between rusted cargo containers, patches of overlapping dragon-scale plating welded crooked over hull cracks, a low ceiling strung with dead cables dripping condensation, single swinging lamp casting a moving disk of light on the water's surface.

## Character (1)
- **Portia Fivetooth** — a debt collector reincarnated in dugong shape who insists every unpaid ledger on the ship still belongs to her, and hums with restless hyperactive energy while doing sums no one asked for. Carries a waterproof abacus case strapped across her back with a cracked leather strap. Complication: she cannot stop auditing things that were never debts — meals, apologies, silences — and adds them all to the total. Look: A heavyset grey-brown dugong body with a broad fluked tail, small deep-set eyes, and a whiskered snout, dragon-scale plates riveted in an overlapping row down her spine like segmented armor, a cracked leather satchel strapped across her back leaking a thin stream of bubbles, front flippers oddly articulated with small metal rings threaded along the edges like tally beads.

## Reward item (1)
- **The Unsinkable Tally Chest** (ITEM, RARE) — a small chest that always floats upright and keeps whatever is sealed inside dry no matter how deep it sinks. Best used when smuggling a debt, a letter, or a secret through flooded or hostile territory. The catch: it always drifts slowly toward whoever is owed something by its contents, regardless of your intentions. Look: A squat iron-bound chest the size of a footlocker, its wood blackened and swollen with old water damage, banded in corroded brass hoops, one corner patched with an overlapping plate of dragon scale gone dull silver-green, a thin rope of barnacles crusted along its waterline seam.

## Reward skill (1)
- **Ledger-Skin Recalibration** (SKILL, EPIC) — the ability to seed a swarm of debt-tracking nanites into any surface, causing faint glowing tally marks to surface on skin, metal, or stone wherever an unpaid obligation exists. Best used when someone denies owing anything and the truth needs to be made visible without a word said. The catch: the nanites cannot be told which debts matter, so old grudges and forgotten kindnesses surface with equal glowing insistence. Look: A skin or surface suddenly stippled with faint blue-white filament lines that crawl like slow lightning beneath the top layer, converging into small glowing tally notches that pulse once and dim, leaving a temporary silvery residue like frost.

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **Three Rings of the Bell** — Under The Slow Bell of Ninth Ledger, deep in the Ninth Ledger Flood Deck, Portia Fivetooth rings the bulkhead bell for the third time in a single day — a violation of the local rule no one has committed in a generation — because the water itself has started keeping a ledger she did not write, and something beneath the surface is finally answering the call.
