## The idea
The flooded cargo decks of a lawless generation ship are full of salvage nobody has collected, because the only route out crosses water that something very large has never let anyone film.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Salvage Rights** — Nobody out here pays you. You go down and take it back yourself.
Art: A flooded ship corridor lit by failing amber strip-lights doubled in still black water, a heavy tow cable running away into the dark, overlapping iridescent scale plating welded across a bulkhead.

## Dream location (1)
- **The Ninth Deck Floodline** — known for a flooded cargo level nobody has yet filmed all the way across. Local rule: keep one lamp pointed at the water behind you. Best scene: a camera rig comes back up with sixty seconds of nothing and one bent mounting frame. Art: Waist-deep black water filling a corridor of rusted cargo containers, overlapping iridescent dragon-scale plates welded crookedly over a breach, amber worklights strung low and doubling in the surface.

## Character (1)
- **Portia Fivetooth** — tow the sunken lifeboat out of Ninth Deck before her claim on it lapses at dawn. Carries a spool of scale-plated tow cable and somebody else's borrowed camera rig. Complication: she cannot hold still for more than a moment, and the water tracks movement. Look: A heavyset grey-brown dugong body with a broad fluked tail and small deep-set eyes, iridescent scale plates riveted along both flanks, a cable spool strapped across her back and a lamp lashed to her snout.

## Reward item (1)
- **The Unsinkable Strongbox** (ITEM, RARE) — always floats upright and keeps whatever is inside it dry. Best used when the way out is underwater and what you are carrying is not. The catch: it floats upright whether or not you are still holding it. Look: A squat iron-bound chest the size of a footlocker, its wood blackened and swollen, overlapping green-gold scale plates nailed across the lid, waterline stains ringing it.

## Reward skill (1)
- **Repossession Swarm** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — marks a piece of salvage as yours so it comes to you when called. Best used when what you are owed is too heavy to carry out. The catch: it comes when called, by whatever route is shortest. Look: Blue-white filament lines crawling across a submerged hull plate and pulsing once, brighter, as the plate begins to drift.

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **Sixty Seconds of Nothing** — In Salvage Rights, out along The Ninth Deck Floodline, Portia Fivetooth has the lifeboat under tow and forty metres of open water left when the borrowed camera rig starts playing back sixty seconds she does not remember swimming.
