## The idea
The flooded cargo decks of a lawless generation ship are full of salvage nobody has collected, because the only route out crosses water that something very large has never let anyone film.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Salvage Rights** — Nobody out here pays you. You go down and take it back yourself.
Art: A flooded ship corridor lit by failing amber strip-lights doubled in still black water, a heavy tow cable running away into the dark, overlapping iridescent scale plating welded across a bulkhead.

## Dream location (1)
- **The Ninth Deck Floodline** — A flooded cargo level stretches out of camera range, and nobody who has tried to film its far end has ever brought back footage of arriving there. Local rule: Keep one lamp pointed at the water behind you. Best scene: A camera rig comes back up with sixty seconds of nothing and one bent mounting frame. Art: Waist-deep black water filling a corridor of rusted cargo containers, overlapping iridescent dragon-scale plates welded crookedly over a breach, amber worklights strung low and doubling in the surface.

## Character (1)
- **Portia Fivetooth** — She means to tow the sunken lifeboat out of Ninth Deck before her claim on it lapses at dawn. A spool of scale-plated tow cable rides across her back, tethered to somebody else's borrowed camera rig she is not supposed to lose. Complication: She cannot hold still for more than a moment, and the water tracks every movement she makes. Look: A heavyset grey-brown dugong body with a broad fluked tail and small deep-set eyes, iridescent scale plates riveted along both flanks, a cable spool strapped across her back and a lamp lashed to her snout.

## Reward item (1)
- **The Unsinkable Strongbox** (ITEM, RARE) — Stays floating upright on its own and keeps whatever is sealed inside it perfectly dry. Best used when: The only route out runs underwater, and whatever is being carried cannot get wet. The catch: It floats upright whether or not you are still holding it, and it will not wait for you. Look: A squat iron-bound chest the size of a footlocker, its wood blackened and swollen, overlapping green-gold scale plates nailed across the lid, waterline stains ringing it.

## Reward skill (1)
- **Repossession Swarm** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — It marks a piece of salvage as yours so that it comes to you when called. Best used when: A debt is too heavy to haul out by hand, and the shortest route to it runs straight through danger. The catch: It comes when called, by whatever route is shortest, whether or not that route is safe. Look: Blue-white filament lines crawling across a submerged hull plate and pulsing once, brighter, as the plate begins to drift.

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **Sixty Seconds of Nothing** — In Salvage Rights, out along The Ninth Deck Floodline, Portia Fivetooth has the lifeboat under tow and forty metres of open water left when the borrowed camera rig starts playing back sixty seconds she does not remember swimming.
