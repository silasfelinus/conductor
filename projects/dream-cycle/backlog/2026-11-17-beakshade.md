## The idea
A city lives in the shade of a sleeping toucan the size of a mountain range, whose huge radiant-amber bill arches overhead as the only sky anyone there has ever known. The bird is honoured in song as one of the old sky-bearers, and the whole city treats it as a neighbour rather than a god. The bird rarely wakes, but when it shifts in its sleep the streets, markets and festivals rearrange themselves around it with the cheerful ease of long practice.
