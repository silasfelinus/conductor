## The idea
A bureaucratic archive stamps the timeline's mistakes into official history, and a capybara masquerading as an ambassador is the fragile hinge holding today together.

## Dream vibe (1)
**The Filing Office at the End of History** — Every artifact that shouldn't exist yet gets stamped, shelved, and quietly negotiated with.
Art: A cavernous archive of impossible objects on endless shelves, lit by floating brass lamps on chains, capybara-sized filing carts rolling between towers of paper down aisles that recede past where the light reaches.

## Dream location (1)
- **The Chronofiling Depot** — known for filing anachronistic artifacts before they destabilize the timeline. Local rule: no object may be shelved until its arrival paperwork is signed by the object itself, or by its own era. Best scene: an artifact from next century starts filling out its own intake form. Art: A vaulted archive of shelving fused from glowing astral ore, humming pale blue; brass punch-card conveyor rails loop overhead; a boxy riveted automaton clerk stamps forms with a hissing piston arm beside stacked capybara-sized filing carts.

## Character (1)
- **Undersecretary Bramble** — keep the timeline from unraveling without admitting he isn't really an ambassador. Carries a carnival ticket stub laminated into an official medallion. Complication: the real ambassador he's impersonating is about to arrive. Look: A large, placid capybara wearing an oversized velvet diplomatic sash over damp brown fur, a tiny striped carnival ticket-booth cap perched between his ears, whiskers dusted white with ledger chalk

## Reward item (1)
- **The Stamp of Unlikely Provenance** (ITEM, RARE) — retroactively legitimizes anything it stamps, regardless of when it's actually from. Best used when an object's timeline paperwork is hopelessly contradictory. The catch: it cannot be un-stamped, so a mistake becomes permanently official. Look: A palm-sized brass stamp, its handle a coiled ribbon of glowing astral ore, the stamp face carved with an ouroboros biting a filing tab, ore-light leaking faintly from the seams

## Reward skill (1)
- **Plausible Deniability** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — talks any clerk, guard, or tribunal into believing you were always supposed to be there. Best used when caught somewhere, or somewhen, with no paperwork to explain it. The catch: the more it's used, the more paperwork quietly retroactively appears to catch up with the lie. Look: A shadow that falls one exposure-stop darker than the room around it, hat brim tilted just low enough to blur the eyes, a thin curl of cigarette smoke bending into the shape of a question mark

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **The Audit That Shouldn't Remember** — In The Filing Office at the End of History, at the Chronofiling Depot, Undersecretary Bramble is dragged into an emergency audit when the depot's automaton clerk starts filing carnival tickets that haven't been sold yet — and one of them is stamped with tomorrow's date.
