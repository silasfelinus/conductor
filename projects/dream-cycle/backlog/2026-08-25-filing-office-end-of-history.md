## The idea
The Repeating Mile preserves every procession it has ever hosted as a ghost layered in its mirrored walls, and this year one of those ghostly years veers left instead of right, threatening the treaty that depends on their perfect match.

## Dream vibe (1)
**The Hundredth Procession** — Walk it exactly as it has always been walked, or the year does not take.
Art: A carnival street at night lined with ore-lit mirrors, one bright procession walking it while dozens of translucent earlier processions overlap them in the glass, blue ore light and streamers overhead.

## Dream location (1)
- **The Repeating Mile** — The Repeating Mile is a ceremonial street that shows you every year it has ever been walked, its mirrored walls holding each procession like a ghost still in step. Local rule: You must step exactly where your predecessors stepped, matching the pace and turns of every prior year or risk breaking the treaty the walk maintains. Best scene: A hundred processions walk the mile at once, translucent year over year in the mirrored glass, and exactly one of them turns the wrong way. Art: A narrow festival street walled in tall mirrors veined with blue glowing ore, layered translucent crowds receding into the glass, paper streamers and lamp strings above wet cobbles.

## Character (1)
- **Absalom Duretti** — He must reach the end of the mile without breaking a ceremony nobody ever taught him. A borrowed sash of office slips off his shoulder, paired with a carnival ticket stub he keeps nervously checking as if it might explain everything. Complication: He was mistaken for the ambassador an hour ago and has been too charming to correct anyone since. Look: A large, placid capybara upright in an oversized brocade coat, sash slipping off one round shoulder, whiskers lit blue by the mirrors, an expression of complete and unearned confidence.

## Reward item (1)
- **The Enormous Ceremonial Key** (ITEM, RARE) — It opens whatever a watching crowd agrees it ought to open. Best used when: The crowd around you is more certain of the ritual than you could ever be, and their conviction is what actually gets it done. The catch: It takes two people to lift, and neither can be the first to put it down. Look: A two-metre key of blue-glowing astral ore with a bow the size of a cartwheel, tasselled cords knotted along its shaft, its teeth worn round with handling.

## Reward skill (1)
- **Confident Nonsense** (SKILL, UNCOMMON) — It carries an outsider through a ritual they were never taught. Best used when: Everyone else already knows what happens next in the ceremony, and only you are faking your way through it. The catch: It works right up until somebody asks a direct question. Look: Hard black-and-white shadow bars sweeping across a figure who keeps walking at exactly the same unbothered pace.

## Scenario (1, authored last)
- **The Year the Mile Disagreed** — In The Hundredth Procession, halfway down The Repeating Mile, Absalom Duretti sees one of the reflected processions turn left where every other year turned right, and has to decide which of the hundred versions the treaty is actually counting.
