## The idea
A border checkpoint where dogs can smell the difference between the newly dead and the not-yet.

## Dream vibe (1)
**The Hush Line** — Every dog in the bog learns the smell of a body before its heart stops.
Art: A chrome-nosed watchtower listing sideways into black peat, fins still polished, its rotating dish antenna hunting a broadcast that ended decades ago, reeds growing up through the rust at its base.

## Dream location (1)
- **Quarantine Spur Nine** — Quarantine Spur Nine is known as the last working checkpoint on the amber-glass rail line, where every train bound for the interior must stop and be judged. Local rule: No one crosses the spur without a dog's nose clearing them first, no exceptions made for rank, papers, or the Undersecretary's own seal. Best scene: The barrier hums violet and an entire boxcar goes silent at once, every voice inside cut off mid-word like a switch thrown somewhere in the dark. Art: A single elevated rail vanishing into fog over black water; a riveted aluminum guard booth half-swallowed by reeds, its window replaced by a slab of branching black-amber fulgurite that pulses faint violet when someone steps close.

## Character (1)
- **Marlow Fenn** — She is driven to keep her sector's outbreak count at zero without ever explaining to anyone why that count matters so much. A cracked fulgurite dosimeter hangs clipped to the canvas strap of her satchel, its needle rarely still. Complication: The dosimeter has started reading warm on people who aren't infected yet, and she has no idea what that means. Look: a wiry woman in a quilted rubber coat the color of swamp water, streaked goggles pushed into cropped grey hair, a rangy mud-caked hound pressed silent against her boot

## Reward item (1)
- **Handshake Coil** (ITEM, UNCOMMON) — It greets anything nearby in its own language, badly, translating first and asking questions never. Best used when: Something is approaching through the fog and it's no longer clear whether it still counts as a person. The catch: Once it starts translating it never stops, chattering away at even the swamp gas. Look: a fist-sized coil of blackened fulgurite wound in tarnished copper wire salvaged off a rocket relay, one cracked vacuum-tube eye glowing dim orange at its center

## Reward skill (1)
- **Full Containment Stance** (SKILL, RARE) — It locks down a spreading outbreak in a single held breath. Best used when: An outbreak is spreading fast enough that the count itself is about to become the story. The catch: Holding the stance too long locks the specialist down along with everything else. Look: a perfect ring of violet fulgurite dust lifting off the ground around a pair of planted boots, coat hem frozen mid-flare, one visible cloud of held breath hanging in the cold

## Scenario (1, authored last)
