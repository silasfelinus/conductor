## The idea
A border checkpoint where dogs can smell the difference between the newly dead and the not-yet.

## Dream vibe (1)
**The Hush Line** — Every dog in the bog learns the smell of a body before its heart stops.
Art: A chrome-nosed watchtower listing sideways into black peat, fins still polished, its rotating dish antenna hunting a broadcast that ended decades ago, reeds growing up through the rust at its base.

## Dream location (1)
- **Quarantine Spur Nine** — known for the last working checkpoint on the amber-glass rail line. Local rule: no one crosses without a dog's nose clearing them first. Best scene: the barrier hums violet and an entire boxcar goes silent at once. Art: A single elevated rail vanishing into fog over black water; a riveted aluminum guard booth half-swallowed by reeds, its window replaced by a slab of branching black-amber fulgurite that pulses faint violet when someone steps close.

## Character (1)
- **Marlow Fenn** — keep her sector's outbreak count at zero without ever explaining why the count matters. Carries a cracked fulgurite dosimeter clipped to a canvas satchel strap. Complication: the dosimeter has started reading warm on people who aren't infected yet. Look: a wiry woman in a quilted rubber coat the color of swamp water, streaked goggles pushed into cropped grey hair, a rangy mud-caked hound pressed silent against her boot

## Reward item (1)
- **Handshake Coil** (ITEM, UNCOMMON) — greets anything nearby in its own language, badly. Best used when you can't tell if what's approaching is still a person. The catch: it never stops translating once it starts, even the swamp gas. Look: a fist-sized coil of blackened fulgurite wound in tarnished copper wire salvaged off a rocket relay, one cracked vacuum-tube eye glowing dim orange at its center

The catch: holding the stance too long locks the specialist down too.
