## The idea
A hollow-country fairground built on a landed alien carnival turns its yearly prize-stealing game into a town-wide mystery, because this year a pangolin has been awarded a trophy nobody remembers winning.

## Dream vibe (1)
**Everybody Gets a Prize at the Moonfair** — The carnival came down from the sky in July, and by autumn the whole valley plays its games.
Art: A sunlit valley fairground of striped canvas tents and oversized painted prizes, hot-air lanterns shaped like moons drifting over crowds, brass-and-porcelain ride cars in saturated reds, teals and yellows, banners snapping in a bright wind, a small armoured pangolin riding a carousel swan

## Dream location (1)
- **Dragonbone Midway** — A fairground laid out along the pale arched ribs of a sky-fallen creature, with every game booth glazed in cracked white porcelain and hung with prizes the size of wagons. Local rule: Every booth must be robbed once a season by someone in costume, and the whole midway applauds the best getaway before the prize is quietly put back. Best scene: A masked crowd of hundreds cheers as a stuffed porcelain dragon the size of a barn is dragged off its pedestal, and a pangolin in a tiny sash is seen riding on its back. Art: A long open-air midway between enormous curved ivory-white ribs glazed like porcelain, striped canvas booths, giant painted prizes, bunting strung rib to rib, bright daylight and a swarm of costumed fairgoers
