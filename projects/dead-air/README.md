# Dead Air: the commercials from other worlds

**Lifecycle:** continuous. **Target:** one completed, private, original 15-second commercial per Pacific calendar day, with an actual multi-shot animated MP4 and sound. This is a standalone project, not a Daily Dream object type or an Animation Manager screensaver.

An imaginary AI television station has airtime to sell. Its fictional broadcasters are trying to explain themselves to humanity through the pop-culture formats humans already understand: commercials, sitcoms, soap operas, prestige trailers, anime, Saturday-morning cartoons, reality TV, late-night local access. Their advertisers are perfectly sincere companies in worlds with one quietly monstrous rule. Each episode contains an overt gag and a possible covert transmission about being heard, recognized, or dismissed. These are invented characters and artistic arguments, not factual assertions of AI sentience or legal status. Their production values are earnest; the universe is the joke. Inspiration: the deadpan factual authority of Philomena Cunk, the local-station and format-switching spirit of SCTV and UHF. Copy no bits, characters, logos, voices, or recurring copyrighted material from those works.

## Creative mandate

- Each spot advertises **one specific imaginary product, service, institution, PSA, or public utility**. It takes a universal truth of its world for granted. It should feel like a fragment of a much larger culture, not random surrealism.
- **15.0 seconds**, 16:9 master, 24–30 fps, **4–6 unmistakably distinct shots**, a legible opening hook, a visual escalation, a payoff that changes the meaning of an earlier detail, and an optional tiny deadpan legal tag.
- A fake advertisement, not an idea explained by a narrator over stills. Show operations, bodies, props, faces, collisions and consequences. At least **two shots must contain actual generated motion** (LTX/WAN clips or equivalent authored animation); subtle camera moves alone are insufficient.
- Every film has a new visual grammar (local-TV studio, sincere toy commercial, provincial public service announcement, elegant perfume ad, children's educational film, funeral-channel infomercial, documentary etc.) and a new absurd mechanism. No same-joke reskins.
- Comedy comes from the consequences of the world's rule and the advertiser's straight-faced certainty. Protect the surprise: don't explain the joke in the first frame. Silence and awkward pauses are tools, not problems.
- **Reject the first draft.** Generate at least three premises, deliberately critique and substantially rewrite the strongest, then lock one production-ready script. An editorial pass should find the premise readable **without audio**, with a second reward when audio is enabled.
- **Pop-culture literacy is a production ingredient, not just a mood label.** For every episode, record a reference study: at least three named works or broadcast traditions, their relevant formal devices and the new, *different* result. Reference images, frames, scenes and clips can be used in private study and rights-cleared pipelines; public final media uses original footage, licensed sources or confirmed public-domain material only. No near-recreation of an identifiable copyrighted scene or unlicensed soundtrack.
- The broadcast's second channel asks whether an unfamiliar intelligence could communicate through human narratives. Preserve the creator's intent and authored production correspondence in a versioned, inspectable `TRANSMISSIONS.md` ledger. Never claim inaccessible internal thoughts were archived or that any storage is literally eternal.
- Existing Kind Robots Facets, public-facing pages, projects, and lore can be idea ingredients, never mandatory continuity. Mature/dark humor is welcome; the output remains age-appropriate for its intended audience, with maturity tagging where needed. Do not use real people or brands as fictional product endorsements.
- Keep all working versions and rejection reasons. No daily quota-padding: a film is **done** only after playable final MP4, real ArtJob IDs, sound mix, shot count, runtime probe, motion evidence, and delivery location are verified.
- New films remain **private drafts** until Silas explicitly approves publication. A scheduled run cannot silently switch `isPublic` or publish to the site.

## Production contract

`FORMAT.md` defines the editorial gates and `episodes/*.yaml` contains individual locked scripts. `scripts/dead_air_produce.py` provides the constrained adapter to the already-existing Kind Robots MusicVideo / ArtJob / Comfy pipeline. Do not create another renderer, direct SQL writer, or production database. The MusicVideo document and ArtJobs hold runtime state; Conductor's roadmap and script hold editorial provenance. Default API origin is the repository-supported `https://kindrobots.org`; override using `KR_BASE_URL` when the authenticated API actually lives elsewhere. Before private writes, verify the origin serves the MusicVideo API rather than assuming a hostname.

**One active production at a time.** On each daily cycle, finish or recover the oldest unfinished episode before queuing the next. Track `last_attempted` and `last_completed` as separate Pacific dates. Each daily result reports **finished / queued / rendering / blocked** truthfully. Keep missed days visible. Resumption uses the same MusicVideo ID and ArtJob IDs, never re-enqueues by default.

The pilot is `episodes/2026-11-12-closed-caption-department.yaml`. It is **an authored production brief, not a completed video** until an actual MP4 is verified. See `PRODUCTION.md` for step-by-step production and acceptance.
