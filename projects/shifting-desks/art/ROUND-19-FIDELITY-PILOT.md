# Shifting Desks: Round 19 subject-fidelity recovery pilot

**Scope:** three private concept tests only. The existing six-image Krea2/Nova pilot has already been delivered and reviewed (Krea2 2/3, Nova 0/3); do not resubmit it. Round 19 delivered 14/14 images, but all 14 were REDO on scene fidelity (Conductor #7352). This follow-up tests a different strategy: **one impossible focal subject per image, no supporting story props**.

**Safety:** generate to a private, uniquely named pilot destination, not any `public/images/shifting-desks` path or live ArtImage URL. Never overwrite existing Classic/Noir art. No automatic KEEP or game linking. An art submission must have its own unique request ID, ArtJob, ArtImage and newer-media evidence before review. Generation is internal only, not a public release.

## Three independent trials

1. **Rear eyes, portrait 2:3.** Extreme rear close-up of a black bob haircut. Exactly two enormous *open eyes embedded in the back of the hair*, whites, irises and eyelids unmistakable. No face, coworker, stapler, computer or room. Cream background. The entire gag must be legible as a thumbnail. **Pass:** two distinct eyes on the back of the head, not on the normal face. **Fail:** ordinary hair, eyes on a face, ambiguous markings.
2. **Living corridor, landscape 4:3.** Straight-on symmetrical corridor containing exactly three doors, each doorframe a giant soft accordion of padded organic ribs integrated into the wall. No tank, people, posters or office furniture. A rigid metal elevator threshold frames the foreground for contrast. **Pass:** ribbed living doorframes dominate all three openings. **Fail:** normal rectangular doorframes or merely patterned wallpaper.
3. **Mutation tank, portrait 2:3.** One large transparent upright glass tank fills the composition, its glass outline and liquid level plainly visible. Inside, a fully submerged smiling office worker with exactly three separate arms, the third growing from the torso and holding an orange pen. No second tank, nurse or extra eyes. **Pass:** entire worker, including all three arms, clearly *inside* the tank. **Fail:** subject floating in front of tank, two arms, or ambiguous limbs.

## Shared visual contract

Original mid-century corporate safety-manual gouache illustration, bold screenprint shapes, coarse halftone grain, muted manila, carbon blue, safety orange and oxblood. Flat painted art, not photography, 3D, vector placeholder, typography, panels, gore or injury. Use a fresh seed per attempt. Record exact engine and seed; test Krea2 first because the prior model comparison favored it.

## Acceptance and next action

Review each image at full resolution and thumbnail scale against its single **pass/fail** criterion. Record ArtJob ID, ArtImage ID, generation timestamp, actual image media provenance, and an explicit KEEP/REDO with reason. Only if a private test passes, prepare a separate full-scene composition using that accepted subject as a reference; that second render must independently pass the original scene brief before replacing a live slot. If all three fail, stop rerendering the same prompt formula and escalate to reference/compositing workflow. No claim of delivery or playtest readiness is made by this brief.
