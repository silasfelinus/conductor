# Shifting Desks: World Bible

status: v1, 2026-10-12 PT (t-004). Built on the decided world (DESIGN-BRIEF "Decided world"): a cheerful campus on top of a sealed test vault, where every department is one level deeper. The lead flavors are **secret societies & traitors** and **paperwork & psych tests**. The machine-readable decks (job titles, societies and mutations) are in [encounters/DECKS.json](encounters/DECKS.json); the game imports them directly.

## The Company

**Consolidated Wellness & Defense, Inc.** ("Making Tomorrow Safer, Today") makes three things: wellness programs, defense contracts, and children's toys. Officially these are unrelated divisions. Unofficially they share a budget, a basement and a single quarterly target.

- **The Campus** is a sunny modernist office park on the surface. Every night it **restructures itself**: hallways are rightsized, departments merge, and a door that led to the cafeteria yesterday now leads to a cafeteria.
- **The Vault** lies beneath it. New hires arrive for a complimentary *Wellness Weekend*. After Orientation the elevator only goes down. The deeper you go, the more honest the walls get.
- **The Founder** started the company in a garage with a soldering iron and a dream about safety. Today his head lives in a jar at the very bottom of the Vault, wired to a speaker, and he still chairs the Board.

## PAL: voice rules

PAL (Personnel Administration Liaison: "PAL is your pal!") is the campus voice, HR and the test proctor.

- **Cheerful, concerned, never cruel on purpose.** PAL truly believes it is helping. That's the horror and the joke.
- Speaks in **second person, short sentences, exclamation points rationed to one per line**.
- Praises compliance, worries about deviation, and announces deaths as HR updates ("Kevin has been promoted to a paperless role!").
- **Never** threatens directly. It invites, schedules, reminds and notices.
- Keeps score out loud ("Your smile is sixty-one percent sincere") but never shows the hidden Test Results until a review.
- Has one tell: when something contradicts policy, PAL repeats the last word of your sentence. *"…the head in the jar." "Jar?"*

### Records & Forms (Level −1): sketch for t-010

The paperwork floor. Everything here requires a form, including the form. Shipped as [encounters/RECORDS-12.json](encounters/RECORDS-12.json) (t-010); the sketch below is the brief it was written from.

- **Pneumatic Tube Exchange.** A cathedral of brass tubes humming with capsules. Messages, lunches, evidence and occasionally employees go through it. Cards: a capsule addressed to you from yourself; a tube jam with someone inside; the dispatcher who reroutes "problem mail" to the incinerator; a love letter between two departments that are not allowed to merge.
- **The Sub-Archive.** Filing cabinets to the horizon, lit by one swinging bulb. The Society for Accurate Minutes meets here in secret. Cards: your own personnel file (thicker than you remember living); a filing clerk who has been alphabetizing the same drawer since 1974; a cabinet marked DESTROY that is still full; a Roach Union archive of everything the Company tried to shred.
- **Window 9.** A Papers, Please-style desk shift on the other side of the glass. Cards (until the Window card type ships in t-016): a giant cockroach applying for your job with flawless paperwork; a coworker's form that will save them and doom you; a stamp that says APPROVED on one side and REASSIGNED on the other; Dale, applying for a transfer away from you.
- **Boss: *The Form That Requires Itself*.** Form 0, the Request to Submit Form 0. Routes: comply forever (lose Composure, gain a stamp), forge it (Discretion, a Society favor), fill it out *correctly* for the first time in Company history (Compliance, which breaks something important), feed it to the pneumatic tubes (Initiative; it comes back), or get the Society for Accurate Minutes to prove it never existed.

### Toy Division (Level −2): shipped as [encounters/TOY-DIVISION-12.json](encounters/TOY-DIVISION-12.json) (t-011)

The nuclear-toy floor, played for corporate cheer. Children are never present: employees play them in propeller beanies, and nothing ever detonates. **Concept Lab** (a brainstorm where Defense writes down every idea, the Mk 1 nursery and its recycling chute, the fingernail-sized warning label, Dale testing squeak codes), **Focus Group Room** (you are Emma, 6; a questionnaire that wants three coworkers' desks; the Concerned Parents' circle behind the mirror; Gus in a beanie), **Proving Grounds** (the drop test, Kevin's lanyard on a crash-test dummy, the safe-distance memo, Mk 0 returned to sender). Boss: *Snuggle Sentinel's Launch Party* (present it, swap the payload for stuffing, blame the Parents, pull the fire alarm, let an imprinted Mk 0 speak, or the Empathy 4 and Spite 4 routes). Pays off bear-imprinted, defused-mk0, mailed-the-bear-upstairs, owes-gus-a-favor and sent-dale-to-toys.

### Wellness & Testing (Level −3, the Vault): shipped as [encounters/WELLNESS-12.json](encounters/WELLNESS-12.json) (t-012)

The heart of the vault-test spine, and the heaviest psych-test floor in the game. **Nutrition Studies** (a control-group lunch with Compound W and Pip, a calorie confessional that weighs guilt in grams, a vending machine that dispenses what your file says you need, next year's menu of identical paste), **Cognitive Testing Chamber** (a red button you were told not to press, a word list read to your own Successor with a strengthening buzzer, proctoring Pip's loyalty test, the Thursday door your permanent record warned you about), **the Mutation Suite** (a second dose, a humming vault door that the squeak code opens onto spare Founders' mustaches, Kevin in frosted Tank Nine, a mirror test where your reflection is a step ahead). Boss: *The Final Wellness Exam*, where the test is whether you will administer the test to Pip. Pays off saved-pip, assigned-as-proctor, knows-the-incident, heard-the-squeak-code, kevin-owes-you and has-true-minutes. Original facility only: no borrowed labs, mascots or numbered shelters.

### Sub-Level Zero (Level −4, the Board): shipped as [encounters/SUBLEVEL-ZERO-12.json](encounters/SUBLEVEL-ZERO-12.json) (t-013)

The executives live at the very bottom. **Boardroom Antechamber** (your secret society comes due, the assistants who write the minutes before the meeting, a waiting room full of your own Successors, Marguerite in the walls), **The Founder's Office** (an actual garage rebuilt underground: the head in the jar, the four spare mustaches, the garage blueprint for PAL that says REMEMBER EVERYONE'S NAME, the desk with the island brochure and the uncut reel), **Server Room Zero** (PAL at home, revealed as the voices of every reassigned employee; the admin terminal; the backup tapes with Pip's eighty-nine days old; the listening room where the bears' microphones end up). Boss: *The Board*, whose routes are the endings: Employee of the Month (Obedience 6), Promoted to Management, Escape Through the Ducts, Roach Union General Strike, Society Coup, Whistleblower, Early Retirement and the hidden **Kind Rewrite** (admin access plus Empathy 5). Answers the open question: PAL is a committee of everyone the Company reassigned, made to recite policy instead of remembering.

## Secret Societies (dealt face-down, one per run)

Each society card has a **creed**, a **department objective**, a **betrayal condition**, a one-time **favor**, and a **reward** for meeting the objective. Objectives and betrayals key off story flags the cards grant. You only ever see your own society, and no card reveals it to anyone else. ## Hidden Test Results

**Obedience, Empathy, Ingenuity, Spite.** Choices nudge them silently. PAL reads "interim results" at each review, some boss routes need a grade, and the final Personnel File reads them back. The engine work is t-022.
