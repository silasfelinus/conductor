# Weird Humboldt: three narrative prototypes

These are **playable-text design samples**, not imported cards. Values, prose and balance are provisional; die rolls are 2d6+named skill vs target. Outcomes illustrate distinct consequences, including on failure.

## WH-006 — The Last Houdini Exit · Minor Theatre

The midnight audience is gone, but applause keeps coming from beneath the stage. A trapdoor lifts one inch. A gloved hand pushes up a handwritten note: **STOP CALLING ME HARRY.** The voice below sounds exhausted rather than spectral. 'Do you know how many amateur magicians have used this door to impress a first date?' it asks. 'Seventy-three. None of them tipped.' Behind the voice, another person is applauding from what sounds like a century away.

- **Ask for the voice's real name** · Rizz 7; on success, learn an intimate detail (`minor_occupant_trusted`, +Goodwill). On failure, the occupant suspects you are press and shuts the trapdoor; hear a useful last name anyway (+Evidence rumor, -1 Time).
- **Inspect the rope system** · Wits 9; on success, identify an impossible extra pulley (+Evidence radio, +1 Weird Exposure). On failure, the curtain falls and the empty auditorium applauds (-1 Nerve, `minor_curtain_fell`).
- **Offer the unseen performer a ride home** · no roll; sacrifice your next free location draw to help; earn `minor_companion`, a callback in the finale. Sometimes decency is more expensive than bravery.

*Provenance:* The trapdoor is reported by a former owner; Houdini's performance is disputed, the voice is invented fiction.

## WH-021 — Bigfoot Family Picnic · Moonstone Beach

Three vast figures in sun hats are arguing over the proper way to pose for a family photo. The youngest holds a phone with a cracked screen. The tallest says you may leave if you promise not to call them **the Sasquatches**. Uncle Glenn is missing again. Everyone agrees this is typical. The cooler contains sparkling water and a truly impossible number of deviled eggs. The tide has begun moving backward, but the family is focused on whether Glenn got the invitation.

- **Become the family's photographer** · Rizz 7; on success gain their trust and notice a fourth family member visible only on the screen (+Goodwill, +Evidence memory). On failure, you mention Glenn at the wrong moment and start an argument; one relative follows you into the next card (`glenn_followup`, -1 Nerve).
- **Follow the backwards tide** · Psyche 9; on success recover Glenn's folded note and a clue to the frequency (+Evidence geography). On failure, return wet and two hours older (-1 Time, +1 Weird Exposure).
- **Ask who brought the eggs** · no roll; receive a recipe, a warning never to photograph the sea at dawn, and `sasquatch_recipe`. The clue is not immediately useful; the friendship may be.

*Provenance:* Moonstone Beach is real; the Sasquatch family is original comic fiction.

## WH-061 — The Perilous Plunge into Monday · F Street waterfront, Eureka

Costumed jumpers form a queue near Humboldt Bay. A marching band strikes up, and a volunteer in a homemade squid costume dives in. They emerge holding a newspaper from Monday, three days too soon, and insist they have already survived this morning twice. The event organizer will not suspend judging because the squid costume is, in fairness, magnificent. A hot tub nearby has begun hissing last year's weather forecast.

- **Pull the squid from the bay** · Burly 7; on success save their memory of the future (+Goodwill, `plunge_saved`) but lose the wet newspaper (-Evidence paper). On failure get them ashore anyway, cold and disoriented; the newspaper floats away while you take -1 Nerve.
- **Retrieve the future newspaper** · Wits 9; on success document one true prediction (+Evidence document, +1 Weird Exposure). On failure snatch a fake edition published by someone watching you (`plunge_false_story`, +1 Weird Exposure).
- **Ask the organizers to hold a second judging** · Rizz 11; on success win an absurdly official day-change waiver (`festival_waiver`); on failure gain a recurring volunteer nemesis and one hilarious participation ribbon.

*Provenance:* Fundraising plunge and location are documented. Time anomalies, speeches and people are invented.

## PROLOGUE — The Woman Who Knows the Trail

The music shakes moss off a huge wooden puppet. Across the meadow a man is juggling five clubs with what appear to be six hands. A handmade sign at the forest edge reads: **THE WOODS ARE NOT RESPONSIBLE FOR WHAT YOU REMEMBER**. A young woman with a smoky joint and a green ribbon in her hair asks if you need directions to the stage or the path behind it. Her reflection in a puddle has branches growing from its head. She notices you noticing.

The player is **adult** and every intake choice is explicit, voluntary and clearly identified. This **fictional composite** scene is not an allegation about real Cannifest organizers or visitors.

- **Water and a sandwich.** `opening_state=sober`; Clarity 3. Wits 7 to notice that the puddle's reflected branches move against the wind. On success gain `nymph_seen_clearly`. On failure the woman gives you a perfectly ordinary map that nonetheless contains an unmarked trail. You may follow it.
- **A social drink and the dance circle.** `opening_state=tipsy`; Clarity 2. Rizz 7 to talk your way into the musicians' inner circle. A drumbeat echoes a split-second before the percussionist strikes. Failure gets you a funny invitation but an unreliable witness.
- **A voluntary cannabis experience and a quiet place to listen.** `opening_state=stoned`; Clarity 2. Psyche 9 to recognize a forest polyrhythm nobody seems to have composed. Even on failure the signal is later discoverable by an independent witness.
- **A clearly labeled *enchanted fictional brownie*.** `opening_state=tripping_balls`; Clarity 1. The forest has acquired a painted moon. Psyche 9 may reveal a route; Rizz 7 can instead ask the woman to sit with you and explain why nobody is looking at the sky. Failure produces a false lead, not game-over or an objectively changed forest.

All four paths proceed with an independent and potentially helpful guide and a real signal that can eventually be proven. No sexual implications, no magical powers from real drugs, no covert dosing.

### Four readings of one canonical scene: WH-021, Bigfoot family picnic

| Lens | First impression | Distinct way through |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sober | The photo shows a fourth person when only three stood in frame | Wits 7 confirms timestamp and the impossible reversed tide |
| Tipsy | It feels like an invitation to a huge family cookout | Rizz 7 defuses Uncle Glenn's feud, but an impulsive toast risks losing the photo |
| Stoned | The surf pulses to an oddly familiar rhythm | Psyche 9 links the music to the forest; corroboration still required |
| Tripping balls | The picnic blanket resembles a county map | Psyche 9 reads a symbol correctly or follows an amusing false lead |

**Truth invariant:** Uncle Glenn, the fourth photographed person and the backwards tide exist independent of the player state. Content variation is not a new canonical encounter.
