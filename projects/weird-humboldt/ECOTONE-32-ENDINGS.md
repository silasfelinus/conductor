# Ecotone: 32 distinct terminal story endings

**Updated 2026-10-19 PT.** Silas's current direction is **4 lead companions × 4 voluntary opening states × 2 earned fates = 32 collectible endings: exactly 16 hopeful and 16 dark**. This replaces the older **ten ending-family collectible** concept. Those ten county futures in `ECOTONE-ENDINGS.md` remain **noncollectible world-state motifs**, available to color these 32 stories. This matrix is **design treatments only**, not 32 fully authored 1/10/30-year epilogues or live features. No art or publication authorized.

## Authoritative cross-product contract

- **Four independent leads:** Juniper, Kit, Mara (working name), Cedar (working name). All four exist in every route. Sober→Mara, Tipsy→Kit, Stoned→Juniper and Tripping Balls→Cedar are *suggested first introductions only*. A player can choose or commit to **any lead companion after initial meetings** via guaranteed authored chapter anchors. Other companions can still participate in scenes; one chosen lead frames each finale.
- **Four opening-state lenses:** `sober`, `tipsy`, `stoned`, `tripping`, with `tripping` being the stable collectible-ID shorthand for `tripping_balls` in runtime. The adult player character voluntarily chooses a lens during the opening. `opening_state` is immutable and is used for personalized finale prose; `current_state` and recoverable `clarity` may change throughout the story. No real-world use is involved.
- **Two earned futures:** `hope` or `dark`, reflecting accumulated choices, commitments, corroborated evidence and transparent fair 2d6 rolls. **Starting sober/tipsy/stoned/tripping does not dictate or automatically modify good/bad outcome.** Failed checks lead to interesting complications and paths to repair. The split is a target count for *content*, not a random 50% probability of failure or an ideology score.
- **Stable ID:** `<lead>.<opening>.<fate>`, e.g. `cedar.sober.hope`. Exactly 32 IDs, 16 of each fate, 8 per companion, 8 per opening lens. IDs only unlock on a naturally completed finale, not simply opening the gallery or the initial meeting. Each eventual finished record needs distinct story, relationship/civic callback, and **1-, 10-, and 30-year epilogue beats** (96 authored temporal beats). Distinct titles alone do not count as authored endings.
- **Cedar's family canon:** His timber-family and grower-family parents remain a couple in all 32 futures. Never turn that enduring love into a divorce/reunion reward. Real Julia Butterfly Hill/Luna and Kit's historically grounded expulsion context are immutable; present-day speculative futures are fiction. Tribal sovereignty and consent aren't stat rolls.

## Cedar's four technoutopia paths are central

Cedar is a younger country Humboldt local with mechanics and CB-radio instincts, not the old ex-grower/hippie/dispensary concept. His **four hopeful companion×opening branches** must show an ethical **technoutopia** under meaningful local control, *not* a blanket refusal of technology. Specific variants: publicly/cooperatively owned rural broadband and forest sensor networks; an enforceable alliance of tradespeople and technology employers; energy-aware community computing supporting rural telework and habitat restoration; and verified fictional forecasting signals used for distributed resilience and accountable investment. Jobs must be real, worker safety and tribal land authority respected, environmental monitoring transparent, energy/water limits considered, and forests still alive. A dark counterpart in each lens examines outside network monopoly, hollow high-tech jobs promises, ecological cost or predictive surveillance/control. Parents together in both good and dark branches.

## Presentation, persistence, reachability, and thematic balance

- One **single-screen** viewport-fitted game surface on mobile, tablet, desktop. Main toolbar **Endings X/32**. Gallery grouped by four companions (8 slots each), then four opening-state pairs (hope/dark); optionally aggregate `Hope X/16 · Dark Y/16`. Internal bounded scrolling okay, never page scrolling.
- Locked slots hide unrevealed titles, art and descriptive accessibility text; unlocked slots show own 1/10/30-year epilogues, date, meaningful choice callbacks and replay. Versioned, deduplicated browser-local ledger independent of current run; no silent conversion of hypothetical ten-world-variant history into 32 slot unlocks. Kind Robots global achievement **All the Possible Humboldts** triggers only at genuine **32/32**.
- QA: for each of 16 lead/opening pairs, prove both good and dark via naturally reachable authored seeded play, including declined original guide, no RNG-only lead lock, failure repair, fair sobriety, intact family and 32/32 persistence. Any lead's positive civic future must be accessible after *sober* opening, although collecting all 32 lens-specific outcomes necessarily means trying the four **fictional** optional openings over multiple runs.
- **The genre is hopeful** although half of the endings are dark. They are avoidable consequences, not a cosmic insistence that Humboldt is doomed. Avoid making human dignity or community harms into a joke or assigning a moral win to one ideology.
