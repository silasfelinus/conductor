# Ecotone: 32 earned endings, 16 hopeful and 16 dark

**User-directed structure, 2026-10-19 PT.** Exactly **4 final active companions × 4 original voluntary opening lenses × 2 consequence outcomes = 32 unique, persistent collectible endings**. The outline below is a 32-story authoring matrix, NOT 32 finished epilogue records. Art, code deployment and publication remain on hold.

**Read [JOURNEY-LOOP-CONTRACT.md](JOURNEY-LOOP-CONTRACT.md) first:** The chosen opening state automatically introduces the first companion (**Sober→Mara, Tipsy→Kit, Stoned→Juniper, Tripping→Cedar**). The player goes through substantial **seeded randomized regional encounters**, meets each other main character at a guaranteed story beat, and explicitly **stays with the current companion or follows the new one**, leaving the old guide to their own independent life. The encounter **order, follow choices, stat shifts and dice outcomes** cause meaningful callbacks and influence the final fate. There is **no upfront independent lead selector**. The **last actively followed companion** is the end-frame character, not automatically the first.

## The collection contract

- `ending_id = final_lead + "." + opening_lens + "." + outcome`, with `final_lead ∈ {mara,kit,juniper,cedar}`, `opening_lens ∈ {sober,tipsy,stoned,tripping}`, `outcome ∈ {hope,dark}`. Actual runtime `opening_state=tripping_balls` maps to string `tripping` only for ID generation.
- **32 total, 16 hopeful / 16 dark; exactly 8 for each final lead; exactly 8 for each opening lens.** `opening_state` is **immutable** across the weekend and informs the epilogue; `current_state` / Clarity may change, and substance use is not a morality score or predetermined win/loss probability.
- **Finale result reflects earlier randomized card choices, persisted stats, companion loyalty and relationships, evidence and visible 2d6 resolution, with reasonable opportunities for repair.** There is no one arbitrary "good/bad" prompt at the end.
- **All four followers appear every run** in an authored, seeded order. The player may stay, leave or later re-meet. Cross-companion order must **change the narrative flavor and flags**, even when two runs unlock the same collectible ID. Those inner variations are authored callback branches, **not additional collectible endings**.
- Previously discussed **ten county-future scenario families** (Redwood Commons, Silicon Estuary, Global Terminal, etc.) are retained as **world-state motifs** inside the 32 endings, not ten more collectible slots.
- Each ID ultimately needs a **distinctly written** 1-year / 10-year / 30-year epilogue and character-specific community consequences (96 temporal scenes in total). Do not satisfy this with a generic text template plus a changed intoxication adjective.
- In every one of the 32 outcomes, Cedar's logger-family parent and cannabis-grower-family parent **remain together**, though their livelihood/community may differ. Julia Butterfly Hill's actual Luna vigil and 1885 Eureka Chinese expulsion remain documented fixed history.
- The story's thesis stays **optimistic overall**: 16 dark ends can be genuinely disastrous, but never imply that choices never mattered. Hope is achievable from **every** opening state, for **every** potential final companion.

## Cedar's technoutopia is essential

His happiest outcomes are **not** simply "the loggers and environmentalists forgive each other." His mechanical aptitude, skepticism of unverified claims, life among timber and grower families, and weirdly accurate CB forecasts lead naturally to an **ethical, locally controlled technoutopia**. Tech includes rural fiber, fair remote employment, energy- and water-accountable computing, workforce training, environmental monitoring and public/community ownership. Timber communities can have technically sophisticated futures without giving up their relationship to working forests. The opposite four Cedar endings show genuinely different failure modes: privatized networks, flimsy investor promises, data-center extraction and authoritarian prediction systems. Avoid claiming any real current company commits those fictional acts.

## How order makes one ending feel different each time

`kit.sober.hope` reached after **Mara → Kit** should remember the player's early respect for client privacy and local leases. The **same** ending reached after **Mara → Juniper → Cedar → Kit** may feature a kinetic arts studio supplied by new community broadband, forest safeguards and compromises earned earlier. Different encounters or rolls can change the venue, which NPC was saved and who attends the opening, without inventing `kit.sober.hope.v27` collectible IDs. In a given lens, players can find one of the four possible lead characters at the end by **actual stay/follow choices**, not by clicking a separate companion-select screen.
