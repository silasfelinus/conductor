# Ecotone scope decision: Wiyot/Tuluwat story material excluded

**Owner decision: October 22, 2026 PT.** The owner directed the project to **drop the Wiyot/Tuluwat-dependent content entirely from the plan**, rather than leaving a review or consultation workstream on hold.

## Effective scope

- **No Wiyot/Tuluwat historical or cultural game content**: remove the proposed Tuluwat/Indian Island episode, "Across the Bay" chapter anchor, 1860 massacre-based beats or flags, the hat-by-the-door anecdote and morality echo, and the held 1849/Mad River/Wiyot expedition roleplay **WH-121** from the planned playable story and future editorial queue.
- **No associated art or audio**: no new portraits, scene prompts, outcome prompts, ending variants, galleries, style promotions or exports based on that material. Previously generated files/job IDs may be retained **internally for audit**, not reopened as a backlog or displayed/promoted in the game or art UI. No destructive deletion of media is authorized by this planning decision.
- **No consultation project**: no Wiyot Tribe outreach, review packet, compensation proposal, approval dependency, speculative cultural research or delayed release gate for this content. Earlier conditional consultation plans are superseded, not postponed.
- **Mara remains a fictional independent tattoo artist**, with her ordinary relationships, professional ethics and chosen-family storylines. Do not restore the discarded tribal-grant/reservation backstory or culturally coded designs. Do not silently substitute some other living tribe's identity or symbols.
- Existing **game and art suppression guards stay in effect** as a permanent exclusion while this content is archived. Do not make the old hidden cards/assets playable simply because the old consultation plan was withdrawn. A later introduction would require a new explicit owner direction and an independent review.
- Ordinary Humboldt fiction, community and environmental disputes, Kinetic race, all other companions, local ecology, and unrelated sourced history continue. Normal creative/art/publication gates **t-004 and t-007** still apply to the rest of the game; they are not cleared by this decision.
- **Planning cleanup**: the dedicated research task **t-030** has been withdrawn from the live roadmap rather than relabeled complete. Future workers should not recreate it from old notes, old prompts or a previously authored pitch.

## Provenance, not a work queue

Prior historical and research documents, including `HISTORY-THREADS.md` and `WIYOT-TULUWAT-DECISION.md`, and the historic roadmap/task-event history, are **superseded archival records only**. Preserve attribution and references for an internal audit, but do not use them as requirements, story prompts, image prompts, tickets, release conditions or presumed approval.

This decision means omission from this fictional game, **not** a claim that this history is unimportant or untrue. It is a project scope choice, not a historical judgment.
