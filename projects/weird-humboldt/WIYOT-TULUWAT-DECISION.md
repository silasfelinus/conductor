# Ecotone | Wiyot / Tuluwat: conditional internal-work approval

**Decision date:** 2026-10-21 (PT)
**Decision maker:** Ecotone project owner, in conversation
**Status:** Approved **for internal development under a Wiyot-community review boundary**. This is not a consultation result, a Tribe-endorsed interpretation, or public-release permission.

## What the owner approved

The owner approved the pre-consultation report's central recommendation: **continue responsibly sourced research, private script revisions, correcting errors, revisable fictional development and preparation of a review packet, while withholding public Wiyot/Tuluwat-specific text, imagery, audio, mapped sacred sites and cultural interpretation until there is an opportunity for voluntary Wiyot-directed review.** No representative of the Wiyot Tribe participated in this owner approval.

This decision is not blanket approval for copyrighted tribal materials, synthetic language voices, religious or ceremonial depictions, invented Wiyot testimony, reproducing sacred visual designs, contact/outreach, publication, commercial release, or statutory consultation. Prior separate Conductor gates `t-004` (creative) and `t-007` (publication) remain in effect; do **not** set either `approved_by_human: true` on the strength of this limited decision.

## Source corrections to preserve in the actual game and packet

1. **Massacre death-count scope.** The Wiyot Tribe's [History](https://www.wiyot.us/148/Cultural) gives an estimated 80–250 deaths in the **series of coordinated massacres that night**, not necessarily on Tuluwat alone. Avoid recycling the draft's island-only formulation; do not substitute an invented count.
2. **1849 Wiyot agency and names.** Public Wiyot language resources identify Mad River as **Baduwa't** ([source](https://www.wiyot.us/450/A-Photographic-Exploration-of-Wigi)). A [Wiyot Tribe newsletter](https://www.wiyot.us/Archive.aspx?ADID=122) describes the welcome from Wiyot leader Ki-we-lat-tah and his family to Gregg's party at Humboldt Bay. These are distinct pieces of evidence; do not fabricate a Wiyot appearance at the *Mad River crossing* merely to fix an omission. WH-121's 1849 gag currently exists in the playable game and needs careful provenance correction; it has not received tribal review.
3. **Unverified hat story.** The recollection of a man hanging a hat to signify nonparticipation is *not documented in the project's cited sources*. Do not assert it as history or spin it into a `hat_by_the_door` score, ending flag or analogy to the massacre. A separately authored fictional bystander dilemma can be evaluated on its own merits.
4. **Living presence.** A proposed Tuluwat chapter should foreground present Wiyot sovereignty, return and stewardship, publicly reported language and history initiatives, not only 1860. The proposed `Across the Bay` scene remains a private *concept*, not an existing verified playable component.
5. **Mara.** Her current narrative is an Indigenous tattoo artist from an unspecified nation with a fictional tribal business grant. Resolve the geography and implications without passing off an invented Wiyot casino/grant arrangement as fact or making Indigenous identity an origin of magical powers. Her draft art and culturally specific designs require separate scrutiny.
6. **Creative boundaries.** No roll, stats, reward, ghosts, violent reenactment, direct historical time travel, comic puzzle, proprietary site map or sacred ceremony representation at Tuluwat. Fictional devices do not change history. Historical trauma cannot stand in for a player's moral minigame.

## Review packet to prepare before any contact

Prepare a concise project synopsis, distribution/monetization explanation, precisely written proposed historical copy (with alternative of linking to Wiyot-authored resources rather than incorporating game copy), WH-121 current/corrected text side-by-side, Mara's full background and proposed image prompts, screen/art restrictions, public/primary-source ledger and explicit open uncertainties. Provide compensation terms, review scope and sufficient time for a genuine no, including the option of no material being used.

**Contact is a distinct human-approved action.** Do not email/call the Tribe on this instruction alone. Before any approach, present the exact invitation, payment terms and plan for the owner's approval. Route through the Wiyot Tribe's [Historic Preservation / Cultural Department](https://www.wiyot.us/156/Historic-Preservation) if it elects to entertain a commercial fiction review; ask whom they designate and what form of review, if any, they would find appropriate. Silence is not assent. Never imply one reader speaks for every Wiyot person or that consulting gives rights to protected culture.

## Release gate remains open

Prior to outward-facing Wiyot/Tuluwat content: confirm the specific Tribe-agreed scope or documented decision not to participate, make proportionate editorial choices respecting that decision, fact-check exact final language, independently clear audio/art permissions and accessibility, and obtain the project owner's explicit final creative/publishing approval. If the Tribe declines or cannot be reached, consider a restrained Wiyot-attributed source link or omit sensitive representation rather than imply tribal review or permission.

## Source precedence and agents

The Wiyot Tribe's own published sources take precedence over unsupported previous draft assertions. Direct Wiyot review, if available, supersedes third-party speculation within its agreed scope. Historical and place-name claims must be attributed; invented characters/events must not be attributed to a real nation. When code/manuscript/art disagrees, fix all representations together in one reversible PR with provenance and tests. Do not alter an existing art queue or publish images without separately checking t-042 scope and cultural controls.
