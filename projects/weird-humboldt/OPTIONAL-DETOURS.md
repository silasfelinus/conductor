# Ecotone: optional shorter roads (historical detour pitches, superseded mandatory ending rule)

**User revision, 2026-10-19 PT:** The earlier rule that **Cocaine / Meth / MDMA must lead to scripted dead ends is superseded.** These are now **three additional legitimate adult voluntary opening lenses**, each permitted to complete the full six-chapter companion journey and reach **hopeful or dark civic futures**. Together with Sober/Tipsy/Stoned/Tripping this yields **7 openings × 4 final companions × 2 fates = 56 collectible endings (28/28)**. All four companions are encountered by authored Stay/Follow crossroads. The full production design is [ECOTONE-ENDING-GRAMMAR.md](ECOTONE-ENDING-GRAMMAR.md); no art/production is authorized by this document.

## Reuse rather than discard the good side-story ideas

- **Cocaine: The Fast Track** can serve as an optional fast-paced initial scene or a short side quest about ambition, contracts and whose labor makes a project possible. Never equate cocaine use with greed.
- **Meth: The Signal That Never Sleeps** can serve as an unsettling radio-investigation prologue or optional loop with a genuine supernatural clue that can be independently verified. Avoid presenting real people with addiction as monsters, violent or inherently delusional.
- **MDMA: Everybody Loves Everybody** can be a heartfelt festival prologue, stand-alone voluntary moment, or one of many meaningful side encounters. A character may experience real emotional connection, while making lasting institutions will still depend on their later choices. Do not call this a failure by definition.

These names and dramatic setups are **working pitches**, not guaranteed substance effects, a therapeutic claim or an ideological alignment. Each new opening receives a real scripted encounter with one of the four established companions (chosen by its story rather than another companion-select dropdown), and later guaranteed opportunity to Stay/Follow any of the others, with full hope/dark finale parity.

## Optional early endings can still exist, but belong to choices

A player in **any of the seven starting states** may deliberately pursue a short, complete side narrative or make choices that lead to an early finish, where authoring justifies it. The early finish is **not automatically triggered by opening chemistry**; a clear, informed option prevents an unintended loss of access to the main county-fate quest. Such closures are not among the 56 main gallery slots and do not earn main-finale achievements.

If a detour is voluntarily committed after beginning a main run, record the actual opening and follower history for callbacks but do not emit a false civic finale. If a player simply chooses cocaine/meth/MDMA as their **opening lens**, they stay in the **main journey** and can earn any one of eight lead×fate endings appropriate to that lens. Do not fabricate a former canonical opening or require taking a second drug to meet a companion.

No involuntary dosing, minors, procurement, preparation, dangerous combinations, intoxicated driving, or promises about real physiological or psychological effects. Maintain PG-13 tone, sober evidence parity, protected real history and living tribal rights.

**Design/accounting:** Original 32 collectible IDs remain, 24 new `lead.opening.fate` signatures are added, but there are **8 authored core futures** and selective verifiable callbacks. This is an authoring compression strategy, **not** an excuse for superficial repeated paragraphs. The title, mood, memorable action and reviewed art brief should make each signature distinct; image generation remains gated until explicit user permission.
