# Ecotone: three optional drug detours

**Silas's latest design direction, 2026-10-19 PT. Text-only proposal, not approved gameplay or art.**

Three additional, adult-only, voluntary alternatives: **cocaine, methamphetamine and MDMA**, available **instead of** the four canonical opening perspectives **or later** during one of the four journeys. Each is a finite, distinct narrative adventure with an authored ending **before Humboldt's civic-fate convergence**. None earns a hopeful county ending, one of its 32 collectible lead/opening/hope-or-dark IDs, or Kind Robots main-ending achievement.

**The road ends in this story, not in the character's life.** This is not an anti-drug moral lesson, a medical forecast, or a claim that a substance inevitably produces certain behavior. MDMA may yield a genuinely meaningful, affirmative personal experience and still leave Humboldt's big political decision beyond the protagonist's involvement. Cocaine or meth characters may act courageously or compassionately. No real substance determines someone's political ideology or worth.

## Three distinct script pitches

| Detour ID and title | Fictional adventure | Terminal human consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **cocaine: The Fast Track** | A wild night of ambitious development offers, fake and genuine contracts, artists, investors and a race to get ahead of Humboldt's changing future. Players may expose a fraud, protect a worker or chase a private opportunity. | The decisive county gathering has passed while the character pursued immediate affairs. Helping a person can still matter; don't equate cocaine use with greed. |
| **meth: The Signal That Never Sleeps** | An eerie trail of radio anomalies around fictional industrial backroads, uncertain signals and people who need help. The protagonist investigates which transmissions are real, may stop to care for someone, or chases the next clue. | The signal yields an unsettling real fact or a compassionate human moment, but the larger convergence is over. Horror belongs to uncertainty and fictional anomalies, **not** to the identity of people who use meth, addiction or poverty. |
| **mdma: Everybody Loves Everybody** | A luminous kinetic-festival night of surprising social honesty, music, friendship, reconciliation and possible personal growth. Immediate feelings still need real follow-through to change institutions. | An authentically tender, perhaps even life-affirming personal ending. The protagonist was not there to negotiate Humboldt's lasting future, so **no canonical hopeful county ending**. No message that MDMA inevitably ruins people. |

These are provisional story treatments. In every route offer at least two meaningful choices and a fair visible skill roll or legitimate no-roll alternative. Different human-scale terminal variations may exist without creating new main ending collectibles. Initially aim for **3–7 distinct authored encounter cards per route**, plus a satisfying closing scene; future creative review may extend length. These do not replace any of the **120 canonical main encounter cards**.

## Entry rules: off-ramps, not main-route modifiers

**Alternative at the festival:** Present an optional Other Roads entry beside the original **Sober / Tipsy / Stoned / Tripping Balls** choices. Before committing, disclose clearly that it is a **self-contained shorter story that will end before the county-wide finale**. Choosing it begins route_kind = detour, detour_source = prelude and detour_id = cocaine, meth or mdma. **Do not fabricate an opening_state or intro_companion** for a player who skipped the four canonical starts.

**From an existing journey:** At a few intentionally authored events, present a voluntary detour, alongside an explicit **Keep following the county's future** choice. Warn before irreversible entry. On choosing the side road, route_kind becomes detour, detour_source = mid_run; save the **actual** original opening_state, active companion and follow history for tailored callbacks, never replace the opening ID or reroute to a canonical finale. The protagonist's former companion retains independent agency. Declining the detour leaves the full main story and its **32** endings reachable. Once accepted, the current run concludes in its distinct finite detour. A saved checkpoint from *before* commitment may naturally be reloaded, but there is no disguised comeback to the convergence within the detour route.

**State union:** Main journey uses route_kind = main and preserves the established seeded six-chapter stay/follow companion loop. Detour uses route_kind = detour, detour_id of three known IDs, detour_source, optional previous_opening_state and previous_companion for callback only, seen_detour_cards, scene_flags and detour_resolution. It must **not** set main ending_id, main fate, or award the site's first-final-ending/global-collection badges. One completed vignette is **not** a 33rd–35th ending.

**Optional discovery:** A compact Journal / Other Roads tab can record three encountered mini-adventures, with spoiler-safe hints before discovery. This optional 3-road collection stays **separate from Endings X/32**, should not be required for game completion and should not offer achievements for merely selecting substances.

## Writing and ethical guardrails

Character is adult, makes an informed optional choice, can refuse before route commitment. No involuntary dosing, minors, sourcing or purchasing drugs, preparation, dosing, mixing instructions, impaired driving or real treatment claims. Avoid clinical or cultural caricature, especially the idea that anyone using meth is violent, homeless or inhuman. Do not map stimulant use to moral defects or MDMA use to inevitable insight; all are **fictional writerly lenses**, not claims of universal drug effects. Player still has agency and can make generous or harmful choices in each short arc. No story tells the user to try any substance to improve their life.

The four primary intros map Sober→Mara, Tipsy→Kit, Stoned→Juniper, Tripping→Cedar as before. No standalone initial companion selector; each main journey has randomized authored side cards and guaranteed later **Stay / Follow** choices. The 32 terminal main endings remain 4 final companions × 4 immutable main opening states × earned hopeful/dark fate, **16 hopeful and 16 dark**. These three optional routes are neither a fifth/sixth/seventh ending lens nor automatically a dark main ending.

## Production and verification

1. Three defined detour IDs, two entry sources, and truthful optionality; no default substance selection or gate to any essential mystery evidence.
2. Main opening skipped entirely for prelude detour; retained unchanged for mid-run detour. A genuine terminal detour cannot call the main ending resolver or unlock main achievement badges.
3. Three reproducible seeded mini-runs with authored choices, actual closing vignette and save/resume. Refusing any detour preserves all 32 canonical pathways.
4. MDMA closure can be clearly affirmative *personally* even though the character misses the *county-wide* fate decision. Other two routes allow dignity and kindness. Drugs cannot be treated as the source of real supernatural facts by themselves.
5. All side encounters, journal and closure use **one viewport-fitted non-page-scrolling surface on phone/tablet/desktop**, with bounded internal scrolling and accessible choice/exit controls.
6. Maintain the historical, tribal sovereignty, narrative, **no-art**, image queue and human publication gates. This file is planning only.
