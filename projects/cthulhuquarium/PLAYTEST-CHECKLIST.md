# Cthulhuquarium: human playtest checklist

For Silas, at `/play/aquarium` on kindrobots.org once the next Force Update has
deployed kind_robots #5472, #5479, #5497 (canon rarity and floor keys), #5498
(settings, title card, endings), #5500 (cross-device fixes), #5501 (RC fixes) and #5503 (schema cleanup)
(see [RC-DOSSIER.md](RC-DOSSIER.md), which also says how to reach the deep floors
in a short session). This is a handoff sheet, not a gate. It
lists what to look at and what each thing should feel like, so a verdict can
be specific. Allow 20–30 minutes on a phone and 10 on a desktop. Use a **fresh
account** for parts 1–3 and your own tank for part 4.

## 1. The first five minutes (fresh account)

- [ ] The intro plays full-screen. The text types out, a tap finishes the line,
      and a second tap moves on. Charlotte's and Wilbur's hero portraits appear.
- [ ] The tutorial beats (buy a free fish, Feed) drop to the small sheet so the
      buttons stay reachable. The intro's last line from Charlotte promises "floors below
      this one".
- [ ] The HUD shows a **"I · The Shop Floor"** capsule. On a phone, nothing in the
      HUD is cut off at the right edge.
- [ ] Reload in a new tab. A **title card** appears once per browser session, over
      the arrival plate: Enter the tank, Memory Book, Settings and Achievements.
- [ ] The **gear** opens Settings: volume, text speed, larger story text, high
      contrast and motion. The changes should show in the next scene. Esc closes it.
- [ ] The Market has today's commons, plus three **black silhouettes** reading
      "Something further down — Below Stairs opens when you know 10 species".
      *Question: does the tease make you want to get there?*

## 2. The first floor down (around 10 species)

- [ ] At 5 species the **Kelp Stair** scene plays full-screen, over its own
      painted plate once that render is harvested, and the room's art until then.
- [ ] At 10 species nothing plays yet. The toast says Charlotte has the key behind
      the counter, and the Market shows **"The key to Below Stairs · 150"**.
      *Question: does buying a key feel like an event, or like a toll?*
- [ ] Buying the key plays **Below Stairs**:
  - the pantry is the backdrop;
  - Charlotte hands over the key;
  - Wilbur takes the lamp;
  - the two characters swap lit and dimmed as they talk.
- [ ] One beat offers **three reply cards** (Lead the way / Less like fish how?
      / Is it safe down there?). Each card shows who answers. Picking one plays the
      answer, and then the scene carries on. *Question: do the replies feel like
      yours?*
- [ ] Charlotte's last beat offers the Sunken Pantry with **Hang it**.
- [ ] The Market now stocks floor II creatures, and the silhouettes are floor III.
- [ ] Buying your **first floor II creature** plays "Up from Below Stairs".
- [ ] **Skip scene / Esc** works on every cinematic scene.

## 3. Weirder further down

- [ ] Look at the creatures you can buy at floor I versus floor II and, if you
      push on, floor III (25). *Question: does each floor read as stranger
      than the last?* This is the core promise. Note any species that feels
      misplaced.
- [ ] Prices: some floor II creatures cost far more than others (formerly
      evolution-only species cost their rarity's default). *Question: does the
      price spread feel like a choice or like noise?*

## 4. Your own tank (veteran path)

- [ ] Your floors are already open. No fish you own or have owned is locked.
- [ ] **World & story → Memory Book** lists every scene you've seen as an
      illustrated card. Tapping one replays it as "A memory", and Esc closes it.
- [ ] If you ever widened the tank, the **Hatchery** background is now yours,
      with a short Wilbur and Charlotte scene.
- [ ] If you bought The Last Aquarium, "After the Last Aquarium" plays once.
      *Question: does it land as a soft ending without breaking the mystery?*
- [ ] The Memory Book's **Endings** shelf shows three cards. Each is dark until
      earned: Every One (book complete), After the Last Aquarium, and The Bottom of
      Things (the floor V key).

## 5. Pacing (decided 2026-11-21: canon rarity + slower floors)

Each fish is now priced and paid by its canon rarity, and each floor needs a
one-time key: 150, 3,000, 30,000 and 200,000 coins (the last halved on 2026-11-21). Tanks that reached a floor
before the change keep it. The simulator (`ECONOMY-SUITE.md`) puts a casual
player (two 10-minute visits a day) at:

| Floor | Day |
|---|---|
| II | 1 |
| III | 2 |
| IV | 5 |
| V | about 10 |
| Book complete | about 12.5 |

- [ ] *Question: does the save for the floor V key still drag?* Once floors I–IV
      are bought out, a casual player now waits about 4 days with no new species
      (it was about 8.5 before the price was halved), with decor, sets and upgrades
      to buy meanwhile.

## 6. Art to judge

- [ ] Wilbur's per-floor portraits: all 20 are delivered (canon #86, #89). He should
      look a little worse on each floor, and nobody should mention it.
      *Question: does he stay sympathetic, never the joke?*
- [ ] The floor scene plates, the post-finale plate and the Bottom of Things ending
      plate.
- [ ] The five plates first rendered 2026-11-02 were never lost. They were private
      images the delivery fetch couldn't read without authentication. That's fixed,
      and they are delivered.

## 7. Sound and music (t-110)

Sound starts off. Turn it on with the speaker in the HUD; Settings has Volume and a
Music toggle. Everything is synthesized in the browser, no audio files.

- [ ] The theme starts a moment after sound is switched on: a slow music box over a
      soft pad. *Question: does it suit the shop, and does it get tiresome?*
- [ ] Buy a key and go down: each floor's variation is lower, slower and darker (the
      Bottom of Things is a drone with a few far bells). It changes at the end of a
      phrase, not mid-note.
- [ ] Every action has a small sound: buying, the reveal, releasing, cleaning,
      upgrades, the floor key (keys, then a lock far below), decor, sets, new
      scenery, milestones, the book complete, the finale, scene page-turns and
      replies, tab changes, and a soft knock when something can't be bought yet.
      *Question: is anything too loud, too frequent or missing?*
- [ ] Music off in Settings stops the theme and keeps the effects.
