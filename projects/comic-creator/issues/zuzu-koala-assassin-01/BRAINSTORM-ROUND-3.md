status: brainstorm, nothing set in stone (the series bible is NOT being solidified yet)
date: 2026-10-03
art: [ART-ROUND-3.yaml](./ART-ROUND-3.yaml), ArtJobs 33315 to 33362 (12 subjects x 4 non-Krea lanes)

## Decisions from Silas

- **The siblings are fennec foxes.** *"I loove the fennec foxes, I think that's it for the siblings."*
  An older sister of about ten and a toddler brother who only babbles.
- **They need more emotion.** *"The siblings are poor, starving, beaten, they are the sole survivors of
  a massacre. They are scared and have no one."*
- **Zuzu is always serious.** *"More Clint Eastwood than unflappable sensei (or unflappable koala)."*
- **Nothing generated for Kind Robots carries over.** *"None of the world generated elements that we did
  for the kind robots characters, scenario, etc will make it into the comic. Those were created by llm,
  and the story WILL evolve."* Out: Gallowsun Junction, the Stationmaster, Tomoe of the Long Road, Old
  Shelba, the Smiling Magpie, Marrow, "Zuzu's Last Name", and the Kind Robots voice for Zuzu (contract
  language, "has he eaten"). Zuzu himself, the koala ronin, stays.
- **How the story is told.** *"This is going to be an action heavy, moody story, so we don't rely on a
  lot of exposition, backstory will come out of movement and action, and drip feeds of conversation."*
- **Old Komodo.** A shock appearance who turns out not to be a villain, *"but that doesn't mean he won't
  try to eat the siblings before Zuzu intercedes."* Key image: he erupts from the sand, mouth open, the
  two children slipping into his jaw, Zuzu springing overhead and attacking with a staff.

## World

- A wasteland that used to be prosperous; everyone is fighting to survive.
- Reality is thin. *"Conan is a great example, he fought dinosaurs at some point."* (In Howard's "Red
  Nails", Conan and Valeria are treed by a "dragon" drawn like a prehistoric reptile.) Monsters can step
  through from elsewhere without anyone explaining why.
- Zuzu is a fish out of water: a samurai in a western, even a weird western where the fabric of reality
  is nebulous.

## Factions

- **Storm Crows.**
- **Hyena tribe.** Slavers, at least in the first plot seed below.
- **The otter house.** *"An otter bordello/geisha house?"* and, in the plot seed, *"a convent of
  otters"* run by "sisters". Possibly one institution with a respectable face. Drawn as a convent and tea
  house; nothing in the art puts the children near the bordello reading.

## Plot seeds

- Zuzu drops the children at the otter convent. Something nefarious is going on: the sisters plan to
  sell them to the hyenas. Zuzu has to go back for them.
- The massacre at Hollow Bell (round 1) and Zuzu finding the children still stand as the opening beats.

## Buried twist (SECRET until a chosen issue ending)

There used to be humans. Reveal it Planet of the Apes style at the end of an issue, never earlier:
- a fallen statue plus a mostly buried pharmacy sign in English letters, or
- the Great Wall of China rising out of the dunes.

Both options are in round 3 (`r3-twist-statue`, `r3-twist-wall`). Keep every other page and note free
of human hints until the reveal issue is picked; the studio notes carry a "secret until" marker for this.

## Conflicts with the v0 bible and script (resolve later, not now)

- The v0 script's interior-monologue captions and the struck-through contract device use the Kind
  Robots voice that is now out. An Eastwood Zuzu says almost nothing and has almost no captions.
- The v0 bible's world (Gallowsun Junction, Tomoe, Old Shelba) is out.
- Hollow Bell, the massacre, the water, the camp, the apples and the stinger tracks are untouched.
