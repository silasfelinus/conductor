# ZUZU: KOALA ASSASSIN #1 — "THE LETTER OF THE CONTRACT"

status: draft script for Silas
length: 20 story pages + cover (a standard indie first issue; every page below is one printed page)
rating: teen and up: aftermath-only violence, implied, no on-panel killing
read first: [SERIES-BIBLE.md](./SERIES-BIBLE.md)

Format key: **PANEL** = art description; *CAPTION* = narration box (Zuzu's interior, in parchment boxes);
"DIALOGUE" = speech balloon; SFX = sound effect lettering. `[beat]` = deliberate silence.
**Panel counts** are suggestions; pages marked SPLASH or WIDE carry the weight and should not be crowded.

---

## COVER

Zuzu from behind, small in the centre of a wide dusty street, katana across his back, the lizard at his
shoulder. The town ahead is a horizon of leaning false fronts and a bell tower, every window dark.
Long gold shadows. One red paper lantern is lit in the far distance. At his feet, small and easy to
miss, a pair of tiny paw prints beside his, with a larger set behind them: someone is already
following. Title treatment: **ZUZU** in tall brushed letters, *KOALA ASSASSIN* beneath in small plain type.

## PAGE 1 (SPLASH)

**PANEL 1 (full page):** Dawn on the road into
Hollow Bell. A wide-shot of the town arch, half torii gate, half frontier sign: **HOLLOW BELL — POP. 212**.
The number has been repainted so many times that the earlier numbers show through as ghosts. Beneath the
arch, in the dust: *tiny* paw prints and the three-toed tracks of a big lizard, walking in step. In the distance the bell tower stands above the roofline.
No people. No sound. A thread of dust on the wind.

*No caption. Let the page breathe.*

SFX (tiny, in the bell tower, the only lettering on the page): *dong.*

## PAGE 2

**PANEL 1 (wide strip):** Under the arch, Zuzu at last: seen from behind, cloak, hat on back, katana across
his back high above his head because the sword is nearly his height. He leads the lizard by a simple lead
rope. He is *tiny* against the gate.
**PANEL 2 (insert):** Close on Zuzu's face in profile. Calm. Heavy nose, old scar on the ear. His
nostrils flare once, reading the air.
**PANEL 3 (insert):** The lizard, flicking its tongue, uneasy. It stops. Zuzu rests a paw on its neck.
**PANEL 4 (wide strip):** Zuzu, ahead of the lizard, takes his hat off his back and holds it
over his chest. (He does this for the dead.) He walks on bare-eared.

*Cluster of three captions, Zuzu's voice, small and flat:*
*There is a smell a town gets when no one in it is cooking.*

## PAGE 3

**PANEL 1 (wide):** Main street. Long gold morning shadows. A false-front saloon, a paper-screened tea
house, a barber's pole, a bank with a katana rack by the door. Every door is open. Nothing moves except a
shutter and flies.
**PANEL 2 (small):** A fallen chair in the middle of the street. A bowl beside it, spilled, still holding
the shape of what was in it.
**PANEL 3 (small):** A pair of small boots, standing upright and neatly together by a doorstep. No one in them.
**PANEL 4 (wide):** The dark pooled stains on the boardwalk, dried to maroon-black. A line of them
leading in through the saloon doors. A long silence: the lizard's footfalls, Zuzu's, then nothing.

*No captions. Silence.*

## PAGE 4

**PANEL 1:** Zuzu stops in front of the saloon. He looks through the swinging doors without touching them.
**PANEL 2 (wide, the interior):** A saloon after. Tables overturned, a piano with its lid shut, a lantern
swinging a little. Sheets and blankets laid over shapes in neat rows: someone took the time to cover them.
The sheets are not neat enough to have been done by the killers. (Planted: somebody came *after*, or the
survivors did it.)
**PANEL 3 (insert):** Zuzu's paw lifts the edge of a sheet. We don't see what's under it; we see his face.
**PANEL 4 (tight):** His eyes. Nothing changes in them. A long beat. He lowers the sheet.

*Captions, in the lower left:*
*Eleven tables. Eleven chairs overturned.*
*They had no time to stand.*

---

## PAGE 5

**PANEL 1:** Zuzu on one knee beside a body under a sheet. Only his paws are shown, examining a cut in
the cloth, then a cut in the plank floor behind it.
**PANEL 2 (insert, extreme close-up):** A single clean diagonal slash through a wooden support post, as
if through water. The edges are smooth. No splinter.
**PANEL 3 (insert):** Zuzu's thumb on the cut. He draws it along the groove.
**PANEL 4 (wide):** Zuzu sits back on his heels. He looks up at the ceiling beams. Three more
cuts, all in the same line, all at the same height. Someone swung once, once, once, with exactly the same arm.

*Captions:*
*Clean work.*
*A swordsman's hand. Better than mine, and I am not often wrong about that.*

---

## PAGE 6 (THE LIST)

**PANEL 1:** Outside, on the boardwalk. Zuzu takes a thin oilcloth wallet from inside his cloak.
**PANEL 2 (insert):** The wallet unfolds. A sheet of heavy paper: a **contract**, the only bright
colour on the page, a red wax seal and a column of names in neat black brush-script. We do not read them
all, only the line at the top: **HOLLOW BELL.** Beneath it a name: *(the name is covered by Zuzu's thumb)*.
**PANEL 3 (insert, the turn):** He moves his thumb. The name is already struck through, one long
red line, fresh enough to be tacky, not Zuzu's ink: the stroke is too confident and the red too bright.
Below the struck name, small, in someone else's neat hand: *paid.*
**PANEL 4 (tight):** Zuzu, holding the paper, absolutely still.

*Captions:*
*The letter of the contract says a name. One name. Paid on delivery.*
*The letter does not say what to do when the delivery has already been made.*
*It does not say who did it, or why they left the town with it.*

## PAGE 7

**PANEL 1 (wide):** Zuzu folds the contract slowly. Puts it away. Puts his hat back on. Takes the lizard's
lead rope.
**PANEL 2 (small):** The lizard looks back at him. A slow blink, plain accusation: *are we leaving?*
**PANEL 3 (insert):** Zuzu's ears turn. Both, together, toward the left side of the street.
**PANEL 4 (wide):** Far down the street, in the shade of the Hollow Bell Tea House, a small sound.
Faint. Wet. A kind of breath that is almost a hiccup.

SFX (small, delicate): *hh— hh—*

*No captions.*

## PAGE 8

**PANEL 1:** Zuzu stands at the tea-house door. Paper screens, one torn in a long diagonal cut.
**PANEL 2 (insert):** His paw on the sliding door. He does not push it yet.
**PANEL 3 (wide, from inside, the reveal):** The back room of the tea house. A long tatami room, low
tables, cushions. It was a gathering of the town, perhaps: a wake, a wedding, a meeting. Now rows
of sheeted shapes and dark stains on the straw mats, the light striping through torn shoji screens.
Tilted in the far corner, with her back to a wall, a **young panda girl**, soot on her fur, in a torn
dress, holding a **baby panda** in a sling made from a tablecloth. She is sobbing without sound.
The baby hiccups and whimpers, hungry and hot.
**PANEL 4 (tight):** The girl's face, wet eyes, looking up at the open door. At the small silhouette.
She sees a koala with a sword. Her sobbing stops. She doesn't breathe.

*Caption (Zuzu), one line only:*
*I came for one name.*

## PAGE 9

**PANEL 1 (wide, low angle):** Zuzu in the doorway, small. The girl at the far end of the room, big in
frame (she is half again his size, sitting). Neither moves.
**PANEL 2 (insert):** The girl's arms tighten around the baby. She's trying to cover him with her whole body.
**PANEL 3 (insert):** Zuzu's paw, resting on the hilt of the katana. (We read the paw as a threat; we will
later read it as habit.) He takes it away. He puts both paws open at his sides.
**PANEL 4 (wide):** He looks at her. A long beat. Then he turns around.

*No dialogue. No captions.*

## PAGE 10

**PANEL 1 (wide):** Zuzu walks back up the empty street, back to the lizard. The tea house is a dark
rectangle behind him.
**PANEL 2 (insert):** He takes the lizard's lead rope. Checks the cinch. Steps into the stirrup (a small
set of steps hanging from the saddle for a rider his size).
**PANEL 3 (insert):** Behind him, from far down the street, the baby begins to cry. A real cry, high and
thin.
**PANEL 4 (tight):** Zuzu in the saddle. He does not turn around. The cry goes on.

*Captions:*
*Nothing in the contract covers children.*
*I have read it three times.*

## PAGE 11

**PANEL 1 (wide):** The lizard walks at a slow pace down the main street, away from the tea house and

**PANEL 2 (insert):** The lizard's foot. A step. Another.
**PANEL 3 (wide):** The arch ahead. The open road. Heat shimmer.
**PANEL 4 (insert, tight on Zuzu's face):** His jaw moves. He is chewing a leaf of eucalyptus, slowly.
His eyes are on the road.
**PANEL 5 (insert):** Up in the bell tower the bell, with no wind, sways once.

SFX: *dong.*

*Caption, small, at the bottom of the page:*
*I have not eaten today either.*

## PAGE 12 (THE TURN)

**PANEL 1 (wide):** Zuzu stops the lizard under the arch. Both of them stand there. The wind rises and drops.
**PANEL 2 (insert):** The gourd canteen at his hip. His paw closes on it. He shakes it. It sloshes, nearly full.
**PANEL 3 (tight):** His face. Something in the grave stillness gives a very small amount. A
breath out through the nose.
**PANEL 4 (wide):** Zuzu turns the lizard around. The camera is behind him now, looking down the street
toward the tea house, small in the distance. He rides back.

*Caption (the only caption on the page, the book's thesis):*
*The letter of the contract is clear.*
*The spirit of it is another matter.*

## PAGE 13

**PANEL 1 (wide):** Zuzu steps down in front of the tea house, takes off his hat again, and leans his
sword against the doorframe. (He disarms himself, and she should see it.)
**PANEL 2 (insert):** Zuzu's empty paws, open. He steps through the door.
**PANEL 3 (wide, interior):** The girl hasn't moved from the corner. She sees him come in, unarmed. The
baby is quieter now, only whimpering. Zuzu walks slowly across the tatami, between the sheeted shapes,
looking at none of them.
**PANEL 4 (tight):** Zuzu stops two arm-lengths from her. He lowers himself to sit cross-legged on the floor, so
that his eyes are now lower than hers.

*No captions.*

## PAGE 14 (THE WATER)

**PANEL 1 (insert):** Zuzu unhooks the gourd from his belt. Uncorks it.
**PANEL 2 (insert):** He sets it on the floor and slides it across the mat, slowly, until it stops against
her knee.
**PANEL 3 (wide):** The girl looks at the gourd. At him. At the gourd. She is very thirsty. She does not
want to be.
**PANEL 4 (insert):** She takes it, drinks one swallow, then pours a few drops into her paw and lets the
baby lick them.
**PANEL 5 (tight):** The baby drinks, wide eyes. The girl, for the first time, cries out loud: a single
breath of sound.

**ZUZU** *(the first thing he says in the book; quiet)*: "Slowly."

## PAGE 15

**PANEL 1 (wide):** Zuzu watches them drink, hat in his lap.
**PANEL 2 (insert):** The girl, finishing, holds the gourd out to him with both paws. Her paws are shaking.
**PANEL 3 (insert):** Zuzu pushes it back toward her, gently, with two fingers. *Keep it.*
**PANEL 4 (wide):** He stands. Takes his hat. Gives the room a last look, and bows once, very low, to the
sheeted shapes. The girl watches him do this.

**LING** *(hoarse; the first thing she says)*: "Are you... the one they were afraid of?"

**PANEL 5 (tight):** Zuzu in profile at the door.

**ZUZU**: "No. Not this time."

*(Reads as a half-truth; he is the one the town would have been afraid of, and will be again.)*

## PAGE 17 (THE CAMP)

**PANEL 1 (wide):** Dusk. A dry creek bed becomes a thin bright stream under a stand of cottonwoods.
Zuzu has found water. The lizard drinks, tail flicking.
**PANEL 2 (insert):** Zuzu kneels at the stream and fills his hat with water, pours it over his own head,

**PANEL 3 (insert):** A small fire. A tin pot. Eucalyptus leaves, steeped. He sits cross-legged with the

**PANEL 4 (wide):** The valley darkening, one star out, the fire a small orange point. Wide, small, alone.

*Caption:*
*The only honest ledger is the one you keep alone, at night.*
*Tonight it does not balance.*

## PAGE 18 (THE WATCHER)

**PANEL 1 (wide, moon-blue):** Midnight. The fire is low. The lizard sleeps, a dark hill. Zuzu lies on
his side under the cloak, back to the fire, the sword within reach of his paw.
**PANEL 2 (insert):** His eyes open. He has not moved. Something has woken him, not a sound but the
absence of a sound.
**PANEL 3 (insert):** The edge of the firelight. Two small round ears at the very edge of the frame.
**PANEL 4 (wide, the reveal):** Across the dying fire, sitting very still on a log, **Ling**, with the
baby asleep in the sling on her chest. She is **watching him**. She has been watching him for some time.
She does not look afraid any more. She looks like someone who is making up her mind.
**PANEL 5 (tight):** Zuzu looks back at her, his paw still near the sword. A long beat.

**ZUZU** *(very quietly)*: "Sleep."

**PANEL 6 (tight):** Ling doesn't move. She just keeps looking. He closes his eyes.

## PAGE 19 (THE ROAD)

**PANEL 1 (wide):** Dawn. Zuzu loads the lizard. The camp is neat, the fire buried. He does not look at the log.
**PANEL 2 (insert):** On the log, a small cloth bundle. A school satchel, buckled. Beside it the empty

**PANEL 3 (wide):** Zuzu sees the gourd. He takes it. He hangs it on his belt. He says nothing at all.
**PANEL 4 (wide, widescreen strip, the road):** Zuzu leading the lizard across red mesa country. Far

**PANEL 5 (insert):** Their shadows on the red dirt: one large, one small, one very small. The large
and the small stay the same distance apart.
**PANEL 6 (insert):** Zuzu's ears, turned back toward them.

*Captions, one per panel, spaced down the page:*
*Noon.*
*She does not call out.*
*Evening.*
*She does not ask for anything.*

## PAGE 20 (THE APPLES)

**PANEL 1 (wide):** Late afternoon. A lone **apple tree**, absurdly alive, in the middle of a dead plain,
heavy with fruit, shadows long, leaning toward the road like it has been waiting.
**PANEL 2 (insert):** Zuzu stops under it. Looks up. Behind him, forty paces back, Ling and Bao stop
too. The baby is reaching for the fruit. Ling's paw is over his hand. She's pulling it down.
**PANEL 3 (insert, tight):** Zuzu's paw on the hilt. His thumb at the mouth of the black scabbard,
worn pale there.
**PANEL 4 (BIG, the one blade stroke in the issue; full-width widescreen strip):** The draw, almost not
there at all: a single curved line of silver and the *kind of stillness that comes just before the
cut*. The katana is already back in the scabbard. Above him, the stems of apples, all parted at once, are
falling around his small figure like slow rain.

SFX: *shk.*

**PANEL 5 (wide):** Apples bouncing in the grass around his boots. He picks up three and puts them in his
cloak. He picks up a fourth and looks at it, and then at the plain, and sets it back.
**PANEL 6 (insert):** He leaves **six apples** in a neat ring in the grass at the base of the trunk, within
reach of a small girl. He steps back out of the way, and walks on without looking back.
**PANEL 7 (final wide):** Ling arrives at the tree, slowly. She looks at the apples. She looks up the
road, where the koala is a small dark figure with a long thin line across his back, shrinking into the
gold light. Bao reaches. She hands him one. He bites it with a smile of pure happiness.
She takes one for herself. She does not eat it yet. She is looking at the road.

*Caption (Zuzu, small, from the distance; the last words of the issue):*
*It is not in the contract.*

## OPTIONAL PAGE 21 STINGER (decide with Silas)

A single wide panel, *no text*: the **empty Hollow Bell arch** at the moment Zuzu left it for the
second time. In the dust beside the pale tracks of koala and lizard, a **third set of prints**
that were not there when he arrived: a long-strided runner in straw sandals, going the same way,
and, in the foreground, a **straight, clean line** drawn in the dust, the width of a sword's edge,
ending in the shadow under the arch. Sets up Issue 2 (the hand that struck the name; Tomoe's
road crossing his). If the book would rather end on the apples, drop it. The apples are the better ending.

## NOTES FOR LETTERERS AND ARTISTS

- **Word count** on the whole issue is deliberately tiny (under ~350 words of narration and ~60 of
  dialogue). If a page feels like it needs more words, it needs a better picture.
- **Eye-line rule:** whenever Zuzu and Ling are in the same frame, Ling is *taller*. Keep that until the
  series gives a reason to break it.
- **The bell rings three times, always once, never with wind:** page 1 (arrival), page 11 (he starts to
  leave), page 16 (he leaves for good). Do not let it ring a fourth time.
- **The sword is only drawn once** (page 20) and for apples. Keep every other appearance sheathed,
  leaning, laid across knees, or being cleaned.
- **Red:** the only fully saturated red in the issue is the contract seal and the strike-through on
  page 6 (plus, optionally, one lantern on the cover). Blood is maroon-black and dry.
- **Names** *Ling* and *Bao* are placeholders (see the Series Bible).
