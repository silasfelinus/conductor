# Brainstorm Round 2: the kids, Zuzu, covers, plots

status: brainstorm, nothing set in stone
date: 2026-10-03

## Decisions from Silas (2026-10-03)

- **No LoRA for now.** Character reference images first; a LoRA maybe later. The Zuzu turnaround and
  the kids' sheet are therefore the first renders to make.
- **The baby is a toddler.** Old enough to babble, walk, drink from a cup. No milk, no carrying
  an infant. Script updated to match.
- **Hollow Bell stays.** The pandas go; open to ideas. Twenty species below, to choose from.
- **Twenty pages plus the stinger, plus covers.** A cover of Zuzu fighting a giant sand monster
  (perhaps Old Komodo) is a must.

## My picks, in order

1. **Tanuki.** Samurai-era folklore fit, shapeshifting is a secret that explains how two children survived,
   and a toddler's babble that makes furniture sprout ears is a running gag that can also get them killed.
2. **Raccoon.** The "hid inside the bell" survival story is the best single reveal in the set.
3. **Red fox.** The most obvious visual rhyme with kitsune and with a western; readable at small size.

Note: a **desert tortoise** pair would be kin to Old Shelba (canon: the tortoise contract broker).
A **meerkat** pair gives the series a built-in lookout gag. A **field mouse** pair makes Zuzu's size joke bigger.

Whichever species wins, the rules hold: the girl is taller than Zuzu, the toddler is a small, round,
babbling child, both wear the same torn pale dress and checked cloth tunic so they read as a pair.

## A. Twenty pitches for the kids (same species within each pair)

Each pitch is a species plus one scene, so it doubles as a plot beat for the book.

- **Tanuki: Swinging Doors** (`kid-01-tanuki`, 16:9): They push into a seedy saloon looking for the koala. Tanuki lore: the toddler's babble makes a barrel wobble and a coat stand grow ears. Shapeshifting kids = a secret the world will want.
- **Red Fox: The Ring of Apples** (`kid-02-red-fox`, 16:9): The discovery beat. Fox girl reads the ring Zuzu left as a message: someone counted them out for two. First time she smiles.
- **River Otter: Creek Day** (`kid-03-river-otter`, 16:9): The joy page. Otters play anywhere there is water, and the reader exhales. Zuzu watches from the camp and, for one panel, forgets his list.
- **Raccoon: Inside the Bell** (`kid-04-raccoon`, 16:9): They survived by hiding inside the town bell. The bell that rang once by itself on page 1 was the girl trying not to be found. Reframes the whole book.
- **Hedgehog: The Long Walk** (`kid-05-hedgehog`, 16:9): The following beat. A hedgehog toddler cannot be carried without a price; the girl pays it in scratches. Show what following costs.
- **Jackrabbit: Ears Up** (`kid-06-jackrabbit`, 16:9): Night watch. The girl's ears hear what Zuzu's do. Rabbit hearing means she knows he is awake long before he admits it.
- **Badger: The Stick Sword** (`kid-07-badger`, 16:9): Girl wants to learn the sword; Zuzu wants her to put the stick down. Seeds issue three: the cub who wants vengeance.
- **Fennec Fox: Saddle** (`kid-08-fennec`, 16:9): Zuzu lifts the toddler onto the lizard. It is the first time Zuzu touches either of them and he does it like handling a sword.
- **Meerkat: Dust on the Horizon** (`kid-09-meerkat`, 16:9): Sentries. They spot a dust cloud first. It is a rider, and the rider is not Zuzu. Instant sequel hook.
- **Armadillo: Under the Table** (`kid-10-armadillo`, 16:9): The massacre as the kids lived it, told in flashback: armour plates tucked tight beneath a low tea table, only eyes visible between the cloth and the floor.
- **Pangolin: Slowly** (`kid-11-pangolin`, 16:9): The water scene. Pangolin scales close at fear; the toddler uncurls only for the gourd. Zuzu notices the exact moment.
- **Capybara: The 4:15** (`kid-12-capybara`, 16:9): Gallowsun Junction crossover. They wait at the station for a train that never stops twice. A calm species makes the dread worse. The Stationmaster tips his cap.
- **Lynx: Branch Watch** (`kid-13-lynx`, 16:9): Climbs a cottonwood to watch the camp. Cat eyes glow in the dark. Zuzu wakes and sees two small lights in the leaves and, being Zuzu, says nothing.
- **Field Mouse: Inside the Hat** (`kid-14-field-mouse`, 16:9): Scale gag with a heart: the toddler sleeps inside Zuzu's upturned straw hat by the fire. Zuzu moves his own bedroll rather than wake him.
- **Mountain Goat: Rope Bridge** (`kid-15-mountain-goat`, 16:9): A goat kid is the one who crosses a canyon rope bridge first. Action beat for a quiet issue: a bridge post cut clean, and the koala who cut it is on the other side.
- **Barn Owl: Cloak** (`kid-16-barn-owl`, 16:9): Storm. Zuzu is smaller than the kids, so the cloak barely covers any of them. The least heroic, most tender page in the book.
- **White-Tailed Deer: The Name** (`kid-17-white-tailed-deer`, 16:9): Girl finds the contract Zuzu dropped and reads it by firelight. She covers one name with her palm. Which name? Plot engine for issue two.
- **Ferret: Hungry** (`kid-18-ferret`, 16:9): The kids steal from a ruined mercantile. Zuzu catches them and puts his own jerky down. Comedy, then a gut-punch.
- **Prairie Dog: The Foot** (`kid-19-prairie-dog`, 16:9): Slapstick: the toddler pops out of a burrow in front of the lizard's enormous foot. The lizard steps over him. Zuzu does not break stride. A joke, and also a promise.
- **Desert Tortoise: Slow Road** (`kid-20-desert-tortoise`, 16:9): Tortoises cannot keep up, which is the whole joke and the whole problem. Old Shelba the broker is a tortoise too: are these kids her kin? Zuzu keeps stopping to wait and pretends to be looking at the scenery.

## B. Ten pitches for Zuzu

Designs and poses. Start with the turnaround.

- **Reference turnaround** (`zuzu-01-turnaround`, 3:2): The one image everything depends on: front, three-quarter and back views for consistency. Generate first; every later Zuzu prompt should be tested against it.
- **Expression sheet** (`zuzu-02-expressions`, 3:2): Six head studies: placid, wary, amused (rare), weary, hungry, resolved. His face barely moves, so the sheet proves how little it takes.
- **Classic ronin** (`zuzu-03-classic-ronin`, 2:3): Default look: dust cloak, straw hat, long katana worn high. The baseline all others riff on.
- **Duster and hakama** (`zuzu-04-duster`, 2:3): Weird west cut: long leather duster over pleated hakama trousers, a bandolier of tea tins, a red scarf. Merges the genres in clothing.
- **Scavenged armour** (`zuzu-05-armour`, 2:3): Mended lamellar armour over a poncho, tin cup on the belt. Looks like someone who has been in the war and stayed for the aftermath.
- **Gambler's coat** (`zuzu-06-gambler`, 2:3): A black frock coat, silk waistcoat, a playing card tucked in the hatband. Zuzu in town clothes, how he looks when he is working the room.
- **The tea cart** (`zuzu-07-tea-cart`, 3:2): Lone Wolf and Cub nod: Zuzu pulling a small two-wheeled cart with a kettle, eucalyptus tins and a cloth awning. Room for the children later.
- **Fireside** (`zuzu-08-fireside`, 3:2): Contemplative hero shot: eucalyptus tea, the sword across his knees, the contract unfolded on a rock. How he thinks.
- **The draw** (`zuzu-09-the-draw`, 16:9): Action key image. The single stroke, the cloak lifted, the blade already returning to the scabbard. Also the apple-tree page.
- **Old Zuzu** (`zuzu-10-old-zuzu`, 2:3): Flash-forward: a white muzzle, a missing ear tip, a cane that is also a sheathed blade. Where this goes if he does not choose the mercy.

## C. Ten covers

Cover one is the sand monster you asked for.

- **Zuzu vs the Sand Monster** (`cover-01-sand-monster`, 2:3): Series-defining cover. A dune that is a spine; Old Komodo is far older than the town and not hostile so much as hungry. Zuzu is the size of one of its teeth.
- **Walking into Hollow Bell** (`cover-02-walk-in`, 2:3): Issue-one standard cover: small figure, huge sky, a lit red lantern far away. The Leone cover.
- **The three of them** (`cover-03-three-walking`, 2:3): A series cover for later: Zuzu in front, a girl and a toddler following twenty paces behind, tiny, on a road with no end.
- **Falling apples** (`cover-04-falling-apples`, 2:3): Quiet cover. Apples dropping around him in slow arcs with the stems cleanly parted. Reads as beautiful until you notice the blade.
- **The eyes** (`cover-05-eyes`, 2:3): Extreme close-up: his eyes and the burning town reflected in them. Tight, graphic, a cover people pick up.
- **Saloon doorway** (`cover-06-saloon-door`, 2:3): The Yojimbo cover: he arrives, hand near the hilt, every head in the room turned.
- **Duel at sunset** (`cover-07-sunset-duel`, 2:3): Zuzu and a tall young samurai (Tomoe) in a town square, blades unsheathed, the whole town watching from windows. A promise for issue four.
- **The bell tower** (`cover-08-bell-tower`, 2:3): Pure silence cover: just the bell tower under a blood-orange sky with a tiny figure at its base. A series-logo cover.
- **The long road** (`cover-09-tomoe-road`, 2:3): A tall young samurai on a thousand-mile road, tattered, a single sandal worn through. The hunter's cover. Mirrors Zuzu's.
- **Tea time at the end of the world** (`cover-10-tea-time`, 2:3): The oddest cover in the set: Zuzu pours a second cup for an unseen guest across a rock, the whole desert behind him on fire. Tone: Leone does a tea ceremony.

## D. Ten fresh plot ideas, aimed at covers

One line each. Each also has an art prompt.

- **The bell rings thirteen** (`plot-01-thirteen`, 2:3): A bell that rings with no wind is one thing. A bell that tolls once more for every name on Zuzu's list is a cover.
- **A child's name on the list** (`plot-02-her-name`, 2:3): On the contract, the last line is a name he cannot read aloud. It is the girl's.
- **First word** (`plot-03-first-word`, 2:3): The toddler's first word is Zuzu. Said wrong. Zuzu's face does the thing it never does.
- **The town sits on his back** (`plot-04-komodo-sleeps`, 2:3): Hollow Bell was built on a sleeping giant. The bell is his heartbeat. A cover for the Old Komodo arc.
- **The train stops** (`plot-05-train-stops`, 2:3): The 4:15 stops for once, a first. Someone gets off who is not on any list. The Stationmaster is not smiling.
- **The orchard shouldn't be alive** (`plot-06-grave-orchard`, 2:3): Rows of fruiting apple trees on a dead plain, each one growing from a grave. The apples were never free.
- **She reads the contract** (`plot-07-contract-theft`, 2:3): The girl has learned to read. She has read it three times. She has questions.
- **Old Shelba's ledger** (`plot-08-ledger`, 2:3): The kids' parents appear in the ledger as a debt, due. Someone paid, in cash, in blood. Introduce Shelba with the scales of a banker.
- **Tomoe finds them** (`plot-09-tomoe-arrives`, 2:3): A tall young samurai stands in the doorway of the tea house where the kids hid. She does not draw her sword. She kneels.
- **Two cups** (`plot-10-two-cups`, 2:3): He pours two cups. Only one is touched. The other is for whoever struck the name. Cover of Issue 2.

## Old Komodo note

In Kind Robots, *The Old Komodo* (bot 427) is a Gallowsun Junction narrator who reads the lizard
mount as "the interesting part." Making him the giant sand beast turns the narrator into the monster,
and gives Zuzu's lizard a possible kinship plot. Worth deciding before the cover is final.
