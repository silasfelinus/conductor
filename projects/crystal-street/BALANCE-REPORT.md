# Terrors on Crystal Street: balance report

Conductor crystal-street t-006 and t-008, 2026-10-25 PT. Regenerate the numbers with
`npx tsx utils/scripts/crystalStreetBalanceReport.ts` in kind_robots (about 20 seconds); the
sections below the line are that script's output at 96 weeks per nightmare per policy.

## What the tuning pass changed (kind_robots #4154)

- **Typecast** now caps the shared style at +2. A camp, sneaky or reckless look in its home clique
  had +3, and Typecast characters reached Best 9 points more often than Against type ones. The gap
  is now about 2 points.
- **Villain cards:** 88 single-target edits across 12 nightmares, chosen by a measuring loop
  (96 to 192 weeks per round). The nightmares careful play rarely beat had choices eased from 9 to 7 (or 11 to 9);
  Screwhead, Roz, the Mothman and Goody Hale got slightly stiffer. No card is left with only easy
  (7) or only hard (11) choices.
- **Night wins are the lever.** The Best ending needs the heart and 12 night wins. Careful play
  averaged 9.6 wins against the hardest nightmares and 12.4 against the easiest, and almost every
  run reaches the heart with all three leads, so easing villain cards moved the hard end directly.
- **Tried and dropped:** stiffening one choice on an easy nightmare's card did nothing (the
  autoplay picks another 9 on the same card); making one act-3 card all-hard did not move The
  Hungry House either.

## Companion arcs (kind_robots #4164, t-008)

- **Hangouts** (one a day, a three-step story per friend) and the **Loyal** finale bonus (+1 per
  Loyal friend, up to +2) lifted careful Best by about 2 points: 291 to 301 of the 460 verifier
  weeks. Most of it comes from the hangouts' small day rewards, not the Loyal bonus itself (capping
  it at +1 moved nothing), so the counterweight went on the villain decks.
- **Villain cards:** 20 choices go from 9 to 11 in Hungry House (7), Counselor Jess (3), Goody Hale (1),
  Screwhead (3), Mickey Moore (4), Mallrat Mothman (1) and Roz Vance (1). That also pulled the three
  known outliers inside the band.
- **Night wins stay at 12.** Raising them to 13 dropped careful Best from 65% to 36%: a cliff, not a dial.

## Known outliers

- **None outside 40–75%** on the report's seeds after #4164; The Hungry House and Roz Vance sit at
  the easy edge (about 74–75%). The noise at 96 weeks is still about ±5 points.
- **Final Girl** is rare for careful players (under 1%) and fires in about 6% of careless weeks
  (plus more weeks that enter the sequence and lose). That is the intended shape: lone wolves
  become Final Girls.
