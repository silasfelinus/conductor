# Rainbow Bullet Hell Space Princess

**Goal:** A lavish original C.A.T.S. space-opera shmup, not a survival-wave demo. Eight authored multi-phase main stages and final boss; branching detours, four distinct princess starfighters, local 1–4-player co-op, meaningful weapons/shields/supers, persistent medals, hangar and save/continue/leaderboard. Pastel hand-drawn oceanic future-tech aesthetic, bioecology enemies, Motherland/colonizer choice; no dog commanders or online co-op. No copied IP.
