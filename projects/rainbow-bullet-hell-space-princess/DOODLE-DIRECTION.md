## The look: a gifted kid's school-folder art

Everything looks drawn by one very talented kid in a 1980s school folder.

- **Palette:** saturated neon airbrush. Hot pink, electric purple, turquoise, lime, sunshine yellow and
  tangerine, blended in rainbow gradients across black or deep-violet starry space.
- **Motifs:** glitter sparkles, four-point twinkles, hearts, rainbows, stars, leopard and zebra spots on things
  that should not have them, bubbles, and doodled hearts in the margins.
- **Line:** clean, confident outlines with soft airbrushed shading. Big glossy eyes on animals and ships.
  Craft is real; it is a kid's subject matter and logic, not a kid's skill.
- **Joy over menace.** Threat reads as cartoon grumpiness: scowls, stomping and tiny storm clouds. Nothing is
  gory or scary.
- **Prompting rule:** never name the artist or brand in a generation prompt or in public copy. Describe the
  qualities instead: *"1980s neon rainbow airbrush school-folder sticker art, glossy, sparkles, hearts and
  stars"*. The game is inspired by an era, not a trademark.

## The kid's logic (the story layer)

- **C.A.T.S.** means *Cute And Tough Starfighters*, written on every ship in bubble letters with a heart over
  the I. Each princess has a cat co-pilot in her colour:
  - Maribel with **Biscuit**, a ginger tabby with one torn ear who also loves fighting;
  - Azure with **Comet**, a sleek blue-cream Siamese;
  - Solenne with **Butter**, a huge fluffy golden Maine Coon who head-butts shields;
  - Vivi with **Glitch**, a lilac sphynx in a tiny leather jacket.
- **The DOGS** don't stand for anything. "They're just dogs." They want the sea creatures to fetch for them
  forever, so they snapped glowing obedience collars on the whole fleet. That is the Prism Eclipse's mind
  control, and Act VIII's captive collars, re-explained. Freeing a creature means breaking its collar.
- **DOG commanders**, one per boss machine. They appear in cut-ins and comms, never as targets to hurt. A beaten
  commander is sent home grumpy: "Bad dog!", then a tiny rocket out of the frame.

  - C.A.T.S. fire hearts, stars and yarn sparks.
  - DOG bosses fire bones, tennis balls and grumpy little storm clouds.
  - The hypnotized creatures keep their t-032 sea looks, so the fight still reads by family.
