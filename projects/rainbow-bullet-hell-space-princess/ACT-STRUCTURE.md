# Act Structure, Seamless Transitions & Dialogue Timing
**Rainbow Bullet Hell Space Princess** · 2026-10-11 PT · Canonical production contract, companion to WORLD-BIBLE.md and COMMS-AND-ENDINGS-BIBLE.md.

## Core direction
Treat the campaign as **one continuous, escalating battle told in eight Acts and a final confrontation**, rather than a menu of eight disconnected levels. Each Act has its own dramatic question, visual region, authored encounters and boss. **An Act ends when its story scene resolves**. A boss defeat often provides that resolution, but a battle may flow immediately into the next Act. The game can show a cinematic act title, a route transition, and an optional checkpoint without resetting the ship or dropping into a stage-select menu.

Keep all eight fully authored stage regions and nine planned bosses from WORLD-BIBLE.md. Their `S01..S08` IDs remain stable for engineering and saves, while the player's presentation calls them Acts I–VIII. `S09` is the final confrontation / coda. This changes pacing, **not** the scope or count of promised content.

## Three required conversation scenes per Act
Every Act has **exactly three major story conversation slots** (additional optional one-line barks may occur, but must not replace these scenes). All slots support Lilt-7 and every active princess composition, including solo.

| Slot / ID | Timing | Narrative function | Combat and cinematic behavior |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Axx.OPEN** | As the new region is revealed, after the previous Act's resolution | New destination, personal banter, immediate stakes, establish one new question | Safe entry corridor / cinematic pan; controls remain responsive. On initial Act I launch, allow optional skippable introduction; subsequent openings can overlap with scenic transitions |
| **Axx.TURN** | Mid-Act during a deliberate low-threat pocket **or** immediately before the boss telegraph | Reveal, disagreement, choice or tactical surprise that changes how the boss is understood | Select midpoint vs pre-boss by authored Act metadata, not arbitrary timer. If mid-Act, use an authored calm corridor. If pre-boss, wait until the arena is clear and boss intro cue has space |
| **Axx.AFTER** | Immediately following boss resolution, after danger has ended | Emotional payoff, rescued-life reaction, consequences and bridge to next Act | Brief boss-free aftermath, rescued creature reveal, optional decision; may transition seamlessly into next Act's OPEN or pause for a stage-clear tableau if the story needs a breath |

**Timing principle:** Each conversation has a beginning, reaction, and emotional punctuation. The game must never stack the outgoing Act's AFTER and incoming Act's OPEN as two back-to-back monologues. Prefer a single linked transition sequence: aftermath -> environmental change -> new stakes. Shorter variants for brisk pacing must preserve each scene's narrative purpose.

**If boss-to-boss pressure is intentional:** Keep a short protected bridge with an AFTER payoff and next OPEN hook while combat moves through harmless debris or low-risk escort motion. Do not insert a conventional stage-clear reset. Major boss warnings interrupt only optional chatter; the three mandatory scenes wait for a safe authored window, then resume. Never require reading dialogue while dodging an untelegraphed lethal pattern.

## Scene transitions as authored gameplay
- **Persistent state across Acts:** Keep ship power levels, shields, score/combo policy, remaining lives, selected pilots, saved survivors, story flags, partyMask and route choices. Do **not** automatically refill everything, silently restore a dead pilot, or discard upgrades at a title card. Deliberate recovery checkpoints may offer a bounded repair/pickup and must communicate the rule.
- **Continuous scroll:** Blend four-layer parallax regions, lighting, enemy fleets and music stems. No black-screen loading pause as the normal path. Streaming assets may preload ahead; if not ready, hold the crew in a safe transit corridor without spawning untelegraphed enemies.
- **Two deliberately quiet intermissions:** After Acts III and V, use a short **optional** salvage/memorial tableau with ship repairs and narrative breathing room. The game can resume seamlessly after a player-confirmed continue; in co-op, use a synchronized continue/timeout and prevent one player from stranding the group. Intermissions are checkpoints, **not** separate stage-select screens.
- **Boss-to-Act bridges:** Defeated enemy weapons cease firing, remaining bullets safely clear, living pods escape, characters react, new environment scrolls into view, next threat telegraphs. Players remain in control of their craft during safe travel. No immediate boss-spawn on the exact frame the previous boss dies.
- **Death/retry/continue:** Restart at the most recent checkpoint with deterministic state; don't replay a long opening cutscene unless chosen. Mark major conversation events as heard only after they finish or are explicitly skipped; a reload must not accidentally omit a crucial plot revelation.
- **Pause, accessibility, co-op:** Players can pause, skip, replay scene dialogue and set `Comms: full | essential | off` while still receiving visible hazard warnings. Combat controls never lock for a mandatory unskippable conversation. Local and online sessions agree on the same Act and scene-event ID; host controls checkpoint and route-flag commits. Optional scene gallery unlocks at the first completed view/skip.
- **Pacing budget:** Dialogue duration is authored and verified by actual playtests; target a few concise responsive exchanges per slot, not paragraphs on top of combat. Stage clear moments should be exceptional and earned, not habitual scoreboards.

## Event and implementation contract
- Stable event IDs: `A01.OPEN`, `A01.TURN`, `A01.AFTER` through `A08.AFTER`; finale has `A09.OPEN`, `A09.TURN` (moral choice), `A09.AFTER` (ending) with the same schema. **27 mandatory scene slots total**, each resolved against the selected pilot mask and story flags.
- Act metadata: `actId`, `openingTrigger`, `turnTrigger: 'midpoint' | 'preboss'`, `bossDefeatedTrigger`, `transition: 'seamless' | 'tableau' | 'finale'`, `safeCorridorFixture`, `parallaxTransition`, `musicTransition`, `checkpointPolicy`, `scriptEventIds`. The boss defeat event fires **once** and transitions only after an authored aftermath beat has completed or been skipped.
- Story selector: use the 15 masks defined in COMMS-AND-ENDINGS-BIBLE.md; always provide a Lilt-plus-present-pilots fallback. Every mandatory event must have a deterministic scene fixture. Conditional party scenes can enrich the slot, never erase it.
- Validation fixtures: assert 27 slots present; every Act has one OPEN, TURN and AFTER; TURN trigger is explicitly authored; 15 masks × 27 slots resolve without absent speakers; 15 masks × 3 ending choices resolve; transitions preserve powerups and story flags; boss bullet cleanup; skip/replay; co-op reconnect; stage III/V tableau resume; Act VIII-to-finale continuity; no dialogue overlay obscures telegraphs. Test with 1, 2, 3 and 4 players at mobile/tablet/desktop widths.
- Cinematic production: Each AFTER and next OPEN can share a transitional illustrated shot and music bridge. Reuse portrait rigs and parallax layers to keep production practical, but don't reuse the same dialogue for all party combinations. Build actual footage/animation before calling the cinematic implemented.
