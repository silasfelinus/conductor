## 5. The art pipeline

The tools are in [`art/tools/`](art/tools/). They expect a work directory at `/tmp/rainbow-art-work`
(`mkdir -p /tmp/rainbow-art-work/backlog /tmp/rainbow-art-work/altkeyed`, then copy the tools there and run them
from there) and the repos at `/home/user/conductor` and `/home/user/kind_robots`.

- They download renders through `scripts/export_rainbow_opening.py`'s `download()`, which needs `KR_API_TOKEN`.
- They are working scripts, not polished CLIs. Read each docstring.

### a. Ledgers, submitting and status

- Add a cell to a ledger, then run `python scripts/art_ledger_authoring.py <ledger>`. It must report
  **0 problems**, or CI fails.
- The prompt contract rejects:
  - the noun "frame";
  - negations;
  - templated prompts: shared five-word runs across cells, or too few words of the cell's own.
- Write each prompt fresh. Say "edge to edge" rather than "frame".
- Submit with `python scripts/enqueue_art_requests.py <ledger> --live`.
- Refresh status with `python scripts/enqueue_art_requests.py <ledger> --status`.
- Record verdicts with `art/tools/verdicts.py <ledger> <verdicts.json>`. A verdict starts with `keep:`,
  `hold:` or `reject:` and a PT date. Name the retake key when you reject.
- An automated checkpoint (`chore: checkpoint art ledger statuses`) commits status updates to these ledgers on
  `main`. Merge `main` into your branch often, and resolve status conflicts in favour of the newer status.

### b. Detour twins (Fever Dream bands for X1–X4)

1. `sheet.py out.png key=artImageId ...` builds a labelled contact sheet and caches the PNGs in `backlog/`.
2. `altkey.py x2-near=backlog/<file>.png` keys the flat matte to alpha at the Classic plane's size.
   - Append `!fit` to crop a band that stops short of the edges.
   - Append `@R,G,B` to also key a flat colour.
   - It writes `altkeyed/<name>.png` and a preview over a checkerboard. **Look at the preview.**
3. Run `altship.py` from kind_robots:
   - `x4-sky=backlog/<file>.png` for an opaque sky (it trims a white border);
   - `x2-near` alone for a keyed band.
   - It writes `public/rainbow-princess-art/doodle/alt/<name>.webp` and registers it in `RAINBOW_DOODLE_ART`.
4. `verifyRainbowDetourArt` checks that every shipped twin matches its Classic plane's size.

**Key-colour gotcha:** the first batch was painted on magenta, and the art's own pinks keyed out with it. Holes,
ghostly towers and seams all came from that. The retakes ask for a flat **green** surround (`source_matte:
'#00ff00'`). `altkey.py` samples the matte from the top rows, so a green surround needs no change; check the
`matte` value it prints, and tune `TIGHT` (the key's near and far distances) per plane if fine shapes fade.

### c. Idle loops (P-01)

1. `anim_review.py angler=280314` downloads the MP4 and writes a strip of eight sampled frames for review.
2. `ffmpeg -i angler.mp4 angler/all%03d.png` extracts every frame.
3. `anim_atlas.py angler <kind> angler-idle.webp` builds an eight-cell atlas, keyed and sized to the shipped still.
   - `<kind>` is the sprite's path under `doodle/`, such as `bosses/b03-phase2` or `ships/halo-fin`. A bare enemy
     name means `enemies/<name>`.
4. Review the `-preview.png`. Judge the motion against the shipped picket and urchin atlases: the angler's
   frames change about a third to half as much as theirs, which is the floor.
5. Run `idleship.py enemies/angler=<path to atlas>` from kind_robots. It copies the atlas to
   `doodle/anim/<name>-idle.webp` (under 200 KB) and registers it in `RAINBOW_IDLE_ATLASES`.
   - **Do not register** picket, urchin, anchor or lantern. They ship through the older `RAINBOW_ENEMY_ATLASES`
     in `rainbowAnimation.ts` at the same file names.
6. `verifyRainbowAnimation` checks every registered atlas.

Spiral, relay, coral and skimmer failed three earlier takes. Their `-v4` cells start from the shipped still.

**Stale sources (found 2026-11-25 PT).** Many first-take cells name a `source_image_id` that is an old draft or a

### d. Ending clips (P-03)

- `ending-motion-{restore,evacuate,sever-open,loyal-restore,loyal-evacuate,loyal-sever}` ship as
  `public/rainbow-princess-video/endings/<name>.mp4`.
- Each must be under 600 KB, H.264 with faststart and no audio. Encode with
  `ffmpeg -i in.mp4 -an -c:v libx264 -pix_fmt yuv420p -crf 28 -movflags +faststart out.mp4`, raising the CRF to

- Add each name to `RAINBOW_ENDING_MOTION` in `utils/arcade/games/rainbowAnimation.ts`.
- They play once in Fever Dream and fall back to the still plate under calm motion or in the Classic look.
