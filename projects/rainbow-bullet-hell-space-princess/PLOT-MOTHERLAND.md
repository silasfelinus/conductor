## Premise

- **The Motherland** is the princesses' empire. It sends C.A.T.S., its brightest young squadron, to "pacify" the
  Luminous Ocean.
- **The space animals** are the enemy: seahorse pickets, jellyfish arrays, mantis-shrimp claws, shark cutters,
  dolphin pairs and manta carriers. They are a living bio-ecological fleet that grew out of the Ocean itself, and
  they fight the squadron at every step.
- **For Acts I–V the crew believe the official story:** the Ocean's creatures went feral under the "Prism
  Eclipse", and pacifying them is a rescue.
- **The truth arrives in pieces:**
  - Act III's salvage vault holds Motherland hardware (already in the script: the evidence "implicates old fleet
    command").
  - Act IV's convoy route data shows colony ships following behind every "pacified" sector.
  - Act V's depot survivors are not victims of the creatures; they are the creatures' own carers, and the
    collars on the captives are Motherland make.

## The turn: Act V's memorial

The quiet memorial tableau after B05 (ACT-STRUCTURE: "rescued crew confronts the cost") is where the four put
it together. The Motherland is a colonizer, and the animals are defending their home. Lilt-7 is honest and does
not decide for them.

**The choice is flown, not picked from a menu.** It uses the same lane grammar as the route forks and the finale:

- **High lane, TURN ON THE EMPIRE.** C.A.T.S. breaks from the Motherland. Flag `allegiance: 'rebel'`.
- **Low lane, STAY THE COURSE.** The squadron keeps faith with the Motherland. Flag `allegiance: 'loyal'`.

In co-op the crew decides together: the lane holding most of the living pilots wins, and a tie stays the
course. The choice is saved with every later checkpoint, so a continue never flips it.

## The cats

The princesses are C.A.T.S. (Cute And Tough Starfighters) and fly with cat co-pilots: Maribel with Biscuit,
Azure with Comet, Solenne with Butter, Vivi with Glitch. Each super is a **Princess Kitty Power**: the co-pilot
leaps in on the cut-in, and the super keeps its name and rules. The doodle look in DOODLE-DIRECTION.md stands.
The DOGS are gone: no DOG commanders, no bones, no King Rufus.
