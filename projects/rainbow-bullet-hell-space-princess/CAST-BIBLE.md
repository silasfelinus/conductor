# Princess Pilot Cast Bible
**Rainbow Bullet Hell Space Princess** · Character canon and production direction · 2026-10-11 PT

**Authority:** Silas's direction, 2026-10-11 PT: retain the whimsical pink/pastel rainbow palette but create *fully realized princesses* with a real gamut of body types, racial/cultural heritage, and gender expressions. In particular: a short-haired tomboy, a muscular princess, a tall trans-coded princess, and a curvy queer punk princess. These are starting points, **never personality templates or a quota exercise**. Existing good visual designs can be merged with these; don't wipe useful art wholesale.

**Status:** Authored character canon / concept-art specifications. Existing ship roles, colors, names, weapons, and stage/story hooks survive unchanged. The princess character designs below are NOT completed ArtJobs or accepted rendered sprites. Record any visual departure in an asset manifest before treating it as canon.

**Backgrounds are character research anchors, not interchangeable costume kits.** The Luminous Ocean's kingdoms are fictional; collaborate on coherent cuisine, family customs, forms of address, music and regional history rather than splicing random real-world cultural symbols onto armor. Heritage should be expressed through chosen lived-in details and community, not accent jokes, token decorative motifs or an ethnically coded "special power." The four protagonists share the title **Princess** because they are qualified members of a flight order, not because all have the same gender experience or prefer dresses.

Every adult pilot must read as a **person first** in action, dialogue and expressions. All four remain equally competent. Their height/size/appearance **do not change in-game hitbox, shield, ship movement speed or damage budget**; all ships have the same fair collision core. Color is a fleet identifier, not a proxy for heritage or gender; ship outlines and numbered HUD shapes must remain legible in grayscale.

## Character sheets: physical identity, voice, agency and arc

### P1 · Princess Maribel Rowan (pink) · The Girl Who Hears Distress Calls
- **Silhouette and features:** a short, wiry, fast-moving adult woman, squared practical stance; cropped copper-auburn pixie/crew cut (never waist-length in canonical gameplay portraits), freckles, strong nose, narrow eyebrows and hands with repair grease. Clear Irish/Norwegian familial styling references without a faux-Viking costume. Pink may be tough, scuffed rose gold with dark navy and pearly white.
- **Wardrobe:** fitted utility flight jacket with asymmetrical zipped collar, old rescue-radio clipped to the shoulder, rolled sleeves, sturdy boots; ceremonial crown is a small four-petal badge, not a tiara interfering with the helmet. Casual off-duty version has weatherproof trousers and a repaired sweater.
- **Voice and humor:** quick, crooked grin, clipped teasing under stress, candid when a crew member needs help. Enjoys restoring obsolete signal receivers, terrible puns and ferry races. Detests hearing a distress tone go unanswered.
- **Inner conflict:** she once ignored a garbled transmission because it looked like interference. It wasn't. Now she risks the squad for every signal. Her arc is **learning to trust other rescuers**, not losing her compassion.
- **Agency in plot:** decodes a late-game emergency frequency; suggests an optional rescue route, then chooses to ask Solenne for safe backup. Her introduction includes an unglamorous workshop scene before a heroic launch.
- **Connections:** Vivi is her old improvised-radio duet partner; Azure is the person who questions her intuitive navigation; Solenne helps her distinguish a call to act from a call to coordinate.
- **Avoid:** "tomboy = secretly wants to be traditionally feminine," childlike body proportions, jokes at the expense of her short hair, making her the only reckless fool.

### P2 · Princess Azure Lin (cyan) · The Cartographer Who Named the Stars
- **Silhouette and features:** tall adult with long legs, upright elegant carriage, practical angular posture; Vietnamese/East-Asian-influenced facial features defined from consistent reference sheets, deep-black hair braided or partially undercut to fit a helmet, long hands used for plotting light trajectories. **She is a trans woman.** The visual design can communicate self-authorship without invoking anatomical caricatures or making her transness a reveal. Her height is not a "male" cue.
- **Wardrobe:** powder-cyan asymmetrical flight coat with engineered seam geometry, compact prism instrument at collar, dark gloves annotated with hand-drawn constellation lines. Chosen name **Azure** appears on her navigation instrument and signed map, not as an exposition exercise.
- **Voice and humor:** dry precision, elegant but occasionally impatient logic, gently excited when she hears an unfamiliar song. Collects folk star names from pilots of different worlds and keeps the source credits.
- **Inner conflict:** she can calculate the fastest lethal path but misses what the captured fleet is trying to tell the team. Her arc is **learning to value testimony alongside certainty**. Her gender is stable, affirmed, and not a plot twist, curse or metaphor for alien infection.
- **Agency in plot:** identifies the S03 safe route and decodes the Glass Current's false reflections. Sometimes *she* initiates a playful exchange rather than serving only as the serious scientist.
- **Connections:** Solenne respects her technical rigor and can call out her haste; Maribel teaches her what unlogged distress sounds like; Vivi samples Azure's hand-built oscillator sounds into music.
- **Avoid:** exaggerated anatomy, obligatory trauma/coming-out monologues, speech stereotypes, defaulting all Asian characters to quiet obedience or all trans characters to mystical transformation.

### P3 · Princess Solenne Adisa (yellow/gold) · The Captain With Two Lives
- **Silhouette and features:** strong adult Black woman with **substantially muscular shoulders, forearms, back and thighs**, broad silhouette and powerful posture without sexualized superhero proportions; deep warm-brown skin, natural textured hair in practical twists gathered under her Halo Fin helmet, a small asymmetric ear cuff inherited from a family ensemble.
- **Wardrobe:** butter-yellow and antique-gold protective pressure suit, visibly reinforced shoulder seams, sleeveless mechanical work layer during repairs. Circular crown insignia and ring decals echo Halo Fin's defensive drones.
- **Voice and humor:** understated jokes that arrive a beat late, generous with praise after real effort, opinionated about music. In civilian life she secretly writes four-part harmony arrangements and longs to conduct a homecoming concert.
- **Inner conflict:** after a training failure she started managing everybody's oxygen, shields and schedules personally. Her arc is **learning to share responsibility**, *not* "strong Black woman exists to save everyone." She can accept care and be vulnerable without surrendering authority.
- **Agency in plot:** orders a tactical retreat when an encounter is unwinnable, designs the shared revive protocol and secures the evacuation fleet; by the end she lets another pilot lead the final flight line.
- **Connections:** mentor/professional equal to Azure, respectful rival to Maribel's rescue instinct, public admirer of Vivi's improvised music and sometimes Vivi's reluctant duet partner.
- **Avoid:** treating strength as a substitute for femininity, presenting her as the only caregiver, hiding muscles beneath generic thin-waisted armor, magic strength affecting collision stats.

### P4 · Princess Vivi Reyes (lilac) · The Rebel Who Remembers Names
- **Silhouette and features:** adult queer/Latina pilot with a **genuinely curvy full-bodied outline**, generous hips, belly and arms with good range of motion, expressive eyebrows, shaved side/undercut paired with dark textured hair and lilac/coral streaks. Piercings, painted nails and small inked sketches mark a punk DIY sensibility, **not** a cartoon shorthand for sexuality.
- **Wardrobe:** lilac armored jacket customized with stitched repair patches, heavy boots and quick-release belt, asymmetrical comet pins; cockpit safety kit is real and functional. Punk motifs are original, not borrowed band logos or existing queer-community symbols pasted on without thought.
- **Voice and humor:** charismatic, theatrical, fast with a wisecrack but also a patient listener. Collects recordings of rescued crew saying their own names and plays them between sets as an act of memorial care.
- **Inner conflict:** wants to be seen as a legend after growing up in a place where no pilots from her neighborhood were recorded. Her arc is **choosing credit-sharing over fame**. The "quiet sacrifice" is surrendering the spotlight, *not dying*; never a compulsory tragic queer ending.
- **Agency in plot:** invents the Comet Parade super's dazzling coordination signal, leads a daring diversion and ensures anonymous rescuers are named in the ending credits.
- **Connections:** mischievous studio history with Maribel, technical music partnership with Azure, warm unresolved arguments with Solenne over whether music belongs in a cockpit.
- **Avoid:** reducing queer to flirtation, making her curviness a joke or sexual spectacle, associating punk with carelessness or crime, requiring romance to establish sexuality.

## Mothership relationship: Lilt-7
**Lilt** (they/them) is the pearl-glass android/alien liaison aboard the *Aurora Mercy*. They are a fifth conversational voice in **every** playthrough, not a fifth selectable pilot. Their independent motivations, humor, secrets and specific relationships to every combination of princesses are canon in [COMMS-AND-ENDINGS-BIBLE.md](COMMS-AND-ENDINGS-BIBLE.md). The four princesses still drive their own decisions and moral dilemmas. Party composition changes actual story reveals and ending cinematic stingers across all 15 nonempty combinations, including four solo campaigns.

## Canonical interaction beats (writers, voice and animators)
- **Intro C04:** Maribel is neck-deep in a broken rescue radio, Azure corrects the navigation star chart by hand, Solenne composes a half-bar of harmony before the alarm, and Vivi edits a concert recording into the fleet's launch signal. Introduce **interests before identities are explained**.
- **S02 Nemi rescue:** Azure initially recommends a direct blast; Solenne calculates risks; Maribel hears the trapped transmission; Vivi improvises a safe synchronized fire rhythm. All four affect the outcome.
- **S05 wreckyard:** Maribel wants every survivor saved. Solenne sets a 40-second escape boundary. Vivi agrees to abandon an instrument but not the recordings inside. Azure finds the route that respects the time limit. The team wins through compromise.
- **S07 Glass Current:** a machine echo addresses Azure by an outdated chart designation; she calmly ignores the script and tells the squad the route is wrong. The scene is about her confidence and authorship, **not** humiliation or a traumatic reveal.
- **Finale:** Solenne delegates defense; Azure listens to Nemi's alternate chart; Maribel calls for escort help; Vivi reads out the names of rescued pilots. Nobody must die to make a minority character "meaningful."
- **Ending image:** all four alive in baseline victory, each in a chosen personal environment rather than a single interchangeable princess pose; other endings may differ in strategic consequences, not arbitrarily punish identity.

## Asset and layout rules for a production-ready cast
- **Fixed links:** P1 Maribel ↔ pink Petal Wing, P2 Azure ↔ cyan Prism Lance, P3 Solenne ↔ yellow Halo Fin, P4 Vivi ↔ lilac Comet Crown. Preserve current ship roles/supers and main-game player-number IDs. Any future visual art conflicting with these details is a *reconciliation candidate*, not something to delete blindly.
- **Four distinguishable body silhouettes at equal portrait scale:** short compact, tall long-limbed, broad muscular, rounded curvy. Use neutral and dynamic turnaround poses and test their silhouettes in monochrome at 96 and 256px.
- **Each pilot's base deliverables:** full-body orthographic front/profile/back, separate face/expression sheet (neutral, delighted, fearful, confident, frustrated, laughing), pilot helmet on/off, operational and off-duty outfit, waist-up communication portrait, seated cockpit pose, 6-frame action key animation, color/material callout and one short narrative scene.
- **Group composition:** no one relegated to background; rotate who is centered in cinematics. Size difference is honestly visible (Azure tall, Maribel short, Solenne broad, Vivi curvy) with no sexualized perspective or forced waist standard.
- **UI/accessibility:** color-coded HUD **and** distinct numerals and glyphs; no body-type-to-speed relationship; optional pronoun caption in introduction/credits when natural, no gendered class restrictions.
- **Supporting world:** Captain Purl, Professor Opal, Nemi, Coral Choir and rescued pilots should span more ages, skin tones, expressions, bodies and genders. Do not use these characters as background diversity wallpaper: named supporting roles get tasks, choices, motivations and agency.
- **Canon conflicts:** keep approved art's strongest materials/pose/accessories when consistent with a character's identity. Log changes in a before/after table with reason, production asset ID and approval status before replacing older frames. No unverified "existing portrait" claims.
