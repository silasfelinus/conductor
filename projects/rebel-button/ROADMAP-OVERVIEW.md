# rebel-button: project outline

A small playful 100-press toy, not a new platform. Presses 11–99 move/shrink the button, walls, cannon and fake-button encounters; press 100 offers high-score continuation, art rating, visitor-sent fundraiser share and donation link. Existing DESIGN-BRIEF.md and t-003/004 record the build foundation. t-001 reconcile the existing brief; t-005 finish and test all four finale choices and mobile/desktop behavior. t-002 is a soft scope checkpoint, not a blocker. Never post or spend on a visitor's behalf.
