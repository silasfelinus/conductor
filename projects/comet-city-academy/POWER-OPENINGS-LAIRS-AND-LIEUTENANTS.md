# Prom of Doom / Every Villain Tactics: 48 Origin Openers, 48 Villain Lairs, 48 Lieutenants

**Silas direction: October 29, 2026 PT.** Each *permanent* hero/power identity now owns (1) a distinct **hero-origin opening level** that asks the player to use that power for good under pressure, (2) a unique **villain lair tactical level** if that identity becomes an antagonist, and (3) a **named powered second-in-command**, categorically above the one-hit minions and completely independent of the 48 permanent heroes. This is an expansion, **not a retcon** of the single stable codename per hero. The already-written villain henchman species and their named captains stay intact.

## Story and gameplay grammar

The six-stage **Prom of Doom: Crossfire** backbone remains, but each selected hero's first mission is their unique origin tutorial, and the custom six-stage foil route can integrate their named foil's lair as a climax, a mid-campaign infiltration or a bonus one-shot. The 48 lairs belong to **villain owners**, not the protagonist's costume or the player's starting power. In the generic any-hero-versus-any-villain path, both are selected independently: the chosen protagonist contributes their opener; the opposing hero-as-villain contributes their lair, lieutenant and one-hit species crew.

**The command hierarchy has three distinct levels:**
- **Main villain:** one of the canonical 48 heroes in an antagonist storyline, keeping the exact permanent codename.
- **Lieutenant / number two:** 48 **new authored named characters**, each with a distinctive silhouette, encounter-changing power, visible counter, an agenda and **4 HP initial tactical benchmark** (balance subject to testing). They aren't a swapped existing hero, a one-hit mob, or a duplicate of the henchman species' named captain. The lieutenant can negotiate, defect or act without their patron.
- **One-hit supporting species:** 48 named *types* from [VILLAIN-HENCHMEN.md](../comet-city-academy/VILLAIN-HENCHMEN.md). Their minor **species captain** is a different cast member from the lieutenant. Common troops are routed/stunned/disarmed by one hit; one-hit does not require death.

Every **opening level** has a real site, an inciting crisis, an action only the chosen power can solve elegantly, and a difficult decision that can alter reputation, witnesses, resources or allies. It must not just swap dialogue onto the same academy tutorial. Every **lair** has distinctive geography, a stage-changing environmental pattern, an actionable objective and a discoverable secret that supports alternate outcomes. The **lieutenant** has a powerful, telegraphed multi-tile move and a counter the player can learn, rather than arbitrary stat inflation.

## Story-rich production examples

**Cornerstone:** *The Street That Sank* opens with a school bus on the lip of a sinkhole. His antagonist lair is **The Deed Below**, where tunnel collapse threatens legal and illegal escape routes alike. **Mags Hardpan**, a seismic drummer with a quarry-stone bass drum, projects chained tremors; disrupting his downbeat stops them. He may betray Cornerstone if the Warren Wreckers' union safety is ignored. The picnic **Warren Wreckers** remain one-hit troops under Foreman Thumpkin. Mags and Thumpkin are **not the same character**.

**Firewall:** *Grandma's Password* uses a bakery rescue as a humane introduction to hacking. **The Biscuit Firewall** is a pastry-factory data center where oven doors rearrange the map. **Mister Crumb Circuit**, a life-sized enamel sous-chef, creates lattices of redirecting energy; the real three-step family recipe is his counter. The smaller **Mabel's Biscuit Bots** are ordinary one-hit minions.

**Theorem:** *When Two Plus Two Screamed* teaches geometry by saving students from a floating staircase. **The Axiom Engine** uses mutable mathematical vertices and a final city-scale proof. **Proctor Phi** constructs shrinking theorem prisons, which players can break by moving a vertex. The **Primelets** provide disposable swarming hazards.

**The Guardian:** *Keys to the Shelter* begins with the moral choice of opening a family shelter without permission. In **The Mercy Ledger**, shields and doors change allegiance depending on whose rights the player recognizes. **Regent Mercy Vane** commands defensive corridors but may prioritize the residents over Seraphine; the **Charity Cherubs** are the one-hit followers.

**Assembly:** *Everyone Inside* asks whether shared thought can rescue a frightened student without swallowing their autonomy. **The House of One Voice** is an underground hive home where repeating actions spreads between groups. **Auntie Concord**, a telepathic counselor, enforces a consent-breaking consensus vote. Calling a captive's chosen name disrupts her coordination. **Welcome Mats** are the one-hit plush swarm.

**Mindsight:** *The Thought That Wasn't Yours* gives agency over implanted arguments in a student debate. The **Mind Market** predicts party moves, but contradictions undermine the prediction system. **Cipher Edda**, a telepathic auditor, secretly guards her own most private memory while enforcing her boss's surveillance network. The **Thoughtbubbles** are one-hit previewers.

**Second Sight:** *The Prophecy Auction* starts at a manipulative fundraiser. **The Clock of Tomorrow** announces impending strikes well in advance. **Oracle Calyx** attacks only if her forecast remains true; changing the hourglass or the predicted movement breaks her fatalism. **Tomorrow Ravens** remain the fragile advance scouts.

**The Confidante:** *The Rumor After Midnight* teaches the mechanics of evidence at a school newspaper. **The Whisper Engine** turns false accusations into tactical walls. **Publisher Hush Fenwick** can temporarily switch targets with a public scandal, undone by exposing an authentic retraction. **Rumor Mites** are the one-hit messengers.

## Source of truth and evidence

The **full scene briefs**, including all 48 inciting crises, signature moves, moral choices, lair geography, environmental phase pattern, objective, secret, lieutenant look, power, counter and personal agenda, live in Kind Robots:

- `utils/cometAcademy/identityCampaignStages.json`: 48 keyed, authored mission/lair/lieutenant blueprints.
- `utils/cometAcademy/identityCampaignStages.ts`: typed `campaignStagesFor(signature)` lookup.
- `utils/scripts/verifyPromCampaignStages.test.ts`: exact 48-signature parity, nonduplicated titles, venues, lieutenants, no reused hero or species captain, useful text/ability/counter coverage.

**Production status (do not inflate):** 48/48 entries authored and machine-checkable; **0/48 fully built opening tactical maps, 0/48 fully built lair levels, 0/48 playable lieutenant combat AI confirmed by this document**. ArtJob/ArtImage delivery is a separate proof gate, and this project does not authorize public release or user-facing publication. The original Prom of Doom 30-day RPG can reuse these scenes as authored day/night incidents and sites, but this plan does not overwrite its canon maps, saved runs or project scope.

## Development sequence and acceptance gates

1. Make the opening/lair/lieutenant descriptions browseable on the existing 48-person dossier with actual evidence links, without implying unbuilt missions are playable.
2. Expand the stage studio to **author 96 named levels** tied to exact stable power signatures with controllable environment triggers, flexible spawns and wave ownership. Preserve and reuse current maps rather than inventing 96 fake scene thumbnails.
3. Implement and test lieutenant rank, encounter-specific abilities, predictable attack telegraphs, 4-HP benchmark and an actual negotiation/defection pathway with persistent consequences. **No automatic death for disabled henchmen.**
4. Script one fully playable vertical pair (opening + villain lair), then a second contrasting pair, red-team the actual choice/secret paths, and only then scale from authored metadata to 48/48 deliverable scenes. Keep the 6-mission authored and 48 fixed-cast foil requirements.
5. Queue and verify original painted art using durable ArtJobs and delivered URLs; track authored / queued / rendered / attached / tested counts independently.
6. Audit 48 exact joins among origins, enemy bosses, lieutenants, troops and lairs, including an arbitrary protagonist versus arbitrary villain run. Perform phone/tablet/desktop/XL game QA and leave final subjective acceptance/public release for Silas.
