## Premise and brand
MONSTRIQUE is a fictional celebrity franchise featuring adult female monsters as movie and television actresses, fashion supermodels, personalities and an idol group. Commercial deals, celebrity rivalry, fan culture and a cast of charismatic monsters fuel a murder-mystery comedy. The convention happens at its own venue in the city, **not** at the murder motel. The Last Call Motor Lodge is the initial location cluster; the story extends across a city. Original characters, not copies of other franchises.

## Playable leads
**Iris Quill** is the main playable character, an attractive, scrappy, ambitious, witty photojournalist and assistant who investigates physically: she picks up evidence, turns knobs, talks to suspects, photographs clues and opens doors.
**Norbert “Norb” Vale**, her boss, is a keenly observant, odd-looking, hunched, Peter-Lorre-meets-Igor flavored investigative journalist with severe germophobia. He refuses to touch objects or people. His utility is deductions, timelines, interrogation and remote observation; Iris does the tactile work. Iris continually flirts playfully with him, while Norb consistently rebuffs her and stays focused on the job. This remains a running joke, never a romance objective nor an invitation to override consent. Player may direct Norb's lines/deductions, but Iris anchors exploration. Shared conversation topics unlock new actor-specific answers, confrontations and cross-witness comparisons.

## Opening and double murder
**Odette Harker** (working name) is the smarmy female MONSTRIQUE agent who managed the monster stars' contracts, licensing, appearances and feuds, leaving plenty of enemies. In a pre-playable cold open, she has hired **Violet Wisp** (working name), an adult female sex worker, for a motel date. They share consensual flirtation and become intimate, presented suggestively with an optional fade rather than explicit sex. An unseen killer interrupts and murders **both** with the same highly distinctive ornamental/stage knife. Neither death is sexualized. The two women are discovered together when Iris and Norb enter the crime room. Both then appear as **independent interrogable ghosts**. Odette cannot remember the killer but has numerous powerful leads and enemies. Violet must have real agency and separate observational clues (voices, sound, room access, scents), not serve as a disposable prop.

**Previous canon is superseded**: Celestine is NOT the murder victim, Odette is NOT the murderer, and the convention is NOT the motel. The true culprit must be proven through a fair clue graph before being locked. **Gavin**, the disturbing stalker fan, is still *not* the murderer; stalking has real consequences.

## Location clusters and progressive unlocking
1. **Last Call Motor Lodge**: exterior/parking, the couple's room, Odette's crime room, neighboring rooms, balcony and hall, ice machine alcove, front office, laundry and service stair, hot tub and maintenance access. Odd sounds and ice timing are functional clues. Ground and upper floors look like a seedy motel, not a convention center.
2. **Monstrique Convention Center**: a **separate city destination**, with registration, booths, star autographs, merch, idol rehearsal stage, fan encounters and backstage dressing areas.
3. **Supernatural gag/novelty shop**: genuinely creepy and funny occult joke merchandise, clever inventory combinations, rumors about the murder weapon.
4. **Draculina's mansion**: the celebrity vampire's gothic residence (final star name TBD), secrets unlocked through earned access.
5. **Sewers beneath the motel**: branching maintenance passages and the hidden former dressing room of Albina, an enormous upright albino alligator and **erased original 1963 Monstrique member**, clinging to her lost celebrity in tragicomic old-Hollywood style. Her early billing, forgotten recordings and signed contracts are material evidence, not a joke that she was never talented.

Cities unlock on earned clues and puzzle outcomes. No padding with empty rooms.

## Mechanics and visuals
A **real** icon-based 2D point-and-click game: Walk, Look, Talk, Take, Use, Combine, Photograph, Ask Topic, Consult Norb. Inventory/puzzle objects can be acted on in multiple sensible ways. Iris owns tactile interactions; Norb's physical contact is restricted by characterization. Dialogue topics are shared and update as evidence emerges, but response branches differ between investigators. The two ghosts can be interrogated separately, with truthful but incomplete perspectives. Every required clue has at least two discoverable routes, non-dead-end recovery and context-sensitive hints. Save state is versioned, account scoped and reloadable.

Single-screen art-first AAA handheld-console aesthetic at phone/tablet/desktop, with expressive character illustrations and authored room panoramas, small icon menus, readable speech, expandable evidence/topic notebook, city map, and no web-dashboard-like UI or decorative gold borders.
