# MONSTRIQUE — Character Lookbook and Cast Continuity
_2026-11-07 PT · author-facing visual, writing, scrapbook and animation brief · internal slug: monstress-convention_

## Non-negotiable visual hierarchy
The characters are all adults. **Apparent age does not equal chronological age.** Draculina *looks* about 20 and is the youngest-looking franchise star despite being an original 1963 charter member; Siobhan is second-youngest in appearance. Cleo is visibly among the oldest. Albina is a retired founding member, not a failed audition applicant. None of these characters should be redrawn as generic human fashion models with a single monster accessory.

## Draculina · the eternally popular queen bee
- **Status:** 1963 charter Monstrique founder; left in 1994 and returned in 2006. Ancient vampire, visual age about **20**, emphatically adult.
- **Look:** short black glossy vintage curls or razor-sharp bob, porcelain-pale but living-feeling complexion, striking fangs and dark eyes, immaculate fashionable uniform-like silhouettes. A 1960s starlet who could walk into a modern campus drama and instantly run the clique. Never drawn as an actual schoolchild or in a real school setting.
- **Personality:** deceptively sweet, socially surgical high-school mean-girl mannerisms: compliments that insult, manufactured friendship hierarchies, plausible deniability and fame as a scoreboard. Capable and very old under the youthful act. Her motives are not reducible to jealousy over another woman's looks.
- **Art beats:** apparent twenty-year-old close-up beside her own 1964 record; youthful 2020s red-carpet lineup against visibly older colleagues; private mansion vanity with impossible archive of changing fashions. Keep the future killer identity out of public-facing art/prompt metadata.
- **Game/scrapbook:** era-specific covers, clique reputation, solo-career and mansion records. Her final-act secrets stay spoiler-locked through Chapter 6.

## Siobhan MacRae · Scottish wolf-pop veteran
- **Status:** adult, second-youngest-looking performer, ex-member of multi-species were-animal pop group **The Pack**; joined Monstrique's 2017 idol era, dropped in 2024. Separate from 1960s founding werewolf Roxie Lupin.
- **Look:** shaggy textured hair merging into fur, wolf ears and unmistakable muzzle/fangs, large leather or fashion collar, tartan accents and energetic layered stagewear; lively, scruffy Scottish rock-pop personality. Physically expressive rather than smoothly airbrushed.
- **Personality:** brave, excitable, funny, loyal until betrayed; has serious musical skill and a competitive streak. Her old pop fame is real, not a pathetic fantasy.
- **Art beats:** an early Pack single, 2017 Blood Moon coordinated costume, 2024 erased photocard, reunion in a sweaty underground venue.
- **Game/scrapbook:** original duet/demo, fractured song rights, stage photographs and a hard-won independent comeback.

## Nyx Sable · the were-panther frenemy
- **Status:** adult female were-panther, formerly Siobhan's Pack bandmate and songwriting partner. She is not a 1960s Monstrique founder or a secret replacement for Roxie.
- **Look:** unmistakable sleek panther head and ears, dark spotted fur that catches light, feline posture and tail, angular avant-pop clothing. No human face with painted-on spots.
- **Personality:** controlled, exacting, brilliant at choreography and branding; capable of generous acts she prefers not to advertise. Siobhan reads Nyx's precision as betrayal; Nyx reads Siobhan's improvisation as abandonment.
- **Conflict:** a stolen solo, a leaked demo, and an Odette-negotiated narrative split that profited from their fights. Both tell believable but partial truths. The player may help restore joint credit without requiring friendship or romance.
- **Art beats:** torn two-sided Pack sleeve, synchronized dance silhouette disrupted by a missing partner, a contract signed by both.

## Cleo Pappatra · the elder fashion monarch
- **Status:** adult living mummy, original-era monster celebrity, returns after her 1986 exclusion. **One of the visibly oldest** cast members, not another indistinguishable eternally 20-year-old glamour model.
- **Look:** mature, richly expressive face and dignified bearing, authentic bandage anatomy, aged linen, couture textiles, hieroglyphic jewelry as personal design, and a silhouette of self-made authority. She is glamorous *because* of age and history, not despite it.
- **Personality:** dry, erudite, patient enough to terrify people; understands contracts and wardrobes from centuries of practice. Her humor emerges from refusing to accept the industry's memory of her.
- **Art beats:** 1964 Beach Hex studio glamour, 1980s rejection, private couture atelier, present-day convention signing.
- **Game/scrapbook:** a recovered royalty barcode and vintage costume archive; strong non-romantic storyline even if flirt settings are off.

## Albina / 'Allie Gator' · the star they buried alive
- **Status:** giant adult albino alligator, **paid, billed 1963 founding Monstrique member**. Cut before the seven-record history begins. Her *existence* was later retconned out of the studio's story.
- **Look:** oversized truly alligator anatomy: long pale snout, heavy jaw, scaled chest, powerful hind legs, big tail, claws, pink eyes and bright lipstick. **No human face, dainty nose, or conventionally gorgeous pin-up body.** Huge aging satin dressing gown, moth-eaten stole, rhinestone crown, pearls and tired stage makeup. Unapologetically monstrous.
- **Personality:** melodramatic old-Hollywood recluse, larger than life and desperately attached to a lost premiere. Haughty one moment, heartbreaking the next. Her performance technique and memories prove she was once good at her work.
- **Sewer stage:** an eerie hidden backstage apartment beneath the Last Call Motor Lodge: spotlight aimed at a cracked mirror, looping applause record, discolored publicity stills, abandoned fan letters, stack of signed roadshow contracts and an old projector pointing at an empty chair. The humor comes from the decaying spectacle of celebrity self-worship; never from ridiculing deformity or her legitimate exclusion.
- **Puzzle:** restore electricity to the film projector, recover the contemporaneous **1963 signed original roadshow program**, compare with doctored 1964 sleeve/contract; give Albina the opportunity to reclaim her billing or choose a quieter release. Both resolutions preserve critical evidence. She is a founder who wants recognition, **not an aspirant looking for a first audition**.
- **Scrapbook:** then/now portrait pairing, omitted signed cover, restored credit, optional dignified glamour reveal. Do not require the player to flirt or flatter her to obtain necessary evidence.

## Cross-media usage
- **Game scenes:** hand-drawn non-identical silhouettes, multiple facial/emotion poses; 390px, 768px and 1440px stage composition. The art should read at gameplay scale rather than only as a beauty portrait.
- **Album history:** keep the **seven official decade covers unchanged in lineup**. Albina's recovered 1963 roadshow is an extra archival artifact, not a retcon of the 1964 replacement or an eighth decade.
- **Art prompts:** use explicit name, species anatomy, apparent age, era, costume and emotion; Draculina adult apparent ~20, Siobhan adult early-mid 20s appearance, Cleo mature/elder, Albina giant alligator. No direct real-person likeness. No explicit imagery.
- **Authoring/scrapbook:** separate portrait, past/current images, expression frames, original and doctored covers, evidence unlock, gratitude, and independent consent-gated romance. Critical clues are available when romance is disabled.
- **Privacy:** admin preview only behind authenticated mature opt-in, excluding CHILD; no release, publishing or unguarded asset URLs absent explicit human approval.
