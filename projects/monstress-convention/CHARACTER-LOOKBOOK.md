# MONSTRIQUE — Character Lookbook and Cast Continuity
_2026-11-07 PT · author-facing visual, writing, scrapbook and animation brief · internal slug: monstress-convention_

## Non-negotiable visual hierarchy
The characters are all adults. **Apparent age does not equal chronological age.** Draculina *looks* about 20 and is the youngest-looking franchise star despite being an original 1963 charter member; Siobhan is second-youngest in appearance. Cleo is visibly among the oldest. Albina is a retired founding member, not a failed audition applicant. None of these characters should be redrawn as generic human fashion models with a single monster accessory.

## Draculina · the eternally popular queen bee
- **Status:** 1963 charter Monstrique founder; left in 1994 and returned in 2006. Ancient vampire, visual age about **20**, emphatically adult.
- **Look:** short black glossy vintage curls or razor-sharp bob, porcelain-pale but living-feeling complexion, striking fangs and dark eyes, immaculate fashionable uniform-like silhouettes. A 1960s starlet who could walk into a modern campus drama and instantly run the clique. Never drawn as an actual schoolchild or in a real school setting.
- **Personality:** deceptively sweet, socially surgical high-school mean-girl mannerisms: compliments that insult, manufactured friendship hierarchies, plausible deniability and fame as a scoreboard. Capable and very old under the youthful act. Her motives are not reducible to jealousy over another woman's looks.
- **Art beats:** apparent twenty-year-old close-up beside her own 1964 record; youthful 2020s red-carpet lineup against visibly older colleagues; private mansion vanity with impossible archive of changing fashions. Keep the future killer identity out of public-facing art/prompt metadata.
- **Game/scrapbook:** era-specific covers, clique reputation, solo-career and mansion records. Her final-act secrets stay spoiler-locked through Chapter 6.

## Siobhan MacRae · Scottish wolf-pop veteran
- **Status:** adult, second-youngest-looking performer, ex-member of multi-species were-animal pop group **The Pack**; joined Monstrique's 2017 idol era, dropped in 2024. Separate from 1960s founding werewolf Roxie Lupin.
- **Look:** shaggy textured hair merging into fur, wolf ears and unmistakable muzzle/fangs, large leather or fashion collar, tartan accents and energetic layered stagewear; lively, scruffy Scottish rock-pop personality. Physically expressive rather than smoothly airbrushed.
- **Personality:** brave, excitable, funny, loyal until betrayed; has serious musical skill and a competitive streak. Her old pop fame is real, not a pathetic fantasy.
- **Art beats:** an early Pack single, 2017 Blood Moon coordinated costume, 2024 erased photocard, reunion in a sweaty underground venue.
- **Game/scrapbook:** original duet/demo, fractured song rights, stage photographs and a hard-won independent comeback.

## Nyx Sable · the were-panther frenemy
- **Status:** adult female were-panther, formerly Siobhan's Pack bandmate and songwriting partner. She is not a 1960s Monstrique founder or a secret replacement for Roxie.
- **Look:** unmistakable sleek panther head and ears, dark spotted fur that catches light, feline posture and tail, angular avant-pop clothing. No human face with painted-on spots.
- **Personality:** controlled, exacting, brilliant at choreography and branding; capable of generous acts she prefers not to advertise. Siobhan reads Nyx's precision as betrayal; Nyx reads Siobhan's improvisation as abandonment.
- **Conflict:** a stolen solo, a leaked demo, and an Odette-negotiated narrative split that profited from their fights. Both tell believable but partial truths. The player may help restore joint credit without requiring friendship or romance.
- **Art beats:** torn two-sided Pack sleeve, synchronized dance silhouette disrupted by a missing partner, a contract signed by both.

## Cleo Pappatra · the elder fashion monarch
- **Status:** adult living mummy, original-era monster celebrity, returns after her 1986 exclusion. **One of the visibly oldest** cast members, not another indistinguishable eternally 20-year-old glamour model.
- **Look:** mature, richly expressive face and dignified bearing, authentic bandage anatomy, aged linen, couture textiles, hieroglyphic jewelry as personal design, and a silhouette of self-made authority. She is glamorous *because* of age and history, not despite it.
- **Personality:** dry, erudite, patient enough to terrify people; understands contracts and wardrobes from centuries of practice. Her humor emerges from refusing to accept the industry's memory of her.
- **Art beats:** 1964 Beach Hex studio glamour, 1980s rejection, private couture atelier, present-day convention signing.
- **Game/scrapbook:** a recovered royalty barcode and vintage costume archive; strong non-romantic storyline even if flirt settings are off.

## Albina / 'Allie Gator' · the star they buried alive
- **Status:** giant adult albino alligator, **paid, billed 1963 founding Monstrique member**. Cut before the seven-record history begins. Her *existence* was later retconned out of the studio's story.
- **Look:** oversized truly alligator anatomy: long pale snout, heavy jaw, scaled chest, powerful hind legs, big tail, claws, pink eyes and bright lipstick. **No human face, dainty nose, or conventionally gorgeous pin-up body.** Huge aging satin dressing gown, moth-eaten stole, rhinestone crown, pearls and tired stage makeup. Unapologetically monstrous.
- **Personality:** melodramatic old-Hollywood recluse, larger than life and desperately attached to a lost premiere. Haughty one moment, heartbreaking the next. Her performance technique and memories prove she was once good at her work.
- **Sewer stage:** an eerie hidden backstage apartment beneath the Last Call Motor Lodge: spotlight aimed at a cracked mirror, looping applause record, discolored publicity stills, abandoned fan letters, stack of signed roadshow contracts and an old projector pointing at an empty chair. The humor comes from the decaying spectacle of celebrity self-worship; never from ridiculing deformity or her legitimate exclusion.
- **Puzzle:** restore electricity to the film projector, recover the contemporaneous **1963 signed original roadshow program**, compare with doctored 1964 sleeve/contract; give Albina the opportunity to reclaim her billing or choose a quieter release. Both resolutions preserve critical evidence. She is a founder who wants recognition, **not an aspirant looking for a first audition**.
- **Scrapbook:** then/now portrait pairing, omitted signed cover, restored credit, optional dignified glamour reveal. Do not require the player to flirt or flatter her to obtain necessary evidence.

## Cross-media usage
- **Game scenes:** hand-drawn non-identical silhouettes, multiple facial/emotion poses; 390px, 768px and 1440px stage composition. The art should read at gameplay scale rather than only as a beauty portrait.
- **Album history:** keep the **seven official decade covers unchanged in lineup**. Albina's recovered 1963 roadshow is an extra archival artifact, not a retcon of the 1964 replacement or an eighth decade.
- **Art prompts:** use explicit name, species anatomy, apparent age, era, costume and emotion; Draculina adult apparent ~20, Siobhan adult early-mid 20s appearance, Cleo mature/elder, Albina giant alligator. No direct real-person likeness. No explicit imagery.
- **Authoring/scrapbook:** separate portrait, past/current images, expression frames, original and doctored covers, evidence unlock, gratitude, and independent consent-gated romance. Critical clues are available when romance is disabled.
- **Privacy:** admin preview only behind authenticated mature opt-in, excluding CHILD; no release, publishing or unguarded asset URLs absent explicit human approval.

## The two leads · 2026-11-22 PT redesign (supersedes 2026-11-10)

Silas, 2026-11-22 PT: *"The two leads are vitally important to the flavor of the game, and both are
falling short."* Code source of truth: Kind Robots `utils/monstrique/characterModels.ts` (`iris`, `norb`).

**Why the old renders missed.** Iris and Norb are not monster stars, so the only art they ever got was
the series-bible *portrait*. That path sent a ~150-word prose prompt to tag-trained Illustrious checkpoints
(Arthemy, Illustrij, NTR Mix, Furrytoon) and hard-coded a "knowing lopsided smirk" on every face, which
flattened Iris's warmth and overrode Norb's eyes. Norb's thick coke-bottle glasses hid the one feature that
sells him, "four foot eleven" means nothing to an image model, and Iris's "dark brown hair" and `bob` sprite
collided with Draculina's black bob. Portraits on tag lanes now get Danbooru-style identity tags, each lead
has a *signature* expression, and Norb carries a per-character caricature note.

### Iris Quill · sassy, open-hearted, brave
- **Read at 64 px:** a big messy mane of **jet-black hair with one thick white streak**, a rose trench
  belted tight, a press camera swinging on its strap, a plaster on her neck.
- **Face:** bright, wide, *curious* eyes with smudged black winged liner; arched, very mobile eyebrows; a
  wide crooked grin; berry lipstick. An open face, not a sultry pout. She looks like she's about to say
  something rude *and* do something kind.
- **Hair:** shoulder-length, tousled, swept bangs; the streak starts at the front hairline. **Never brown,
  never a sleek bob** (Draculina's shape) and never multiple lightning streaks or a beehive (Francesca's).
- **Sassy-tramp wardrobe:** belted rose-and-plum trench over a black crop top and high-waisted pencil skirt,
  laddered fishnets, scuffed ankle boots, big gold hoops, chipped nail polish, press pass on the belt, the
  MONSTRIQUE tramp stamp when she bends. Cheap glamour worn with total confidence.
- **Personality on the body:** talks with her hands and eyebrows, leans *in* toward anything strange, first
  through the door, laughs at her own bad ideas. Sharp tongue, open heart: she teases everyone and roots for
  all of them. Never cold, bored, mousy or a damsel.
- **Expression sheet:** `sass` (signature: cocked eyebrow, crooked grin, mid-wisecrack, hand on hip),
  `curious` (leaning in, eyes bright, delighted grin), `brave` (chin up, camera raised like a weapon),
  `flirt`, `disgust`, `focus`.

### Norbert "Norb" Vale · Quasimodo × Igor × Peter Lorre, drawn like a 1990s TV cartoon
- **Read at 64 px:** a **little person barely waist-high to Iris**: a hump, a stoop, a huge lumpy head,
  two mismatched eyes, a hot-pink MONSTRIQUE T-shirt and two raised yellow gloves.
- **Body:** adult man with dwarfism, about four feet tall: a huge head, short bowed legs, stubby arms
  ending in oversized gloved hands. A big Quasimodo hump on one shoulder, Igor stoop, head thrust forward
  on no neck. About 2.6 heads tall.
- **The eyes are the character:** huge, wide-open, bulging eyes; the **left one much larger and set
  higher** than the right; **heavy, drooping lids** half-lowered over both, with bags underneath. The
  lids droop when he's smug and fly open when he's horrified. The big eye swivels on its own.
- **Glasses:** small round wire spectacles that *show* the eyes. The left lens is thick and magnifies the
  big eye even bigger. (No opaque coke-bottle lenses: they hid the face. The spare pair still exists
  for Vespa's favour.)
- **Face:** lumpy, misshapen, lopsided skull; sallow skin and stubble; almost no chin; enormous bulbous
  doorknob nose; wide froggy mouth with crooked buck teeth; a nervous, wheedling grin; a few greasy strands
  combed over a bald scalp.
- **Drawing style:** a rubbery 1990s TV-cartoon caricature: squash-and-stretch, exaggerated takes,
  grotesque comic distortion. He should be the ugliest face in any room and still have the most acting.
  He's never handsome, cute, symmetrical, tall, upright, young, bearded or a fantasy dwarf.
- **Wardrobe:** oversized hot-pink MONSTRIQUE convention T-shirt tucked into high-waisted rumpled grey
  trousers, a grey jacket too small for the hump, crooked bow tie over the T-shirt, galoshes.
- **Dignity rule:** the comedy is his neuroses, his priorities and those eyes, never his height alone. He is
  the boss and the smartest person in every room.
- **Expression sheet:** `unblinking` (signature: mismatched eyes bulging under heavy lids, nervous
  wheedling grin), `recoil` (eyes popping, gloves up), `deduce` (one gloved finger raised, the big eye
  glinting through its lens, heavy-lidded and smug).

## Laguna Brine and the bayou brood · 2026-11-22 PT

Silas, 2026-11-22 PT: *"One of the scenes is a bayou trailer park, where the fish lady now lives. She's in
a trailer, overweight, pregnant, with a bunch of younglings, in various stages from teenager to tadpole.
Southern white redneck tropes. She does have helpful adult daughters that provide some of the T and A."*

**Proposed reconciliation, to confirm with Silas:** the fish lady is Laguna Brine, the only amphibian star.
FRANCHISE-HISTORY lists "Laguna" in the 2017 and 2024 line-ups, and the album ledger stays authoritative. So
since 2017 the Laguna on stage has been a **licensed likeness**: a younger look-alike in a luxury
"amphibian rebrand" that Odette cast and controlled. The real Laguna has lived on the bayou since she was
pushed out in 1974, and has never seen a cent from either Laguna. That's her grudge against the agent.

- **Where:** **Brine Bottoms Mobile Home Park**, out past the city limits where the stopped clocks can't
  reach: cypress, Spanish moss, an airboat dock, trucks on blocks, a satellite dish, lawn flamingos and a
  crawdad boil. Laguna's 1970s single-wide sits on stilts, with a kiddie pool of tadpoles out front, gold
  records nailed up as hubcap shine, and a garden hose she sprays herself with to keep from drying out.
- **Laguna now:** a big, heavyset, heavily pregnant adult amphibian (frog and salamander, green-gold skin,
  pink feathery gills, wide golden eyes, webbed hands and feet). She wears her 1974 gold-sequin fishtail
  gown cut down into a house robe over a tank top, with flip-flops on webbed feet. She's still the best
  songwriter in the franchise, playing a beat-up guitar on the porch. Warm, tired and wry, with a drawl and
  a sharp memory for contracts.
- **The gag that's also the clue:** a box of **Lagoon Lass Fish Sticks** with her own 1974 face on it.
  Odette licensed Laguna's likeness to frozen fish sticks and kept the royalties. The kids eat them because
  they're cheap and call the box "Mama". Puzzle evidence: the licensing statement, the 1974 reversed
  songwriting credit (the Velvet Crypt sleeve) and the 2017 look-alike contract.
- **The younglings:** tadpoles in the kiddie pool, froglets with tails hopping on the couch, a gilled tween
  running a crawdad-racing book, and a sullen teen with gills and a phone. They're comic chaos and puzzle
  helpers. **The children and teen are never in flirt, gratitude, pin-up or mature content, and they're never
  framed near it.** Laguna's weight and pregnancy are her design, not the joke. The joke is Odette's fish
  sticks and the absent baby daddy, **Earl**, who's always "out on the boat".
- **Delta Brine** (adult, about 27): the eldest daughter, half-amphibian, green-tinged skin, short gill frills
  behind her ears, golden eyes and webbed fingers. She wears grease-stained coveralls tied at the waist over a
  tank top, runs the airboat tours and bait shop, and is deadpan and competent. She helps Iris with the boat
  and swamp puzzles.
- **Cherie Brine** (adult, about 23): the flirt, also half-amphibian, with big teased hair and frills, a knotted
  plaid shirt, denim cutoffs and cowboy boots. She waits tables at the Gator Bait Bar & Grill and brings the
  cheesecake. She gets flirt and gratitude routes and mature pin-ups like the monster stars, adults only and
  opt-in.
- **Tone guard:** affectionate Southern-redneck tropes (mudding, cutoffs, crawdads, porch music). It never
  punches at poverty, weight or species, and has no hate symbols. The punchline is the agent who got rich off
  the family.
- **Model sheets:** add `delta` and `cherie` to `characterModels.ts`, and add a then/now split for Laguna,
  once Silas confirms the reconciliation above. Until then, Laguna's existing sheet and pin-ups are unchanged.
