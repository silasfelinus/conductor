# Aesthetic Guidelines — the bar for auto-created art

These are the qualities the daily style assessor rewards when it scores the art
the pipeline generated in the last day. The goal (Silas, 2026-07): **up the ante**
— the auto-created output should look intentional, not like a random batch.

## Originality inputs

- When making fresh, original images, actively consider Kind Robots' available
  randomizers and special lists as optional inspiration pools. Facets join this
  pool as they are imported and expanded.
- Sample a small number of unexpected elements, usually one to three, then
  synthesize them into one coherent concept. Randomizers are sparks, not a demand
  to cram every rolled noun into prompt soup.
- Keep the actual brief, subject, utility, and ethical boundaries in control.
  Discard combinations that weaken the idea or merely make it noisier.
- When practical, preserve the selected randomizer/list/facet values in the
  ArtJob prompt or metadata so curator and human feedback can reveal which kinds
  of combinations consistently produce strong work.

## What we're going for

- **Intentional composition** — a clear subject, a considered frame, real focal
  hierarchy. Not a centered blob on empty background.
- **Cohesive palette** — a deliberate, harmonious set of colors with mood, not
  muddy or clashing. Warm/cozy with an edge is the house signature.
- **Light with intent** — direction, contrast, atmosphere. Lantern glow, dusk,
  bioluminescence, weather — light that tells you how the place feels.
- **Character & story** — the image implies a world and invites a scene. It has
  a point of view, a little strangeness, a reason to look twice.
- **Craft** — clean rendering, believable anatomy and perspective, no melted
  hands, no accidental extra limbs, no garbled structure.
- **Finish** — reads as a finished piece at card/hero size, not a rough draft.

## Automatic knocks (lower the score)

- Blank, degenerate, or near-empty frames; flat dead lighting.
- Watermarks, readable garbled text, signatures, UI chrome, borders/title bars.
- Collage/grid/contact-sheet layouts when a single subject was intended.
- Melted or duplicated anatomy; broken perspective; obvious AI artifacts.
- Generic stock-fantasy filler with no point of view.
- Off-brief: a glamour portrait or cosmic poster where a scene was wanted.

## Scoring intent

- **80-100 (promote):** portfolio-worthy for the site — feature it.
- **60-79 (revise):** strong idea, one fixable flaw.
- **0-59 (reject):** off-brief or a craft failure.

The digest's daily gallery features the **highest-scored** renders of the day,
so this bar directly shapes what Silas sees celebrated each morning.
