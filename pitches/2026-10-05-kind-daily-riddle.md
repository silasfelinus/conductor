# Pitch: Kind Daily Riddle
date: 2026-10-05
project-target: new
status: awaiting-silas

## The idea
One hand-written riddle every day, chosen from a bundled calendar of 365, with three gentle hints that unlock over time and a pre-made illustration revealed on the right answer. Streaks count quietly and a missed day never erases anything you have earned.

## Why it's worth doing
It needs no LLM at runtime, so it is free for every visitor. Art plan: 60 riddle answer illustrations to start, with the remaining riddles reusing a shared frame.

small

## Suggested first task
Thirty riddles, the date-based picker, the hint ladder, and one answer reveal.
