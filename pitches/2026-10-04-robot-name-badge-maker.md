# Pitch: Robot Name Badge Maker
date: 2026-10-04
project-target: new
status: awaiting-silas

## The idea
Pick a robot face, a colour and a role, type your name, and get a printable name badge as a PNG or PDF sized for standard badge sleeves. It runs fully in the browser, and a sheet mode lays out six badges per page for classrooms and parties.

## Why it's worth doing
It needs no LLM at runtime, so it is free for every visitor. Art plan: 20 robot faces and 8 border frames, generated ahead of time as transparent PNGs.
