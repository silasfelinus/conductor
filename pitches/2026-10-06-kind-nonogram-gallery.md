# Pitch: Kind Nonogram Gallery
date: 2026-10-06
project-target: new
status: awaiting-silas

## The idea
Picture-cross puzzles where numeric row and column clues reveal a pixel portrait of a Kind Robot species, with a gentle mistake-forgiving mode. Each solved grid is stored in a gallery and colours in using the species palette.

## Why it's worth doing
It needs no LLM at runtime, so it is free for every visitor. Art plan: 30 hand-authored grids plus 30 colour reveal illustrations generated ahead of time.

## Rough effort
medium

## Suggested first task
One 8x8 puzzle with clue generation from a stored bitmap, tap-to-fill, and a colour reveal.
