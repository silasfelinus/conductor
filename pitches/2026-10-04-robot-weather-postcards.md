# Pitch: Robot Weather Postcards
date: 2026-10-04
project-target: new
status: awaiting-silas

## The idea
Pick a season and a mood and a pre-made postcard of a robot visiting a cosy place appears, with a hand-written greeting assembled from authored phrase parts. Download the card as an image to send to a friend, no account required.

## Why it's worth doing
It needs no LLM at runtime, so it is free for every visitor. Art plan: 24 postcard scenes across four seasons and six moods, generated with a shared border and stamp area.

small

## Suggested first task
Six scenes, the season and mood pickers, a phrase assembler and a canvas export to PNG.
