# Pitch: Robot Weather Postcards
date: 2026-10-04
project-target: new
status: awaiting-silas

## The idea
A small page that reads the visitor's local weather from a free public forecast API and shows a matching pre-made robot postcard, such as a robot in a raincoat or one asleep under snow. Each postcard carries a pre-written caption, and a collection page tracks which weathers you have seen.

## Why it's worth doing
It needs no LLM at runtime, so it is free for every visitor. Art plan: 16 postcards covering weather type and day or night, generated ahead of time and captioned by hand.

## Rough effort
small

## Suggested first task
4 postcards, the weather lookup, the weather-to-postcard mapping, and the caption.
