# Pitch: Robot Dress-Up Workshop
date: 2026-10-02
project-target: new
status: approved

## The idea
A paper-doll workshop where you pick a robot body and layer on pre-generated hats, arms, antennae, paint jobs and accessories, save the result to your device and download it as a sticker. Pure client-side layering, with parts that snap to anchor points so nothing needs generation.

## Why it's worth doing
It needs no LLM at runtime, so it is free for every visitor. Art plan: 6 bodies and about 80 transparent parts on matching anchor grids, generated and cleaned in batches.

medium

## Suggested first task
Anchor-grid spec, 1 body with 10 parts, and the layering canvas with PNG export.
