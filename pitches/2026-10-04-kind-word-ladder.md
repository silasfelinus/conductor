# Pitch: Kind Word Ladder
date: 2026-10-04
project-target: new
status: rejected

## The idea
Change one letter at a time to climb from a start word to a kind end word, such as grump to happy, using hand-checked ladders and a bundled dictionary. Each solved ladder reveals one panel of a pre-made illustration, and a hint button reveals the next rung.

## Why it's worth doing
It needs no LLM at runtime, so it is free for every visitor. Art plan: 20 four-panel illustrations, one revealed per solved ladder, generated ahead of time.

small

## Suggested first task
10 verified ladders, the tap-letter editor with dictionary check, and one revealed panel.
