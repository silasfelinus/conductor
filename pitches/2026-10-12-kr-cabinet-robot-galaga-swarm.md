# Pitch: Swarm Wing
date: 2026-10-12
project-target: kr-arcade
status: awaiting-silas

## The idea
An arcade cabinet riffing on Galaga's diving alien formations, where a small robot ship shoots waves of paper-kite bugs that swoop out of formation in looping dives. Each stage adds more divers, faster loops and a bonus stage, with a score submitted to the leaderboard.

## Why it's worth doing
It needs no LLM at runtime, so it is free for every visitor. Art plan: Cabinet marquee, ship sprite, four kite-bug enemies, bullet and explosion frames and 3 starfield backdrops generated ahead of time.

## Rough effort
medium

## Suggested first task
Intro splash, one formation with scripted dive paths, shooting, lives, rising dive speed and a score submit.
