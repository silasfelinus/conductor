# Pitch: Kind Pocket Orchard
date: 2026-10-09
project-target: new
status: rejected

## The idea
A slow idle-garden toy where players plant pre-drawn fruit trees, wait real minutes for them to ripen, and trade baskets of fruit for decorations in a small orchard. Everything is a fixed table of trees and rewards, saved locally in the browser with no accounts.

## Why it's worth doing
It needs no LLM at runtime, so it is free for every visitor. Art plan: 12 fruit trees at 3 growth stages, 20 decorations and 4 seasonal backdrops generated ahead of time.

## Rough effort
medium

## Suggested first task
Three tree types, plant and harvest timers, a basket counter, and a decoration shop with five items.
