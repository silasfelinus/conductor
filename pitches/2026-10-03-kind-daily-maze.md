# Pitch: Kind Daily Maze
date: 2026-10-03
project-target: new
status: rejected

## The idea
One new seeded maze every day where a small robot collects stars on the way to a charging station, generated deterministically in the browser from the date so everyone gets the same puzzle. A streak calendar and ten themed tile sets give it a reason to return.

## Why it's worth doing
It needs no LLM at runtime, so it is free for every visitor. Art plan: 10 themed tile sets (forest, ice, desert, space and more) of about 8 tiles each, plus a robot sprite sheet.

## Rough effort
small

## Suggested first task
Seeded maze generator, one tile set, swipe and arrow-key controls, and the streak counter.
