# Pitch: Robot Species Sorting Quiz
date: 2026-10-04
project-target: new
status: approved

## The idea
A short personality quiz of twelve whimsical questions that sorts you into one of sixteen robot species, each with a pre-written profile card and a matching illustration. It reuses the Robot Field Guide look and gives a result that is easy to screenshot and share.

## Why it's worth doing
It needs no LLM at runtime, so it is free for every visitor. Art plan: 16 species portrait cards generated in one style batch, plus a shared result-card frame.

## Rough effort
small

## Suggested first task
Six questions, four species results, and the scoring logic with a result card.
