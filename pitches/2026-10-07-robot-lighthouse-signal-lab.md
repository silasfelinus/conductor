# Pitch: Robot Lighthouse Signal Lab
date: 2026-10-07
project-target: new
status: rejected

## The idea
A tinkering toy where players flip switches and rotate lenses to send light patterns across a painted harbour, and each correct flash sequence guides a tiny paper boat home. Every pattern and boat route is authored in advance, so there is no server logic and no model at runtime.

## Why it's worth doing
It needs no LLM at runtime, so it is free for every visitor. Art plan: 8 harbour backdrops, 12 boat sprites and 6 lens illustrations generated ahead of time.

small

## Suggested first task
One harbour with three switches, a flash-pattern check against a stored sequence, and one boat arriving.
