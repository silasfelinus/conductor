# Pitch: Robot Tarot of Kindness
date: 2026-10-04
project-target: new
status: awaiting-silas

## The idea
A twenty-two card deck of robot archetypes, each with a pre-written upright and reversed meaning about kindness, drawn as a one, three or daily card spread with a card-flip animation. It is a reflective toy that needs no model and no accounts.

## Why it's worth doing
It needs no LLM at runtime, so it is free for every visitor. Art plan: 22 portrait card illustrations in one consistent frame, plus a card back, generated through ArtJobs.

## Rough effort
medium

## Suggested first task
6 cards, the daily draw seeded by date, and the flip animation with meaning text.
