# Pitch: Creek Crossing Cadet
date: 2026-10-10
project-target: kr-arcade
status: rejected

## The idea
An arcade cabinet riffing on Frogger's lane-hopping road and river crossing, where a tiny robot hops across rolling carts and then drifting lily-pad rafts to reach a home dock. Each round adds faster lanes, diving turtles and a bonus firefly for extra points.

## Why it's worth doing
It needs no LLM at runtime, so it is free for every visitor. Art plan: Cabinet marquee, robot hop sprite sheet, cart and raft sprites, dock icons and 3 creek backdrops generated ahead of time.

## Rough effort
small

## Suggested first task
Intro splash, four road lanes and four river lanes, five home docks, a timer, rising speed and a score submit.
