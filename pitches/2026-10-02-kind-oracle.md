# Pitch: Kind Oracle
date: 2026-10-02
project-target: new
status: approved

## The idea
A 22-card oracle deck of Kind Robots, each card a full piece of art with an upright and a reversed meaning, where you draw a one, three or five card spread and read a pre-written reading assembled from hand-authored pair and position texts. Every combination is written ahead of time, so it feels personal and costs nothing to run.

## Why it's worth doing
It needs no LLM at runtime, so it is free for every visitor. Art plan: 22 card faces plus a back through ArtJobs under one style bible; spread layouts composed in code.

## Rough effort
medium

## Suggested first task
Write the 22 card meanings and the 3-card position grammar, then a draw page with flip animation.
