# Pitch: Pocket Pet Robot
date: 2026-10-02
project-target: new
status: approved

## The idea
A tiny virtual pet you adopt, name, feed, play with and put to bed, whose mood sprites and room scenes are all pre-generated and whose state lives in your own browser. It grows through three forms over a couple of weeks of casual visits and never needs a server or a token.

## Why it's worth doing
It needs no LLM at runtime, so it is free for every visitor. Art plan: 3 forms x 8 mood sprites and 6 room backdrops, generated for sprite-sheet consistency.

## Rough effort
medium

## Suggested first task
The needs and decay model as a pure tested engine, then a one-room page with placeholder sprites.
