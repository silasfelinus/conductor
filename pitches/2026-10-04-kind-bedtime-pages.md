# Pitch: Kind Bedtime Pages
date: 2026-10-04
project-target: new
status: awaiting-silas

## The idea
Thirty short pre-written bedtime stories, each a few illustrated pages with a gentle ending, that read aloud with the browser's built-in voice and dim the screen as the story winds down. A favourites shelf remembers which stories you loved on this device.

## Why it's worth doing
It needs no LLM at runtime, so it is free for every visitor. Art plan: 4 soft watercolour illustrations per story, 120 total, generated in batches by theme.

## Rough effort
medium

## Suggested first task
Three stories with art, the page reader with read-aloud toggle, and the night-light dimmer.
