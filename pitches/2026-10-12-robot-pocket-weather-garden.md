## The idea
A calm idle toy where a tiny robot tends a window garden, and the player taps clouds to bring sun, rain or breeze so seedlings grow into pre-illustrated flowers. Each flower has a short pre-written note, and the collection is saved locally with no accounts.

## Why it's worth doing
It needs no LLM at runtime, so it is free for every visitor. Art plan: Twelve flower growth sets, three weather overlays and a gardener robot sheet generated ahead of time.

## Suggested first task
One window box with three weather taps, a growth timer, four flowers and a saved collection page.
