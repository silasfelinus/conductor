# Pitch: Robot Sky Lantern Letters
date: 2026-10-09
project-target: new
status: rejected

## The idea
A quiet writing toy where players pick words from a fixed rhyme bank to compose a short kind note, then watch it rise as a glowing paper lantern over a pre-painted night festival. Every word, rhyme and lantern pattern ships in a static file, so no model or server is needed.

## Why it's worth doing
It needs no LLM at runtime, so it is free for every visitor. Art plan: 8 festival night backdrops and 20 lantern designs generated ahead of time; 200 words in 12 rhyme groups pre-written.

small

## Suggested first task
One night backdrop, a twelve-word rhyme picker, a lantern that rises and fades, and a saved keepsake page.
