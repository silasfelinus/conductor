# Pitch: Kind Garden Idle
date: 2026-10-04
project-target: new
status: awaiting-silas

## The idea
A slow idle game where small acts of kindness grow a garden over real time, each seed type taking minutes or hours to bloom into a collectible plant. Everything runs from local timers and a fixed table of plants, so it costs nothing to run and nothing to keep.

## Why it's worth doing
It needs no LLM at runtime, so it is free for every visitor. Art plan: 20 plants with 4 growth stages each, plus a garden backdrop that changes by time of day.

## Rough effort
medium

## Suggested first task
Five seeds, the growth timer logic with offline catch-up, and a tap-to-water plot grid.
