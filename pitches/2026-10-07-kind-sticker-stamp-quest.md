# Pitch: Kind Sticker Stamp Quest
date: 2026-10-07
project-target: new
status: awaiting-silas

## The idea
A daily passport game where each day's small kind act, written in advance, earns a hand-drawn stamp for a booklet that slowly fills with places. Completed pages unlock a bonus sticker sheet, and the whole year of prompts ships as a static file.

## Why it's worth doing
It needs no LLM at runtime, so it is free for every visitor. Art plan: 36 stamp designs and 6 passport page backgrounds generated ahead of time; 120 act prompts pre-written.

## Rough effort
small

## Suggested first task
A passport with seven stamps, a date-based daily unlock, and a page-complete celebration.
