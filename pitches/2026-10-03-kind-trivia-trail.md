# Pitch: Kind Trivia Trail
date: 2026-10-03
project-target: new
status: approved

## The idea
A walking trail of 200 pre-written multiple-choice questions about robots, animals, space and kindness, where each correct answer moves your robot along an illustrated map and wrong answers show a friendly hand-written hint. Every question and hint is written ahead of time by sessions.

## Why it's worth doing
It needs no LLM at runtime, so it is free for every visitor. Art plan: 8 illustrated map regions and 20 landmark pictures, generated through ArtJobs under one style bible.

## Rough effort
medium

## Suggested first task
30 questions in one region, the trail map with a moving robot token, and local progress saving.
