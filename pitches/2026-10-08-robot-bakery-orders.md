# Pitch: Robot Bakery Orders
date: 2026-10-08
project-target: new
status: rejected

## The idea
A cozy sorting game where robot customers hand over pre-written orders and players assemble the matching pastry from layered ingredient cards before the oven timer rings. All orders and dialogue ship in a fixed file, with difficulty ramping by day number.

## Why it's worth doing
It needs no LLM at runtime, so it is free for every visitor. Art plan: 12 customer portraits, 30 ingredient and pastry sprites, and 4 shop backdrops generated ahead of time; 60 orders pre-written.

## Rough effort
medium

## Suggested first task
One shop day with five orders, drag-to-assemble, a timer, and a star rating at closing.
