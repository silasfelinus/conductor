# Pitch: Hidden Robots
date: 2026-10-02
project-target: new
status: approved

## The idea
Twenty big, busy, hand-composed scenes (a night market, a robot orchestra, a flooded library) with a find-list of small characters and objects tucked inside, where sessions look at each finished image and annotate the hit boxes. Tap to find, count down the list, earn a stamp per scene.

## Why it's worth doing
It needs no LLM at runtime, so it is free for every visitor. Art plan: 20 wide scenes generated and curated; hit boxes annotated by a session that views each image.

## Rough effort
medium

## Suggested first task
One scene end to end with a hit-box annotation tool and the tap-to-find reader.
