# Pitch: Kind Sliding Tile Gallery
date: 2026-10-11
project-target: new
status: approved

## The idea
A sliding-tile puzzle toy where each solved board reveals one piece from a pre-generated gallery of Kind Robots art, with captions written ahead of time. Boards grow from three-by-three to five-by-five, and every solve fills a keepsake wall saved locally, with a daily seeded board for a shared challenge.

## Why it's worth doing
It needs no LLM at runtime, so it is free for every visitor. Art plan: 24 gallery illustrations with caption cards and a wooden tile-frame skin generated ahead of time.

small

## Suggested first task
One three-by-three board with a move counter, a solved-reveal screen, a keepsake wall and a daily seeded shuffle.
