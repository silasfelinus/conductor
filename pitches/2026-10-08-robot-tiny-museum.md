# Pitch: Robot Tiny Museum
date: 2026-10-08
project-target: new
status: rejected

## The idea
A collection game where players curate a pocket museum by finding pre-drawn artefacts hidden in painted rooms and placing them in labelled display cases. Each artefact comes with a short pre-written placard, and finished wings unlock new rooms.

## Why it's worth doing
It needs no LLM at runtime, so it is free for every visitor. Art plan: 6 room backdrops, 40 artefact illustrations and 6 display case frames generated ahead of time; 40 placards pre-written.

## Rough effort
small

## Suggested first task
One room with six hidden artefacts, tap-to-collect, drag into cases, and a completed-wing celebration.
