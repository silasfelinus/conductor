# Pitch: Paper Robot Foldables
date: 2026-10-01
project-target: new
status: rejected

## The idea
Printable papercraft nets of Kind Robots characters, from a flat cut-and-fold bookmark up to a standing tabletop robot, each with a numbered fold guide and a difficulty badge. Everything is a pre-rendered PDF or SVG sheet, so families can print and build offline.

## Why it's worth doing
It needs no LLM at runtime, so it is free for every visitor. Art plan: 12 characters unwrapped onto net templates in three difficulty tiers, with textures generated to tile cleanly.

## Rough effort
medium

## Suggested first task
One net template, one character texture, and a print page with fold-line and tab conventions.
