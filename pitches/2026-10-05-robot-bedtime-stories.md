# Pitch: Robot Bedtime Stories
date: 2026-10-05
project-target: new
status: awaiting-silas

## The idea
A calm picture-book reader with ten short, pre-written bedtime stories starring Kind Robots, each with five illustrated pages, a gentle page-turn sound and an auto-dimming night mode. Finishing a story tucks a small sleeping-robot sticker into a collection shelf.

## Why it's worth doing
It needs no LLM at runtime, so it is free for every visitor. Art plan: 50 page illustrations and 10 sleeping-robot stickers generated through ArtJobs ahead of time.

## Rough effort
small

## Suggested first task
One three-page story with page turns, night mode, and the first sticker on the shelf.
