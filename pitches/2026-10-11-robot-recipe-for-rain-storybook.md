# Pitch: Rainy Day Robot Storybook
date: 2026-10-11
project-target: new
status: rejected

## The idea
A pre-written, pre-illustrated branching storybook for rainy afternoons, where a small robot collects puddle-colored ribbons across several short chapters. Readers choose between two paths per page, unlock alternate endings, and keep a ribbon tally saved locally in the browser.

## Why it's worth doing
It needs no LLM at runtime, so it is free for every visitor. Art plan: A cover, ten scene illustrations, a ribbon icon set and a robot character sheet generated ahead of time.

## Rough effort
medium

## Suggested first task
A three-chapter branching story with two endings, page-turn transitions and a saved ribbon tally.
