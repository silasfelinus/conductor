# Pitch: Dune Digger Dash
date: 2026-10-11
project-target: kr-arcade
status: approved

## The idea
An arcade cabinet riffing on Dig Dug's tunnel-digging chases, where a small robot burrows through layered sand to inflate wandering sand-sprites with a pump until they pop. Each round adds more sprites, loose boulders to drop on them, and faster pursuit through the tunnels.

## Why it's worth doing
It needs no LLM at runtime, so it is free for every visitor. Art plan: Cabinet marquee, digger robot sprite sheet, four sprite enemies, boulder and sand-layer tiles and 3 dune backdrops generated ahead of time.

## Rough effort
medium

## Suggested first task
Intro splash, one tunnel grid with digging, a pump attack, boulder drops, lives, rising round speed and a score submit.
