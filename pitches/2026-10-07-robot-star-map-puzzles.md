# Pitch: Robot Star Map Puzzles
date: 2026-10-07
project-target: new
status: rejected

## The idea
Connect-the-dots constellation puzzles where each finished picture is a robot silhouette in the night sky, with a short pre-written legend about the figure. A gentle hint mode highlights the next star, and all puzzles are fixed coordinate lists.

## Why it's worth doing
It needs no LLM at runtime, so it is free for every visitor. Art plan: 20 night-sky backdrops and 20 finished-constellation illustrations generated ahead of time; 20 legends pre-written.

One twelve-star constellation with drag-to-connect, a hint pulse, and a legend reveal.
