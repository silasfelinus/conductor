# Pitch: Kind Paint-by-Number
date: 2026-10-03
project-target: new
status: awaiting-silas

## The idea
Pre-generated Kind Robots illustrations are reduced in the browser to a numbered palette of flat regions, and you tap a colour then tap regions to fill them in, watching the finished picture appear. Progress saves on the device, and a completed canvas unlocks the full-colour original.

## Why it's worth doing
It needs no LLM at runtime, so it is free for every visitor. Art plan: 30 simple bold-outline illustrations generated through ArtJobs, region maps computed offline by a script.

## Rough effort
medium

## Suggested first task
One image, a region-quantize script, and the tap-to-fill canvas with a palette bar and undo.
