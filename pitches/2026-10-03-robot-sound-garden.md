# Pitch: Robot Sound Garden
date: 2026-10-03
project-target: new
status: awaiting-silas

## The idea
A calm music toy where tapping plants flowers that each play a note from a pentatonic scale, and the garden loops into a gentle, never-wrong song built with Web Audio. Pick a mood and the scale, tempo and flower art change, and nothing is generated at runtime.

## Why it's worth doing
It needs no LLM at runtime, so it is free for every visitor. Art plan: 5 mood backdrops and 8 flower sprites per mood, generated and cleaned in batches.

## Rough effort
medium

## Suggested first task
One mood, 8 flowers, the Web Audio sequencer, and tap-to-plant with a looping playhead.
