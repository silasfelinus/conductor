# Pitch: Robot Dodger
date: 2026-10-07
project-target: kr-arcade
status: awaiting-silas

## The idea
An arcade cabinet riffing on the Frogger lane-crossing mechanic, where a small robot hops across belts of conveyor traffic to reach a charging dock. Speed rises each crossing and the score goes to the cabinet leaderboard.

## Why it's worth doing
It needs no LLM at runtime, so it is free for every visitor. Art plan: Cabinet marquee, one robot sprite sheet, 4 traffic sprites and 3 backdrops generated ahead of time.

## Rough effort
small

## Suggested first task
Intro splash, five lanes, collision check, rising speed per crossing, and a score submit.
