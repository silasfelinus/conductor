# Pitch: Robot Sticker Album
date: 2026-10-01
project-target: new
status: awaiting-silas

## The idea
A collectible album where each day of visiting opens one pre-generated sticker pack that you peel and place onto themed album pages, with shiny and rare variants decided by a seeded draw. Progress and placements stay in your browser, and a completed page unlocks a pre-written page of lore.

## Why it's worth doing
It needs no LLM at runtime, so it is free for every visitor. Art plan: 8 album pages and about 160 die-cut stickers with a shiny variant for 40 of them, all generated ahead of time.

## Rough effort
medium

## Suggested first task
The seeded pack-draw engine with tests, then one album page with drag-to-place and persistence.
