# Pitch: Kind Jigsaw
date: 2026-10-01
project-target: new
status: rejected

## The idea
A daily jigsaw cut from a curated piece of Kind Robots art, with a choice of 12, 48 or 120 pieces, edge-first hints and a finished-picture reveal that names the artist-robot and tells its pre-written story. Cut shapes are computed in the browser from a seed, so there is nothing to generate or serve.

## Why it's worth doing
It needs no LLM at runtime, so it is free for every visitor. Art plan: 60 existing gallery images chosen for clear focal areas, each with a short caption written ahead of time.

small

A seeded piece-cutting engine with snap-and-drag, one picture, and the completion card.
