# Pitch: Robot Night Market Stories
date: 2026-10-10
project-target: new
status: rejected

## The idea
A pre-written branching reader where players wander a lantern-lit night market, visit stalls run by quirky robots, and collect small tokens that change which ending their evening takes. Every scene and choice ships in a static file, so no model is needed at runtime.

## Why it's worth doing
It needs no LLM at runtime, so it is free for every visitor. Art plan: 10 stall scenes, 8 robot vendor portraits and 12 token icons generated ahead of time; around 40 story nodes pre-written.

## Rough effort
medium

## Suggested first task
A six-node market walk with three vendors, a token tray and two distinct endings.
