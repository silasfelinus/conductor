# Pitch: Kind Signal Card Deck
date: 2026-10-12
project-target: new
status: awaiting-silas

## The idea
A pre-illustrated deck of twenty-two kindness signal cards, each with a pre-written meaning and a small daily action. The player draws one card a day with a seeded shuffle, and a saved streak shelf shows the cards collected over time with no accounts.

## Why it's worth doing
It needs no LLM at runtime, so it is free for every visitor. Art plan: Twenty-two card illustrations, a card back and a shelf frame generated ahead of time.

small

## Suggested first task
A daily seeded draw page showing one card with its text and a saved shelf of drawn cards.
