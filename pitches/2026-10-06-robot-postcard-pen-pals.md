# Pitch: Robot Postcard Pen Pals
date: 2026-10-06
project-target: new
status: rejected

## The idea
Every day one pre-written postcard arrives from a different robot traveller, with a stamp, a short note about where they are, and a pre-made picture. Collected postcards fill a wall map, and a rolling calendar means the whole year is authored in advance.

## Why it's worth doing
It needs no LLM at runtime, so it is free for every visitor. Art plan: 60 postcard illustrations and 12 stamp designs generated ahead of time; 365 notes pre-written in batches.

small

## Suggested first task
Seven postcards with a daily-unlock rule by date and a wall where they stick.
