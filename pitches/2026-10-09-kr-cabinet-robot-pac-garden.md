# Pitch: Maze Muncher Garden
date: 2026-10-09
project-target: kr-arcade
status: rejected

## The idea
An arcade cabinet riffing on Pac-Man's maze-chomping chase, where a little watering robot waters every seedling in a hedge maze while four wandering weed-bots patrol it. A sun-drop power-up lets it briefly scare the weeds, and each maze adds speed and a new patrol pattern.

## Why it's worth doing
It needs no LLM at runtime, so it is free for every visitor. Art plan: Cabinet marquee, one robot sprite sheet, four weed-bot sprites, seedling and sun-drop icons and 4 hedge backdrops generated ahead of time.

small

## Suggested first task
Intro splash, one hedge maze, four patrol patterns, sun-drop scare mode, rising speed and a score submit.
