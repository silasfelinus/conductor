# Pitch: Robot Garden Sticker Scenes
date: 2026-10-05
project-target: new
status: awaiting-silas

## The idea
A drag-and-drop sticker toy where you decorate a garden backdrop with pre-made robots, flowers and bugs that wiggle when dropped. Scenes save on the device and export as a picture to share, and there is no way to fail, only to arrange.

## Why it's worth doing
It needs no LLM at runtime, so it is free for every visitor. Art plan: 6 backdrops and 40 transparent stickers generated and cut out ahead of time.

## Rough effort
medium

## Suggested first task
One backdrop, eight stickers, drag and drop with wiggle, and a local save.
