# Pitch: Robot Tiny Moon Calendar
date: 2026-10-11
project-target: new
status: rejected

## The idea
A calm collectible toy where each real-world moon phase unlocks a pre-illustrated robot stargazer scene with a short pre-written night story. Players tick off the nights they looked up, and a full lunar cycle unlocks a bonus constellation page, all saved locally with no accounts.

## Why it's worth doing
It needs no LLM at runtime, so it is free for every visitor. Art plan: Eight moon-phase scenes, a bonus constellation page and a robot stargazer character sheet generated ahead of time.

## Rough effort
small

## Suggested first task
A phase-aware calendar page showing today's pre-written scene, a nights-looked-up checklist saved in the browser and one bonus page.
