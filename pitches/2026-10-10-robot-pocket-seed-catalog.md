# Pitch: Robot Pocket Seed Catalog
date: 2026-10-10
project-target: new
status: awaiting-silas

## The idea
A collectible browsing toy where players flip through a pre-illustrated seed catalog, tick off the packets they have sown in a real garden, and unlock bonus pages for each season finished. All entries, tips and blurbs are written ahead of time and saved locally with no accounts.

## Why it's worth doing
It needs no LLM at runtime, so it is free for every visitor. Art plan: 40 seed packet illustrations, 4 seasonal cover pages and 12 plant portraits generated ahead of time.

small

## Suggested first task
A twelve-packet catalog with page flips, a sown checklist saved in the browser and one seasonal bonus page.
