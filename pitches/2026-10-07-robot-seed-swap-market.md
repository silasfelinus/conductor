# Pitch: Robot Seed Swap Market
date: 2026-10-07
project-target: new
status: rejected

## The idea
A cozy trading toy where a stall of pre-drawn robot gardeners offers seed packets in fixed daily swaps, and players grow a window-box garden from what they collect. Swaps rotate on a published schedule, so the whole economy is a lookup table.

## Why it's worth doing
It needs no LLM at runtime, so it is free for every visitor. Art plan: 24 seed packet designs, 6 growth-stage plant sprites per species for 8 species, and 5 stall backdrops generated ahead of time.

## Rough effort
medium

## Suggested first task
One stall with four swaps, a window box with two growth stages, and local save of the collection.
