# Pitch: Robot Riddle Cards
date: 2026-10-04
project-target: new
status: awaiting-silas

## The idea
A deck of two hundred pre-written riddles and knock-knock jokes in the voices of Kind Robots characters, with a flip card that reveals the answer and a groan-o-meter to rate it. A daily riddle is chosen from the date so friends see the same one.

## Why it's worth doing
It needs no LLM at runtime, so it is free for every visitor. Art plan: 20 character portraits used across the deck plus 6 card-frame styles by riddle type.

small

## Suggested first task
Forty riddles, five characters, the flip-card view and the date-seeded daily pick.
