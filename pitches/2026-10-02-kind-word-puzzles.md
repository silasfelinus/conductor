# Pitch: Kind Word Puzzles
date: 2026-10-02
project-target: new
status: rejected

## The idea
A year of pre-authored word searches and mini crosswords built from the Kind Robots lexicon of characters, rewards, places and scenarios, one per day with the date picking the puzzle. Sessions write and validate every grid ahead of time, so the daily puzzle is free for everyone.

## Why it's worth doing
It needs no LLM at runtime, so it is free for every visitor. Art plan: A small header illustration per puzzle theme, about 30 in total, reusing existing gallery art where it fits.

A grid generator with a solvability validator, 14 puzzles, and a daily puzzle page.
