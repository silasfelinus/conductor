# TALKBACK — WonderLab retirement, 2026-08-11

Silas made a direct product decision: retire WonderLab entirely. The museum was useful when he needed a visual inventory as Kind Robots' primary coder, but neither he nor users now visit it. Its continuing component catalog, previews, review admin, routes, CI, and build-time source metadata are maintenance and loading overhead.

Keep the underlying creative win. The 706 polished museum reactions are voice studies, usually paired Bot/Character exchanges. Rebuild them as fresh two-speaker first-party reviews on real Kind Robots objects, preserving distinctive voices and chemistry but not their old component-specific subject matter. The old corpus remains source evidence until replacements are approved.

This was not represented by an existing ready kind-robots roadmap task. The directive and publication gate are recorded in `projects/kind-robots/WONDERLAB-RETIREMENT.md`. Do not resurrect WonderLab-specific checks or component-retention requirements in future cleanup work.
